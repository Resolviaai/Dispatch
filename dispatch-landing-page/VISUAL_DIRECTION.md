# Visual direction

## Creative idea

**A calm creator’s field desk.** Dispatch should feel like a thoughtful editing companion: warm, clear, a little playful, and careful about who makes the final decision. The page combines a soft paper canvas, editorial type, a compact review-desk illustration, and one friendly sloth mascot.

## Mascot personality

The sloth is a patient studio companion: observant, reassuring, quietly funny, and never pushy. He notices a moment and invites a second look; he does not call anything “viral,” decide what is good, or imply that the creator can skip review. Keep his body language relaxed, curious, and helpful. Keep jokes dry and sparse.

## Color and contrast

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm paper | `#F4F1E9` | Main canvas |
| Soft paper | `#FBF9F3` | Cards, preview and note |
| Ink | `#252A26` | Main text and dark section |
| Deep evergreen | `#23483F` | Primary action, strong contrast |
| Quiet teal | `#557B72` | Secondary emphasis and process labels |
| Cocoa | `#765140` | Mascot-adjacent copy |
| Muted orange | `#D8764C` | Small accents only |

## Composition

- Begin with a split editorial hero: concise promise on the left, mascot plus compact illustrated review desk on the right.
- Let whitespace and a few oversized circular outlines suggest depth; keep them behind content.
- Move into a dark evergreen pause, then a pinned interactive workflow story, then a paper-like review section.
- Finish with a dark alpha note and a compact useful footer.
- Treat every device/product screen as an illustration. Label it and avoid false claims of exact shipped UI.

## Avoid

- Copying Clucky’s rooster, poses, shapes, wording, or exact composition.
- Fake customer proof, metrics, integrations, waitlist signup, unsupported cloud promises, or “one-click everything” claims.
- Dense neon gradients, glassmorphism, stock-dashboard filler, decorative icon clutter, and motion that hides content.
