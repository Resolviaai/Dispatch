Data Deletion

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data Dispatch holds about you, including data received from Facebook and Instagram through the Meta Platform.

1. Disconnect a connected platform

You can remove a connected YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or X account at any time:

Dispatch app → Settings → Connected Accounts → Disconnect

This immediately deletes the stored access tokens and account identifiers for that platform. Your Dispatch account, recordings, and clips remain until you request account deletion below.

2. Delete your Dispatch account and all associated data

Email resolviaai@gmail.com with the subject “Dispatch data deletion request” from the email address associated with your Dispatch account.

We verify ownership of the account, then delete the data below and confirm by email. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Your Dispatch profile and authentication record (email address, account ID).
  • All connected-account records and encrypted tokens, including Facebook Page and Instagram identifiers, names, and avatar URLs received from Meta.
  • Recordings, clips, captions, transcripts, and rendered media stored in Dispatch cloud storage.
  • Publication records, schedules, and their metadata.

4. What is not deleted automatically

  • Content you already published to YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram stays on those platforms. Remove it there; deleting your Dispatch account does not remove published posts or videos.
  • Files you exported or that live on your own devices (phone, PC) are under your control and are not touched by a server-side deletion.

5. Revoking access without deleting your account

You can also revoke Dispatch's access from the platform side: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Apps and Websites; Instagram → Apps and websites; Google → Third-party apps with account access.

6. Contact

Questions about deletion: resolviaai@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
