Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Dispatch ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile recording and AI-assisted vertical clip generation platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android application, web studio, and associated cloud services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver video recording, processing, and publishing features:

  • Account Information: Your email address and account ID when you authenticate via Supabase.
  • Video and Audio Content: Videos recorded through the Dispatch camera or submitted for processing. Video chunks are uploaded to your authorized cloud storage (e.g. Cloudflare R2) and/or your connected YouTube account.
  • Third-Party Platform Tokens: When you connect YouTube, Google OAuth tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) are securely encrypted and stored in our cloud token vault to enable direct video uploads and publishing on your behalf.
  • Facebook & Instagram (Meta) Data: When you connect a Facebook Page or an Instagram Professional account, we store the Page ID and name, the Instagram account ID, username and profile picture URL, and the Page/user access tokens issued by Meta. We never receive your Facebook or Instagram password.

2. Google API Services & YouTube Data

Dispatch uses YouTube API Services to allow you to upload raw recorded sessions and publish vertical shorts directly to your YouTube channel.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Dispatch's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use your YouTube data to train generalized AI models, and we do not sell, transfer, or disclose your personal data or Google credentials to any third parties.

By using Dispatch with YouTube, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Revoking Access to Your Google Data

You can view, disconnect, or revoke Dispatch's access to your YouTube and Google account at any time via your Google Security Settings — Third-party apps with account access.

4. How We Process Videos

Videos and audio tracks are transcribed using speech-to-text models and evaluated with Google Gemini AI strictly to identify highlight moments, generate captions, and render vertical clips. We do not retain your raw videos longer than necessary to complete processing and export.

5. Data Security

All authentication credentials and API tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. All communication between the mobile application, cloud workers, and third-party APIs uses TLS/HTTPS encryption.

6. Facebook & Instagram (Meta) Platform Data

Dispatch lets you publish clips to a Facebook Page and to an Instagram Professional account linked to that Page. We request only the Meta permissions needed to identify those assets and publish to them:

  • public_profile — identifies the Facebook account you authorize.
  • pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement — list the Pages you manage and read basic Page information.
  • pages_manage_posts — publishes Reels to your Page.
  • instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish — identifies the Instagram Professional account linked to your Page and publishes Reels to it.

We store the Page and Instagram identifiers, display names, avatar URLs, and the access tokens issued by Meta. Tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and are used only to perform the publishing actions you request. We do not post, message, or modify anything on your behalf beyond the clip you explicitly publish.

We do not sell Meta data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it to train generalized AI models. Meta data is retained only while your account remains connected.

Our use of Meta Platform data complies with the Meta Platform Terms and the Meta Developer Policies.

7. Data Deletion & Retention

You can disconnect any platform at any time in Dispatch under Settings → Connected Accounts → Disconnect, which deletes the stored tokens for that platform. To delete your Dispatch account and the data associated with it, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page or email resolviaai@gmail.com.

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Content you have already published to YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram is not removed from those platforms by deleting your Dispatch account — remove it in the respective platform's settings.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at resolviaai@gmail.com or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
