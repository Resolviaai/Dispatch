How it works

Your phone records. Your PC does the heavy lifting. You just review and approve.

STEP 1 — PHONE

Record your session

Open Dispatch and talk as long as you like. Recording survives screen-off and interruptions; finished sessions upload to your own YouTube channel as private-to-you unlisted footage.

STEP 2 — WINDOWS PC

AI processes locally

The Dispatch companion on your Windows PC fetches the session, transcribes it, and its AI picks the strongest moments. Your PC renders finished vertical clips with burned-in captions — no video leaves your machine unprocessed.

STEP 3 — REVIEW

Approve & publish

Check each suggested clip, fine-tune the selected range if you want, then approve. Approved clips render exactly as previewed and upload to YouTube from your PC.

What you need

Dispatch is a two-part system: the phone app plus a companion app on your own Windows PC.

Android phone

  • Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer
  • Camera and microphone permission
  • A Google/YouTube account for session uploads

Windows PC companion

  • Windows 10/11 PC on the same Wi-Fi
  • Python 3.11 installed from the Dispatch repository
  • FFmpeg (the setup guide walks you through it)
  • Your own Gemini API key for AI clip selection

Installing the app

Android blocked the install with "unknown sources"

That is expected for any app outside the Play Store. Tap Settings → Allow from this source in the dialog (it only trusts this one download), then tap Install again. If it says "App not installed" and you previously installed an early test build of Dispatch, first uninstall the old app (long-press its icon → Uninstall) and install this download again — the first public build uses a new secure signature, and Android never updates across different signatures. Uninstalling clears the old app’s local data, so re-pair it with your PC afterwards.

Where is the Windows companion?

Clone or download the Dispatch repository on your PC and follow the README setup steps. The phone app needs the PC companion running on your Wi-Fi to process sessions.

Something broke — who do I ask?

Open an issue on the GitHub issue tracker and include what you were doing plus any error text.
