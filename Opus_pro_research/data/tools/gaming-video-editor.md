# Gaming Video Editor

The Gameplay layout puts 30% speaker view on top and 70% gameplay below, so the facecam and the action both survive a 9:16 crop.

ClipAnything finds the moments from visual, audio and sentiment cues, including stretches with little to no dialogue.

## Why stream VODs break normal clippers

### The action is not the speaker

A transcript-only clipper misses a clutch play. ClipAnything analyses each frame for objects, scenes, actions, sounds and emotions as well as speech.

### Two things, one frame

The Gameplay layout splits the 9:16 frame 30% facecam over 70% gameplay instead of making you choose between them.

### A VOD is hours long

Videos up to 10 hours are supported, and a recording over 120 minutes typically yields 42 to 55 clips.

## How to edit a gaming video

### Import the VOD

Paste a Twitch, Kick, Medal.tv or YouTube link, or upload a local file. A long stream can take YouTube 2 to 12 hours to make available for import.

### Choose Gameplay layout

Select Gameplay in Clip layout settings and save the template, or set the layout per segment from the Layout dropdown on the canvas.

### Prompt for the moments

Describe what you want clipped. ClipAnything scores each scene and returns the matching moments, usually slightly more clips than ClipBasic.

### Caption, brand and post

Add captions with highlighted keywords, apply your channel template, then download or schedule to your connected accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can it clip gameplay with no commentary?

Yes. ClipAnything uses visual, audio and sentiment cues and explicitly covers videos with little to no dialogue, rating each scene for virality potential.

### What is the Gameplay layout?

A layout with 30% speaker view on top and 70% gameplay below. Turn it on in Clip layout settings, or apply it to a single segment from the canvas.

### Can I clip a stream while it is live?

No. Uploading live videos while they are still streaming is not supported. A short VOD can take YouTube 10 to 30 minutes to make available, a long one 2 to 12 hours.

### How many clips will a long stream produce?

A video over 120 minutes typically produces 42 to 55 clips, and 60 to 120 minutes produces 32 to 42. ClipAnything usually yields slightly more than ClipBasic.
