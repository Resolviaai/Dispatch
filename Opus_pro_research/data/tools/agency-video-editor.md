# Agency Video Editor

Agency work is the same job repeated with different fonts, different platforms and different approvers.

Set a brand template per client and every clip generated with it inherits those fonts, caption style, layout and headline automatically. Collections keep each account's clips separate and searchable by name.

## Why agencies run accounts here

### A brand template per client

Fonts, caption style, reframing layout and the auto headline are saved once per client and applied to every clip generated with that template.

### Collections per account

Organize clips by campaign, client, series or any theme, and search collections by name in the Collections tab of OmniSearch.

### Approval before anything publishes

Share a project by link for review. When an agent drives OpusClip through our MCP server, posting, scheduling, cancelling and sharing return a link a person confirms rather than committing.

## How to run client work in OpusClip

### Create a brand template per client

Set fonts, caption style, reframing layout, Pin first speaker and the auto headline once. Every clip generated with that template picks them up.

### Import the client's footage

Eighteen link sources including YouTube, Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom, Frame.io, Google Drive and Dropbox, plus public MP4 URLs and local uploads up to 10GB per video.

Pull moments by describing them, trim or extend sections, remove filler words, then file the clips into that client's collection.

Share the project for review, schedule the approved clips to the client's connected accounts, and export an XML when an edit needs finishing elsewhere.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can one account handle several clients?

Yes. Brand templates hold each client's fonts, caption style, layout and headline, and collections keep their clips apart by campaign, client or series. Packs add seats, up to four at two packs.

### How do team seats work?

Seats come with packs: additional packs grant more credits and seats, up to a maximum of four seats at two packs. You can add or remove packs and seats each month and the change takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Seats cannot be bought on their own.

### Can clients approve a clip before it goes live?

Yes. Share the project by link for review, then publish or schedule from the project page. Shares created through our MCP server expire after twenty four hours, and posting and scheduling through it return a confirmation link instead of publishing.

### Can we automate the repetitive part of client work?

Yes, with our REST API and MCP server, which cover browsing projects and clips, pulling transcripts, organizing collections, editing clips, exporting downloads and preparing posts. The posting tools hold for human confirmation.

### How can I transcribe a video?

Upload your video file or paste a link to our AI free video transcription tool. The system automatically converts speech to text with over 95% accuracy. Once complete, you can download your transcript in various formats like TXT, SRT, or VTT for captions, blog posts, or other content.

### How accurate are AI YouTube video transcription tools?

AI YouTube video transcript extractors typically achieve 95%+ accuracy for clear audio in English, with strong performance in over 50 other languages. For best results, videos with clear audio and minimal background noise produce the most accurate transcripts, though you can edit any errors afterward.

### What formats can I download YouTube video transcripts in?

YouTube video transcript generators typically allow you to download transcripts in multiple formats including TXT (plain text), SRT (SubRip Subtitle), and VTT (Web Video Text Tracks) files. These different formats support various use cases from content creation to adding captions to videos.

### Can I transcribe someone else's YouTube video?

This is useful for research, content repurposing, or accessibility purposes.

To get a YouTube video transcript, copy the video URL and paste it into a transcript generator tool. Click "Generate Transcript" and the AI will process the audio, delivering a complete text version in under a minute. You can then edit, format, and download the transcript in your preferred format.

Yes, YouTube offers auto transcription for many videos, but these transcripts often contain errors.
