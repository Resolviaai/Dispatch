# Logo Reveal Maker

A logo reveal is the few seconds at the start or end of a video where your mark appears with some style: a light sweep, a burst, a clean build.

Motion Studio in OpusClip Labs makes one from a written idea. Upload your logo, start from the Logo sting template or describe your own reveal, check the shot plan and render it.

No motion design software and no stock template that a thousand channels already use.

## Why make your logo reveal with Motion Studio

### Start from Logo sting

Pick the Logo sting template and edit the prompt to fit your brand.

### Approve the beats first

A timed shot plan shows how the reveal will play before it renders.

### Sound included, or not

Turn generated audio on for a full sting, or off to add your own sound.

## Describe the reveal you want

Be specific about the start, the movement and the final frame. "Start on black, sweep a soft light across the logo, settle on a clean title card" gives Motion Studio far more to work with than "cool logo intro".

Add your logo as a reference and type @ to mention it in the prompt.
