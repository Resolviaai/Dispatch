## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the mic enhancer improve audio quality?

OpusClip's mic enhancer uses AI to analyze your audio waveform, isolate your voice, and remove background noise like hum, echo, and environmental sounds. It then applies professional-grade EQ, compression, and normalization to boost clarity and balance volume. The result is studio-quality sound from any microphone—phone, webcam, or budget USB mic—without manual editing or expensive plugins.

### Can I use the mic enhancer on videos recorded with a phone?

Yes. The mic enhancer works on any video file, including recordings from smartphones, tablets, webcams, and entry-level mics. AI detects and removes device-specific noise like handling rustle, wind interference, and low-frequency hum. Upload your phone video, apply the enhancer, and get broadcast-ready audio in seconds—no additional equipment required.

### Does the mic enhancer work in real time during recording?

The mic enhancer processes uploaded videos, not live recordings. Upload your finished video to OpusClip, apply the enhancement, and preview the cleaned audio instantly. This approach ensures maximum quality because AI analyzes the entire audio track at once, removing noise and balancing levels with precision you can't achieve in real-time processing.

### Will the mic enhancer remove all background noise?

The mic enhancer removes most common background sounds—traffic, fans, keyboard clicks, room echo, and ambient chatter. Extremely loud or overlapping noises may still be partially audible, but AI significantly reduces their impact and keeps your voice clear. For best results, record in the quietest space available and let the enhancer handle the rest.
