## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does OpusClip's video cutter work?

Upload your video and OpusClip generates a transcript. Select any words or sections you want to remove, and the video trims automatically. You can also remove filler words, split clips by topic, and export clean cuts in seconds—no timeline scrubbing required.

### Can I cut videos by text instead of using a timeline?

Yes. OpusClip's video cutter lets you edit by reading and deleting words in the transcript. When you remove text, the corresponding video segments are cut instantly. It's faster and more intuitive than dragging playheads or hunting for frames in a traditional editor.

### Does the video cutter remove filler words automatically?

Absolutely. OpusClip detects "um," "uh," long pauses, and dead air, then removes them in one click. You can preview the cleaned cut before exporting, ensuring your final video is tight, professional, and ready to publish without manual trimming.

### What video formats can I cut and export?

OpusClip's video cutter supports MP4, MOV, and most common formats. You can export trimmed clips in any aspect ratio—vertical for TikTok and Reels, square for Instagram feed, or landscape for YouTube. Each cut is optimized for the platform you choose.
