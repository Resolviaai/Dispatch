# AI Social Media Post Generator

Most post generators write text. The hard part is the video. OpusClip takes a long video, finds the moments worth posting, reframes them to 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9, and adds captions and contextually relevant B-roll.

Then it writes the post. Our AI generates the description and hashtags for each platform you picked, and you edit them before anything publishes.

## Why start from the video

### The clip is the post

ClipAnything reads visual, audio and sentiment cues to pull postable moments from any video type, including footage with little or no dialogue.

### Copy per platform, not one caption reused

Social copy generation runs for each clip and each selected destination, so every account gets text written for it.

### Nothing posts without you

Generated titles, descriptions and hashtags are editable before publishing, and you choose between posting now and a scheduled time.

## How OpusClip generates a post

### Bring the source video

Upload a file or drop a link from YouTube, Google Drive, Vimeo, Zoom, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom and other supported sources.

Pick the clips, the accounts and the time. Descriptions and hashtags are generated per platform for review, then post now or schedule.
