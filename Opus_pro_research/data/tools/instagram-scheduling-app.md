# Instagram Scheduling App

Instagram Reels is a scheduler destination on the Pro plan. Connect a Business or Creator account, choose a date on the calendar, pick the clip, and the Reel goes out at that time.

One honest limit: scheduling runs on the web. The OpusClip iOS app posts immediately from any clip, but time-based scheduling and bulk scheduling are not on mobile yet.

## What to know before you connect

### Professional accounts only

We support Instagram Business and Creator accounts. Personal profiles are not supported, which is the most common connection error.

### 30 to 90 seconds for local uploads

If you upload your own file to the calendar, each clip must run 30 to 90 seconds. One clip outside that range blocks the whole upload.

### Reconnections happen

Instagram expires access tokens for security, so a connected account occasionally needs reauthorising. Reconnect and the queue continues.

## How to schedule Instagram Reels

### Convert to a professional account

Instagram profiles start as Personal. Convert to Business or Creator in Instagram before connecting.

### Connect it in OpusClip

Click the Social Accounts icon, click Add Account, and sign in with the credentials for the specific Instagram Business or Creator account.

### Pick a date and a clip

Click Calendar in the left sidebar, choose the date, then choose the clip and the Instagram account.

### Review and confirm

Check the generated description and hashtags, then schedule. The Reel appears in the calendar where you can still change it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can OpusClip schedule Instagram Reels?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Instagram Reels is one of nine scheduler destinations, and you schedule it from the Calendar page in the web app.

### Does the OpusClip mobile app schedule Instagram posts?

Not yet. The iOS app can post a clip immediately to Instagram and other platforms, but bulk scheduling and time-based scheduled posting are web only for now.

### Why will my Instagram account not connect?

The most common reason is account type: we support Business and Creator accounts, not Personal profiles. Expired sessions and restricted accounts also block the connection, in which case you reconnect and try again.

### How long can a Reel be when I upload my own file?

Between 30 and 90 seconds for local uploads scheduled from the calendar. If you upload several clips and any one falls outside that range, the entire upload is blocked until the timing is fixed.
