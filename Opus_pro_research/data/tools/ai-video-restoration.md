# AI Video Restoration

Upscale uses AI to make blurry or low resolution footage sharper, clearer and more high definition, with no editing experience needed. Videos up to 5 minutes are supported, and the Upscale button shows the base credit cost before you start.

It is available on Pro, Max and Business. Finished videos stay in the History tab for 7 days.

## What restoration looks like in practice

### One button in Quick Start

Upscale sits in the Quick Start menu behind the diamond icon. Paste a YouTube link, upload a file, or import from Google Drive.

### The price is on the button

The Upscale button shows the base charge, for example 23 credits. 4K upscaling adds 11 credits per 10-second block, rounded up.

### Seven days to collect it

Finished videos appear in the History tab with a Download button, and stay available for 7 days.

## How to restore a video with AI

### Open Upscale

From the Quick Start menu, click Upscale, marked with the diamond icon.

### Add the footage

Paste a YouTube link, upload a file from your device, or import from Google Drive. Videos up to 5 minutes long are supported.

### Check the preview and the cost

Review the preview, then click Upscale. The button shows the base credit charge, with an additional amount if 4K upscaling applies.

### Download from History

When processing finishes, open the History tab and click Download. The upscaled video is available for 7 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does OpusClip have AI video restoration?

We ship Upscale, which uses AI to make blurry or low resolution footage sharper and more high definition. We could not find a separate tool for film damage such as scratches or missing frames.

### How long can the video be?

Up to 5 minutes per upscale. Longer recordings are better handled as a clipping project, where we crop rather than compress and hold a 1080p floor.

### What does it cost in credits?

The base charge is shown on the Upscale button, for example 23 credits. On Pro, Max and Business, 4K upscaling adds 11 credits for every 10-second block, rounded up, so 30 seconds adds 33 credits.

### Where do I find the finished file?

In the History tab, where each completed upscale has a Download button. Files stay available for 7 days, so save them locally.
