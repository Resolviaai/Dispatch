## What HD actually means here

### A 1080p floor

If either the width or the height would fall below 1080, we upsample the output to 1080x1920 so the minimum resolution stays 1080p.

### No quiet downscaling

We will not compress your video to a lower resolution. We crop it, and the output size follows the input size.

### 4K only when it is real 4K

4K export is not upscaling. It activates when your source video is genuinely 3840x2160.
