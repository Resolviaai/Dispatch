Paste an Apple Podcast link, a YouTube link, or a recording from Riverside, Zoom, Dropbox or Google Drive. OpusClip transcribes the episode and uses that transcript to find moments, generate captions and cut vertical clips.

## How podcast transcription works

### Bring the episode

Paste an Apple Podcast or YouTube link, or upload the recording from Riverside, Zoom, Dropbox, Google Drive or your computer. Links have to be public or unlisted.

### We transcribe it

The episode is transcribed in one of more than 20 supported languages, and that transcript drives captions and moment detection.

### Correct and clean

Edit words inline in the transcript, then use Speech Cleanup for filler words and pauses and Speech Enhancement for the audio itself.

### Export both outputs

Download the transcript as .txt and the subtitles as .srt from the More menu, and export or schedule the clips from the project page.

## What comes with a transcribed episode

### Apple Podcast import

Episode links from Apple Podcast are supported alongside YouTube, Vimeo, Riverside, Zoom, StreamYard, Kick and Twitch.

### Transcript download

Download Transcript gives a .txt of the original recording for show notes or a write-up.

### Subtitle download

Download Subtitles gives a .srt, compatible with YouTube and other video platforms.

### Speaker aware clipping

ClipAnything identifies which speaker is talking and understands sounds such as laughter and cheering, not only the words.

### Multi guest layouts

Split, Three and Four layouts show two, three or four people when they share the original frame. Pin first speaker keeps your host in the top slot.

### Speech Enhancement

Noise reduction, background noise removal, voice isolation and voice volume boost run together on the episode audio.

### Post or schedule from the project

Use the calendar icon on a clip to schedule, or Bulk Schedule several at once, and manage or cancel them from the Calendar page.

### Transcripts over the API

The transcripts endpoint returns paragraphs with start and end times, text, an optional speaker field and per-word timings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can OpusClip transcribe a podcast from a link?

Yes. Apple Podcast and YouTube links are supported import sources, and we transcribe the episode as part of processing the project.

### How do I get the transcript as a file?

Open the project result page or My projects, click More, then Download Transcript for a .txt file. Download Subtitles in the same menu gives a .srt.

### Does it label who is speaking?

ClipAnything identifies which speaker is talking, and the transcripts API returns an optional speaker field on each paragraph. We could not find documentation that the downloadable .txt transcript carries speaker labels.

### What languages are supported?

More than 20 languages are supported for video. Languages that read right to left, including Arabic, Hebrew and Persian, are not supported today.
