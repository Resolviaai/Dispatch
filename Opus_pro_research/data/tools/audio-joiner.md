## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the audio joiner handle different file formats?

OpusClip's audio joiner automatically converts and normalizes MP3, WAV, M4A, and other formats during the merge process. You upload mixed formats, and the tool outputs a single file in your chosen export format—no manual conversion needed. Audio levels are balanced so all clips play at consistent volume.

### Can I rearrange audio clips after uploading them to the joiner?

Yes. After upload, you can drag clips into any order on the timeline. The audio joiner updates the preview in real time, so you can test different sequences and hear transitions before exporting. Reordering is instant and requires no re-upload.

### Does the audio joiner add silence or gaps between clips?

No. By default, the audio joiner places clips end-to-end with smooth fade transitions. If you need silence between segments, you can manually insert pauses in the timeline. Otherwise, your merged file plays continuously without gaps.

### What audio formats can I export after joining files?

You can export your joined audio as MP3, WAV, or AAC. These formats are optimized for video editing platforms like Premiere, Final Cut, and mobile editors. Choose the format that matches your workflow, and OpusClip handles encoding automatically.
