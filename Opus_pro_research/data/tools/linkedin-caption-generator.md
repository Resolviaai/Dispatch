# LinkedIn Caption Generator

Choose a clip and your LinkedIn account in OpusClip and our AI generates the description and hashtags for that post, written from the video rather than from a prompt you have to compose first.

You edit the wording in the posting window before anything publishes, and the same clip posted elsewhere gets its own separate copy.

## Why generate the copy here

### It starts from what was said

Every clip carries a transcript, so the description is grounded in the argument in the video instead of a topic you summarise again.

### One caption per destination

Social copy generation runs for each clip and each destination, so the LinkedIn version differs from the TikTok one.

### Fix the on-screen words too

Separate from the post copy, you can correct caption wording in the clip without touching the video or its timing.

## Written from the transcript

Every clip has a transcript behind it, and that is what the copy is generated from. You can also fetch the transcript through our API or MCP server.

That makes the description specific to the clip instead of a restatement of its title.

Yes. When you pick a clip and a LinkedIn account, our AI auto-generates the description and hashtags for that post, and the copy is editable in the posting window before you publish.

Yes. Social copy generation runs for each clip and each destination, so posting the same clip to LinkedIn and TikTok produces separate descriptions and hashtags.

Yes. Send a POST to the social copy jobs endpoint on api.opus.pro and poll the job for the result. The same capability is exposed as a tool on our MCP server.

Yes. A caption fix changes the wording in place without touching the video or its timing. There is no bulk caption edit, so clips are corrected one at a time.
