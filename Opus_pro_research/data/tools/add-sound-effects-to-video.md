# Add Sound Effects to Video

Sound design is the difference between a clip people scroll past and one they watch twice. It is also the part of editing most creators skip, because placing every pop, whoosh and hit by hand takes forever.

Auto SFX does it for you. It watches your video, detects the key moments and places production-quality sound effects on your timeline, matched to the content and tone of the clip.

Keep what works, swap what does not, and you are done.

## How to add sound effects to a video

### Open Auto SFX

Click Auto SFX in the Quick Start on the homepage, or open a clip in the editor and click Audio.

### Click Auto generate sounds

OpusClip analyzes the video and adds sound effects at the key moments.

### Review the blocks

Each effect appears as a colored block on the timeline. Play it back and adjust the volume or duration.

### Swap or add sounds

Edit a block's prompt to regenerate it, or generate a new sound from a prompt.
