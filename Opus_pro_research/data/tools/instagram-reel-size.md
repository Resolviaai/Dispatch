# Instagram Reel Size

An Instagram Reel is 1080x1920 pixels at a 9:16 ratio. That part is easy to look up.

What costs you time is reformatting a landscape recording into it without cutting the speaker out of shot, and keeping your captions clear of the buttons Instagram lays over the bottom of the frame.

OpusClip handles both. Reframe Anything tracks your subject as the frame narrows, and captions reposition to stay readable.

## Why Reels start here

### Exact Reel dimensions

Export at 1080x1920 and 9:16 every time. No guessing, no Instagram re-compressing something it had to pad first.

### The speaker stays centred

Reframe Anything tracks the key objects and actions across frames, so going from a 16:9 recording to a vertical Reel does not decapitate your presenter.

### Captions clear of the interface

Instagram puts its own buttons and text over the frame edges. Captions are placed to stay readable underneath all of it.

Instagram Reels are 1080x1920 pixels at a 9:16 aspect ratio, and can run up to 3 minutes.

### What is the Instagram Reel safe zone?

Instagram overlays the username, caption and action buttons on the frame. Keep important content out of roughly the bottom fifth and clear of the right edge so nothing sits behind a button.

### Can I turn a landscape video into a Reel?

Yes. Upload the landscape recording, choose 9:16, and Reframe Anything tracks the subject across frames so they stay in shot as the aspect ratio changes.

### What size is a Reel cover photo?

The cover is 1080x1920, but the profile grid crops it to a square, so keep faces and text near the centre.
