# Animated Logo Maker

Upload your logo, describe how it should move, and Motion Studio in OpusClip Labs turns it into an animated logo for intros, outros and brand videos.

Before anything renders you get a shot plan with timed beats to review and change, so the animation matches what you had in mind.

No keyframes, no motion design software and no template that looks like everyone else's.

## Why brands animate their logo with Motion Studio

### A plan before the render

Motion Studio writes a beat-by-beat shot plan from your prompt. Fix the timing or the idea before you spend credits on a render.

### Your real logo, not a lookalike

Add your logo file as a reference and mention it in the prompt, so the animation is built around your actual mark.

### Styles that fit your brand

Choose a visual style, length, aspect ratio and audio, or start from the Logo animation template for a head start.

## Write the motion, not the keyframes

Describe the subject, the movement, the pacing and the final frame. For example: an 8-second logo animation that starts on a dark background, reveals the logo with a soft light sweep and ends on a clean title card.

Short, concrete prompts work best. If the first plan is not right, revise the idea or edit the shot plan before you submit.
