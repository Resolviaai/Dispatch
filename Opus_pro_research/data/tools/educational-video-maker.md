# Educational Video Maker

Full length recordings get watched once, if at all. Cut them into one clip per concept and each idea can stand on its own.

Import the recording and our Screenshare layout keeps the slides on the top half of the frame and the instructor on the bottom half, automatically.

## Why educators start with the recording they have

### Slides and instructor together

Screenshare layout places the screen on the top half and the speaker on the bottom half. It needs screenshare content and one speaker, and falls back to another layout when no screenshare is identified.

### Terminology stays correct

We provide features that keep proper nouns, technical vocabulary and key terms transcribed consistently in a clip or across every clip in the course.

### Dubbing you can correct

Once a clip is dubbed, open it in the editor and the translated transcript appears in the left panel, where you can click any word and fix it.

## How to make an educational video

### Import the recording

Paste a link from Zoom, Zoom Clips, YouTube, Vimeo, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom, Google Drive or Dropbox, or upload a file up to 10GB. The link must be public or unlisted.

### Cut it by concept

Describe each idea you want as its own lesson and preview the matching clips before you keep them.

### Set layout, captions and terms

Apply Screenshare layout for slide heavy sections, style the captions once in a brand template, and set the terminology you want transcribed consistently.

### Export or publish

Export in full 4K when your source is genuinely 3840 x 2160, or schedule each lesson to YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or X from the project page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can I make an educational video from?

Any recording we can import: YouTube, Vimeo, Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom, Frame.io, Google Drive, Dropbox, Apple Podcast, public MP4 URLs and local uploads up to 10GB per video. Links need to be public or unlisted.

### Can the slides and the instructor both stay on screen?

Yes, with the Screenshare layout, which puts the screen on the top half of the frame and the speaker on the bottom half. It requires screenshare content and one speaker, and we apply an alternative layout if no screenshare is identified.

### Can I publish lessons to YouTube on a schedule?

Yes. You can publish or schedule to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and X from the project page. To connect a YouTube channel you need to be set as a manager of that brand account.

### Can I translate a lesson for another cohort?

Yes, through dubbing. When the dubbed clip opens in the editor, the translated transcript appears in the left panel and you can click any word to correct it before you export.
