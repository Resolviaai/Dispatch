# Convert Video to HD

Footage shot years ago, exported at a small size or squeezed by a messaging app rarely holds up on modern screens.

OpusClip's Upscale feature uses AI to turn low-resolution video into a sharper, clearer, more high-definition version. Add the video, click Upscale and download the result from your History tab.

On Pro, Max and Business you can go further and upscale to 4K.

## Why convert to HD with OpusClip

### AI, not stretching

Resizing a small video just makes a bigger blurry one. Upscale uses AI to bring back clarity as it raises the definition.

### No editor needed

There are no export settings or codecs to choose. Add the video and click one button.

### Works from a link

Paste a YouTube link or import from Google Drive instead of hunting for the original file.

## Rescue 480p and 720p footage

Old vlogs, archive interviews, webinar recordings and screen captures are often stuck at 480p or 720p. Upscale is designed for exactly that: low-resolution, compressed or pixelated video.

It sharpens what is there and makes it look more high definition, so it holds up next to the rest of your content.
