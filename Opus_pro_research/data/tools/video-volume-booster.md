# Video Volume Booster

Speech Enhancement lifts low or uneven speech to a clear, professional level, so quiet recordings are no longer too soft to hear. It applies recommended defaults, then hands you two sliders: Noise reduction and Voice enhancement.

Turn it on in the editor and your adjustments apply as you make them.

## Why the volume problem is rarely only volume

### Voice Volume Boost

Lifts low or uneven speech to a clear, professional level rather than raising everything in the mix by the same amount.

### Two sliders, sensible defaults

Noise reduction sets how much background noise is removed. Voice enhancement sets how loud and clear the voice sounds. Both start at a recommended default.

### Music sits under the voice

Music added from the Audio panel defaults to 15% volume, with its own slider for how loud it sits beneath your video.

## Audio controls in the editor

### Voice volume boost

Lifts low or uneven speech so quiet recordings are no longer too soft to hear.

### Voice enhancement slider

Controls how much voice volume and clarity are boosted, starting from a recommended default.

### Noise reduction slider

Controls how much background noise is removed from the track.

### Voice isolation

Separates the primary voice from other audio and creates a separate audio track in the editor.

### Background noise removal

Filters out environmental sounds to leave a cleaner voice track.

### Music volume control

Music you add sits at 15% by default, with a slider for how loud it sits under the video.

### Sound effect levels

Sound effects appear as coloured blocks on the timeline, and each block has its own Volume and Duration controls.

### Plan availability

Speech and voice enhancement are not available to trial users.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I increase the volume of a video in OpusClip?

Turn on Speech Enhancement in the editor and drag the Voice enhancement slider, which boosts voice volume and clarity. It starts at a recommended default you can change at any time.

### Does boosting the volume also boost the background noise?

No, because the same pass reduces background noise and isolates the voice. Noise reduction and Voice enhancement are separate sliders, so you can lift the voice while pulling the background down.

### Can I set music and speech levels separately?

Yes. Music added from the Audio panel has its own volume slider and sits at 15% by default, and each sound effect block on the timeline has its own Volume control.

### Is it available on every plan?

Trial users cannot use speech or voice enhancement. On a paid plan, open a clip in the editor and Speech Enhancement is there with its defaults applied.
