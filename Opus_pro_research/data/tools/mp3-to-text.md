# MP3 to Text

OpusClip accepts speech audio in MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG and WEBA. Upload the file and we transcribe it, and the transcript becomes the script behind captions, clips and a finished video.

Audio-only uploads have to contain speech. For an instrumental track, use Song to Video instead.

## Why transcribe with OpusClip

### Speech audio in, text out

Upload a .wav or .mp3 and we transcribe it. The transcript is used directly as the script, so you are not copying text between tools.

### More than 20 languages

OpusClip supports videos in over 20 languages. Languages that read right to left, including Arabic, Hebrew and Persian, are not supported today.

### The text stays editable

Click any word or segment in the transcript to fix it inline. Choose Apply, or Apply to all, and the change shows in both the transcript and the video.

## How to go from MP3 to text

### Upload the audio

Open the Add audio dialog and drop in your MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG or WEBA file. You can also paste a public audio or video link and we fetch it and pull the audio out.

### We transcribe the speech

The file is transcribed and the text is used directly as the transcript. In Story Mode, a transcript can run up to 450 words, roughly four minutes of audio.

### Edit the words

Click a word or a segment in the transcript to correct it. Use Speech Cleanup to remove filler words and pauses in one pass.

### Export the text or the video

For a video project, download the transcript as .txt and the subtitles as .srt, or keep going and let the transcript drive captions and clips.

## What you get with the transcript

### Transcript as .txt

Pro plan users can download the transcript of a project's original video from the result page or from My projects, under the More menu.

### Subtitles as .srt

The same More menu exports a .srt subtitle file, the format YouTube and other video platforms accept.

### Inline transcript editing

A text field with Apply, Apply to all and Cancel opens on any word or segment you click.

### Speech Cleanup

Remove filler words such as um, uh and like, or remove pauses. Filler word detection is English only today.

### Speech Enhancement

Reduce background noise, isolate the voice and boost quiet speech before the words get locked in.

### Links as well as files

Paste a public video or audio URL in the Add audio dialog and we fetch it and pull the audio out.

### Reusable in your library

Audio you upload is saved to Media and the Asset Library, so you can use it in another clip without uploading again.

### Transcripts over the API

A GET request to the transcripts endpoint returns paragraphs with start and end times in seconds, text, an optional speaker field and per-word timings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What audio formats can I upload?

MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG and WEBA. The upload has to contain speech, so for an instrumental track use Song to Video instead.

### How long can the audio be?

In Story Mode the transcript cannot exceed 450 words, roughly four minutes of audio. Longer recordings are better handled as a video project.

### Can I download the text as a file?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Open the project result page or My projects, click More, then Download Transcript for a .txt file or Download Subtitles for a .srt file.

### Will it transcribe languages other than English?

OpusClip supports more than 20 languages for video. Filler word detection is English only today, and languages that read right to left are not supported.
