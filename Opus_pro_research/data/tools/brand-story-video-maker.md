### How does OpusClip create brand story videos from my long-form content?

OpusClip uses AI to analyze your long-form videos like founder interviews, webinars, and testimonials. It identifies the most emotionally compelling moments that communicate your brand values, mission, and differentiation. Each segment is scored for virality and audience impact. You can then customize these clips with your logo, brand colors, captions, and templates to create cohesive brand story videos ready for every platform.

### Can I customize brand story videos with my own logo and colors?

Yes. OpusClip includes a brand kit feature where you save your logo, color palette, fonts, and layout preferences once. Every brand story video you create can then apply these settings instantly, ensuring visual consistency across all your social channels. You can also adjust captions, overlays, and templates on a per-clip basis for specific campaigns or audiences.

### What video formats work best for brand story videos on social media?

OpusClip exports brand story videos in vertical (9:16 for TikTok, Reels, Shorts), square (1:1 for Instagram feed), and horizontal (16:9 for YouTube, LinkedIn) formats. AI auto-reframes each clip to keep speakers centered and text readable. You can generate all three aspect ratios from a single upload, so your brand story reaches audiences on every platform without manual resizing.

### How long should my brand story videos be for maximum engagement?

OpusClip automatically creates clips optimized for each platform's algorithm. For TikTok and Reels, brand story videos typically perform best between 15 and 60 seconds. For LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts, 30 to 90 seconds works well. AI scoring helps you identify the exact length and hook that will drive the most views and shares for your specific brand content.
