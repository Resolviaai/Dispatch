## How it Works

### Upload your first video

Start by uploading the first video you want to join. Once the upload finishes, you'll be taken straight into the OpusClip Editor with your clip already open.

### Open the Media panel

Inside the Editor, click **Media** in the right-side navigation, then click the file upload area to upload your second video.

### Add the second clip to your timeline

Once your second video finishes uploading, click it to add it to the timeline next to your first clip. Drag clips to reorder, trim them, or arrange them however you want.

### Reframe, edit, and export

Reframe your joined video for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, or LinkedIn, add captions or brand templates, and export directly from the Editor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I join two videos together in OpusClip?

OpusClip doesn't have a one-page video joiner where you drop in multiple clips at once. Joining happens inside our Editor in a few steps: 1) Upload your first video — once it finishes uploading, you'll be taken straight to the Editor with your clip open. 2) Click 'Media' in the right-side navigation. 3) Click the file upload area to upload your second video. 4) When the second video finishes uploading, click it to add it to your timeline next to the first clip. From there, you can trim, reorder, reframe, and export.

### Can I reorder or trim clips after joining videos together?

Yes. Once both clips are on your timeline in the OpusClip Editor, you can drag to reorder them, trim by deleting words from the transcript, or split clips and rearrange them. Changes apply instantly, so you can preview and export as many versions as you need.

### What video formats and aspect ratios can I use?

OpusClip's Editor accepts MP4, MOV, AVI, and WebM files. After joining your clips on the timeline, you can export in 16:9 (YouTube, LinkedIn), 9:16 (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), 1:1 (Instagram feed), or 4:5 (Facebook, Pinterest). Our auto-reframe feature detects speakers and action, then crops intelligently for each aspect ratio — no manual adjustments required.
