# Sermon Clips

Upload the service recording, up to 10 hours long. A video over 120 minutes typically yields 42 to 55 clips, each one scored for how far it is likely to travel.

Captions, a 9:16 crop that follows the speaker, and a posting schedule come with it.

## Why a full service does not post well

### The point is in the middle

A prompt pulls the specific moments you want, and you can restrict processing to a timeframe so the worship set is not clipped as teaching.

### One speaker, one frame

Fill layout focuses on the speaker and crops them to fill a 9:16 screen, with subject tracking holding them in frame as they move.

### Captions reach further

Captions are supported in over 25 languages, and you can upload your own SRT when the transcript has to be exact.

## One recording, a month of posts

Clip counts scale with length: 30 to 60 minutes gives 23 to 32 clips, 60 to 120 minutes gives 32 to 42, and over 120 minutes gives 42 to 55.

Group them in a collection, bulk download the set, or spread them across the month from the calendar.

## How to make sermon clips

### Upload the service

Upload a local file up to 10 GB and 10 hours, or paste a link from YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook, Google Drive, Dropbox or a public .mp4 URL.

### Narrow it down

Clip a specific timeframe to skip announcements and music, and set the clip length instead of the Auto 0 to 3 minute default.

### Caption and brand it

Apply your brand template for fonts, colours, caption style and intro and outro cards, then check the keywords it highlights.

### Schedule the month

Post from the project page, or choose dates on the calendar where descriptions and hashtags are drafted for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many sermon clips will one service produce?

A recording over 120 minutes typically yields 42 to 55 clips. A 60 to 120 minute service yields 32 to 42, and 30 to 60 minutes yields 23 to 32.

### How long a recording can I upload?

Up to 10 hours, with local uploads capped at 10 GB. For a longer recording, select a specific portion to process instead.

### Can I stop it clipping the worship set?

Yes. Clip a specific timeframe so only the teaching section is processed, or write a prompt describing the moments you want.

### Is there a sermon-specific feature in OpusClip?

We could not find one in the help centre. Sermon clips use the same clipping, captioning, layout and scheduling tools as any long recording, configured through a brand template.
