# Interview Transcription

Upload the recording or paste a link from Zoom, Riverside, StreamYard, Google Drive or YouTube. OpusClip transcribes the conversation and reads it alongside the video, so a clip keeps the question and the answer together.

For panels and multi guest interviews, the Three and Four layouts place three or four speakers on screen when they appear together in the original frame.

## Built for conversations, not monologues

### Speaker aware by design

ClipAnything identifies which speaker is talking, and Split, Three and Four layouts show several people when they share the original frame.

### Your interviewer stays put

Pin first speaker is a switch in your Brand Template that keeps the first speaker in the top slot of a Split, Three or Four layout.

### Timings down to the word

The transcripts endpoint returns paragraphs with start and end times in seconds, text, an optional speaker field and per-word timings.

## Quote the answer accurately

Click any word or segment in the transcript and a text field opens with Apply, Apply to all and Cancel. A name or a piece of jargon gets fixed once, and the change shows in the transcript and the video.

## How to transcribe an interview

### Add the recording

Paste a Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Google Drive, Dropbox or YouTube link, or upload the file from your computer.

### We transcribe and read it

The conversation is transcribed, and ClipAnything uses the transcript and the picture together to find complete exchanges rather than half answers.

### Fix and tidy

Correct wording inline, remove filler words and pauses with Speech Cleanup, and run Speech Enhancement if the room was noisy.

### Choose the layout

Pick Split, Three or Four for multi speaker clips, turn on Pin first speaker, then export the clips, the .txt transcript or the .srt subtitles.

## Features for interview footage

### Split layout

Shows both speakers on screen, and works when both appear together in the original video frame.

### Three and Four layouts

Place three or four speakers on screen, suited to panel discussions and group interviews.

### Pin first speaker

A Brand Template switch that keeps the first speaker in the top slot of Split, Three and Four layouts.

### Screenshare layout

Puts shared screen content on the top half and the speaker on the bottom half when a screenshare is detected.

### Inline transcript edits

Apply a fix to one instance, or use Apply to all for a name that recurs through the interview.

### Speech Cleanup

Removes filler words and silences at or above 0.5 seconds by default, with bad takes highlighted in the transcript.

### Transcript and subtitle files

Download Transcript for .txt and Download Subtitles for .srt from the project More menu.

### API and MCP access

Pull transcripts through the REST API, or use the MCP server to fetch a transcript and apply editing operations from your agent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can OpusClip transcribe an interview with several speakers?

Yes. The interview is transcribed and ClipAnything identifies which speaker is talking, which is also what drives the multi speaker layouts.

### Will the downloaded transcript name each speaker?

The transcripts API returns an optional speaker field on each paragraph. We could not find documentation that the downloadable .txt transcript includes speaker labels, so treat that as unconfirmed.

### What if the guests were recorded in separate frames?

Split, Three and Four layouts only work when the speakers appear together in the original video frame. When they do not, pick another layout or reframe by hand in the crop window.

### Can I correct a misspelled name everywhere at once?

Yes. Click the word in the transcript and choose Apply to all, and the correction applies across the clip in both the transcript and the video.
