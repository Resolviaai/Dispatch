# YouTube Shorts Aspect Ratio

Shorts are vertical. 9:16 is the default frame for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, and anything wider gets cropped or padded to fit it.

The question behind the question is usually what happens to a 16:9 recording. OpusClip reframes it to 9:16 by tracking the subject rather than cropping blind.

## Why a 16:9 recording does not become a Short on its own

### Fill crops, Fit pads

Fill focuses on the speaker and crops them to fill the 9:16 screen. Fit crops your original to 4:3 and adds opaque padding at the top and bottom instead. Two very different looking Shorts.

### Speakers move out of a static crop

Auto Reframe tracks moving and active speakers on screen so the vertical frame follows them, rather than locking to the centre of the original footage.

### The Shorts feed is where the reach is

If your views come mostly from profile visits, search results or shared links, the video is not being picked up by the Shorts feed, which is where the majority of organic reach happens.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the YouTube Shorts aspect ratio?

9:16 vertical. It is the default frame for vertical video including Shorts, Reels and TikTok, so a 16:9 recording has to be cropped or padded to fit it.

### Can I upload a 16:9 video as a Short?

You can, but it will not fill the 9:16 frame. Reframing to 9:16 either crops to fill around the speaker, or fits the whole frame with padding at the top and bottom.

### Does OpusClip reframe Shorts automatically?

Yes. Auto Reframe tracks moving and active speakers on screen, and AI Reframe covers 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9, with Manual Reframe there for shots that need adjusting.

### Can I schedule Shorts to YouTube from OpusClip?

Yes, on plans that include social scheduling. You post or schedule from the project page through YouTube's official API, and scheduled posts can be cancelled before they publish.
