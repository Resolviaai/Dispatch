# Claude Video Editor

OpusClip is listed in the Claude connector directory. Add it, sign in once, and Claude can browse projects and clips, pull transcripts, organize collections, edit clips, export HD downloads and prepare posts for your connected accounts.

Posting, scheduling, unscheduling and project sharing do not commit here: Claude hands back a link you confirm in OpusClip.

## Why this is safer than a custom endpoint

### Two confirmations, not one

Claude asks whether the agent may make the call. OpusClip then asks whether the action may actually happen. Both apply to posting, scheduling, unscheduling and sharing.

### One click to add

Open OpusClip in the Claude connector directory and click Add, then complete the OAuth sign-in. You can also find it under Settings, Connectors by searching OpusClip.

### A documented catalog

The connector exposes the same 29 tools as our MCP server: 13 read tools that prompt for nothing, 14 write tools that ask per call, and 2 deprecated signposts kept for compatibility.

## Getting Claude editing

### Add OpusClip in Claude

Open the OpusClip listing in the Claude connector directory, click Add, and complete the OAuth sign-in with the same identity provider your OpusClip account uses.

### Ask it to look around

"List my recent OpusClip projects" confirms the connection. Any account can connect and see the catalog; tool calls need Pro Beta, Max or Business access.

### Make an edit

Ask for a caption fix, a trim or filler-word removal on a specific clip. The write tool prompts, then the preview re-renders.

### Confirm anything public

When Claude prepares a post or a share link, open the link and confirm it in OpusClip. Nothing publishes on the connector without that step.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Claude edit video?

Yes, through a connected editor. With the OpusClip connector added, Claude calls tools that clip, trim, recaption and export, and the rendering happens in OpusClip rather than in the model.

### Will Claude post to my social accounts without asking?

No. On the connector, posting, scheduling, unscheduling and project sharing do not commit: they return a link you confirm in OpusClip, which is separate from the approval prompt Claude itself shows.

### Can Claude generate video or voiceover here?

Not through the connector. It exposes no image, video or audio generation tools, and options such as clip dubbing are unavailable on it. Thumbnail generation and dubbing live on the MCP server at mcp.opus.pro/mcp.

### What about Claude Code rather than Claude.ai?

Use the MCP server at mcp.opus.pro/mcp, or install the skill with /plugin marketplace add opus-pro/opus-skills and set OPUSCLIP\_API\_KEY in the shell that launches the agent, never in chat.
