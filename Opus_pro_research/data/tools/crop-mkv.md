## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I crop MKV files without losing video quality?

Yes. OpusClip processes MKV files natively without re-encoding, so your cropped output retains the original codec, bitrate, and resolution. You select the crop area and aspect ratio, and we export a new MKV file with only the spatial dimensions changed. No generational quality loss occurs because we do not transcode the video stream unless you explicitly choose a different format for export.

### How do I crop an MKV video to vertical format for TikTok or Reels?

Upload your MKV file to OpusClip's reframe tool, then select the 9:16 aspect ratio preset. Our AI automatically detects faces and subjects, centering them in the vertical frame. You can manually adjust the crop region if needed, preview the result in real time, and export a vertical MKV or MP4 file ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts. The entire process takes under two minutes per video.

### Does OpusClip support batch cropping for multiple MKV files?

Yes. Upload multiple MKV files, apply your crop settings and aspect ratio once, and OpusClip processes all videos in parallel. Batch cropping is ideal for creators repurposing podcast episodes, webinar recordings, or tutorial series. Each file gets the same crop dimensions and AI reframing rules, ensuring consistent framing across your entire content library without repeating manual work for every clip.

### What aspect ratios can I crop MKV videos to with OpusClip?

OpusClip offers presets for 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, 16:9 widescreen, and 4:5 feed formats, plus custom dimensions. Each preset is optimized for specific platforms: 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, 1:1 for Instagram feed, 16:9 for YouTube landscape, and 4:5 for Facebook and LinkedIn. You can also enter pixel-exact dimensions if you need a non-standard crop for a particular use case or display requirement.
