## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the audio looper create seamless transitions?

OpusClip's audio looper analyzes your track's waveform to identify natural loop points where audio patterns repeat or fade smoothly. It applies intelligent crossfades at these points, blending the end of your audio with the beginning so loops sound continuous. For music, it detects beats and measures to align loops rhythmically. You can preview and adjust loop points before finalizing to ensure the transition feels natural for your specific track and video context.

### Can I loop different audio tracks independently in one video?

Yes, the audio looper supports multi-track processing. You can loop background music to one length, extend a voiceover segment separately, and repeat sound effects independently—all within the same video project. Each audio layer maintains its own loop settings, crossfade style, and duration control. This gives you full flexibility to match complex audio arrangements to your video edits without compromising any single track's quality or timing.

### What audio formats work with the looper tool?

The audio looper accepts all standard formats including MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, and FLAC. You can loop audio embedded in video files or upload standalone audio tracks. OpusClip preserves your original audio quality throughout the looping process, and you can export in your preferred format. The tool handles stereo and mono tracks, maintains sample rates, and supports high-bitrate files for professional-grade output across all your social video content.

### Does the audio looper adjust automatically when I trim my video?

Absolutely. When you use OpusClip's clipping and editing tools to trim video length, the audio looper recalculates loop duration in real time. If your clip shortens, the looped audio contracts to match; if you extend the video, the loop expands seamlessly. This automatic sync eliminates the need to manually re-loop audio every time you adjust your edit, keeping your workflow fast and your audio perfectly aligned with every frame of your final video.
