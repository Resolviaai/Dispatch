# Zoom In Effect

A quick punch-in on a key line is one of the oldest tricks for holding attention on a talking-head video. Doing it by hand means keyframing every zoom.

OpusClip's Auto Zoom adds zoom-ins and zoom-outs for you, so the frame tightens on the moments that matter and pulls back again.

Every zoom lands as an editable block in the editor, so you can move, trim or remove any of them.

## Why creators use Auto Zoom

### Zooms where they count

Auto Zoom punches in on the moments that matter instead of zooming at random.

### Editable zoom blocks

Each zoom is a block in the editor that you can adjust or delete.

### Made for short-form

Built for vertical Shorts, Reels and TikToks, where pacing is everything.

## Why the punch-in works

A zoom resets the viewer's attention without a cut. On a single-camera talking-head video it makes one shot feel like several.

Auto Zoom gives you that rhythm automatically, so a straight-to-camera recording stops looking static.
