# YouTube Bulk Uploader

Bulk Schedule takes the clips on a project results page and publishes them to YouTube in the order you set, starting at a time you pick and repeating on a fixed interval such as every 2 hours, every 4 hours or daily.

Two limits to know before you start: bulk scheduling is a Pro feature, and today it supports YouTube only.

## Why bulk scheduling beats uploading one by one

### You set the running order

Number each clip to fix its position in the sequence. Clip 1 goes out first, clip 2 next, and so on.

### One start time, one interval

Pick when clip 1 publishes and how often the rest follow. The system follows your selected interval exactly.

### It is honest about timing

Bulk scheduling does not skip weekends, holidays or ideal posting times, so set the interval around the gaps you want.

## How to bulk upload to YouTube

### Open the results page

Go to your project results, where every generated clip is listed.

### Select the clips

Click Select in the top right, tick each clip or use Select all, then click Bulk Schedule. You can also click the calendar icon on one clip and choose Bulk Schedule.

### Set the order

Click the number next to each clip and enter its position in the posting sequence.

### Set time and frequency

Pick the date and time for clip 1, then choose the interval for the rest. Confirm, and the queue appears on the calendar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I bulk upload clips to YouTube from OpusClip?

Yes. Bulk Schedule on the project results page queues multiple clips to YouTube with a posting order, a start time and a fixed interval. It is available exclusively to Pro users.

### Which platforms does bulk scheduling support?

YouTube only at the moment. Our help centre says support for additional platforms is coming soon, and that bulk scheduling to several platforms at once is not available yet.

### Does bulk scheduling avoid weekends or pick the best time?

No. It follows your interval exactly and does not skip weekends, holidays or ideal posting times. Five clips starting Monday 9:00 AM on a 2-hour interval go out at 10:00, 12:00, 2:00 and 4:00.

### Can I change a bulk schedule after setting it?

Yes, from the Calendar page in the left sidebar, where you can edit posting times, reschedule, or delete individual clips. Each clip can be scheduled once per session, so reposting the same clip means scheduling it again.
