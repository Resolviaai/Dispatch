# Training Video Maker

Drop in a Zoom, Loom, Riverside or StreamYard recording. Screenshare layout puts the shared screen on the top half and the speaker on the bottom half.

From there you cut the dead air, fix the captions and export modules people will actually finish.

## Why recorded sessions make good training

### Screen and face together

Screenshare layout needs screenshare content and one speaker, then places the screen above and the presenter below automatically.

### Dead air comes out

Speech Cleanup detects over 90% of fillers and pauses, and shows you every one of them before anything is removed.

### Over 25 languages

Captions are supported in more than 25 languages, so a session recorded once can be read by a wider team.

## How to make a training video

### Bring in the recording

Paste a link from Zoom, Zoom Clips, Loom, Riverside, StreamYard, Vimeo, Google Drive or YouTube, or upload a local file up to 10 GB and 10 hours.

### Choose Screenshare layout

Turn on Screenshare in Clip layout settings. If no screenshare is identified we apply an alternative layout instead, so select it only when you want it.

### Clean the transcript

Run Speech Cleanup, tick which filler types to remove, set your own pause threshold, then scan for bad takes with Detect now.

### Brand it and export

Apply your brand template, add intro and outro cards, then download the modules or schedule them from the calendar.
