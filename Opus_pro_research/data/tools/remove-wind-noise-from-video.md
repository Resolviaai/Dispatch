# Remove Wind Noise from Video

Speech Enhancement runs four things at once: noise reduction, background noise removal, voice isolation and voice volume boost. Outdoor recordings come back clearer without a separate audio tool.

The Noise reduction and Voice enhancement sliders start at recommended defaults, and your adjustments are applied automatically.
