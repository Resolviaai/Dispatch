# YouTube Scheduler

YouTube Studio schedules one upload at a time. Most creators are posting a long video, a handful of Shorts cut from it, and the same clips to TikTok and Instagram.

OpusClip puts all of that on one calendar. Pick the date, choose the clip and the channels, and it posts on time, with descriptions and hashtags written for you.

### One calendar, every channel

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn and more, planned in the same view.

### Bulk schedule Shorts

### Descriptions written for you

AI drafts a description and hashtags for each post.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you schedule YouTube Shorts?

Yes. OpusClip schedules Shorts to your YouTube channel for a set date and time, one at a time or in bulk with the Bulk Scheduler.

### Why schedule with OpusClip instead of YouTube Studio?

YouTube Studio schedules YouTube. OpusClip schedules YouTube and your other platforms from one calendar, bulk schedules a batch of clips, and writes descriptions and hashtags for you.

### Which plans can schedule YouTube posts?

Pro plan users can schedule future posts. Starter users can post to YouTube in real time.

### What happens if my project expires before the post goes out?

The post still goes out. The clip is saved when you create the schedule, so expiry does not affect it.
