# AI Sound Effect Generator

Type what you want to hear, like a deep cinematic boom or a soft paper rustle, and OpusClip generates three versions for you to compare.

Pick the one that fits and it lands on your video's timeline as its own block, where you can change its volume, trim its length or rewrite the prompt and try again.

No sound library to scroll through and no separate audio app to export from.

## Why creators generate their sound effects

### The exact sound you pictured

Libraries make you settle for the closest file. A prompt lets you describe the sound you actually want, down to its length and mood.

### Three versions every time

Each prompt returns three takes, so you compare and choose instead of re-rolling one result until it works.

### Already on your timeline

Generated sounds drop straight into your video as editable blocks. There is nothing to download, import or line up by hand.

## Describe it the way you hear it

Good prompts are short and concrete: what the sound is, how it should feel and how long it lasts.

"Deep cinematic boom." "Quick cartoon pop." "Soft whoosh, left to right." Set the duration before you generate and OpusClip makes three versions at that length.

If none of them land, change a word in the prompt and generate again. Each block keeps its own prompt, so you can come back to it later.

## How to generate a sound effect with AI

### Open your video in OpusClip

Start from Auto SFX on the homepage, or open a clip in the editor and click Audio in the right sidebar.

### Describe the sound

On the Auto SFX tab, click Generate by prompt, write what you want to hear and set the duration.

### Pick one of three versions

Click Generate 3 versions, play each one and keep the best fit.

### Fine-tune it on the timeline

Adjust the volume and duration, or edit the prompt and regenerate that one sound.
