## How to edit a real estate video

### Upload the tour

Upload a local file up to 10 GB and 10 hours, or paste a link from YouTube, Google Drive, Dropbox, Vimeo, Frame.io or a public .mp4 URL.

### Reframe for the feed

Set the aspect ratio, let subject tracking follow the walkthrough, and double-click the video to open Manual Reframe wherever the automatic crop misses.

### Add the listing details

Drop in text overlays for price, address and features, drag your own stills onto the timeline, and add a music bed under the tour.

### Export or schedule

Download in up to 4K, bulk download a set of clips, or schedule the posts from the social media calendar.
