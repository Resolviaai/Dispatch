## How to make a speaker reel

### Import your talk footage

Paste links from YouTube, Vimeo, Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom or Frame.io, or upload files up to 10GB each. Private links cannot be accessed, so keep them public or unlisted.

### Pull your strongest sections

Describe the moment you want: the opening hook, the audience laugh, the line you always close on. We return matching clips to preview.

### Set the look once

A brand template stores your fonts, caption style, layout and auto headline, then applies them to every clip generated from that template.

### Assemble and share

Trim or extend sections, remove filler words, then export, or schedule the reel to LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or X.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a speaker reel?

A speaker reel is a short edit of your strongest moments on stage, used to get booked. There is no standard length, so keep it watchable in one sitting and make sure the first seconds show you delivering to a real audience.

### Can I make a speaker reel from panel footage?

Yes. Our clip search identifies which speaker is talking, and the Split, Three and Four layouts keep several people on screen when the original frame needs them. Turn on Pin first speaker in your brand template to hold yourself in the top slot.

### What video sources can I import?

Eighteen link sources plus public MP4 URLs and local uploads up to 10GB per video. They include YouTube, Vimeo, Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom, Frame.io, Google Drive and Dropbox.

### Can my editor finish the reel in Premiere Pro?

Yes, export an XML for Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Finish and save your caption adjustments in the OpusClip editor before you export the XML.
