### How does a lyric video maker handle timing and synchronization automatically?

Agent Opus uses advanced audio analysis to automatically sync your lyrics with your music track. When you provide your audio file and lyrics text, our AI analyzes the song's tempo, rhythm patterns, and vocal phrasing to determine optimal timing for each lyric line. The system identifies natural breaks, chorus repetitions, and verse transitions, then generates text animations that appear precisely when those words are sung. This eliminates the manual process of scrubbing through audio waveforms and setting keyframes for every line. The lyric video maker also adapts animation speed and style based on your song's genre. Fast-paced hip-hop tracks get quick, punchy text reveals, while slower ballads receive smooth, flowing transitions. You can describe timing preferences in your prompt, such as word-by-word reveals versus full-line appearances, and the AI adjusts accordingly. For songs with complex structures like bridges or breakdowns, Agent Opus recognizes these sections and varies the visual treatment to match the musical intensity. The result is a professionally timed lyric video that feels hand-crafted, but requires zero manual synchronization work from you. If your audio has instrumental sections, the system intelligently pauses lyric display and fills those moments with motion graphics or visual effects that complement your song's aesthetic.
