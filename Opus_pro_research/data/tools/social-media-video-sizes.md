# Social Media Video Sizes

9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, 16:9 landscape and 4:5 portrait cover almost every placement you will post to. Everything after that is a resolution detail.

Set the frame once in Clip Layout Settings, reframe or expand to the others, then publish to nine platforms from the same project.

## Why one master file does not fit everywhere

### Four frames, not forty

Vertical feeds take 9:16. Square posts take 1:1. Landscape players take 16:9. Portrait feed posts take 4:5. You can switch a clip between all four in Clip Layout Settings.

### Cropping costs you pixels

A 1920 x 1080 file cropped to 9:16 leaves a 608 pixel wide column. We upsample anything below 1080 to an output of 1080 x 1920, so the export still lands at 1080p.

### Every new frame is a new reframe

Changing the ratio moves the subject within the frame. Reframe Anything identifies key objects and actions and tracks them across frames, so each version keeps the subject in shot.

## How to size one video for every platform

### Upload the highest resolution master you have

A 4K source leaves more pixels after cropping than a 1080p one. A 4K file at 3840 x 2160 cropped to 9:16 exports at 1215 x 2160 with no upsampling.

### Set your primary frame

Pick the ratio most of your posting goes to in Clip Layout Settings, or preset it in a brand template so every new clip starts in the right frame.

### Branch the other sizes

Duplicate the clip and change its ratio, or use AI Expand when cropping would cut something important out of the frame.

### Schedule each version

Send each size to the platform it was made for. Post immediately or schedule it for later from the project page.
