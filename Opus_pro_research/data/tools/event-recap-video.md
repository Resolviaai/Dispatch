## How to make an event recap video

### Import the full recording

Paste a link from Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom, Frame.io, YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive or Dropbox, or upload a local file up to 10GB. Links need to be public or unlisted for us to reach them.

### Search for the moments that matter

Describe what you want: the standing ovation, the product reveal, the panel disagreement. We return the matching moments as clips you can preview before you keep them.

### Shape the cut

Trim or extend each section, remove filler words, add stock or AI B-roll over the stretches where the camera was pointed at a slide, and set your captions.

### Export or schedule

Export in full 4K when your source is genuinely 3840 x 2160, send an XML to Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, or schedule the post from the project page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should an event recap video be?

There is no single right length, so cut two. A short version for feeds and a two to three minute version for the recap email come from the same project and the same brand template.

### Can I make an event recap video from a Zoom or StreamYard recording?

Yes. Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, StreamYard, Loom and Frame.io are supported import sources, along with YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive, Dropbox and public MP4 URLs. The link has to be public or unlisted, or you can upload the file directly, up to 10GB.

### Will the recap export in 4K?

Yes, if the source is genuinely 4K at 3840 x 2160, because 4K export is not upscaling and only activates on true 4K footage. Separately, Upscale shows its base credit cost on the button, and 4K upscaling is available on Pro, Max and Business for an additional incremental charge.

### Can I post the recap to several platforms at once?

Yes, to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and X from the project page, with per platform titles, descriptions and hashtags you can edit first. If you edit and save the clip after scheduling, schedule it again, because the scheduled post keeps the version it was created from.
