# Content Calendar Generator

The OpusClip social media calendar holds everything you have published, drafted and planned in one month view, and you drag posts between days and times to change the plan.

Pick a date, pick the clips and the accounts, and our AI generates the description and hashtags for each post. Pro accounts can schedule to nine platforms, including YouTube, TikTok Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Page and LinkedIn.

## Why creators plan in the calendar

### One month view

Published, drafted and planned posts sit in the same calendar, so you can see the gaps before they happen.

### The copy comes with it

When you pick clips and platforms for a date, our AI generates the description and hashtags for each post. You edit them before anything goes out.

### Drag to reschedule

Move a post from one day or time slot to another by dragging it, or open a scheduled post to change its time.

## How the content calendar works

### Connect your accounts

Click the Social Accounts icon in the left sidebar, then Add Account, and connect each platform you publish to.

### Open the calendar

Click Calendar in the left sidebar and choose the date you want to post on.

### Pick clips and platforms

Choose the clips and the accounts for that slot. Descriptions and hashtags are auto-generated, and you can edit them before confirming.

### Adjust as the month moves

Drag posts to new days or times, open a post to change its caption or time, or delete it and get the frozen credits back.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does OpusClip have a content calendar?

Yes. The Social Media Calendar in the left sidebar shows everything you have published, drafted and planned in one month view, and posts can be dragged between days and times. It is also where you edit and cancel scheduled posts.

### How long does it take to plan a month of posts?

Our help centre describes scheduling a whole month of posts in about 10 minutes. You choose a date, choose clips and platforms, and the descriptions and hashtags are generated for you.

### Can I upload my own video to the calendar?

Yes, you can upload a local video and schedule it from the calendar. Unedited local files are limited to mp4 under 200MB, TikTok uploads need a frame rate above 23, and Instagram clips must each run 30 to 90 seconds.

### Does the calendar suggest what to post about?

No. The calendar schedules videos you already have and writes the description and hashtags for each post. We could not find a feature that generates topic ideas or a content plan for you.
