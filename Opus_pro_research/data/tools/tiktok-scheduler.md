# TikTok Scheduler

Yes, you can schedule TikTok posts. In OpusClip you pick a date and time, choose the clip, and it posts to your TikTok Feed automatically, with a description and hashtags written for you.

Want to add a sound or effect in the TikTok app first? Send the clip to your TikTok Inbox instead and finish it on your phone.

Schedule one video, or line up a whole week of clips at once.

## Why creators schedule TikToks with OpusClip

### A calendar for every post

See your scheduled TikToks on one calendar alongside every other platform you post to.

### Captions written for you

AI writes a description and hashtags for each post. Edit them or post as is.

### Bulk scheduling

Select a batch of clips, set the order, the start time and how often to post, and the whole run is queued.

## Feed or Inbox: pick how it posts

**TikTok Feed** schedules and publishes straight to your Feed, with AI-generated captions and descriptions, and you never have to open the app.

**TikTok Inbox** sends the clip to the TikTok app as a draft notification. Open it, add TikTok's native effects or music, and post to your Feed or Story yourself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you schedule posts on TikTok?

Yes. OpusClip schedules TikTok posts to your Feed for a specific date and time, one at a time or in bulk. You can also send clips to your TikTok Inbox to finish and post them in the app.

### What happens if I edit a clip after scheduling it?

The post uses the version of the clip saved when you scheduled it. If you edit the clip afterwards, schedule it again so the new version goes out.

### Which plans can schedule TikTok posts?

Pro plan users can schedule future posts. Starter users can post to TikTok in real time but cannot schedule ahead.

### Can I upload a video that was not made in OpusClip?

Yes. Choose a date in the Social Media Calendar and upload an MP4 under 200MB. For TikTok, make sure the video is above 23 frames per second.
