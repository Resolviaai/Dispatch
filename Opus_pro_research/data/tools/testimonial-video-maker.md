## How to make a testimonial video

### Import the recorded interview

Paste the link from Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside, Loom, Frame.io, YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive or Dropbox, or upload the file, up to 10GB. Links must be public or unlisted.

### Pull the quote

Describe the moment you remember and preview the clips we return. Keep the one where the customer says the number.

### Frame, caption, clean

Choose the layout, apply the brand template, remove filler words and pauses, and check the captions on company and product names.

### Export or schedule

Export in full 4K from a genuine 4K source, or schedule to LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or X from the project page.
