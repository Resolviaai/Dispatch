# UGC Video Editor

UGC lives or dies on the first two seconds. Auto headline writes a one-line text overlay for the opening of your top 10 clips, styled by your brand template.

Captions, filler removal and a 9:16 reframe take the rest of the edit off your plate.

## How to edit a UGC video

### Bring in the raw footage

Upload a local file up to 10 GB, or import from Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, Loom or a public .mp4 URL.

### Pick the moments

Set a clip length instead of the Auto 0 to 3 minute default, or write a prompt to pull the specific moments the brief asks for.

### Hook, captions, brand

Leave Auto headline on, style the captions and the headline in your brand template, then check the keywords it highlights.

### Export the set

Bulk download the variants, or schedule them to the client's connected accounts from the calendar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the UGC video editor add a hook for me?

Yes. Auto headline generates a one-line text overlay for the opening of your clips and applies it to the top 10 clips in the project. It is on by default for manual uploads.

### Can I keep a different look per client?

Yes, through brand templates. Each one stores fonts, colours, caption style, auto headline styling, clip layouts and intro and outro cards, and you switch templates per clip.

### How do I get rid of the ums without recutting?

Open Speech Cleanup, which detects over 90% of fillers and pauses. You tick which filler types to remove and set your own pause threshold, 0.5 seconds by default.

### Can I batch this for an agency workload?

Yes. Collections group a shoot's variants, bulk download exports them together, and the REST API and MCP server run the same pipeline programmatically.
