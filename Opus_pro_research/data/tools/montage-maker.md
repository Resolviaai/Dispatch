## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the AI montage maker select which clips to include?

OpusClip's montage maker analyzes audio, motion, and engagement signals across your footage. It identifies peaks in energy, key dialogue, visual action, and emotional moments, then scores each segment for impact. The highest-scoring clips are automatically sequenced into a montage that tells a cohesive story. You can review the selection and swap or trim clips to match your creative vision before exporting.

### Can I customize the order and length of clips in my montage?

Yes. After AI generates your montage, you can drag clips to reorder them, trim start and end points, or remove segments entirely. The montage maker gives you full control over pacing and narrative flow while handling the technical work of transitions, captions, and rendering. Adjust as much or as little as you like, then export when you're satisfied with the final sequence.

### What video formats and lengths work best for montage creation?

OpusClip's montage maker accepts MP4, MOV, and most common video formats. You can upload a single long video (webinars, events, interviews) or multiple shorter clips (product demos, testimonials, B-roll). The AI works best with footage between 5 minutes and 3 hours, but you can process longer files by splitting them first. Final montages typically range from 30 seconds to 3 minutes depending on your platform and audience.

### Does the montage maker add music or sound effects automatically?

The montage maker focuses on assembling your video clips and adding captions. It preserves the original audio from your footage, including dialogue and ambient sound. If you want to add background music or sound effects, you can upload an audio track in the editor and layer it beneath your montage. This gives you full control over the audio mix while AI handles the visual sequencing and captioning.
