## How to edit a Zoom recording

### Paste the Zoom link

Add a Zoom or Zoom Clips link, or upload the recording file. Make sure the link is public or unlisted, because private videos cannot be accessed.

### Let it transcribe and clip

We transcribe the call and pick key moments, and captions are generated from that transcript.

### Clean it up

Remove filler words and pauses with Speech Cleanup, correct wording inline in the transcript, and run Speech Enhancement on the audio.

### Frame and publish

Choose Screenshare, Split, Three or Four layout, adjust the crop if needed, then export the clips or schedule them from the project page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I import a Zoom recording directly?

Yes. Zoom and Zoom Clips are both supported import sources, so you can paste the link instead of uploading a file. The link has to be public or unlisted.

### How do I get the screenshare and the speaker in one vertical clip?

Use the Screenshare layout, which places the screen on the top half and the speaker on the bottom half. It needs screenshare content and one speaker, and we apply an alternative layout if no screenshare is identified.

### Can it cut out the dead air?

Yes. Speech Cleanup removes pauses, with silences at or above 0.5 seconds as the documented default, and detects over 90% of filler words. Removals are shown in the transcript before you commit.

### What about a call recorded in gallery view?

Split, Three and Four layouts show several participants when they appear together in the original frame, which gallery view satisfies. You can also open the crop window and reframe manually.
