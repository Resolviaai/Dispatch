## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I edit MOV files without downloading software?

Yes. OpusClip's MOV editor runs entirely in your browser. Upload your MOV file, and you can trim, add captions, reframe, and export without installing any desktop software. All processing happens in the cloud, so it works on Mac, Windows, or any device with a web browser.

### How does text-based editing work for MOV files?

OpusClip automatically transcribes your MOV file when you upload it. You can then edit the video by selecting text in the transcript—highlight a sentence to trim that section, delete filler words like 'um' or 'uh,' or search for keywords to jump to specific moments. Changes to the text instantly update the video timeline.

### What aspect ratios can I export my MOV file to?

OpusClip's MOV editor supports 16:9 (landscape for YouTube), 9:16 (vertical for TikTok and Reels), 1:1 (square for Instagram feed), and 4:5 (portrait for Facebook and LinkedIn). AI auto-reframing keeps speakers centered when you change aspect ratios, and you can manually adjust the crop if needed.

### Can I add captions to MOV files automatically?

Yes. OpusClip generates animated captions for your MOV file as soon as you upload it. You can customize caption style, font, color, and positioning, and the editor supports speaker labels if your MOV has multiple voices. Captions are burned into the export or available as SRT files.
