# Upscale Video to 4K

Footage shot at 720p or 1080p looks soft the moment it goes full screen on a decent display. Rescaling it in a normal editor just makes the same pixels bigger.

AI upscaling reconstructs detail rather than stretching it, so the result holds up at 4K instead of looking blown up.

Upscaling runs on Pro, Max and Business plans, and the cost is shown on the button before you start.

## Why upscale at all

### Detail, not just size

Stretching 1080p to 4K gives you a larger soft image. AI upscaling reconstructs detail so the extra resolution is doing something.

### Old footage, current standards

Archive recordings, older phone footage and screen captures were fine at the time. Upscaling brings them up to what viewers now expect.

### Cost shown up front

The Upscale button shows the base credit cost before you run it. 4K upscaling on Pro, Max and Business adds an incremental charge on top, calculated from the job.

## What upscaling is good for

Footage shot at 720p or 1080p that needs to hold up on a large screen.

Older recordings you want to reuse rather than refilm.

Screen captures and webinar recordings, which are often captured at low resolution by default.

Clips that will be viewed full screen rather than in a feed thumbnail.

## Upscaling is not a rescue for everything

Upscaling adds resolution. It does not fix a shot that was out of focus, badly lit or shaky.

If the detail was never captured, no amount of upscaling invents it convincingly. Soft-but-sharp footage upscales well. Genuinely out-of-focus footage does not.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you really upscale a video to 4K?

Yes, with the caveat that upscaling reconstructs detail rather than inventing what was never captured. Footage that is soft but in focus upscales well. Footage that is genuinely out of focus does not.

### What does upscaling cost?

The Upscale button shows the base credit charge before you run the job. On Pro, Max and Business, 4K upscaling adds an incremental charge calculated for that job, so the final total can be higher than the base figure.

### Which plans include 4K upscaling?

4K upscaling is available on Pro, Max and Business.

### Will upscaling fix shaky or badly lit footage?

No. Upscaling increases resolution and reconstructs detail. It does not stabilise, relight or refocus a shot.
