# Transition Sound Effects

A cut without a sound feels flat. A cut with a whoosh feels intentional.

OpusClip's Auto SFX detects the transitions and key moments in your video and places matching sound effects on them. Want a specific whoosh, swoosh or riser? Describe it and OpusClip generates three versions to pick from, right on your timeline.

No digging through sound packs and no dragging files into place.

## Why add transition sounds with OpusClip

### Lands on the cut

Auto SFX detects transitions in your video and places sounds where the cuts happen.

### Any whoosh you can describe

Generate a sound from a prompt like "fast airy whoosh" instead of searching sound packs.

### Edit any sound later

Each effect is its own block. Change its volume or length, regenerate it or delete it.
