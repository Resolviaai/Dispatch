## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I resize an MP4 video for TikTok and Instagram Reels?

Upload your MP4 to OpusClip, open the Reframe tool, and select 9:16 vertical aspect ratio. The AI automatically tracks your subject and keeps it centered as it crops your landscape or square footage into a vertical format. Preview the result, adjust if needed, and export a publication-ready MP4 optimized for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts in under two minutes.

### Can I resize MP4 files to multiple aspect ratios at once?

Yes. OpusClip lets you batch-resize a single MP4 into 9:16, 1:1, 16:9, and 4:5 simultaneously. Upload once, select all target ratios, and the AI generates separate exports for each platform. This eliminates repetitive manual cropping and ensures you have every format ready to publish without re-uploading or re-editing your original video file.

### Does resizing an MP4 reduce video quality?

No. OpusClip maintains your original MP4 resolution and bitrate during the resize process. The AI reframes and crops your footage without re-encoding at lower quality, so your exported videos stay sharp and clear. Whether you're resizing 1080p or 4K MP4 files, you get full-resolution output ready for professional publishing on any social platform.

### What if the AI crops out important content when resizing my MP4?

OpusClip's auto-reframe tool tracks faces, speakers, and on-screen text by default, but you can manually adjust the crop area if the AI misses something. Use the preview mode to scrub through your resized MP4 frame by frame, then drag the crop box to reposition your subject. This gives you full control while still saving hours compared to manual keyframe editing in desktop software.
