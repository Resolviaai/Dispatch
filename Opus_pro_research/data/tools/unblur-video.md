# Unblur Video

Old recordings, compressed uploads and grainy phone clips share one problem: they look soft the moment they hit a big screen.

OpusClip's Upscale feature uses AI to make blurry, low-resolution footage sharper, clearer and more high definition. Upload a file, paste a YouTube link or import from Google Drive, then download the cleaned-up version.

No editing experience and no plugins to install.

## Why creators fix blurry footage with OpusClip

### Sharper in one pass

Upscale works on the whole video at once. There is no frame-by-frame sharpening and no settings to tune.

### Built for imperfect footage

It is designed for old recordings, compressed video and anything that looks grainy or pixelated.

### Cost shown up front

The Upscale button shows the credit cost before you start, so there are no surprises.

## What Upscale can and cannot fix

Upscale is made for footage that lacks sharpness or clarity: low-resolution recordings, video that an app or platform has compressed, and clips that look grainy or pixelated.

It cannot invent detail that was never captured. A shot that was badly out of focus or smeared by heavy motion blur will look better, but it will not look like it was filmed in focus.
