# YouTube Video Size

Standard YouTube videos are 1920x1080 at 16:9. Shorts are 1080x1920 at 9:16. Thumbnails are 1280x720.

Knowing the number is the easy part. Getting your footage into it without cropping the speaker out of frame is the part that wastes an afternoon.

OpusClip resizes your video to any YouTube format and keeps the subject centred while it does it, because Reframe Anything tracks whoever is talking rather than cropping blindly to the middle.

## The YouTube sizes you actually need

Standard upload: 1920x1080, 16:9. This is the default for anything landscape.

YouTube Shorts: 1080x1920, 9:16, up to 60 seconds.

Thumbnail: 1280x720, 16:9, under 2MB.

Square, if you are cross-posting the same cut elsewhere: 1080x1080.

Minimum width for HD is 1280 pixels. Below that YouTube will serve it, but it will look soft on a desktop screen.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size should a YouTube video be?

For a standard upload, 1920x1080 at a 16:9 aspect ratio. For YouTube Shorts, 1080x1920 at 9:16. Keep the width at 1280 pixels or above so YouTube serves it in HD.

### What is the YouTube Shorts size?

1080x1920 pixels, a 9:16 vertical ratio, and up to 60 seconds long. Anything wider gets pillarboxed on the Shorts shelf.

### Can I turn a landscape video into a Short without cropping the speaker out?

Yes. Reframe Anything tracks the subject across frames and keeps them in the shot as the aspect ratio changes, rather than cropping to the centre of the original frame.

### Is resizing free?
