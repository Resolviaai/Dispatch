# LinkedIn Post Scheduler

LinkedIn is one of the nine scheduler destinations on the Pro plan. Pick a date on the calendar, choose the clip and your LinkedIn account, and the post goes out at that time.

Scheduling saves the clip as it is at that moment, so if you edit the clip afterwards, schedule it again to publish the new cut.

## Why schedule LinkedIn video here

### A supported destination, not a workaround

LinkedIn sits in our scheduler list alongside YouTube, TikTok Feed, Instagram Reels and Facebook Page on the Pro plan.

### The calendar is the queue

Scheduled LinkedIn posts appear in the calendar with everything else you have published, drafted or planned.

### Cancel before it runs

Click the post in the calendar and delete it, or change its time. Nothing is locked once it is scheduled.

## How to schedule a LinkedIn post

### Connect LinkedIn

Click the Social Accounts icon in the left sidebar, click Add Account, and authorise LinkedIn.

### Pick the clip

From the project results page or the calendar, click the calendar icon on the clip you want to post.

### Choose the time

Click Select time and pick the date and time, or publish immediately with Post Now.

### Manage it in the calendar

Open the post from the Calendar tab to edit its caption or time, or click Delete to cancel it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can OpusClip schedule posts to LinkedIn?

Yes, on the Pro plan. LinkedIn is one of nine scheduler destinations, alongside YouTube, TikTok Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Page, X (Beta), Pinterest, Threads and Bluesky.

### Can I cancel a scheduled LinkedIn post?

Yes. Click the Calendar tab in the sidebar, click the scheduled post, and click Delete. You can also change its time instead of cancelling it.

### What happens if I edit the clip after scheduling it?

The scheduled post keeps the version of the clip from when the schedule was created, so later edits are not reflected. Schedule the clip again to publish the new version.

### Does OpusClip post to LinkedIn through the official API?

Yes. Our help centre states we use official APIs provided by the platforms and do not auto-post without user action. Connections occasionally need reauthorising because platforms expire access tokens for security.
