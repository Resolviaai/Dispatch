## How to make an internal communications video

### Import the recording

Zoom, Zoom Clips, Loom, Riverside, StreamYard, Frame.io, Google Drive, Dropbox and public MP4 URLs are supported, or upload a local file up to 10GB. The link must be public or unlisted for us to fetch it.

### Split it by topic

Describe each segment you want, preview the matching clips, and keep the ones worth sending.

### Caption and brand

Apply the brand template, check the captions on names and internal terms, trim the preamble, and remove filler words and pauses.

### Export and distribute

Download the finished clip to share through your own internal channels, or schedule it to a public platform from the project page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can OpusClip post an internal communications video to our intranet or Slack?

Our built in posting connects to six platforms: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and X. There is no intranet or chat destination in that list, so for internal distribution export the finished clip and upload it wherever your team watches.

### What can we import an all-hands recording from?

Zoom and Zoom Clips are supported, along with Loom, Riverside, StreamYard, Frame.io, YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive, Dropbox, public MP4 URLs and local uploads up to 10GB per video. Private links cannot be accessed, so share them as unlisted or public.

### How do we keep names and internal terms correct in the captions?

We provide a suite of features for keeping proper nouns, brand vocabulary and key terms transcribed consistently in a clip or across all clips. Set them once and captions stop guessing at your product and team names.

### Can we keep these videos on brand without a designer?

Yes. A brand template saves fonts, caption style, layout and the auto headline, including its font, color and text box width, and applies them automatically to every clip generated with that template.
