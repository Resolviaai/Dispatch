# AI Social Media Manager

Connect six destinations: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X. OpusClip turns one long video into clips, drafts the description and hashtags for each, then posts or schedules them from the calendar.

It is not a strategist. You still decide what to make and when it goes out. We take the afternoon of cutting, captioning and uploading.

## Why teams run posting through OpusClip

### Six destinations, one calendar

Connected accounts report as YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X. Pick a date in the calendar, pick the clips and the platforms, and the queue is built from there.

### Copy drafted from the clip itself

Descriptions and hashtags are generated per post from the clip, so you edit a draft instead of staring at an empty caption box at 11pm.

### Scheduled is not final

Open a scheduled post in the calendar and delete it to pull it back before publish time. Nothing goes out of an account you have not connected.

## How to schedule a week of clips

### Connect the accounts you post from

Add your social destinations once. Connected accounts come back as YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X, and posting runs through each platform's official API.

### Drop in a long video

Paste a link or upload a file. OpusClip finds the strongest moments and renders vertical clips with captions and your brand template applied.

### Pick a date on the calendar

Choose the clips and the platforms for that slot. A description and hashtags are generated for each post, ready for you to edit.

### Publish or queue

Send it now or leave it scheduled. To withdraw a post, open it in the calendar and click Delete before publish time.

### Posting from iOS

### Cancel a scheduled post

Open the post in the calendar and delete it. Nothing publishes after that.

### Local video uploads

Schedule your own MP4 files from the calendar, under 200MB. TikTok uploads need a frame rate above 23.

### Brand templates

Caption style, layout and logo come from your template, so every clip in the queue looks like you.

### API posting

POST /api/post-tasks publishes a clip immediately, POST /api/publish-schedules queues it for a future UTC timestamp.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which platforms can OpusClip post to?

Six: YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X. Connected accounts report one of those platform values, and posts go out through each platform's official API.

### Can an AI social media manager replace a human one?

No, and we would not sell it as one. It replaces production and uploading: clipping, captioning, drafting copy and queueing posts. Strategy, community and judgement stay with you.

### Can I cancel a scheduled post?

Yes. Open the post in the Calendar tab and click Delete, any time before its publish time.

### Does editing a clip update a post I already scheduled?

No. We save the version of the clip as it was when the post was created, so re-save the clip and schedule it again to push the change.
