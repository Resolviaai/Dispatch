# Talking Head Video Maker

Multi-Angle adds AI-generated shots to a podcast, interview or talking head recording and keeps the original audio untouched.

Upload one video up to 1 hour and 4 GB, choose how much extra coverage you want, then review every planned shot before you pay to render it.

## Why single-camera footage stalls

### No cutaways to cut to

A continuous single-camera take gives you nothing to cut away to. Multi-Angle generates the extra shots and leaves your audio alone.

### You see the plan first

Create shot plan lists the proposed moments with their time ranges and the reason each was selected. Planning does not start a paid render.

### Shot by shot control

Show this shot decides whether the main edit uses the generated result or your source footage at that moment, so a bad shot costs you nothing.

## Coverage you choose

Shot coverage has three settings: Fewer for key moments, Balanced for a natural pace, More for frequent extra shots. They are not fixed shot counts.

Keep the whole recording or select a section with the range handles, or by entering exact start and end times in seconds.

## How to make a talking head video

### Upload the source

Open Multi-Angle, sign in with your OpusClip account, and upload an MP4, MOV or WebM up to 4 GB and 1 hour. Keep the page open while it prepares.

### Choose a section and coverage

Keep the full recording or set a range, pick Fewer, Balanced or More coverage, then select Create shot plan.

### Review the planned moments

Inspect each shot's timing and why it was picked. Trim its edges within its original window, add a shot elsewhere, or delete one you do not want.

### Render and download

Pick 480p or 720p, check the credit amount on the render button, then render the batch or a single shot. Download the MP4 when the edit is right.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can OpusClip generate a talking head video from a script?

We could not find a script-to-avatar generator in the OpusClip help centre. Multi-Angle works from a recording you already shot, adding generated shots while keeping your original audio.

### How long can the recording be?

Up to 1 hour, as MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 4 GB. It is tuned for continuous single-camera footage with clear faces and a relatively stable scene.

### What quality are the generated shots?

480p or 720p, chosen on the render button. The credit amount changes with shot duration, quality and any current discount.

### How long are my projects kept?

Sources and generated outputs expire 30 days after the initial upload. Opening a project or rendering more shots does not extend that, so download what you want to keep.
