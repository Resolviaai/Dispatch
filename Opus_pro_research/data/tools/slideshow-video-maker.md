# Slideshow Video Maker

Audio to Image Video takes a voiceover and returns an illustrated video: up to 200 scenes, 25 picture styles, and 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.

You read the scene plan and rewrite any scene description before a single frame renders.

## Why this beats a drag-and-drop slideshow

### The plan comes first

You see every planned scene with its description, and can edit it or regenerate it, before you spend credits on rendering.

### Recurring subjects stay recurring

Up to 8 visual references keep a person, place or object looking the same from scene to scene.

### Pacing is a setting

Calm uses longer shots, Balanced a natural mix, Dynamic changes visuals more frequently. One control instead of timing 200 slides.

## Narration drives the cuts

Upload or record audio, or upload a video and only its audio is used. The export keeps your original voiceover and sets generated scenes against it.

Correcting the transcript improves the scene plan. It does not re-record your narration.

## How to make a slideshow video

### Add the speech

Upload or record audio, or upload a video. Supported files include MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG, Opus, FLAC, MP4, MOV, M4V and WebM, up to 50 MB.

### Pick style, ratio and pace

Choose one of 25 picture styles, set 16:9 landscape, 9:16 portrait or 1:1 square, then choose Calm, Balanced or Dynamic pacing.

### Review the story plan

Open any scene to edit its visual description or regenerate it. Use Edit references to change how a recurring person, place or object looks, then Redraw.

### Generate and download

The Generate video button shows the credit price for the reviewed plan. Credits are charged only when the video finishes. Then download the MP4.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I make a slideshow from photos alone?

We could not find a photos-only slideshow builder documented in the OpusClip help centre. Audio to Image Video starts from a voiceover, and the editor timeline accepts .png and .jpg uploads inside a project.

### How long can the slideshow be?

Audio to Image Video supports voiceovers up to 5 minutes and files up to 50 MB, with the scene count capped at 200.

### Does the export include music or captions?

No. The current beta exports your original voiceover with the generated scene images and hard cuts. Captions and music are not burned into the MP4.

### How long does each uploaded image stay on screen?

Two seconds by default. Uploaded images land on the timeline at the playhead and can be resized for a custom duration, including past the clip's maximum length.
