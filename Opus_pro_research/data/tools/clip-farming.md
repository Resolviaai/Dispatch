# Clip Farming

Clip farming is the practice of taking long-form content, usually a stream, podcast or interview, cutting it into many short vertical clips, and posting them at volume across accounts.

Done badly it is a thousand low-effort reposts that nobody watches. Done well it is a repeatable pipeline that puts a creator in front of audiences their long-form never reaches.

The difference is almost entirely in how fast you can produce clips that are actually worth watching.

## Why clippers work this way

### The moments are already there

A three-hour stream contains dozens of genuinely good thirty-second moments. The work is finding them, and that is the part that does not scale by hand.

### Volume is the strategy

Short-form distribution is a numbers game. Posting thirty clips a week beats agonising over three, provided the thirty are cut from real moments rather than padded.

### Speed decides the margin

If a clip takes forty minutes to make, the economics do not work. If it takes two, they do. That is the whole business model.

## What clip farming actually involves

Source long-form: streams, podcasts, interviews, VODs.

Find the moments: the punchline, the hot take, the reaction, the story that lands.

Cut and reframe to vertical, add captions, keep the subject in frame.

Post across accounts and platforms, consistently, and read what performs.

Steps two and three are where the time goes, and they are the steps that automate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is clip farming?

Clip farming is cutting long-form content such as streams, podcasts or interviews into many short vertical clips and posting them at volume, often across multiple accounts, to reach audiences the long-form does not.

### Is clip farming allowed on YouTube and TikTok?

Posting clips of content you own or have permission to use is standard practice. Reposting someone else's content without permission is a different matter and risks takedowns or strikes, so get the rights sorted before you scale.

### How many clips can you get from one video?

It depends on the source. A three-hour stream usually holds dozens of genuinely good moments. OpusClip surfaces them from one upload rather than requiring you to scrub for each.

### Do I need video editing experience?

No. The cutting, reframing and captioning run automatically. You review the shortlist and adjust anything you want changed.
