OpusClip uses intelligent gain adjustment and peak limiting to boost audio levels while preventing clipping and distortion. Our system analyzes your video's audio waveform, identifies quiet segments, and applies optimal amplification to increase volume safely. The result is clear, loud audio that maintains quality across all playback devices and platforms without harsh artifacts or blown-out sound.

### Can I increase volume of video selectively for dialogue vs background music?

Yes. OpusClip's audio controls let you adjust overall volume or target specific audio elements. You can boost dialogue levels independently from background music or sound effects, ensuring voices are clear and prominent while maintaining balanced audio. This selective volume increase is perfect for interviews, tutorials, and vlogs where speech clarity is critical to viewer engagement and comprehension.

### What video formats can I upload to increase volume levels?

OpusClip accepts all major video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WebM. Upload footage from your phone, camera, screen recording, or any source, and our platform will process the audio track to increase volume automatically. After enhancement, export in MP4 format optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, and other social platforms with consistent loudness standards.

### Does increasing video volume affect caption timing or video quality?

No. When you increase volume of video files in OpusClip, only the audio track is modified—your video frames, resolution, and caption timing remain unchanged. Our system preserves visual quality and subtitle synchronization while boosting audio levels. If you add captions after audio enhancement, they'll sync perfectly to your louder, clearer dialogue for maximum accessibility and engagement across all platforms.
