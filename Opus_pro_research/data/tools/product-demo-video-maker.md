## How to make a product demo video

### Record and import

Bring a Loom, Zoom, Zoom Clips, Riverside or StreamYard recording in by link, or upload a local file up to 10GB. Public and unlisted links work, private ones cannot be accessed.

### Choose the Screenshare layout

Set it in your brand template so every demo clip uses it. The screen goes to the top half of the frame and the speaker to the bottom half.

### Trim, caption, annotate

Cut the dead air at the start, remove filler words and pauses, set caption font, size, color, stroke, shadow and case, and add B-roll where the screen sits still.

### Export or publish

Export in full 4K from a true 4K source, hand an XML to Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, or schedule the post from the project page.
