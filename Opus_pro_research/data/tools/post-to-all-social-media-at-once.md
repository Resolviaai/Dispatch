# Post to All Social Media at Once

Pick a clip, tick the accounts, and post now or pick a time. On Pro you can post or schedule to YouTube, TikTok Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Page, LinkedIn, X (Beta), Pinterest, Threads and Bluesky.

Each destination gets its own AI-generated description and hashtags, which you edit before confirming. Starter accounts post in real time to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

### Nine destinations on Pro

YouTube, TikTok Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Page, LinkedIn, X (Beta), Pinterest, Threads and Bluesky, with more listed as coming.

### Multiple accounts per platform

Pro connects up to 6 social accounts, and an add-on pack doubles the cap to 12.

### Official platform APIs

We publish through the APIs the platforms provide, and we do not auto-post without your action.

## How to post everywhere at once

### Connect the accounts

Click the Social Accounts icon in the left sidebar, then Add Account, and authorise each platform.

### Open the posting window

On the results page or the calendar, click the calendar icon on the clip you want to publish.

### Tick every destination

Select the accounts you want. Credit usage is shown beside each account name and totalled at the bottom of the window.

### Post now or choose a time

Click Publish now, or Select time to schedule. Scheduled posts appear in the calendar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many platforms can OpusClip post to at once?

Nine on the Pro plan: YouTube, TikTok Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Page, LinkedIn, X (Beta), Pinterest, Threads and Bluesky. Starter accounts post in real time to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

### Does posting to every platform use credits?

Some destinations do. Each X post uses 3 credits per X account, shown next to the account name and totalled at the bottom of the posting window before you confirm.

### Can I bulk schedule the same clip to several platforms at once?

Not yet. Posting to several platforms from one window works today, but bulk scheduling, which queues many clips with an order and interval, supports YouTube only.

### Does OpusClip auto-post without me?

No. Our help centre states that we use the official APIs provided by platforms such as YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and that we do not spam, auto-post without user action, or manipulate engagement.
