## How to make an explainer video

### Pick your mode first

Standard is for narrated explainers and stories. Standard+ handles people or text on screen, Premium covers multi-shot scenes and dialogue, Ultra is for photorealistic visuals.

### Write the prompt

State what the video should cover, who it is for and the tone. The guide's own example: an informative explainer about future space tourism, with vibrant visuals and a friendly narrator.

### Set style, ratio and voiceover

Choose a visual style or build one in Style Maker, set the aspect ratio, then pick a voiceover. You can clone a voice from a sample up to 30 seconds long.

### Review, then edit

Each mode has its own review stages before the final render. Afterwards you can request edits, and Reimagine rebuilds a video from the same idea.
