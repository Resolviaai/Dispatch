## What this workflow does

Some stories are too good to spend in one post. This workflow takes a single long video (a story, a case study, a tutorial with a payoff) and cuts it into a numbered series: Part 1, Part 2, Part 3. The trick is where the cuts land. Instead of ending each clip at a natural pause, the agent deliberately ends it mid-tension, right before the reveal, and closes with a prompt that Part 2 drops tomorrow. Then it schedules the whole series on consecutive days so the payoff always arrives on time.

## Why it works

Serialization is the single most proven organic growth mechanic on TikTok: a viewer who watches Part 1 has a reason to follow you (so they don't miss Part 2), a reason to come back tomorrow, and a reason to binge the earlier parts if they arrive late. Creators who do this by hand agonize over where to cut. The agent reads the transcript, finds the narrative beats, and places every cut at maximum suspense, which is a different job from finding the best standalone moments. Highlights make viewers say nice clip. Cliffhangers make them hit follow.

## How it works

1. **Give the agent the story.** One long video and how many parts you want. It reads the transcript first to map the arc: setup, escalation, reveal.
2. **It plans the cuts for suspense, not completeness.** Each part ends just before the next payoff. The agent can shift a cut a few seconds earlier on purpose, so the part ends on the sharpest open question.
3. **Each part gets serialized packaging.** Numbered titles (Part 1/3), a caption that opens with a one-line recap for late arrivals, and an ending prompt pointing at tomorrow.
4. **The series schedules itself.** Consecutive days, same time slot, so the audience learns the rhythm. Part 1 posts first; the payoff part lands last.
5. **You approve the plan once.** The agent shows you the cut map (where each part starts, ends, and what question it leaves open) before anything renders or gets scheduled.

## Try this prompt

Here's a 12-minute video where I tell the full story of \[topic\]: \[link or file\]. Read the transcript and cut it into a 3-part cliffhanger series. Each part should end mid-tension right before the next reveal, with a caption that recaps the previous part in one line and teases tomorrow. Show me the cut map first, then schedule the parts to TikTok on three consecutive days at 6pm.

## Tips

- Three parts is the sweet spot: enough to build a habit, short enough that nobody drops off waiting for the ending.
- Ask the agent to end Part 1 on the strongest open question of the whole story, even if that means Part 1 is the shortest part. The first cliffhanger decides whether the series works.
- Pin a comment on each part linking to the previous one; late arrivals binge backwards, and every part becomes a door into the series.
- Save the format: once a story structure works, the same instruction turns every future long video into a series with one message.
