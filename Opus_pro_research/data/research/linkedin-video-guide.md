\<p>The LinkedIn algorithm heavily weights watch time and completion rate. A strong opening hook is essential regardless of your video length.\</p>
\<p>Understanding the LinkedIn algorithm helps explain why certain video lengths and content types outperform others.\</p>
\<h3>#1 Professional Relevance\</h3>
\<p>LinkedIn's algorithm prioritizes content relevant to professional interests. Industry insights and career content perform best.\</p>
\<h3>#2 Dwell Time & Comments\</h3>
\<p>LinkedIn weighs time spent reading and comment quality heavily. Thoughtful comments boost visibility more than likes.\</p>
\<h3>#3 Native Video Priority\</h3>
\<p>LinkedIn strongly favors native video over external links. Video posts get significantly more reach than text-only posts.\</p>
\<h3>#4 Network Engagement\</h3>
\<p>Early engagement from your 1st-degree connections determines broader distribution. Timing posts for when your network is active matters.\</p>
