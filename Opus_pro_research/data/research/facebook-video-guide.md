\<p>The Facebook algorithm heavily weights watch time and completion rate. A strong opening hook is essential regardless of your video length.\</p>
\<p>Understanding the Facebook algorithm helps explain why certain video lengths and content types outperform others.\</p>
\<h3>#1 Meaningful Social Interactions\</h3>
\<p>Facebook prioritizes content that generates comments and shares between friends. Video that sparks conversation wins.\</p>
\<h3>#2 Watch Time & Completion\</h3>
\<p>Videos that retain viewers get broader distribution. This is why 30-60 second clips work well — engaging but completable.\</p>
\<h3>#3 Native Video Priority\</h3>
\<p>Facebook heavily favors native video uploads over links. Reels get additional algorithmic boost in the feed.\</p>
\<h3>#4 Group & Page Engagement\</h3>
\<p>Content shared within active groups gets amplified. Consider cross-posting to relevant Facebook groups.\</p>
