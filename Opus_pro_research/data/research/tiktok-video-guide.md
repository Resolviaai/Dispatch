\<p>Understanding the TikTok algorithm helps explain why certain video lengths and content types outperform others.\</p>
\<h3>#1 Watch Time & Completion Rate\</h3>
\<p>TikTok's algorithm heavily prioritizes videos that are watched to completion. This is why shorter clips (30-60s) often outperform — they're easier to finish.\</p>
\<h3>#2 Engagement Velocity\</h3>
\<p>Likes, comments, shares, and saves in the first hour determine how broadly TikTok distributes your content. Early engagement is critical.\</p>
\<h3>#3 For You Page Discovery\</h3>
\<p>TikTok's FYP serves content to users who don't follow you. Every video has a chance to go viral regardless of follower count — quality over audience size.\</p>
\<h3>#4 Posting Consistency\</h3>
\<p>TikTok rewards creators who post regularly. Consistent daily posting keeps you in the algorithm's rotation and builds momentum over time.\</p>
