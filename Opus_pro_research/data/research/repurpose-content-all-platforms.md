\<h3>1. Identify your best-performing content\</h3>
\<p>Review analytics across all your platforms. Look for videos with high engagement rates (not just views) — these have proven concepts worth repurposing. Sort by watch-through rate to find content that holds attention, which translates well across platforms.\</p>
\<h3>2. Create a master clip library\</h3>
\<p>Organize your top content into a library tagged by topic, format, and original platform. This becomes your repurposing pipeline. Prioritize content that performed well organically, as these concepts have validated audience interest.\</p>
\<h3>3. Adapt hooks for each platform\</h3>
\<p>Every platform has different hook preferences. TikTok rewards bold statements, YouTube favors expertise, and Instagram prioritizes visual appeal. Adapt your hook strategy per platform. Start each repurposed clip by re-recording or re-editing the opening 1-3 seconds to match platform expectations.\</p>
\<h3>4. Adjust editing features per platform\</h3>
\<p>Create a master clip, then adjust editing features per platform: add intros/outros for YouTube, increase visual polish for Instagram, add music and transitions for TikTok. Use OpusClip to automatically generate platform-optimized versions with the right editing features enabled.\</p>
\<h3>5. Optimize format, length, and tone\</h3>
\<p>Build a content pipeline: record once in a longer format, then create platform-specific cuts at optimal lengths (15-30s for Instagram, 15-60s for TikTok, up to 60s for YouTube Shorts). Match tone to platform culture: professional for YouTube/LinkedIn, casual for TikTok, aspirational for Instagram. Test different versions and track performance to refine your cross-platform strategy over time.\</p>
\<h3>What is the best way to repurpose content across platforms?\</h3>
\<p>The most effective approach is to create a master clip from your best-performing content, then adapt it for each platform by adjusting hooks, editing features, and tone. Our analysis of 398 million clips shows that each platform has distinct preferences for features like captions, B-Roll, intros, and music.\</p>
\<h3>Do I need to re-edit my content for every platform?\</h3>
\<p>Yes, simply cross-posting the same clip rarely works. Each platform has different audience expectations, feature norms, and algorithmic preferences. At minimum, adjust your hook, length, and key editing features. Tools like OpusClip can automate much of this adaptation.\</p>
\<h3>Which platform should I repurpose content for first?\</h3>
\<p>Start with the platform where your content style naturally fits best. If you create conversational, personality-driven content, try TikTok first. If your content is polished and visual, Instagram Reels is a natural fit. For educational or expertise content, YouTube Shorts.\</p>
\<h3>How do editing features differ across platforms?\</h3>
\<p>Our data shows significant differences. YouTube content uses more intros, outros, and B-Roll. TikTok content uses more captions, transitions, and music. Instagram sits between the two with emphasis on visual quality and text overlays.\</p>
\<h3>How often should I repurpose content?\</h3>
\<p>Repurpose your top 10-20% of content — the clips that already proved they resonate with audiences. Most successful creators repurpose 3-5 pieces per week across 2-3 platforms. Consistency matters more than volume.\</p>
