\<p>Understanding the YouTube algorithm helps explain why certain video lengths and content types outperform others.\</p>
\<h3>#1 Watch Time & Session Duration\</h3>
\<p>YouTube prioritizes videos that keep viewers on the platform. Longer watch sessions signal quality content to the algorithm.\</p>
\<h3>#2 Click-Through Rate (CTR)\</h3>
\<p>Thumbnails and titles drive initial clicks. YouTube Shorts compete in a swipe-feed, so the first frame matters most.\</p>
\<h3>#3 Engagement Signals\</h3>
\<p>Likes, comments, shares, and subscribes all signal quality. Comments carry significant weight in YouTube's algorithm.\</p>
\<h3>#4 Posting Consistency\</h3>
\<p>Regular uploads signal an active channel. YouTube rewards consistent creators with better recommendations.\</p>
