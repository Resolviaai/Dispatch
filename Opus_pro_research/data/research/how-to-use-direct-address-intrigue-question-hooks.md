\<h3>1. Speak Directly to Your Viewer\</h3>
\<p>Use 'you' and second-person language to create an immediate personal connection. 'Have you ever wondered why...' pulls viewers in because it feels like a conversation, not a broadcast.\</p>
\<h3>2. Ask Questions Your Audience Genuinely Wants Answered\</h3>
\<p>The best question hooks tap into real pain points or curiosities. Research what your audience is searching for and frame those topics as compelling questions.\</p>
\<h3>3. Create an Information Gap\</h3>
\<p>Pose an intriguing statement that hints at valuable knowledge without revealing it. 'There's one thing every successful creator does that nobody talks about' makes viewers need to keep watching.\</p>
\<h3>4. Keep Questions Specific, Not Generic\</h3>
\<p>'What's the best marketing strategy?' is weak. 'What's the one TikTok strategy that tripled my revenue in 30 days?' is specific and irresistible.\</p>
\<h3>5. Follow Up Immediately With Value\</h3>
\<p>After hooking with a question or intriguing statement, deliver the first piece of value within 5 seconds. This cements the viewer's decision to keep watching.\</p>

\<h3>What is a direct address or question hook?\</h3>
\<p>A direct address hook speaks directly to the viewer using 'you,' while a question hook poses a compelling question. An intrigue hook teases valuable information without fully revealing it. All three create a personal connection or curiosity gap that motivates continued viewing.\</p>
\<h3>What types of questions work best as hooks?\</h3>
\<p>Specific, audience-relevant questions outperform generic ones. 'What's the one thing every viral TikTok has in common?' works better than 'Want more views?' Questions that tap into pain points, curiosities, or desires your audience actually has are most effective.\</p>
\<h3>How long should a question hook be?\</h3>
\<p>Keep question hooks to one sentence, ideally under 10 words for short-form video. The question should be immediately understandable. If viewers need to re-read or re-listen, you've already lost them.\</p>
\<h3>Should I answer the question in the same video?\</h3>
\<p>Yes, always answer the question within the video. Leaving questions unanswered leads to viewer frustration and poor retention. The question hook creates an open loop — your video content should close it satisfyingly.\</p>
\<h3>Can I combine question hooks with other hook types?\</h3>
\<p>Yes, hybrid hooks often perform well. For example, combining a question with a shock element ('Did you know that 90% of creators are doing this wrong?') leverages both curiosity and surprise for a stronger opening.\</p>
