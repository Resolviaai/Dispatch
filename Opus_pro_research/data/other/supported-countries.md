# Supported countries and regions

Dear OpusClip Community,

At Opus, our mission is to democratize video content creation and make it accessible to anyone with a story to tell or a passion to share. As our vibrant community flourishes, we're more dedicated than ever to providing an outstanding experience for each member. However, as an early stage startup, we must also recognize our current infrastructure constraints.

To ensure we can focus on elevating and perfecting our product's capabilities and accuracy, we've made the tough decision to temporarily limit our service to 182 selected countries and regions. This is due to the high costs of AI computations and operations, as well as the need to concentrate our limited staff resources.

Rest assured, our commitment to global expansion remains strong, and we plan to extend our services to additional countries and regions as soon as we secure the next round of funding. We're confident it'll just be later this year!

Thank you for your understanding and continued support. We deeply value the geographic diversity of our community, and we're excited to grow together on this journey.
