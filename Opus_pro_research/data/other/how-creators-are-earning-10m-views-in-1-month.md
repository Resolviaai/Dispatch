# How creators are earning 10M+ views in 1 month using video clipping

## What is clipping?

Clipping is the process of turning long-form content into short, high-impact pieces and distributing them across TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts to generate views and compounding reach. In 2025, clipping isn't “just editing", it's a distribution strategy.

## The Limits of Traditional (Non-AI) Clipping Models

| Model | What is It | Pros | Cons | 
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | Buy your own editing software, cut your own clips, and post. | Highest control, lowest cost | Slow, cannot scale | 
| Freelance | You hire editors, pay them a retainer or CPM, manage them directly, and receive videos from them across a few accounts. | Higher output | Time consuming and requires hefty management | 
| Agencies | Partner with an agency, hand over your content, pay a setup fee plus retainer and budget. | Can scale fast | Very expensive and not sustainable long term | 
| Clipping Community | Set up a discord or internal system with existing clippers, manage operations, and pay them through a standard CPM model. | Viewership potential is high | Unpredictable, no ownership | 

Creators are spending more to chase reach, but losing control in the process.
