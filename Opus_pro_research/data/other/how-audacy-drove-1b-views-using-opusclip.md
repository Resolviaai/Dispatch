# How Audacy Drove 1B+ Views by Taking a Tech-Forward Approach to Radio with OpusClip

## Reinventing Legacy Media for a Social-First World

Radio has traditionally been viewed as legacy media by being powerful in reach but slow to adapt to modern content consumption trends. The winners in today's digital age are the broadcasters who know how to translate that reach into new platforms at scale. With 50 stations producing high-quality live content daily, Audacy recognized the opportunity to transform real-time radio moments into a scalable social growth engine by using AI-powered automation.

Audacy is one of the largest audio content creators in the United States, delivering live, local, and national programming across broadcast radio, streaming, podcasts, and social platforms. With hundreds of stations producing newsworthy content every day, Audacy has recognized the opportunity to extend the reach of its on-air moments beyond traditional radio channels.

To make that vision possible, Audacy needed a solution that could:

- Clip and publish content across 20 stations to all major social platforms.
- Accelerate time from live programming to social distribution.
- Scale output without exponentially increasing expenses.
- Focus on content outcomes, not just stream management.

## Why Audacy Chose OpusClip

OpusClip stood out by approaching the problem from the opposite direction.

Instead of starting with stream ingestion and storage, OpusClip was built around:

- AI-driven clipping optimized for social platforms
- Automated camera switching based on who’s speaking
- Intelligent audio detection
to surface high-performing moments
- Built-in multi-platform distribution
- Trending topics ranking to filter 1000+ hrs of weekly live content

This aligned directly with Audacy’s priorities: speed, quality, and scale. While other content creators optimized for infrastructure and over-indexed on one or two platforms, Audacy optimized for outcomes, publishing at scale across all major platforms directly from OpusClip. OpusClip’s features enabled Audacy to streamline how live radio moments were transformed into social-ready content.

Audacy was able to expand its content output without increasing headcount. By leveraging OpusClip, Audacy was able to generate over 1 billion views+ across all participating stations.
