### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI video generator for real estate agents?

The quality of your output depends on how clearly you describe what you want. Start with the property type and key selling points: 'luxury waterfront condo with panoramic bay views and resort-style amenities' gives the AI a strong foundation. Include the tone and target buyer: 'upbeat and aspirational for young professionals' or 'warm and family-focused for suburban buyers' shapes the pacing, music, and visual style. If you have a script or talking points, paste them in. If you're working from an MLS description, add a sentence about what makes this listing special or timely, like 'highlight the newly renovated kitchen and private backyard oasis' or 'emphasize walkability to downtown and top-rated schools.' Specify the video length and platform: '30-second Instagram Reel' or '60-second YouTube listing tour' ensures the AI paces scenes appropriately. Mention any branding elements: 'include my brokerage logo in the lower third' or 'use my voice clone for the narration.' If you want a specific visual sequence, outline it: 'start with an aerial neighborhood shot, then exterior curb appeal, living room, kitchen, master suite, backyard, and end with contact info.' The more context you provide about the property, the buyer persona, and the desired outcome, the more precisely Agent Opus can assemble a video that matches your vision. You can also reference examples: 'create a video similar to luxury hotel promos' or 'match the style of high-end real estate Instagram accounts.' Avoid vague prompts like 'make a listing video'; instead, describe the story you're telling and the emotion you want buyers to feel. With clear input, the AI video generator for real estate agents becomes a tool that understands your market and amplifies your unique selling style.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of an AI video generator for real estate agents?

Agent Opus is designed to handle the majority of real estate video needs, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, the system generates videos from text and static images; it doesn't create original video footage. If you need drone flyovers, walk-through video tours, or live-action shots of you speaking on camera, you'll need to capture that footage separately. However, you can integrate those clips into your prompts by describing them, and the AI will sequence them with motion graphics, voiceover, and branding. Second, highly specialized or niche property types may require more detailed prompts. If you're marketing a historic estate, a commercial property, or a unique architectural design, provide extra context so the AI understands what to emphasize. Generic prompts work well for standard residential listings, but unusual properties benefit from specificity. Third, while Agent Opus sources royalty-free and stock images, you'll get the most authentic results by uploading your own property photos. Relying entirely on auto-sourced visuals can result in generic neighborhood shots that don't perfectly match your listing's exact location or vibe. Fourth, the AI excels at social-ready short-form content (15 to 90 seconds), which is ideal for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook. If you need a 5-minute long-form property documentary, you may need to generate multiple shorter videos and combine them, or provide a very detailed script with clear scene breakdowns. Fifth, voice cloning requires a clean audio sample; background noise or poor recording quality will affect the output. Use a quiet space and a decent microphone for best results. Finally, while the AI handles motion graphics and visual assembly, it doesn't replace strategic marketing decisions. You still need to decide which properties to feature, what messaging resonates with your audience, and how to distribute videos across platforms. Agent Opus accelerates execution, but your expertise as a real estate agent drives the strategy. Understanding these limitations lets you use the tool effectively and know when to supplement AI-generated content with custom footage or manual adjustments.
