### What are best practices for writing prompts in AI Twitch Video Maker to get the most polished results?

The quality of your AI Twitch Video Maker output depends heavily on how you structure your prompt or script. Start by clearly defining the video's purpose: is it a stream announcement, a highlight reel, a sponsor promo, or a tutorial? Be specific about the tone you want. For example, 'energetic hype video for Friday night stream' will generate faster pacing and dynamic motion graphics, while 'calm tutorial explaining game mechanics' will produce cleaner visuals and slower transitions. Include key details like game titles, streamer names, dates, times, and any specific callouts you need. If you're promoting a tournament, mention the bracket structure, team names, and prize pool. If you're recapping highlights, list the key moments or clips you want referenced. The more context you provide, the better Agent Opus can source relevant visuals and structure the narrative. For brand consistency, mention your channel's visual style in the prompt: 'use neon purple and cyan colors,' 'include retro pixel art aesthetic,' or 'modern minimalist gaming graphics.' If you have uploaded brand assets like logos or overlays, reference them: 'feature my channel logo in the intro and outro.' When writing scripts, use natural language and conversational phrasing; the AI voice will sound more authentic. Avoid overly technical jargon unless it's essential to your audience. For longer videos, break your script into clear sections or scenes, which helps Agent Opus pace the visuals appropriately. If you're generating from a blog URL, ensure the article is well-structured with clear headings, as the AI uses that structure to organize the video. Finally, experiment with different prompt styles. Try a detailed script for precise control, or use a short creative brief for more AI-driven interpretation. Review your first few videos to see how Agent Opus interprets your style, then refine your prompts accordingly. Over time, you'll develop a prompt template that consistently delivers the look and feel your channel needs.
