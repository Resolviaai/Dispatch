### What are best practices for prompts in a building video maker?

Effective prompts for a building video maker start with clear project context and desired outcome. Instead of 'make a video about our building,' write 'create a 45-second Instagram Reel showcasing our LEED-certified office renovation, emphasizing sustainable materials and natural light.' Specificity helps Agent Opus select appropriate imagery, set the right pacing, and apply motion graphics that reinforce your message. Include your target audience in the prompt. 'Video for potential commercial tenants' signals different visual priorities than 'video for architecture award submission.' The building video maker adjusts tone, pacing, and emphasis based on who will watch. For construction progress updates, structure prompts chronologically: 'Show foundation pour, framing completion, and exterior finishing for our Main Street mixed-use development.' This gives Agent Opus a clear narrative spine. For property showcases, lead with standout features: 'Highlight the rooftop terrace, chef's kitchen, and skyline views in this luxury condo listing.' When working with technical content, define terms or provide context. Instead of assuming Agent Opus knows your project's specifics, write 'our adaptive reuse project converted a 1920s warehouse into modern lofts, preserving original brick and timber beams.' This helps the building video maker source appropriate historical and contemporary imagery. Specify visual style preferences when relevant: 'clean, minimalist motion graphics' or 'bold, dynamic transitions.' Mention brand requirements upfront: 'include our logo in the lower third and use our brand colors for text overlays.' For voiceover, note whether you want authoritative, conversational, or enthusiastic delivery. If you have existing assets like drone footage or interior photos, mention them: 'incorporate our aerial shots of the construction site.' The building video maker can blend your assets with sourced imagery. Finally, state your distribution plan: 'optimized for LinkedIn with captions' or 'vertical format for TikTok.' This ensures Agent Opus generates the right aspect ratio and pacing for your platform.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of a building video maker?

Agent Opus as a building video maker handles most construction, real estate, and architecture content exceptionally well, but understanding its boundaries helps you set realistic expectations. The system generates videos from text inputs, so it cannot process raw video files you upload. If you have existing drone footage or site walkthroughs, you can describe them in your prompt and Agent Opus will source similar imagery, but it will not edit your specific footage into the final video. For highly specialized technical content, the building video maker performs best when you provide context. If your project involves proprietary construction methods or rare architectural styles, explain them in your prompt rather than assuming Agent Opus will infer details. The more context you provide, the better the system sources relevant imagery and structures the narrative. Agent Opus sources royalty-free and web images automatically, which works well for most building content. However, if your project requires specific branded product placements or exclusive imagery, mention those requirements in your prompt. The building video maker will do its best to match your needs, but extremely niche visual requests may require you to describe alternatives. For projects with complex multi-phase timelines, break your content into clear segments in your prompt. Instead of a dense paragraph covering two years of construction, structure your input chronologically: 'Phase 1: site preparation and foundation, Phase 2: structural framing, Phase 3: exterior finishing.' This helps the building video maker organize scenes logically. Voice cloning requires a clean audio sample of the voice you want to replicate. Background noise or poor recording quality can affect clone accuracy. For best results, provide a clear 30-second sample of the voice speaking naturally. The building video maker also works within platform constraints. A 60-second TikTok video cannot cover the same depth as a 5-minute YouTube explainer. If your content is dense, consider generating multiple shorter videos rather than cramming everything into one piece. Finally, while Agent Opus generates publish-ready videos, highly specific client requests like exact frame-by-frame timing or custom animation sequences may require adjustments to your prompt. The building video maker optimizes for overall quality and coherence, so if you need precise control over every visual element, provide detailed scene-by-scene instructions in your input.
