### What are best practices for writing prompts in an AI video generator for plumbers to get the most effective service videos?

Effective prompts for plumbing videos balance clarity, specificity, and marketing intent. Start by defining the video purpose: is this a service explainer, an emergency offer, a seasonal promotion, or an educational piece? For a service explainer, describe the problem your service solves, the process you follow, and the outcome. For example, a prompt like 'Create a 60-second video explaining our 24/7 emergency plumbing service. Highlight fast response, licensed technicians, and upfront pricing. Show a burst pipe scenario, our truck arriving, and a satisfied homeowner' gives Agent Opus clear structure and emotional beats. Include your unique selling points, such as same-day service, financing options, or warranty coverage. Mention specific services by name: tankless water heater installation, sewer line camera inspection, or whole-home repiping. This helps the AI source relevant visuals and terminology. If you want to feature your own assets, reference them explicitly: 'Use our logo, truck photo, and before-and-after shots from the recent bathroom remodel.' Specify the tone and audience. A video for homeowners should be reassuring and educational, while a video for property managers might emphasize reliability, bulk discounts, and maintenance contracts. For social media, indicate the platform and format: 'Create a 30-second Instagram Reel promoting our spring drain cleaning special with a strong call to action.' If you have a script or outline, paste it directly. Agent Opus will follow your structure and add visuals accordingly. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a plumbing video.' Instead, be concrete: 'Explain why tankless water heaters save money, show installation steps, and end with a free consultation offer.' The more context you provide about your service, audience, and goals, the more targeted and effective the generated video will be.
