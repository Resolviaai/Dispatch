Effective prompts for an economics explainer video maker balance clarity, structure, and audience awareness. Start by defining your core concept and target viewer. A prompt about monetary policy might outline: definition of money supply, central bank tools, interest rate mechanisms, then real-world effects. When converting blog posts or articles, ensure the source content has clear section breaks and examples. Agent Opus performs better with well-structured writing that separates concepts into digestible chunks. Dense academic paragraphs may need light editing for optimal video pacing.
