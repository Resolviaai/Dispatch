### What are best practices for writing prompts in AI Q&A Video Maker to get high-quality results?

\<p>The quality of your AI Q&A video depends heavily on how you structure your input, whether you're using a simple prompt, a full script, or a blog URL. For best results, start by clearly separating questions from answers in your text. Use explicit markers like 'Q:' and 'A:' or number your questions if you're providing a list. This helps Agent Opus understand the structure and create distinct visual segments for each pair. When writing questions, be specific and conversational. Instead of 'What is this product?', try 'What makes this product different from traditional solutions?' The more natural and specific your question, the better the AI can frame the visual context and voiceover delivery. For answers, aim for clarity and completeness, but avoid overly long paragraphs. If an answer has multiple points, use bullet points or numbered lists in your script. Agent Opus will translate these into visual separators or animated lists in the final video. Include context about your brand, product, or topic in the prompt. For example, 'Create a Q&A video about our AI video tool, Agent Opus, targeting marketers who want to create content faster. Use a professional but approachable tone.' This context helps the system select appropriate stock imagery, set the right pacing, and choose visual styles that match your audience. If you want specific visuals, mention them explicitly: 'Show product screenshots when answering the features question' or 'Use office/team imagery for the company culture question.' You can also specify voice and avatar preferences in the prompt: 'Use a female AI voice with a friendly tone' or 'Include an AI avatar presenting the answers.' For blog-to-video conversions, make sure your blog post has clear Q&A formatting with headers or bold text marking each question. Agent Opus will extract the structure, but clean formatting improves accuracy. Finally, test with a short 3-5 question video first to see how the system interprets your style, then refine your prompt based on the output. The more you use AI Q&A Video Maker, the better you'll understand how to structure prompts for your specific content style and audience needs.\</p>

### What are the limitations or edge cases of AI Q&A Video Maker that creators should know about?

\<p>While AI Q&A Video Maker is powerful and handles most question-answer scenarios automatically, understanding its limitations helps you set realistic expectations and structure your content for best results. First, Agent Opus is optimized for text-to-video generation, not video editing. If you already have recorded Q&A footage and want to trim, rearrange, or add graphics to existing video, this tool is not designed for that workflow. It generates new videos from text inputs, so your starting point should always be written questions and answers, not raw video files. Second, extremely long answers (over 500 words for a single question) may result in videos that feel too lengthy for social platforms. Agent Opus will generate the full content, but for social media use cases, consider breaking very long answers into multiple shorter question-answer pairs. This keeps each video segment tight and engaging. Third, highly technical or niche topics may require more explicit visual guidance in your prompt. Agent Opus sources royalty-free and stock imagery automatically, but if your Q&A covers very specialized subjects (like rare medical procedures or obscure industrial processes), the AI may default to generic visuals. In these cases, provide specific visual instructions in your prompt or upload custom images as part of your brand assets. Fourth, while Agent Opus supports voice cloning, the quality of the clone depends on the quality of your source audio. For best results, provide clear, noise-free voice samples. If your source audio is muffled or has background noise, the cloned voice may not sound as natural. AI voices are a reliable alternative if voice cloning doesn't meet your standards. Fifth, Agent Opus generates complete, publish-ready videos, but it does not offer a manual editing interface. You cannot open a timeline and adjust individual scenes, trim clips, or rearrange segments after generation. If the output isn't quite right, the best approach is to refine your prompt and regenerate. This is faster than traditional editing, but it does require a different mindset: think of it as iterative prompting rather than post-production tweaking. Sixth, while the system handles most Q&A formats automatically, highly unconventional structures (like nested questions, branching logic, or choose-your-own-adventure style Q&As) may not translate cleanly. Stick to linear question-answer pairs for best results. Finally, Agent Opus is designed for net-new video creation, not for repurposing or clipping existing content. If you need to extract clips from a long video or add subtitles to pre-recorded footage, those workflows fall outside the scope of AI Q&A Video Maker. Understanding these boundaries helps you use the tool effectively and avoid frustration. For the vast majority of Q&A video use cases, from customer FAQs to educational content to interview-style videos, Agent Opus delivers fast, professional results with zero manual editing.\</p>
