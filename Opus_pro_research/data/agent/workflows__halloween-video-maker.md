Mention "jack-o-lanterns," "haunted houses," "costumed kids," "fog effects," or "candy close-ups" so Agent Opus prioritizes those assets during auto-sourcing. Whether it's "End with 'Shop now at our Halloween sale'" or "Close with 'RSVP by October 25th'," Agent Opus will script that CTA into the final scene and ensure the voiceover emphasizes it. Avoid vague prompts like "Make a Halloween video." Instead, try "Create a 20-second Instagram Reel promoting our haunted maze with fast cuts, eerie music, and a voiceover saying 'Get tickets before they vanish'." The more specific your prompt, the less iteration you'll need, and the faster you'll have a publish-ready halloween video maker output.
