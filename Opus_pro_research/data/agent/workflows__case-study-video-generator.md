### What are best practices for prompts when generating case study videos?

Effective case study video generation starts with clear structure in your prompt or input. Begin by identifying the customer, their industry, and the specific challenge they faced. Include concrete details like company size, previous solutions they tried, or quantifiable pain points. The more specific your setup, the more compelling the generated video's opening will be. Next, outline the solution implementation with enough detail for the generator to create meaningful visuals. Instead of just saying you provided software, describe the key features used, the implementation timeline, or the team involved. These details give Agent Opus material to create varied scenes rather than generic stock footage. The results section is where metrics matter most. Provide specific numbers with context. Rather than stating revenue increased, specify the percentage, timeframe, and comparison point. Include multiple types of results if possible: efficiency gains, cost savings, revenue growth, customer satisfaction improvements. The case study video generator uses these data points to create impactful motion graphics and visual emphasis. For voiceover, decide whether you want a narrator explaining the story or the customer's voice delivering testimonial quotes. If using quotes, provide them verbatim in your input. The generator will integrate them at appropriate moments in the narrative. Consider your target platform when crafting your prompt. Mention if you need a 60-second LinkedIn version versus a 3-minute website version. The generator adjusts pacing and detail accordingly. Include any must-have visual elements in your prompt. If specific product screenshots, interface views, or process diagrams are critical to the story, reference them explicitly. Upload these assets to your Agent Opus library so the system can incorporate them. Finally, specify your call-to-action. Case study videos work best when they guide viewers to a next step, whether that's booking a demo, downloading a full case study PDF, or visiting a landing page. The generator will incorporate your CTA naturally into the video's conclusion.
