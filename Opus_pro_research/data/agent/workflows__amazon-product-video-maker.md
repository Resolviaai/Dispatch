### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-converting Amazon listing videos?

Effective amazon product video maker prompts start with a clear structure: problem, solution, features, and call-to-action. Begin by stating the customer pain point your product solves, then introduce your product as the solution. List 3-5 key features or benefits in priority order, and end with a purchase prompt or guarantee statement. Be specific about visual emphasis. Instead of 'show the product,' write 'open with close-up of stainless steel blade, then pull back to show full knife set on marble counter.' Specify which product angles matter most. For apparel, mention fabric texture shots and fit demonstrations. For tools, call out grip details and action shots. The more visual direction you provide, the better the scene assembly. Include exact text you want on screen. Write out feature callouts like 'Waterproof IP68 Rating' or 'Includes 3 Bonus Attachments' so the motion graphics layer displays your precise messaging. Specify voiceover tone and pacing. Add notes like 'energetic, fast-paced for fitness audience' or 'calm, reassuring for baby products.' If using voice cloning, record your script sample with the exact energy and pacing you want in the final video. Reference your target customer in the prompt. 'For busy parents' or 'for professional contractors' helps the system choose appropriate visual contexts and lifestyle imagery. Mention competitor advantages if relevant, like 'unlike plastic alternatives' or 'twice the battery life of leading brands.' Keep prompts between 150-400 words for best results. Shorter prompts work for simple products, longer prompts suit complex items with multiple features. Test different prompt structures and compare outputs to find what converts best for your product category.
