### How does Lisa AI video generator handle different input types like scripts versus short prompts?

Lisa AI video generator is designed to accept multiple input formats and intelligently adapt its generation process to match. When you provide a full script, Agent Opus treats each paragraph or section as a distinct scene, matching visuals, pacing, and motion graphics to the narrative flow. The system parses your script for key concepts, emotional tone, and visual cues, then assembles a sequence of scenes that mirror your written structure. If you supply a short prompt or brief instead, Lisa AI video generator expands that concept into a complete video by inferring scene transitions, selecting appropriate stock or web images, and generating motion graphics that align with your core message. For outlines, the tool uses each bullet or heading as a scene anchor, filling in visual details and voiceover pacing automatically. Blog URL inputs are processed by extracting the article's main points, headers, and supporting details, then translating those into a video narrative with corresponding visuals and voiceover. In every case, Lisa AI video generator maintains consistency in voice, branding, and visual style, ensuring the final output feels cohesive whether you started with two sentences or two thousand words. The key difference is depth: longer inputs give Agent Opus more narrative structure to work with, while shorter prompts rely on the AI's ability to infer context and expand ideas into a full video sequence. Both paths deliver publish-ready videos without requiring you to manually storyboard, source assets, or sync audio.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts that maximize Lisa AI video generator's output quality?

To get the most from Lisa AI video generator, focus on clarity, specificity, and context in your prompts. Start by defining your core message or goal in one or two sentences. For example, instead of writing 'make a video about productivity,' try 'explain three morning habits that boost focus for remote workers.' This gives Agent Opus a clear structure and target audience. Next, include any visual or tonal preferences. If you want a professional look with minimal text overlays, say so. If you prefer bold, kinetic motion graphics with vibrant colors, mention that. Lisa AI video generator uses these cues to select appropriate stock images, apply motion effects, and set the pacing. When you provide a script, break it into clear sections or paragraphs. Each section becomes a scene, so logical breaks help Agent Opus pace the video naturally. If you're using a blog URL, ensure the article has clear headings and subheadings; Lisa AI video generator parses structure to build scene transitions. For voice and avatar, specify whether you want a formal tone or conversational style. If you're cloning your own voice, record a clean sample with consistent volume and minimal background noise. For AI voices, choose a style that matches your brand: authoritative, friendly, energetic, or calm. Brand consistency matters, so upload your logo, product shots, or any visual assets you want included. Lisa AI video generator integrates these automatically, but the more context you provide, the better the tool can align visuals with your brand identity. Finally, think about your platform and audience. If you're targeting TikTok, mention that you want fast pacing and vertical format. For LinkedIn, specify a professional tone and horizontal or square aspect ratio. Lisa AI video generator adapts to these details, but explicit guidance ensures the output matches your distribution strategy. Avoid vague language and focus on actionable details: who the video is for, what it should accomplish, and how it should look and sound.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of Lisa AI video generator that creators should know about?

Lisa AI video generator is designed to handle a wide range of text-to-video use cases, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, the system works best with clear, structured input. If your prompt is extremely vague or contradictory, Agent Opus may interpret your intent in ways you didn't expect. For example, asking for 'a video about everything' without specifying a topic, audience, or goal will produce a generic result. The more specific your input, the more aligned the output. Second, Lisa AI video generator relies on royalty-free stock libraries and web-sourced images for visuals. If your topic is highly niche or requires very specific imagery that isn't available in stock databases, the tool may substitute similar but not exact visuals. You can mitigate this by uploading your own product shots, screenshots, or branded graphics, which Agent Opus will prioritize over stock. Third, voice cloning quality depends on the sample you provide. If your recording has background noise, inconsistent volume, or poor audio quality, the cloned voice may carry those artifacts. For best results, record in a quiet environment with a decent microphone. AI voices, while natural-sounding, may not capture the exact emotional nuance of a human performance in every context. If your video requires highly expressive delivery, consider recording your own voiceover and uploading it as a custom asset. Fourth, Lisa AI video generator generates videos based on the input length and structure you provide. If you paste a 5,000-word blog post, the resulting video will be longer and may require trimming for certain platforms. Conversely, a two-sentence prompt will produce a short video. Plan your input length to match your desired output duration. Fifth, while Agent Opus supports social aspect ratios, it doesn't automatically create multiple versions of the same video in different formats. If you need both vertical and horizontal versions, you'll need to generate them separately by specifying the aspect ratio in your prompt. Finally, Lisa AI video generator is a text-to-video system, not a manual editor. It doesn't offer timeline controls, scene-by-scene adjustments, or frame-level tweaking. If you need to make changes after generation, you'll need to refine your input and regenerate rather than edit the output directly. This trade-off is intentional: Lisa AI video generator prioritizes speed and automation over granular control. For most creators, the time saved far outweighs the lack of manual editing, but if your workflow requires precise frame-by-frame adjustments, this may not be the right tool for that specific task.
