### What are best practices for prompts when using a physics explainer video maker?

Effective prompts for physics explainer videos balance conceptual clarity with visual guidance. Start by stating the physics principle or phenomenon you want to explain, then outline the explanation structure. For instance, instead of just 'explain momentum,' write 'explain conservation of momentum using collision examples, starting with definition, showing elastic vs inelastic collisions, and ending with real-world applications.' This gives Agent Opus a narrative arc to visualize. Include specific visual cues you want emphasized. Mention if you need force diagrams, energy graphs, particle animations, or lab demonstration footage. The more specific your visual requests, the better the motion graphics engine can generate relevant animations. Specify your audience level. A high school physics explainer needs different pacing and visual complexity than a graduate-level quantum mechanics video. Agent Opus adjusts diagram sophistication and explanation depth accordingly. If you have a full script, paste it directly. The system will parse the explanation flow and generate visuals that sync with each concept introduction. For blog or article URLs, ensure the source material has clear section breaks and concept progression. The physics explainer video maker performs best when the input text has logical structure. Consider including real-world examples or applications. These give the system opportunities to source compelling footage like rocket launches, bridge engineering, or particle accelerator imagery that makes abstract physics tangible. Finally, indicate your preferred tone, whether formal academic, enthusiastic educator, or casual science communicator, so voiceover and pacing match your teaching style.
