### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-converting marketing videos?

Effective prompts for an AI marketing video generator balance creative direction with strategic clarity. Start by defining your core marketing objective in the first sentence, such as driving product trial signups, announcing a limited-time offer, or establishing thought leadership. This frames the entire video structure around conversion goals. Next, outline your key message hierarchy, listing the three to five most important points you want viewers to retain, in priority order. The AI uses this to allocate screen time and visual emphasis appropriately. Include specific brand voice guidelines, whether that is authoritative and data-driven, friendly and conversational, or bold and disruptive. Mention any mandatory elements like logos, product shots, or testimonial quotes you want incorporated, and the system will source or integrate those assets. For voiceover, specify whether you want your cloned voice for personal brand consistency or an AI voice that matches your target demographic. Describe your ideal viewer journey: do you want to start with a problem statement, lead with social proof, or open with a bold claim? This guides scene sequencing. Reference visual style preferences such as minimalist motion graphics, energetic transitions, or cinematic pacing. If you are adapting existing content like a blog post or script, add context about what to emphasize or condense, since written content often needs restructuring for video format. Avoid vague requests like make it engaging; instead, specify engagement tactics like opening with a surprising statistic, using on-screen text to reinforce key points, or ending with a clear next-step CTA. The more you treat the prompt as a creative brief you would give a video producer, the more precisely the AI marketing video generator delivers on your vision while handling all technical execution.
