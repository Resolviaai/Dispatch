You can also submit an outline with bullet points or key messages, and the system will expand those into a cohesive narrative with matching visuals and motion graphics. The more context you provide about tone, audience, and goal, the more precisely the AI can match your vision. ### What are the best practices for writing prompts that generate high-performing Twitter videos?

The most effective prompts for AI Twitter Video Maker combine clarity, context, and outcome. Start with the core message or hook you want viewers to remember. For example, instead of 'Make a video about our product,' write 'Show how our tool cuts meeting prep time in half for remote teams.' That gives Agent Opus a clear narrative arc and visual direction. Include your target audience so the AI can adjust tone and pacing. A prompt like 'Explain blockchain to crypto beginners' will produce a different video than 'Explain blockchain to enterprise CTOs.' Specify the desired length if you have a preference: 'Create a 30-second teaser' or 'Build a 60-second explainer.' Twitter videos perform best between 15 and 60 seconds, so Agent Opus defaults to that range unless you request otherwise. If you have brand assets like logos, product screenshots, or founder headshots, mention them in the prompt and upload the files. For example, 'Use our logo in the intro and show our dashboard UI in the middle section.' This ensures the video feels cohesive with your brand identity. Describe the emotional tone or style: 'Make it energetic and fast-paced' or 'Keep it calm and educational.' Agent Opus adjusts motion graphics, music, and scene transitions to match. If you want a specific call-to-action, include it in the prompt: 'End with a CTA to visit our landing page.' The AI will structure the video to build toward that moment. Avoid vague language like 'Make it cool' or 'Do something creative.' Instead, use concrete details: 'Open with a problem statement, show three quick benefits, and close with a testimonial quote.' The more specific your prompt, the less iteration you need. You can also reference successful Twitter videos you admire and ask Agent Opus to match that style. For example, 'Create a video similar to how tech influencers explain SaaS tools on Twitter, with quick cuts and bold text overlays.' Finally, test different prompts for the same idea. AI Twitter Video Maker generates videos fast enough that you can experiment with multiple angles and pick the one that resonates most with your audience.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using AI Twitter Video Maker for niche or technical topics?

AI Twitter Video Maker excels at generating videos for a wide range of topics, but there are a few edge cases to keep in mind, especially for highly niche or technical content. First, if your topic requires very specific jargon, proprietary terminology, or industry-specific visuals that are not widely available in stock libraries, you will get better results by uploading custom assets and providing detailed context in your prompt. For example, if you are explaining a rare medical procedure or a cutting-edge AI research paper, Agent Opus may default to generic science visuals unless you supply diagrams, charts, or footage. The more specialized your topic, the more important it is to include reference materials and explicit instructions. Second, Twitter videos perform best when they simplify complex ideas into digestible hooks and quick takeaways. If your topic is extremely technical and requires nuanced explanations, you may need to break it into a series of shorter videos rather than trying to cover everything in one 60-second clip. Agent Opus can help you identify the key points to feature in each video, but the platform is optimized for clarity and engagement over exhaustive detail. Third, if your brand voice is highly unconventional or relies on inside jokes, cultural references, or a very specific tone that deviates from mainstream marketing language, you may need to iterate on prompts to train the AI on your style. The system learns from your feedback, so the more videos you generate and refine, the better it gets at matching your unique voice. Fourth, real-time or breaking news topics can be challenging if you need hyper-current visuals or references that were not part of the AI's training data. In those cases, uploading your own screenshots, news clips, or trending images ensures the video feels timely and relevant. Fifth, if you are targeting a non-English audience or using a language with limited AI voice options, double-check the available voiceover languages and accents before committing to a campaign. Agent Opus supports multiple languages, but some languages have more natural-sounding AI voices than others. Finally, if your video concept relies on live-action footage of people, places, or events that do not exist in stock libraries, you will need to film and upload that footage yourself. Agent Opus can integrate user-uploaded video clips into the final output, but it cannot generate photorealistic live-action footage from scratch. For most use cases, these limitations are minor and easily addressed by providing clear prompts and custom assets. The key is to treat AI Twitter Video Maker as a co-pilot: the more context and materials you provide, the more precisely it can execute your vision.
