### What are the limitations or edge cases when using the Katy Perry AI video generator for complex creative concepts?

While the Katy Perry AI video generator excels at producing bold, pop-inspired videos from text prompts, scripts, and blog URLs, there are a few edge cases and limitations to keep in mind. For example, a prompt like 'evoke the feeling of nostalgia' without specific imagery cues (vintage filters, retro props, soft lighting) gives the AI less to work with. To avoid this, always include concrete visual details and mood descriptors. Second, the generator relies on royalty-free stock libraries and web-sourced imagery, so if your concept requires hyper-specific visuals (e.g., a rare product angle, a unique location, or a custom illustration style), you'll need to upload those assets manually. Agent Opus integrates them seamlessly, but it can't generate original artwork from scratch. Third, while AI motion graphics are dynamic and professional, they follow preset animation styles. If you need frame-by-frame custom animation or 3D modeling, those workflows fall outside the scope of a text-to-video generator. Finally, the Katy Perry AI video generator is optimized for short-form content (15 seconds to 3 minutes). Longer videos with complex narratives may require breaking your script into multiple scenes or generating separate clips.
