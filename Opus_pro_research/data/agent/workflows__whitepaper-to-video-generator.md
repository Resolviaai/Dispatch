### What are the best practices for prompts when using a whitepaper to video generator?

Effective prompts for whitepaper to video conversion start with clarity about your goal and audience. Specify whether you want an executive summary for LinkedIn, a detailed walkthrough for YouTube, or a teaser for webinar promotion, because each format requires different pacing and depth. Include the target video length in your prompt, such as 60 seconds for social or 3 minutes for a comprehensive overview, so Agent Opus structures content accordingly. Highlight the core insight or argument you want to lead with, especially if your whitepaper covers multiple topics. For example, if your whitepaper explores five trends but one stat is particularly compelling, note that in your prompt so the AI prioritizes it in the opening scene. Describe your audience explicitly, such as CFOs evaluating software, HR leaders researching retention strategies, or technical buyers comparing solutions, because this shapes vocabulary, pacing, and visual choices. If your whitepaper includes specific data points, quotes, or case study results you want featured, list them in the prompt to ensure they appear in the final video. For tone, specify whether you want authoritative and formal, conversational and accessible, or provocative and challenger-brand style. Include any visual preferences, such as minimalist motion graphics, data-heavy charts, or lifestyle imagery, to guide the AI's visual sourcing. If you have brand assets like logos or product shots, mention them in the prompt so the system integrates them into relevant scenes. For whitepapers with technical jargon, indicate whether to simplify language for broader audiences or maintain technical precision for specialist viewers. The more context you provide about distribution channel, viewer intent, and desired outcome, the better Agent Opus tailors the video to drive your specific business goal.
