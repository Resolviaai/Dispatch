### What are best practices for prompts when using a coffee shop video maker?

Effective prompts for a coffee shop video maker balance specificity with creative freedom. Start with your core message and desired emotional tone. Instead of 'make a video about my café,' write 'showcase our Sunday morning rush with steam rising from espresso machines, baristas calling out orders, and regulars chatting at communal tables, warm and energetic vibe.' This gives Agent Opus clear visual direction while leaving room for creative interpretation. Include sensory details that define your café's personality. Mention the 'rich aroma of freshly ground Ethiopian beans,' 'sunlight streaming through large windows,' or 'chalkboard menu with hand-lettered specials.' These details help the system select appropriate stock footage, apply relevant motion graphics, and set the right mood through pacing and music. Specify your target audience and platform. A prompt for Instagram Reels might emphasize quick cuts and trending music, while a LinkedIn video for corporate catering services would focus on professionalism and convenience. State your call-to-action clearly: 'end with our location and hours' or 'drive viewers to our online ordering page.' For product-focused videos, describe not just the drink but the experience. Rather than 'show our cold brew,' try 'highlight our 24-hour cold brew process, from coarse grounds to smooth pour over ice, emphasizing patience and craft.' This narrative approach creates more engaging content than simple product shots. When promoting events, include context: 'announce our Saturday latte art throwdown with barista competitors, live music, and free tastings, fun and community-focused.' If you have specific brand assets, mention them: 'feature our new logo animation and signature teal color scheme throughout.' The coffee shop video maker performs best when you provide clear intent, sensory details, emotional tone, and desired outcome, but avoid over-prescribing exact shot sequences or technical specifications. Let the AI handle production logistics while you focus on your café's unique story and value proposition.
