### How does an AI teaser video maker handle different teaser formats and hooks?

Agent Opus adapts to the teaser format you need by analyzing your input and matching it to proven teaser structures. If you're promoting a product launch, the AI teaser video maker will prioritize problem-solution pacing with a strong visual reveal. For event teasers, it emphasizes urgency and social proof. For course or content teasers, it leads with transformation and credibility. You control the hook by describing it in your prompt or script. For example, write 'Open with a bold question about productivity' or 'Start with a before-and-after visual,' and the AI will structure the first three seconds accordingly. The motion graphics engine adjusts timing, transitions, and text overlays to match teaser best practices: fast cuts for high-energy launches, slower builds for premium or aspirational offers. You can also specify tone—hype, curiosity, urgency, or educational—and the AI will modulate pacing, music, and voiceover delivery. If you're testing multiple hooks, generate variations from the same script by tweaking the opening line or visual direction in your prompt. Each teaser will share the same core message but lead with a different angle, letting you A/B test hooks across platforms without manual re-editing. The AI teaser video maker also respects platform norms: TikTok teasers get faster pacing and bolder text, while LinkedIn teasers use subtler motion and professional voiceover. This format intelligence is baked into the generation process, so you don't need to specify aspect ratios or platform rules—just describe the teaser, and Agent Opus handles the rest.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts or scripts for an AI teaser video maker?

The quality of your teaser depends on how clearly you communicate the goal, hook, and key message in your input. Start with a one-sentence summary of what you're teasing and why it matters: 'Teaser for a productivity app launch targeting remote workers who struggle with focus.' This gives the AI teaser video maker context for tone, pacing, and visual style. Next, outline the hook—the first three seconds that stop the scroll. Be specific: 'Open with a split-screen showing chaos vs calm' or 'Start with a bold stat: 73% of remote workers feel overwhelmed.' The AI will translate this into motion graphics and scene composition. Then describe the core message or transformation: 'Show how the app turns scattered tasks into a single focused dashboard.' This becomes the middle of your teaser, where the AI will layer product shots, UI demos, or metaphorical visuals. Finally, end with a clear call to action: 'Close with launch date and waitlist link.' For voiceover, specify tone and energy: 'Confident and energetic' or 'Calm and reassuring.' If you're using a script instead of a prompt, write it like you're narrating the teaser yourself—short sentences, punchy language, and natural pauses. The AI teaser video maker will match visuals to each line, so avoid long paragraphs or abstract concepts. Instead of 'Our platform empowers users,' write 'See your tasks. Prioritize instantly. Get more done.' Concrete verbs and visuals make better teasers. If you're generating from a blog URL, the AI will extract key points and structure them into a teaser narrative, but you'll get tighter results by pasting a custom script or outline. Test different hooks and CTAs by generating multiple versions—change the opening question, swap the closing offer, or adjust the tone—and see which teaser drives the most engagement. The AI teaser video maker is fast enough that iteration costs time, not budget.

### How does this AI teaser video maker handle pacing and timing for different platforms?

Pacing is everything in teaser videos, and Agent Opus tailors timing to both your content and the target platform. TikTok and Instagram Reels demand fast cuts, bold text, and a hook in the first second. YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn allow slightly longer builds but still reward front-loaded value. The AI teaser video maker analyzes your script or prompt and maps it to platform-specific pacing templates. For TikTok, expect scene changes every two to three seconds, with motion graphics and text overlays that reinforce the voiceover. For LinkedIn, the AI will use longer holds on key visuals, subtler transitions, and a more measured voiceover delivery. You can override these defaults by specifying pacing in your prompt: 'Fast-paced teaser with quick cuts' or 'Slow build with dramatic pauses.' The AI also adjusts timing based on teaser length. A 15-second teaser will compress your message into three to four scenes with rapid transitions. A 30-second teaser allows for a setup, payoff, and CTA structure with breathing room between beats. If you're teasing a complex product, the AI will prioritize clarity over speed, giving each feature or benefit enough screen time to register. Music and sound design also influence pacing—upbeat tracks push faster cuts, while ambient or cinematic scores support slower reveals. The AI teaser video maker syncs visual transitions to musical beats when appropriate, creating a polished, professional rhythm. If you're generating teasers for multiple platforms from the same script, Agent Opus will export platform-optimized versions automatically: a 9:16 vertical cut for TikTok and Reels, a 16:9 horizontal cut for YouTube, and a 1:1 square cut for LinkedIn and Twitter. Each version will have platform-appropriate pacing, text sizing, and safe zones, so you're not manually resizing or re-editing. This multi-platform intelligence is built into the generation process, saving hours of post-production work.
