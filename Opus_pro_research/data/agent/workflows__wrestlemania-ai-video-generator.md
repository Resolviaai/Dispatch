### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate authentic WrestleMania-style hype and drama in the final video?

Effective prompts for the WrestleMania AI video generator focus on storytelling stakes, character conflict, and emotional intensity rather than just listing facts. Specify key visual moments you want emphasized, such as a signature move, a championship belt reveal, or a dramatic staredown. The AI uses these anchors to build scenes around peak hype moments. Use wrestling terminology naturally, like referencing stipulations (cage match, ladder match, no disqualification), championship lineage, or career milestones. This vocabulary signals to the AI that you want authentic sports entertainment production values rather than generic sports coverage. For maximum drama, structure your prompt with escalating tension, starting with background context, building through conflict points, and climaxing with the match stakes or event payoff. Agent Opus mirrors this narrative arc in the video pacing and visual intensity. Include specific branding elements in your prompt, like mentioning your promotion name, event logo placement needs, or fighter entrance music cues. The more context you provide about the wrestling universe and storyline history, the better the AI can generate video that feels like it belongs in an ongoing narrative rather than a standalone clip. Avoid overly technical video production instructions and instead focus on the emotional experience you want viewers to feel, whether that is anticipation, shock, excitement, or intimidation.
