### How should accountants structure prompts and scripts for the best results with an AI video generator?

Effective prompts and scripts for an AI video generator for accountants follow a clear structure: define the topic and audience, break content into logical sections, and include visual cues. Start by stating the video's purpose and who it is for. For example, 'Create a 90-second explainer for small business owners on how to calculate estimated quarterly taxes.' This tells Agent Opus the scope, length, and audience, which shapes the pacing and complexity of the output. Next, organize your content into distinct sections or talking points. Instead of writing a dense paragraph, use numbered steps or bullet points. For instance: '1. Explain what estimated taxes are and why they matter. 2. Show the formula: prior year tax liability divided by four. 3. List the quarterly deadlines: April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15. 4. Mention penalties for underpayment.' Each section becomes a scene in the video, and the AI video generator for accountants creates motion graphics and visuals to support each point. If you want specific visuals, include them in your script or prompt. Write 'show a calendar highlighting the four quarterly deadlines' or 'display the estimated tax formula on screen with animated numbers.' Agent Opus interprets these cues and sources or generates the appropriate visuals. For client-specific videos, personalize the script with placeholders or segments. For example, 'Hi \[Client Name\], here is your Q4 tax planning summary. Based on your income this year, here are three strategies to consider before December 31.' You can generate variations by swapping in different client names and strategies, and the AI video generator for accountants will produce each version with consistent quality. If you are converting a blog post or article, paste the URL or full text and add a brief prompt like 'Turn this article into a 2-minute video for LinkedIn, focusing on the top three takeaways.' Agent Opus will extract the key points, condense the content, and create a video that fits the platform and time constraint. For voiceover, write your script in a conversational tone, as if you are speaking directly to the viewer. Avoid jargon-heavy sentences or overly formal language unless that matches your brand. The AI video generator for accountants performs best with natural, spoken-style scripts that sound like a real conversation, not a legal document. If you are using voice cloning, this approach ensures the final video feels authentic and engaging. Finally, specify the output format in your prompt: 'Generate a vertical video for Instagram Reels' or 'Create a horizontal video for YouTube.' Agent Opus will format the video accordingly, including aspect ratio, pacing, and visual layout. By structuring your input clearly and including visual and format instructions, you give the AI video generator for accountants everything it needs to produce a polished, professional result on the first try.
