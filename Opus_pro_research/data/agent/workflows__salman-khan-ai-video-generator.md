### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce high-quality educational videos with the Salman Khan AI video generator?

To get the best results from the Salman Khan AI video generator, structure your prompt or script with clear learning objectives and logical flow. Start by stating the topic and the key takeaway you want viewers to understand. For example, instead of writing 'Explain photosynthesis,' write 'Explain how plants convert sunlight into energy through photosynthesis, covering the light-dependent and light-independent reactions.' This specificity helps Agent Opus select relevant visuals and pace the explanation correctly. Break complex topics into numbered steps or bullet points in your script. The generator uses these structural cues to create distinct scenes with matching graphics. If you want a diagram or chart at a specific moment, mention it explicitly: 'Show a diagram of the chloroplast structure here.' For voiceover, write in a conversational tone as if you are speaking directly to a student. Avoid overly formal or jargon-heavy language unless your audience expects it. Include transitions and examples: 'Now let's see how this applies in the real world' signals the system to shift to practical visuals. If you are cloning your own voice, record a clean sample with consistent pacing and energy. For AI voices, choose one that matches your content's tone. You can also specify visual style preferences in your prompt: 'Use clean, minimalist graphics' or 'Include real-world photos and footage.' The more context you provide about your audience and teaching goals, the better the generator can tailor scene selection, pacing, and visual complexity. Finally, review the output and refine your prompt if needed. Agent Opus learns from your input patterns, so iterating on a few videos will help you develop a prompt style that consistently delivers the results you want.
