### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce effective board meeting video recaps?

The most effective prompts for a board meeting video recap maker focus on outcomes, decisions, and action items rather than procedural details. Start by listing the major topics discussed, such as financial performance, product roadmap updates, hiring plans, or market expansion. For each topic, include the decision reached or the key takeaway, like approved budget increase, delayed product launch, or new VP of Sales hire. Agent Opus uses these outcome-focused statements to create video segments that emphasize what changed or what happens next, which is what stakeholders care about most. Avoid writing prompts that read like transcripts or include every comment made during the meeting. Instead, distill the conversation into its essential points. For example, rather than writing that the CFO presented three slides on Q3 revenue and the CEO asked two clarifying questions, write that Q3 revenue exceeded projections by 12 percent and the board approved reinvestment into R&D. The board meeting video recap maker will generate visuals that highlight the 12 percent figure, show upward growth graphics, and transition smoothly to the R&D investment decision. If your meeting included multiple agenda items, structure your prompt with clear separations, such as numbering each topic or using short headers. This helps Agent Opus create distinct video segments with visual transitions that guide viewers through the recap logically. Include specific names, dates, and numbers when relevant, because the system can emphasize these details with on-screen text, motion graphics, or voiceover emphasis. For tone, write your prompt in the voice you want the video to have. If you want a formal, boardroom-appropriate recap, use complete sentences and professional language. If you want a more casual update for internal teams, write conversationally and Agent Opus will match that tone in the voiceover and pacing. Always mention any branding or visual preferences in your prompt, such as using the company logo prominently or including product screenshots for specific discussion topics. The board meeting video recap maker will integrate these elements automatically, but explicit mentions ensure they appear in the right context.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using a board meeting video recap maker for complex or sensitive meetings?

A board meeting video recap maker like Agent Opus is designed for efficiency and automation, which means it works best when your input is clear, structured, and outcome-focused. One limitation is that the system cannot infer context or subtext that was not explicitly stated in your prompt or notes. If your board meeting included nuanced discussions, off-the-record comments, or strategic debates where the final decision was not clearly documented, you need to provide that clarity in your input. For example, if the board debated three options for market expansion but only approved one, your prompt should specify which option was chosen and why, rather than listing all three options without resolution. The board meeting video recap maker will generate a video based on what you write, so ambiguous or incomplete input will produce a video that lacks focus or leaves stakeholders confused. Another edge case involves highly sensitive or confidential information. While Agent Opus does not store or share your content beyond what is necessary to generate the video, you should be mindful of what you include in your prompts. If certain board decisions are not yet public or are subject to regulatory restrictions, do not include them in your recap prompt unless you intend for the video to be shared only with authorized viewers. The system does not automatically redact or filter sensitive content, so you are responsible for ensuring your input is appropriate for the intended audience. For meetings with extensive data, charts, or financial tables, the board meeting video recap maker can incorporate these elements if you provide them as part of your input or reference them in your prompt. However, the system is optimized for narrative-driven recaps rather than data-heavy presentations. If your board meeting was primarily a financial review with dozens of metrics, you may need to summarize the key takeaways in your prompt rather than expecting the video to display every data point. Agent Opus will create visual representations of the most important numbers, such as revenue growth or budget allocations, but it is not a replacement for detailed financial slide decks. Timing is another consideration. If your board meeting lasted several hours and covered many topics, a single video recap may feel rushed or incomplete if you try to include everything. The board meeting video recap maker is most effective when you focus on the top three to five outcomes or decisions and let those drive the video narrative. You can always generate multiple shorter recaps for different audiences or topics rather than trying to fit everything into one video. Finally, if your organization has strict compliance or legal review requirements for external communications, you should treat the generated video as a draft that requires approval before distribution. The board meeting video recap maker produces publish-ready videos, but that does not mean they are automatically approved for all contexts. Build a review step into your workflow if necessary, and use the speed of Agent Opus to create drafts quickly so your legal or communications team has more time to review and refine.
