### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating Wayne Rooney AI video content?

The Wayne Rooney AI video generator works best when you provide clear direction and understand its content sourcing approach. Agent Opus auto-sources royalty-free and web imagery, which means it finds publicly available photos and graphics of Rooney, football matches, and related sports content. However, it cannot access premium licensed footage from broadcasters like Sky Sports or ESPN. If your video concept requires specific match clips that are behind licensing walls, Agent Opus will source alternative imagery that conveys the same moment or theme. For example, if you request 'Show the exact broadcast footage of Rooney's 2011 bicycle kick,' the system will find still images, fan photos, or graphics representing that goal rather than the copyrighted video clip. To work within this constraint, focus prompts on moments, themes, and analysis rather than demanding exact broadcast footage. Write 'Highlight Rooney's most spectacular goals with dynamic graphics and commentary' instead of 'Use the official Premier League footage of his top 10 goals.' Agent Opus will create compelling content using available imagery and motion graphics that illustrate your points without copyright issues. Another edge case involves very recent or obscure Rooney content. If you request a video about a brand-new interview or a little-known charity appearance from last week, Agent Opus may have limited imagery because the content hasn't proliferated online yet. For best results, focus on well-documented aspects of Rooney's career: his playing days, major tournaments, famous goals, managerial career, and widely covered public moments. The system excels at generating content around these established topics because abundant imagery exists. For voiceover and avatar content, Agent Opus does not impersonate Wayne Rooney himself. You cannot generate a video where an AI avatar pretends to be Rooney speaking in first person. Instead, create content about Rooney with your own voice, your brand's narrator, or an AI voice that discusses him in third person. This respects personality rights and keeps your content clearly positioned as analysis or tribute rather than impersonation. If you want Rooney's actual voice or likeness, you would need separate licensing agreements outside Agent Opus. The system also works best with focused concepts rather than sprawling multi-topic requests. A prompt like 'Create a complete documentary covering Wayne Rooney's entire life, career, family, and legacy in 10 minutes' will produce a surface-level overview because Agent Opus must compress too much information. Instead, break large projects into focused videos: one about his England career, another about his Manchester United years, a third about his transition to management. Each focused video will have better pacing, deeper insight, and more engaging visuals than trying to cover everything at once.
