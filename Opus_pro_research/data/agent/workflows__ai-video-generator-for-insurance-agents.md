### How does an AI video generator for insurance agents handle policy scripts versus short prompts?

Agent Opus accepts both detailed policy scripts and brief prompts, adapting its scene assembly and pacing to match the input length and complexity. When you provide a full script with specific coverage details, premium breakdowns, and exclusion clauses, the system parses each section and creates distinct scenes that visually reinforce key points. For example, a whole life policy script might generate scenes showing premium payment timelines, cash value growth charts, and beneficiary payout scenarios, each with motion graphics that highlight dollar amounts and policy terms. The voiceover reads your script verbatim, synchronized with visuals that illustrate abstract concepts like underwriting or actuarial tables. If you supply a short prompt such as 'Explain term life insurance to young families in 60 seconds,' Agent Opus generates a concise script internally, selects family-focused stock imagery, and assembles a fast-paced video that covers death benefit, affordability, and application simplicity without overwhelming the viewer. The difference lies in scene count and depth. Long scripts yield multi-minute explainers suitable for email nurture sequences or website resource pages, while short prompts produce social-ready clips optimized for attention-grabbing hooks and quick calls to action. Both workflows use the same AI motion graphics engine to animate policy tables, highlight coverage limits, and transition between concepts smoothly. You can also paste an outline with bullet points covering deductibles, co-pays, and out-of-pocket maximums, and the system expands each bullet into a narrated scene with relevant healthcare or accident visuals. This flexibility means you don't need to write a perfect script upfront. Start with rough notes or a client FAQ list, and Agent Opus structures the narrative, sources visuals, and applies voiceover to create a coherent video that educates prospects and supports your sales process.

### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI video generator for insurance agents?

Effective prompts for an AI video generator for insurance agents balance specificity with clarity, guiding the system toward the exact message and tone you need without overloading it with jargon. Start by stating the policy type and target audience explicitly, such as 'Create a 90-second video explaining Medicare Supplement Plan G to retirees unfamiliar with Medigap.' This tells Agent Opus to use senior-friendly language, slower pacing, and visuals like doctor visits, prescription bottles, and hospital scenes rather than generic stock footage. Include the key points you want covered in order of priority. For instance, 'Cover original Medicare gaps, Plan G benefits, premium costs, and enrollment deadlines' ensures the video addresses objections and questions prospects ask most often. Mention any compliance or regulatory language that must appear, such as 'Include disclaimer that rates vary by state and carrier,' so the voiceover script incorporates legally required disclosures naturally. Specify the desired video length and platform. A prompt like 'Generate a 30-second Instagram Reel highlighting term life affordability for millennials' produces a vertical, fast-cut video with bold text overlays and upbeat pacing, while 'Create a 3-minute YouTube explainer on whole life cash value for high-net-worth clients' yields a landscape video with detailed charts, slower narration, and professional tone. If you have brand assets, reference them in the prompt: 'Use my agency logo in the lower third and my voice clone for narration.' This ensures consistent branding across all videos. Avoid vague prompts like 'Make a video about car insurance' because the system won't know whether to focus on liability limits, collision coverage, discounts, or claims processes. Instead, say 'Explain how uninsured motorist coverage protects drivers in hit-and-run accidents, targeting urban commuters.' The more context you provide about audience pain points, policy features, and desired outcomes, the more precisely Agent Opus tailors scene selection, visual metaphors, and voiceover emphasis to match your sales strategy and compliance requirements.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of an AI video generator for insurance agents?

While an AI video generator for insurance agents automates scene assembly, voiceover, and visual sourcing, certain scenarios require human oversight to ensure accuracy and compliance. Highly regulated disclosures, such as state-specific insurance mandates or carrier-mandated language, should be reviewed before publishing because the AI generates scripts based on your input but doesn't interpret legal nuances. For example, if your prompt asks for a video on flood insurance, Agent Opus will create a general explainer, but you must verify that any NFIP-specific language matches current federal guidelines. The system sources royalty-free stock images and web visuals, but it cannot access proprietary carrier materials like branded policy documents or internal rate charts unless you upload them as custom assets. If your video requires a specific carrier's logo or a unique infographic your agency designed, you'll need to provide that file so Agent Opus can incorporate it. Voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples. Background noise, heavy accents, or inconsistent recording conditions may reduce the naturalness of the cloned voice, so recording a clean sample in a quiet environment improves results. The platform generates videos in standard aspect ratios for social platforms, but if you need a custom resolution for a trade show display or a specific CMS, you may need to export and reformat externally. Agent Opus excels at educational and promotional content but isn't designed for live-action testimonials where a real client speaks on camera. You can generate testimonial videos using text quotes and stock footage or an AI avatar, but authentic client video requires traditional filming. The system also doesn't integrate directly with CRM platforms or policy management systems, so if you want to auto-generate personalized videos triggered by policy renewals or claims events, you'll need to manually input the data or use API workflows if available. Finally, while the AI handles pacing and scene transitions well for most topics, highly technical subjects like actuarial modeling or reinsurance structures may benefit from a human review to ensure the visuals accurately represent complex concepts. These limitations are manageable with clear prompts, asset preparation, and a quick review process, allowing you to leverage automation for the bulk of video production while maintaining the accuracy and compliance insurance content demands.
