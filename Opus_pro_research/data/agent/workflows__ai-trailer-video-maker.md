It infers pacing, determines which points deserve visual emphasis, and creates motion graphics that highlight key benefits or features. For full scripts, Agent Opus respects your exact wording and timing cues, mapping each line to a scene with appropriate visuals, voiceover pacing, and transitions. The AI analyzes script tone and intent to select matching motion graphics styles, whether that means fast cuts and bold text for a product launch or smooth transitions and elegant overlays for a brand story. Regardless of input type, Agent Opus maintains consistent visual branding, ensures voiceover sync, and outputs a cohesive trailer that feels intentionally designed rather than auto-generated. The key is that you control the message depth: minimal input gets you a punchy teaser, detailed scripts give you precise control, and everything in between works seamlessly.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-quality trailers with AI trailer video maker?

Effective trailer prompts balance clarity with creative direction. Start by defining your core message in one sentence: what you're promoting, who it's for, and why it matters. Then add 3-5 key points you want the trailer to communicate, whether those are product features, event highlights, or content themes. Include tone and style cues like energetic, cinematic, minimal, or bold so Agent Opus can select appropriate motion graphics and pacing. Specify your target platform if you have a preference, since a TikTok trailer often benefits from faster cuts and vertical framing while a LinkedIn trailer might use longer scenes and professional overlays. Mention any brand assets you want featured, such as your logo placement, product shots, or specific color schemes. If you have voiceover preferences, note whether you want a conversational tone, authoritative narration, or enthusiastic pitch style. For best results, describe the emotional journey you want viewers to experience: start with curiosity, build excitement, end with urgency. Avoid overly generic language; instead of saying make it exciting, specify what makes your offer exciting, like exclusive early access or a limited-time launch. If you're promoting an event, include date, location, and what attendees will gain. For product trailers, highlight the transformation or problem solved rather than listing features. Agent Opus uses all these details to construct scene flow, select visuals, time voiceover emphasis, and create motion graphics that reinforce your message at every moment.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using AI trailer video maker for complex narratives?

Agent Opus excels at straightforward promotional trailers but has specific constraints for highly complex or abstract narratives. Trailers that require precise timing to match external audio, like syncing to a specific music track beat-by-beat, may need prompt refinement to achieve exact alignment since the AI optimizes for narrative flow over rigid timing. If your trailer concept involves intricate visual metaphors or symbolic imagery that isn't easily described in text, the AI may interpret those directions literally rather than abstractly, so you'll get better results by describing the emotional impact you want rather than the specific visual metaphor. Trailers with more than 8-10 distinct scenes or talking points can become rushed or lose focus, so it's often better to create multiple shorter trailers than one overloaded piece. Agent Opus sources royalty-free and web images automatically, but if your trailer requires very specific niche visuals like rare product angles or unique event photography, you'll need to upload those assets rather than relying on auto-sourcing. The AI handles standard aspect ratios and platform formats well, but if you need unusual dimensions or multi-screen layouts, those may require custom prompts or fall outside current capabilities. For trailers that depend on real human testimonials or specific on-camera talent, you'll need to provide that footage as an asset since Agent Opus generates AI avatars or uses your uploaded video rather than sourcing third-party talent. Finally, highly technical or jargon-heavy content may need simplified language in your prompt to ensure the AI creates visuals that support rather than confuse the message.
