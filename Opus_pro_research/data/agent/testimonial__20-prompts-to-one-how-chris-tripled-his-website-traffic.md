## **The Claw Machine Problem**

Chris has ridden a platform shift before. He started at Yellow Book selling phone-book ads, taught himself SEO when clients stopped renewing, and built his first website on Homestead.com. It looked horrendous. It also rang 30 to 50 calls a month for his first client — and within six months of going out on his own in 2009, demand forced him to leave an $80,000 job. Seventeen years and hundreds of websites later, he saw AI coming and embraced it early.

The tools didn't hold up their end. Across HeyGen, Suno, and InVideo, one video would be great and the next 30 unusable. An opening scene would be perfect, then the rest would drift; recovering the original look could take another 20 prompts, with CapCut patching in between. He built a ChatGPT prompt system, kept the winning prompts in Notion, spent $200 a month, and still couldn't get consistency.

I felt like I was playing that machine where you go pick up the bear. You put your money in, you drop the claw down, and you had these bits and pieces of videos that weren't usable.
