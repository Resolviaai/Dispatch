Upload reference images if you have specific visual inspiration—a photo of your sanctuary, a graphic from your sermon series, or a color palette guide. For multi-campus consistency, create a base prompt template, then customize timing and campus-specific announcements. Test different prompt styles to discover what works best for your context, and save successful prompts for future services.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using a church countdown video maker?

Understanding Agent Opus limitations helps set realistic expectations and optimize results. The AI excels at standard countdown formats—timer text, worship imagery, announcements, music—but highly specialized visual effects may require iteration. If you need precise animation timing synchronized to specific musical beats, describe that rhythm in your prompt, though perfect musical synchronization sometimes needs a second generation attempt. Complex multi-layered announcements with many details work better split across multiple countdown videos or simplified into key points. The system handles 3-5 announcement items well; 10+ items create visual clutter. For very specific imagery—your exact sanctuary interior, particular stained glass windows, or custom illustrations—upload those images directly rather than relying on AI sourcing. Stock and web imagery covers most worship themes effectively, but unique architectural details or proprietary graphics need manual upload. Voice cloning for announcements requires a clean audio sample of the speaker; background noise or poor recording quality affects clone accuracy. If your pastor's voice sample includes echoes or crowd noise, the cloned voice may sound less natural. For best results, record a short script in a quiet space specifically for voice cloning. Extremely short countdowns—under 60 seconds—limit how much visual variety and announcement content fits naturally. Conversely, very long countdowns—over 10 minutes—may feel repetitive unless you provide varied content ideas in your prompt. The AI works within copyright-safe content: it won't recreate specific copyrighted worship songs, album artwork, or branded imagery without proper uploads. Background music comes from royalty-free libraries, not commercial worship albums. If your church has specific licensing for particular songs, you'll need to add that audio separately after generation. Language support focuses primarily on English; non-English countdown text and announcements work but may have fewer motion graphics style options. For churches with multiple language services, generate separate countdowns for each language rather than attempting multilingual text in one video. Technical limitations include output resolution—Agent Opus produces high-quality HD video suitable for most projection systems, but if you need 4K for massive LED walls, check output specifications. The system optimizes for standard worship projection setups. Finally, while Agent Opus eliminates editing, it doesn't replace strategic planning: the AI builds what you describe, so unclear or contradictory prompts produce unclear results. Invest time in thoughtful prompt writing for best outcomes.
