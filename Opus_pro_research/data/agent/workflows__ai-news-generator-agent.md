When you paste a short prompt or headline, the AI news generator creates a concise video segment with tight pacing and punchy visuals perfect for social media. For longer scripts or articles, it automatically breaks the content into logical scenes, sources relevant visuals for each segment, and adjusts motion graphics and voiceover pacing to maintain viewer engagement throughout. The system analyzes your text to identify key facts, quotes, and narrative beats, then assembles scenes that flow naturally from intro to conclusion. You can input a bullet-point outline and Agent Opus will expand it into a full narrative with corresponding visuals, or provide a polished script and the generator will follow it precisely. This flexibility means you can use the same AI news generator for daily updates, weekly deep dives, and everything in between without changing your workflow or learning different tools for different formats.

### What are best practices for writing prompts and scripts for an AI news generator?

The most effective approach is to write clear, structured scripts that separate facts from context. Start with a strong lead sentence that summarizes the story, then organize supporting details in descending order of importance, just like traditional news writing. When using an AI news generator, include specific visual cues in your script where you want particular images or graphics to appear. For example, write 'Show product launch event' or 'Display quarterly revenue chart' and Agent Opus will source or generate appropriate visuals for those moments. Use short paragraphs and natural speech patterns since the voiceover will read your text verbatim. Avoid overly complex sentences or jargon unless your audience expects technical language. If you're pasting an article URL instead of a script, the AI news generator will extract the key points automatically, but you'll get better results if the source article has clear section headers and well-structured paragraphs. For breaking news or quick updates, a simple prompt like 'Create a 45-second video explaining \[event\] with focus on \[key impact\]' works well. The generator will build a complete narrative from that brief. Include any brand-specific terminology, proper nouns, or pronunciation notes in your script so the voiceover handles them correctly. If you want specific pacing or emphasis, use punctuation strategically: periods create pauses, exclamation points add energy, and em dashes signal transitions. The AI news generator respects these cues when assembling scenes and timing cuts.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using an AI news generator?

Agent Opus excels at creating news videos from text, but there are scenarios where you'll need to provide additional guidance or assets. The AI news generator sources visuals automatically from stock libraries and web images, but if your story requires very specific, proprietary footage like exclusive interview clips, internal company photos, or custom animations, you'll need to upload those assets directly. The system will integrate them into your video, but it can't generate truly unique footage that doesn't exist anywhere online. For highly technical or niche topics, the AI may select generic visuals if it can't find relevant stock imagery. In those cases, providing a few key images or product shots ensures your video has the right specificity. Voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples. If you provide a recording with background noise or inconsistent volume, the cloned voice may not sound as natural. The AI news generator handles most accents and speaking styles well, but extreme vocal characteristics or very fast speech patterns might require multiple sample recordings to capture accurately. For stories that rely heavily on data visualization, like financial reports or scientific studies, you'll get better results by creating charts or graphs externally and uploading them rather than expecting the AI to design complex infographics from scratch. The generator can animate and integrate these visuals beautifully, but it's not a data visualization tool. Finally, while Agent Opus handles scene pacing automatically, stories with very unconventional narrative structures like non-linear timelines or multiple parallel storylines may need more explicit scene-by-scene direction in your script. The AI news generator assumes a straightforward beginning-middle-end structure unless you specify otherwise. These aren't dealbreakers, just areas where a bit of extra input from you produces significantly better results.
