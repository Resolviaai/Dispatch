## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does a real estate explainer video maker handle property listings versus market updates?

Agent Opus adapts to both listing-focused and educational real estate content by analyzing your input and structuring scenes accordingly. For property listings, the real estate explainer video maker emphasizes key features like square footage, bedrooms, location highlights, and unique selling points. It sources property photos you provide or pulls relevant neighborhood and architectural imagery, then layers AI motion graphics to highlight amenities, floor plans, or price points. For market updates and buyer guides, Agent Opus shifts to data visualization and trend explanation. You can paste a script discussing interest rates, inventory levels, or seasonal trends, and the system generates charts, neighborhood maps, and contextual visuals that support your narrative. The voiceover pacing adjusts to match educational versus promotional tone. Best practice: for listings, lead with the property address and top three features in your prompt; for market updates, structure your script with clear sections like 'Current Inventory,' 'Price Trends,' and 'Buyer Tips' so Agent Opus can create distinct visual chapters. Both formats benefit from including your brokerage logo and agent headshot to maintain brand consistency across all video types.

### What are best practices for prompts when using a real estate explainer video maker?

Effective prompts for real estate explainer videos balance detail with clarity. Start with the video's purpose: 'Create a listing video for a 3-bedroom colonial in downtown Boston' or 'Explain why spring is the best time to buy in Austin.' Then provide 3 to 5 key points you want covered, such as 'highlight the renovated kitchen, walkable neighborhood, and low property taxes' for a listing, or 'cover inventory trends, mortgage rate outlook, and seasonal competition' for a market update. Agent Opus performs best when you write in natural, conversational language rather than bullet points. Instead of 'Kitchen: granite counters, stainless appliances,' write 'The kitchen features granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, perfect for entertaining.' This gives the real estate explainer video maker better context for scene pacing and voiceover tone. If you have specific visuals in mind, mention them: 'Show the backyard patio at sunset' or 'Include a map of nearby schools.' For voice and avatar, specify 'Use my cloned voice with a professional but friendly tone' or 'AI voice, authoritative male narrator.' Avoid jargon unless your audience expects it; Agent Opus will mirror your language style. Finally, indicate the target platform if it affects length: 'YouTube long-form' versus 'Instagram Reel under 60 seconds.' The system adjusts pacing and visual density accordingly.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of using a real estate explainer video maker for property marketing?

Agent Opus excels at generating polished videos from text, but real estate professionals should understand a few constraints. First, the system does not film original footage. If you need drone shots of a specific property exterior or walk-through video of a custom home's interior, you must provide those images or video clips as inputs. Agent Opus can incorporate your uploaded media into the final video, layering motion graphics and voiceover, but it cannot create new footage of physical spaces. For properties with unique architecture or luxury features, supply high-quality photos to ensure the video showcases them effectively. Second, hyper-local neighborhood context requires clear prompts. If you want to highlight a specific coffee shop, park, or school, mention it by name in your script so Agent Opus can source relevant imagery. Generic prompts like 'great neighborhood' will yield generic visuals. Third, the real estate explainer video maker is optimized for educational and promotional content, not interactive tools. It cannot generate clickable floor plans, 3D walkthroughs, or virtual staging. If your marketing strategy relies on interactive elements, use Agent Opus for top-of-funnel awareness videos and pair them with specialized tools for deeper engagement. Fourth, compliance and disclosure language must be included in your script. Agent Opus will narrate and display any text you provide, but it does not auto-generate fair housing disclaimers, MLS compliance statements, or legal disclosures. Always review your local regulations and include required language in your prompt. Finally, video length affects detail. A 60-second Instagram Reel cannot cover 10 property features in depth; prioritize the top 3 for short formats and save comprehensive walkthroughs for YouTube long-form content.
