### What types of prompts work best for generating Khaby Lame-style reaction videos?

The most effective prompts for the Khaby Lame AI video generator follow a simple structure: describe an overcomplicated problem or process, then describe the simple alternative. The clearer your contrast, the stronger your video. For example, instead of a vague prompt like make a funny video about cooking, try: Show someone using five different gadgets to crack an egg, then show someone just cracking it with their hands. That specificity gives Agent Opus the visual beats it needs to structure your reaction video. Strong prompts identify a relatable frustration. Think about everyday tasks people overcomplicate: opening packaging with scissors and knives when there's a tear strip, using complex apps when a simple built-in feature exists, following ten-step tutorials for something that takes one click. The best Khaby-style content highlights these universal moments of unnecessary complexity. Your prompt should name the complicated approach with enough detail that Agent Opus can visualize it, then contrast it with the obvious simple solution. You can also provide context about your audience and goal. If you're targeting marketers, your prompt might focus on overcomplicated analytics dashboards versus simple reporting. For fitness creators, it might contrast elaborate workout equipment setups versus bodyweight exercises. Agent Opus adapts the visual storytelling and avatar reactions to match your audience's frame of reference. Longer prompts work well when you want to control pacing. You can specify: Start with a confused expression while showing the complicated method, hold on the confusion for two seconds, then cut to a confident look while demonstrating the simple way, and end with the signature shrug. This level of direction helps Agent Opus nail your comedic timing. You can also input full scripts with scene descriptions, or paste a blog URL about a complicated process you want to simplify visually. Agent Opus extracts the key contrast and translates it into reaction video format automatically.
