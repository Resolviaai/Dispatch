Agent Opus reads the script structure and creates corresponding visual sequences with appropriate pacing and transitions. Third, you can provide an outline with bullet points or section headers. The AI expands each point into a complete scene with supporting visuals and motion. Fourth, you can drop in a blog post or article URL. ### What are best practices for writing prompts in an AI video generator for agencies to ensure brand-aligned results?

Effective prompts for Agent Opus combine creative direction with specific brand requirements. Start with the core message or campaign goal in one sentence. For example, instead of saying create a video about our product, say create a 30-second product demo showing how our app saves small business owners time on invoicing. This gives the AI video generator for agencies a clear outcome to build toward. Next, specify visual tone and style. Mention whether you want clean and minimal, bold and energetic, or warm and conversational aesthetics. Agent Opus uses these cues to select motion graphics, pacing, and visual treatments. Third, reference any brand assets you have uploaded. Say use the client logo in the lower third and feature the product hero shot in the opening scene. The system knows where to place these elements for maximum impact. Fourth, indicate the target platform and audience. A LinkedIn video for B2B decision-makers will have different pacing and tone than a TikTok video for Gen Z consumers. Agent Opus adjusts scene length, text density, and visual complexity based on platform norms. Fifth, mention voice and avatar preferences. Specify whether you want a professional female voice, an energetic male voice, or a cloned voice from a client sample. If you want an on-screen avatar, describe the persona. Finally, include any must-have story beats or product features. List three key benefits that must appear or note that the video should end with a specific call to action. The more context you provide, the closer the first output will be to your vision. That said, you do not need to write a novel. A well-structured 100-word prompt with clear goals, brand notes, and platform context will generate strong results. The AI video generator for agencies is designed to interpret creative briefs the way a human video producer would, filling in production details while respecting your strategic direction.
