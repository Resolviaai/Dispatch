## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does article to video generation work in Agent Opus?

Agent Opus reads your article URL or pasted text and extracts the core narrative, key points, and structure. You provide a brief prompt describing the tone, style, or target platform, and Agent Opus assembles a complete video from scratch. The system breaks your article into logical scenes, sources relevant visuals from royalty-free web and stock libraries, generates AI motion graphics for text overlays and transitions, and syncs everything to a voiceover. You can clone your own voice or choose an AI narrator. If you want an on-screen presence, add an AI avatar or upload your own face. Agent Opus handles pacing, scene timing, and visual composition automatically, so you never touch a timeline or editing interface. The result is a polished, publish-ready video that matches your article's message and your brand's look. You can generate multiple formats from the same article, such as a vertical short for TikTok, a square post for Instagram, and a landscape explainer for YouTube, all in one workflow. This approach eliminates the traditional bottleneck of scripting, storyboarding, and manual editing, letting you turn written content into video as fast as you can write the article itself.

### What types of articles work best for article to video conversion?

Agent Opus handles a wide range of article formats, from how-to guides and listicles to opinion pieces and case studies. The key is clear structure and actionable or visual concepts. Articles with numbered steps, bullet points, or distinct sections translate especially well because Agent Opus can map each segment to a dedicated scene. For example, a blog post titled 'Five Ways to Boost Productivity' becomes a five-scene video with motion graphics highlighting each tip. Longer articles work too, but you should provide a prompt that tells Agent Opus which sections to emphasize or condense. If your article includes data, quotes, or examples, Agent Opus will pull those into text overlays and visual callouts. Articles with abstract or heavily technical content may require a more detailed prompt to guide tone and visual style, but the system adapts to almost any written format. You can also paste an outline or bullet-point draft instead of a finished article, and Agent Opus will expand it into a full video narrative. The tool is especially powerful for evergreen content, product announcements, tutorials, and thought leadership pieces that benefit from visual reinforcement. If your article already performs well in search or social, turning it into video extends its reach and lifespan across platforms like TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

### Can I customize visuals, voiceover, and branding in article to video workflows?

Yes. Agent Opus gives you full control over brand assets, voice, and visual style without requiring manual editing. Upload your logo, product images, or custom graphics, and Agent Opus will integrate them into the video automatically. You can specify color schemes, font preferences, or visual themes in your prompt, and the AI motion graphics engine will adapt accordingly. For voiceover, you can clone your own voice by uploading a short audio sample, or choose from a library of AI voices with different accents, tones, and speaking styles. If you want an on-screen presence, add an AI avatar that matches your brand persona, or upload a video of yourself and Agent Opus will sync your face to the generated voiceover. The system also lets you set the aspect ratio and platform target in your prompt, so you can generate a vertical video for TikTok, a square video for Instagram, or a landscape video for YouTube, all from the same article. Background music is added automatically, but you can specify mood or genre in your prompt if you want a particular vibe. Because Agent Opus is a prompt-to-publish system, all customization happens through natural language instructions rather than sliders or menus. This makes it fast to iterate: if the first video is close but not perfect, refine your prompt and regenerate in minutes.

### How does article to video compare to manual video production?

Traditional video production from an article involves scripting, storyboarding, sourcing stock footage, recording voiceover, editing timelines, adding text overlays, color grading, and exporting multiple formats. Even with templates and editing software, this process takes hours or days per video. Article to video with Agent Opus collapses that entire workflow into a single prompt. You paste your article URL or text, describe your vision in a few sentences, and Agent Opus generates a finished video in minutes. There is no timeline to scrub, no clips to trim, and no export settings to configure. The system handles scene assembly, visual sourcing, motion graphics, voiceover synthesis, and aspect ratio formatting automatically. This speed advantage is critical for creators and marketers who publish frequently or need to test multiple video variations. For example, you can generate a 30-second teaser, a 60-second explainer, and a 90-second deep dive from the same article in under 15 minutes, then publish the version that performs best. Manual production cannot match that iteration speed. Agent Opus also eliminates the need for expensive stock footage subscriptions or freelance editors, making high-quality video accessible to solo creators and small teams. The trade-off is less granular control over individual frames or transitions, but for most use cases, the time savings and output quality far outweigh the loss of manual tweaking.
