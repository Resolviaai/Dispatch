### How does a sales video maker like Agent Opus turn a brief into a finished video?

Motion graphics are applied automatically, with text animations, transitions, and visual effects that emphasize value propositions and keep viewers engaged. Voiceover is generated using either your cloned voice (if you provide a sample) or a professional AI narrator you select. The system synchronizes the narration timing with scene changes and on-screen text for a cohesive flow. If you choose to include an avatar, Agent Opus composites a presenter into the video, matching lip sync and gestures to the script. Background music is selected to fit the energy and tone of your sales message. The final output is rendered in social-ready aspect ratios, optimized for platforms like LinkedIn, email embeds, and landing pages. The entire process happens in minutes, with no manual timeline editing, trimming, or asset hunting required. You go from sales brief to publish-ready video in one automated workflow, allowing you to iterate on messaging and test different pitches faster than traditional video production ever allowed.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts in a sales video maker to maximize conversion?

To get the most effective sales videos from Agent Opus, structure your prompts and scripts with clarity and conversion intent. Start with a strong hook that addresses a specific pain point or desire your prospect has. For example, instead of a generic opening like 'Welcome to our product,' try 'Tired of losing deals because your proposals take too long to create?' This immediately signals relevance and grabs attention. Next, clearly articulate the core value proposition in simple, benefit-driven language. Avoid jargon or feature lists without context. Instead of 'Our platform leverages AI-driven automation,' say 'Generate professional proposals in 5 minutes instead of 5 hours.' Agent Opus will translate these concrete benefits into visual scenes and motion graphics that reinforce the message. Include a clear structure: problem, solution, proof, and call to action. The AI performs best when it can identify these narrative beats in your script. If you are pasting a longer script, use short paragraphs or bullet points to help the system parse scene breaks naturally. When describing visuals you want, be specific but not overly prescriptive. For instance, 'Show a dashboard with rising revenue graphs' gives Agent Opus enough direction to source or generate relevant imagery, while 'Use stock photo ID 12345' limits the AI's ability to optimize. If you have brand assets like logos, product screenshots, or team photos, upload them and reference them in your prompt. The system will prioritize these authentic visuals over generic stock, making your sales video feel more credible and personalized. For voiceover, decide whether your own voice or a professional AI narrator better matches your brand. Founder-led sales videos often benefit from voice cloning for authenticity, while enterprise product demos may perform better with a polished AI voice. Test both. Keep your script concise. Sales videos perform best at 60 to 90 seconds for social platforms and email, or up to 2 minutes for landing pages and detailed product demos. Agent Opus will pace scenes to match your target length, but shorter, punchier scripts generally convert better. Finally, end with a single, clear call to action. 'Book a demo,' 'Start your free trial,' or 'Download the guide' work better than multiple competing CTAs. Agent Opus will emphasize this final frame with motion graphics and voiceover pacing to drive the desired action.
