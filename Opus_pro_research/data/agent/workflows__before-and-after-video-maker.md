### How does a before and after video maker handle different types of transformation content?

Agent Opus adapts to any before and after scenario by analyzing your prompt or script to understand the transformation narrative. For product improvements, the AI emphasizes feature upgrades and benefit callouts with side-by-side comparisons. For fitness or beauty transformations, it creates timeline-based reveals with progress indicators and motivational pacing. For home renovations or space makeovers, the system generates room-by-room wipe transitions and wide-to-detail shot progressions. You describe the transformation in plain language—'show our app's old cluttered interface versus the new streamlined design' or 'demonstrate three-month weight loss progress with weekly check-in photos'—and the before and after video maker interprets the narrative structure. It automatically determines optimal comparison layouts: split-screen for simultaneous viewing, sequential reveals for dramatic impact, or overlay wipes for spatial transformations. The AI also adjusts pacing based on content type. Quick product demos get snappy transitions and fast cuts. Personal transformation stories receive slower, more emotional pacing with longer holds on key moments. You can guide this by specifying tone in your prompt—'energetic and fast-paced' or 'inspirational and reflective'—and the system adjusts motion graphics speed, transition timing, and voiceover cadence accordingly. For complex transformations with multiple stages, the before and after video maker breaks your narrative into logical chapters, creating visual separators and progress markers that help viewers track the journey. This works whether you're showing a single dramatic change or a multi-step process with intermediate milestones.

### What are best practices for prompts when using a before and after video maker?

Effective prompts for before and after videos start with clear framing of the transformation timeline and outcome. Begin with context: 'Create a 60-second before and after video showing how our skincare serum reduced acne over 8 weeks.' This tells the AI the duration, subject, and timeframe. Next, specify your visual assets: 'Use the uploaded before photo from January and after photo from March, plus stock footage of the product being applied.' If you don't have real images, describe what to source: 'Find stock images of acne-prone skin and clear skin for comparison.' Then define the comparison style: 'Start with a side-by-side split screen, then transition to a full-screen before image that wipes to reveal the after image.' This guides the motion graphics engine. Include voiceover direction: 'Narrate the transformation journey with an empathetic female voice, emphasizing the emotional impact of clearer skin.' For product-focused before and after videos, list specific features or improvements to highlight: 'Call out reduced redness, smaller pores, and improved texture with animated labels.' For progress transformations like fitness or construction, specify milestone markers: 'Show week 1, week 4, and week 8 checkpoints with date stamps.' The before and after video maker responds well to emotional tone cues: 'Keep the mood inspirational and hopeful' or 'Make it energetic and celebratory.' If you have brand guidelines, mention them: 'Use our logo in the bottom right, maintain our blue and white color scheme for text overlays.' For multi-stage transformations, break your prompt into acts: 'Act 1: Establish the problem with before footage. Act 2: Show the solution being applied. Act 3: Reveal the dramatic after results.' This narrative structure helps the AI pace the video effectively. Avoid vague requests like 'make it look good'—instead, reference specific visual styles: 'Use clean, minimal motion graphics like Apple product videos' or 'Create bold, high-energy transitions like fitness influencer content.' The more concrete your vision, the better the before and after video maker can execute it.

### How does a before and after video maker handle technical limitations like image quality differences?

Agent Opus intelligently manages common technical challenges in before and after videos, starting with image quality disparities. When your before photo is lower resolution than your after photo—common in transformation stories where early images came from phone cameras and later ones from professional shoots—the AI applies consistent upscaling and color grading to minimize visual jarring. It analyzes both images for lighting, saturation, and sharpness, then normalizes them so the comparison feels cohesive rather than disjointed. For extreme quality gaps, the before and after video maker uses motion graphics overlays and transition effects that draw attention away from resolution differences. A well-timed wipe or animated frame can make a lower-quality before image feel intentional rather than amateurish. Aspect ratio mismatches get similar treatment. If your before image is 4:3 and your after image is 16:9, the system intelligently crops, letterboxes, or uses creative framing to maintain visual balance in split-screen layouts. It won't awkwardly stretch images—instead, it applies cinematic black bars or subtle zoom animations that make the format difference feel like a stylistic choice. Color temperature inconsistencies also get automatic correction. Before photos shot in warm indoor lighting and after photos in cool outdoor light are balanced so the transformation reads clearly without the distraction of clashing color casts. For before and after videos using stock footage alongside user-uploaded images, the AI matches visual styles by adjusting saturation, contrast, and grain to create a unified aesthetic. Timing challenges—like when you have five before images but only two after images—are solved through smart pacing and repetition. The before and after video maker might create a montage of all five before images with quick cuts, then hold longer on the two after images for dramatic impact. Or it might use the strongest before image multiple times with different zoom levels or angles to maintain visual interest. Audio sync issues, like when voiceover length doesn't match visual content, are resolved by adjusting motion graphics speed, adding B-roll, or extending transition durations. The system ensures your narration never feels rushed or awkwardly padded. For creators worried about technical perfection, the before and after video maker prioritizes narrative clarity over pixel-perfect uniformity, making even imperfect source material work in service of the transformation story.
