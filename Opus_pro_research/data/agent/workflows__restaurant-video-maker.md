## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does a restaurant video maker handle different types of content like menus versus brand stories?

Agent Opus adapts its video generation approach based on the content you provide. For menu-focused videos, the system emphasizes food visuals, pricing callouts, and appetite appeal—think close-ups of signature dishes with dynamic text overlays for item names and descriptions. When you input a brand story or restaurant origin narrative, the AI shifts to a storytelling structure with longer scene durations, ambient shots of your space, and voiceover pacing that lets the narrative breathe. You can provide a short prompt like 'promote our new brunch menu with pancakes and mimosas' for a 15-second social clip, or paste a full script about your farm-to-table sourcing philosophy for a 90-second brand video. The restaurant video maker analyzes your input length, tone, and keywords to determine scene count, visual style, and motion graphics intensity. If you upload your own food photography, those images anchor the visual narrative. If you only provide text, Agent Opus sources relevant stock footage of similar dishes and dining environments, then applies your logo and brand colors to maintain consistency. This flexibility means one tool handles everything from daily special announcements to investor pitch videos without requiring you to learn different workflows or templates.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate effective restaurant videos?

Effective prompts for a restaurant video maker balance specificity with creative freedom. Start with your core message—are you announcing a new menu, promoting an event, or building brand awareness? Then add sensory details that help the AI select appropriate visuals and pacing. Instead of 'make a video about our Italian restaurant,' try 'showcase our handmade pasta process, from flour to plate, highlighting our chef's 20 years of experience in Tuscany.' Include the emotional outcome you want: 'make viewers crave our wood-fired pizza' or 'convey the elegance of our tasting menu experience.' Mention specific dishes by name, especially signature items that differentiate your restaurant. If you have particular visual assets you want featured, note them: 'use our patio sunset photos and the new logo.' For voiceover, specify tone—'warm and welcoming for families' versus 'sophisticated and refined for date nights.' Indicate your call-to-action clearly: 'end with our reservation link' or 'promote our happy hour discount code.' The restaurant video maker performs best when you provide context about your audience and platform. A TikTok video promoting late-night tacos needs punchy, fast-cut visuals and trending music cues, while a LinkedIn video for corporate catering requires professional voiceover and testimonial-style messaging. You can also reference successful video styles: 'create a video similar to Chipotle's ingredient transparency videos but for our farm partnerships.' The AI understands these creative directions and translates them into scene selection, transition speed, and graphic emphasis.

### Can a restaurant video maker maintain consistent branding across multiple videos for different locations or concepts?

Yes, Agent Opus is built for brand consistency across restaurant video campaigns. When you first set up your account, upload your complete brand kit—logos in various formats, color hex codes, approved fonts, food photography style guides, and any existing video assets. The restaurant video maker stores these as your brand profile, then automatically applies them to every video you generate. If you operate multiple locations, create separate brand profiles for each, or use a master profile with location-specific overlays. For restaurant groups with different concepts under one umbrella, the system can switch between brand profiles mid-project. This matters when you're generating a week's worth of social content—Monday's fine dining video uses your upscale brand palette and serif fonts, while Wednesday's casual concept video switches to bold colors and playful motion graphics, all without manual adjustments. Voice consistency works the same way. Clone your brand spokesperson's voice once, and every video can use that same vocal signature, whether you're promoting a new appetizer or announcing extended hours. For restaurants with multiple languages or regional markets, you can generate the same video concept in English, Spanish, and Mandarin, each with culturally appropriate visuals and voiceover, while maintaining your core brand identity. The AI also learns from your feedback—if you consistently adjust certain elements in your generated videos, the system adapts future outputs to match those preferences, creating a self-improving brand consistency engine that gets smarter with each video you produce.
