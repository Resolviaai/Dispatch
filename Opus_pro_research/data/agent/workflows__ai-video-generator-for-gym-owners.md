### How does an AI video generator for gym owners handle different types of content like class promos versus transformation stories?

Agent Opus adapts its video structure based on the content type you provide. For class promos, describe the workout format, intensity level, target audience, and schedule details in your prompt. The AI will emphasize energy and movement, using quick cuts, upbeat pacing, and motion graphics that highlight class benefits like calorie burn or strength gains. It sources dynamic fitness footage showing exercises similar to your class and overlays your gym logo with the class time and sign-up call-to-action. For transformation stories, provide member results, timeline, and training approach. Agent Opus shifts to a narrative structure with before-and-after visual comparisons, testimonial-style pacing, and graphics that showcase metrics like weight loss or strength improvements. You can paste a written member testimonial or outline the journey in bullet points. The system interprets the emotional arc and builds scenes that progress from challenge to achievement. If you include specific facility photos or trainer images, those get woven into the story to reinforce authenticity. The key difference is input framing. A promo prompt might read: 'New 6am HIIT class, 45 minutes, burns 500 calories, focuses on functional movements, starts Monday.' A transformation prompt might read: 'Sarah lost 40 pounds in 6 months through our strength training program and nutrition coaching, went from struggling with stairs to deadlifting 200 pounds.' Agent Opus recognizes these patterns and adjusts scene selection, pacing, music energy, and graphic emphasis accordingly. Both outputs maintain your brand colors and logo placement, but the storytelling rhythm changes to match the content goal.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate effective gym marketing videos?

Effective prompts for gym video generation balance specificity with flexibility. Start with the video's core purpose in one sentence: 'Promote our new yoga class to busy professionals' or 'Showcase our personal training results.' This frames the AI's creative direction. Next, include concrete details that differentiate your gym. Instead of 'We offer great workouts,' write 'Our 30-minute express classes fit lunch breaks, combining strength circuits with mobility work.' Mention your unique selling points like small group sizes, specialized equipment, or coaching credentials. If you have facility photos, reference them: 'Use our turf area photo for sled push scenes' or 'Show our recovery lounge when discussing post-workout amenities.' For voiceover, specify tone: 'Energetic and motivating' versus 'Calm and educational' produces different AI voice selection and pacing. Include a clear call-to-action: 'Book a free trial class at our website' or 'DM us to claim your first month discount.' Agent Opus will emphasize this in closing graphics. When generating workout demonstration videos, list 3 to 5 key exercises or movements rather than full routines. 'Kettlebell swings, goblet squats, Turkish get-ups' gives the AI enough to build a cohesive sequence without overloading the video. For blog-to-video conversion, your existing article structure works well, but add a summary sentence at the top: 'This post explains why strength training prevents injuries in runners, covering muscle balance, joint stability, and programming tips.' That helps the AI prioritize which sections become video scenes. Avoid vague language like 'make it engaging' or 'use cool graphics.' Instead, request specific elements: 'Highlight our 500+ member community' or 'Show transformation metrics in animated bar charts.' The more you describe what success looks like for this specific video, the better Agent Opus can deliver a result that drives your gym's goals.

### How does this AI video generator handle gym-specific technical details like exercise form cues or training program structures?

Agent Opus processes fitness terminology and training concepts with context awareness, but your input clarity determines output accuracy. When you include exercise names, the AI sources footage showing those movements or similar patterns. If you write 'barbell back squat with emphasis on depth and knee tracking,' the system prioritizes visuals that demonstrate proper squat mechanics rather than generic gym scenes. For form cues, structure your prompt as instructional steps: 'First, set up with feet shoulder-width apart. Second, brace your core and initiate the descent. Third, drive through your heels to stand.' Agent Opus translates these into sequential scenes with text overlays highlighting each cue. The pacing slows slightly to give viewers time to absorb technical details, unlike the rapid cuts of a hype promo video. When explaining training program structures like periodization or progressive overload, use simple frameworks: 'Our 12-week strength program starts with a 4-week foundation phase building movement patterns, then a 6-week hypertrophy phase adding volume, finishing with a 2-week peak phase testing new maxes.' The AI creates a visual timeline or phase breakdown graphic, then illustrates each phase with appropriate training footage. It recognizes that foundation phase content should show learning and technique, while peak phase content should show intensity and achievement. For nutrition or recovery content, provide clear takeaways: 'Post-workout nutrition window, aim for 20-30 grams protein within 90 minutes, supports muscle repair and growth.' Agent Opus generates graphics displaying these numbers prominently and sources visuals of relevant foods or supplement prep. One limitation to understand: the AI sources existing footage and images, so highly specialized movements or proprietary equipment may not have exact visual matches. In those cases, provide your own photos or video clips of your gym's setup. Upload a clip of your unique sled track or your custom-built rig, and Agent Opus integrates it into the generated video. For voiceover accuracy on technical terms, use phonetic spelling in parentheses if needed: 'Supraspinatus (soo-pruh-spy-NAY-tus) muscle activation.' This ensures AI voices pronounce specialized anatomy or exercise names correctly. The system won't replace a certified trainer's judgment on exercise prescription, but it translates your expertise into visual content that educates your audience and positions your gym as knowledgeable and professional.
