For reports heavy on numerical data, Agent Opus automatically generates animated charts, comparison graphics, and trend visualizations. ### What are best practices for prompts when using a report to video generator?

Effective prompts for report to video generation balance detail with clarity, giving Agent Opus enough context to make smart creative decisions while avoiding over-specification that limits AI capabilities. Start by identifying your report type and primary audience. A prompt like 'quarterly sales report for executive team highlighting 23 percent revenue growth and new market expansion' immediately frames tone, complexity level, and key metrics. Include the report's core message or conclusion upfront. 'Research findings showing customer preference shifted from feature A to feature B due to mobile usage patterns' tells the system what narrative thread to emphasize throughout the video. Specify any critical data points that must appear, but let the AI determine visual treatment. Instead of 'show a bar chart comparing Q1 to Q4,' say 'compare Q1 and Q4 revenue performance' and trust the motion graphics engine to choose the most effective visualization. Mention brand requirements explicitly. 'Use our logo, maintain professional tone, include product screenshots from our dashboard' ensures visual consistency without micromanaging every frame. If your report has a specific structure you want preserved, outline it: 'Start with problem statement, show three supporting data points, conclude with recommended action.' This guides scene assembly while leaving creative execution to the AI. For voice and pacing, indicate audience expertise level. 'Technical audience familiar with industry terminology' produces different narration than 'general stakeholder overview requiring plain language explanations.' When working from existing report documents, you can simply upload and add brief context: 'Annual impact report for nonprofit donors, emphasize community outcomes over operational details.' The generator reads the full document but uses your guidance to prioritize content. Avoid overly prescriptive prompts that specify exact scene counts, transition types, or visual styles. The report to video generator's strength is intelligent interpretation. Focus your prompt on what matters strategically and let AI handle tactical execution.
