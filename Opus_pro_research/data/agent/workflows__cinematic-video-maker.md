### What are best practices for writing prompts that maximize cinematic quality in Agent Opus?

Effective prompts for a cinematic video maker like Agent Opus balance specificity with creative freedom, providing enough detail to guide the AI while leaving room for intelligent interpretation. Start by defining the core message and emotional tone. For example, instead of saying 'Make a video about our app,' write 'Create a 90-second cinematic video that showcases our meditation app as a calming escape from daily stress, using serene nature visuals, soft transitions, and a warm, reassuring voiceover.' This gives Agent Opus clear direction on mood, pacing, and visual style. Next, specify key scenes or narrative beats. You might structure your prompt like a mini-outline: 'Scene 1: Morning chaos, fast cuts, urban imagery. Scene 2: User opens app, visuals slow down, nature footage begins. Scene 3: Guided meditation session, close-ups of peaceful faces, soft music. Scene 4: User returns to day feeling centered, confident body language, uplifting finale.' This level of detail helps the AI understand narrative flow and allocate appropriate screen time to each segment. Include sensory and stylistic cues. Words like 'cinematic,' 'dramatic,' 'minimalist,' 'vibrant,' or 'moody' signal the visual treatment you want. Mention specific colors, lighting styles, or reference aesthetics if relevant. For example, 'Use golden hour lighting and earthy tones' or 'Apply a modern tech aesthetic with blues and whites.' These cues guide the AI's motion graphics and image sourcing decisions. Specify voiceover characteristics. Indicate whether you want an energetic, authoritative, conversational, or soothing tone. If using voice cloning, note any emphasis points or pacing preferences. For example, 'Voiceover should be calm and measured, with pauses after key benefits.' If you have brand assets, mention them in the prompt: 'Include our logo in the intro and product screenshots at the 30-second mark.' This ensures Agent Opus integrates your materials seamlessly. Finally, indicate the target platform and duration. Saying 'Create a 60-second Instagram Reel' versus 'Create a 3-minute YouTube video' helps the AI optimize pacing, aspect ratio, and content density. The best prompts are conversational yet precise, treating Agent Opus like a creative collaborator who needs context to deliver your vision.
