### What are best practices for prompts in a promo video maker?

Getting the best results from a promo video maker like Agent Opus starts with clear, structured prompts that give the AI enough context to generate a compelling video. Begin by defining your core message: what is the one thing you want viewers to remember? State this upfront in your prompt or script. For example, instead of a vague brief like 'make a video about our app,' try 'create a promo video that shows how our app saves small business owners 10 hours per week by automating invoicing and expense tracking.' Specificity helps the promo video maker understand the narrative arc and prioritize the right features. Next, outline the key points or scenes you want covered. You do not need to script every word, but a simple structure like 'intro hook, problem statement, solution demo, customer benefit, call to action' gives the AI a clear roadmap. If you have a script, paste it in full. Agent Opus will interpret the pacing, tone, and emphasis from your writing. If you are working from a blog post or article URL, the promo video maker will extract the main ideas and translate them into visual storytelling, but you can still add a short prompt to guide the focus, such as 'emphasize the cost savings and ease of use.' Include any brand-specific language, taglines, or calls to action you want featured. For example, 'end with our tagline: Simplify your business, amplify your growth.' If you have visual preferences, mention them: 'use modern, minimalist motion graphics' or 'include our product demo screenshots prominently.' Upload your logo and any product shots before generating, so the promo video maker can integrate them seamlessly. Finally, specify the platform and audience if relevant: 'create a 30-second Instagram Reel for e-commerce entrepreneurs' or 'generate a 60-second LinkedIn video for B2B decision-makers.' This context helps Agent Opus choose the right pacing, tone, and aspect ratio. With these best practices, your prompts will consistently produce polished, on-brand promotional videos that resonate with your target audience and drive results.
