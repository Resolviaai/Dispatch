### What are best practices for prompts when using a Mark Zuckerberg AI video generator?

Effective prompts for generating Mark Zuckerberg AI videos balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by defining the core message or topic in one or two sentences. The more context you provide about audience, tone, and purpose, the better Agent Opus can tailor the output. Specify the intended platform if it matters: TikTok content benefits from faster pacing and visual hooks, while LinkedIn videos work better with longer explanations and professional framing. If you want the Mark Zuckerberg avatar to reference specific products, companies, or concepts, name them explicitly in the prompt. The system will source relevant visuals and integrate them into the narrative. For voice and delivery style, indicate whether you want conversational, authoritative, enthusiastic, or measured tone. Agent Opus adjusts avatar gestures and voice cadence accordingly. If you have brand assets like logos or product images, mention them in the prompt so the system knows to incorporate them. Avoid overly vague instructions like 'make it engaging' or 'add some graphics.' Instead, describe what engagement looks like for your use case: 'open with a surprising statistic, then show three real-world examples with on-screen text highlights.' The system interprets structured guidance better than abstract goals. You can also specify video length: '30-second teaser' or '90-second explainer' helps the AI pace content appropriately. If you're generating a series of videos, maintain consistent prompt structure across episodes so the output style stays cohesive. Finally, test iteratively. Generate a first draft, note what works and what needs adjustment, then refine your prompt language for the next version. The AI learns from specificity, so the more precise your instructions, the closer the first output will be to your vision.
