### How does a dentist video maker handle different types of dental content like procedure explanations versus patient testimonials?

Agent Opus adapts to the content type you provide. For procedure explanations, you can input a clinical script or outline describing steps, benefits, and recovery. The dentist video maker will generate scenes with medical-style visuals, step-by-step motion graphics, and a professional voiceover tone. For patient testimonials or practice introductions, you can use a conversational script or even record a brief outline. Agent Opus will create a warmer visual style with office photos, smiling patient imagery, and a friendly voiceover tone. The key is in your prompt: specify the audience (new patients, existing patients, referring doctors) and the goal (educate, reassure, promote). Agent Opus interprets that context and adjusts scene pacing, visual style, and narration accordingly. You can also upload specific assets like before-and-after photos for cosmetic dentistry videos or X-ray images for educational content. The system integrates these assets intelligently, placing them where they reinforce your message. If you are creating a series of videos on different procedures, you can maintain consistency by reusing your practice logo, color scheme, and voice clone across all videos. This ensures every video feels like it came from the same professional source, even though each one is generated independently from a new prompt or script.

### What are best practices for writing prompts or scripts for a dentist video maker to get the most professional results?

Start with a clear structure: introduction, main points, and call to action. For patient education videos, open with the problem or question (e.g., 'Many patients ask about teeth whitening options'), then explain the solution in simple terms, and close with next steps (e.g., 'Schedule a consultation to find the best option for you'). Use plain language, not jargon. Agent Opus works best when your script sounds like you are talking to a patient, not reading a textbook. If you are explaining a procedure, break it into numbered steps or phases. This helps the dentist video maker create distinct scenes for each part of the process. Include specific visual cues in your script. For example, write 'Show our office reception area' or 'Display a diagram of a dental implant' so Agent Opus knows which assets or stock imagery to prioritize. If you want your own voice, record a short audio sample first and clone it in Agent Opus. Then your generated videos will sound like you, building trust with patients who recognize your voice. For social media videos, keep scripts under 60 seconds of spoken content. Agent Opus will pace the visuals to match, but shorter videos perform better on Instagram and TikTok. If you are converting a blog post, choose posts with clear subheadings and concise paragraphs. Agent Opus will map those sections to video scenes, so well-organized writing translates to well-organized videos. Finally, specify your brand elements in the prompt: 'Use our blue and white color scheme' or 'Include our practice logo in the lower corner.' This ensures every video reinforces your brand identity without manual editing.
