### What are best practices for prompts when using the Kevin Hart AI video generator?

Effective prompts for Kevin Hart-style videos balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by defining the core topic or scenario, like 'a comedian's take on online dating' or 'motivational speech about waking up early, Kevin Hart style.' Then add tone or energy cues such as 'high energy,' 'exaggerated reactions,' or 'self-aware humor' so the AI knows how to pace and deliver the content. If you want specific visual elements, mention them: 'include gym equipment visuals' or 'show before-and-after comparisons with comedic contrast.' For avatar and voiceover, specify whether you want an AI avatar with animated expressions or a user avatar if you've uploaded your own. If you're using voice cloning, note that in the prompt so the AI prioritizes your vocal style over default AI voices. Structure matters too. If you provide a script, break it into clear beats or sections so the AI can map scenes logically. For example, 'Intro: set up the problem. Middle: three escalating examples. Outro: punchline and call to action.' This helps the AI generate tighter pacing and stronger comedic flow. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a funny video' because the AI needs context to generate relevant visuals and jokes. Instead, say 'create a 60-second video where Kevin Hart-style comedian roasts people who hit snooze five times, include alarm clock visuals and exaggerated morning struggle scenes.' The more you guide the AI on topic, tone, and key visual moments, the closer the output will match your vision. You can also reference specific Kevin Hart bits or themes if you want the AI to emulate a particular style, like 'channel his 'Chocolate Droppa' persona' or 'use his motivational speaker energy from his stand-up specials.' Finally, test and iterate. Generate a first version, review it, then refine your prompt with more detail or adjust the tone descriptor to get closer to your target style.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of the Kevin Hart AI video generator?

Agent Opus excels at generating complete videos from text, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, the AI generates videos based on the input you provide, so if your prompt or script lacks structure or clear comedic beats, the output may feel flat or unfocused. The system can't read your mind, so vague prompts like 'make something funny' will produce generic results. Always provide context, tone, and key moments you want emphasized. Second, while the AI sources royalty-free stock images and web visuals automatically, it can't access proprietary or copyrighted content unless you upload it. If you want specific celebrity images, branded products not in stock libraries, or custom illustrations, you need to provide those assets in advance. Third, avatar and voice quality depend on the input. If you upload a low-resolution photo for a user avatar or record voice samples with background noise, the AI's output will reflect those limitations. Use high-quality source material for best results. Fourth, the AI generates videos in one pass based on your prompt. If you want multiple variations of the same concept, you'll need to submit separate prompts with different angles or tone descriptors rather than expecting the AI to auto-generate alternatives. Fifth, comedic timing is subjective. The AI uses pacing algorithms based on Kevin Hart's delivery style, but if your audience prefers slower, deadpan humor or different comedic structures, you may need to adjust your prompts or provide more detailed scripts that guide the AI toward your preferred timing. Sixth, the system is optimized for social video lengths, typically 15 to 90 seconds. If you need longer-form comedy content like a five-minute sketch, you may need to break it into segments and generate multiple videos, then combine them externally if needed. Seventh, the AI doesn't perform live edits or real-time adjustments. Once a video is generated, you can't tweak individual scenes, swap out graphics, or adjust voiceover pacing within the tool. If the output isn't quite right, refine your prompt and regenerate. Finally, while the AI is trained on Kevin Hart's comedic style, it's not Kevin Hart himself. The videos are inspired by his energy, pacing, and delivery, but they won't replicate his exact voice, mannerisms, or proprietary material. Use the tool to create original comedy content in a similar style, not to impersonate or replicate his copyrighted work.
