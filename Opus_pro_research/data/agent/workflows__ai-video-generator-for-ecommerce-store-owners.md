### How does an AI video generator for ecommerce store owners handle product descriptions versus full scripts?

Agent Opus accepts both short product descriptions and detailed scripts, adapting its generation strategy to match your input. When you paste a brief product description or bullet points, the AI expands it into a narrative structure, selecting scene pacing, visual hooks, and voiceover tone that align with ecommerce best practices. It identifies key selling points like features, benefits, and social proof cues, then assembles scenes that highlight those elements visually. If you provide a full script with specific callouts or timestamps, Agent Opus respects that structure while still automating scene selection, motion graphics, and asset sourcing. For ecommerce store owners, this flexibility means you can generate quick product teasers from a few sentences or produce in-depth explainer videos from longer copy without changing tools. The AI also infers product category context, so a fashion item gets different visual treatment than electronics or home goods. You can mix inputs too: start with a product page URL, let Agent Opus draft a script, then refine it before final generation. This hybrid workflow saves time while preserving creative control over messaging and brand voice.

### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI video generator for ecommerce store owners?

Effective prompts for Agent Opus combine product details, target audience context, and desired video outcome. Start by naming the product and its primary benefit in the first sentence, then list 2 to 3 key features or differentiators. Mention your target customer if relevant, such as busy parents, fitness enthusiasts, or small business owners, so the AI can tailor tone and pacing. Specify the video goal: product launch, seasonal sale, feature demo, or testimonial-style ad. Include any must-have visual elements like logo placement, product color variants, or lifestyle settings. If you have a brand voice guideline, summarize it in a few words like professional, playful, or aspirational. Agent Opus uses these cues to select motion graphics styles, voiceover tone, and scene transitions. For ecommerce store owners managing large catalogs, create a prompt template with placeholders for product name, features, and call-to-action, then swap details per SKU. Avoid overly generic language; instead of saying make it engaging, specify what engaging means for your audience, such as fast cuts for Gen Z or slower pacing with detailed explanations for B2B buyers. If you are generating videos from a product page URL, ensure the page includes structured data like headings, bullet points, and high-quality images, as Agent Opus parses that content to build the video narrative. Test short prompts first, review the output, then add constraints or creative direction in follow-up generations to refine results without starting from scratch.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of an AI video generator for ecommerce store owners?

Agent Opus excels at generating product videos from text, URLs, and scripts, but certain scenarios require workarounds or fall outside its current scope. First, the system does not support live-action filming or custom animation beyond AI motion graphics. If your product requires hands-on demonstration footage, such as unboxing or assembly tutorials shot in a specific environment, you will need to capture that separately and provide it as a reference asset. Agent Opus can incorporate uploaded video clips into scenes, but it does not generate original live-action footage. Second, highly technical products with complex specifications may need more detailed scripting than a simple prompt can convey. For example, if you sell industrial equipment with multi-step installation processes, write a structured script with timestamps and scene descriptions rather than relying on a brief product description. Third, Agent Opus sources royalty-free and web images automatically, but if your product has not been photographed yet or requires very specific angles, upload your own high-resolution product shots to ensure accuracy. The AI cannot invent product visuals that do not exist in its training data or your asset library. Fourth, while Agent Opus supports voice cloning, the quality depends on the input audio sample. Provide at least 30 seconds of clear, noise-free speech for best results. If your brand voice is highly stylized or uses industry jargon, review the first generated video and refine the script to guide pronunciation and emphasis. Fifth, Agent Opus generates social-ready outputs, but platform-specific compliance rules like Facebook ad policies or TikTok content guidelines are your responsibility. The AI does not audit for regulatory claims or restricted content categories. Finally, while the system handles most ecommerce use cases, extremely niche products or highly localized cultural references may require manual review to ensure the AI interprets context correctly. For these edge cases, generate a draft, review it, then iterate with more specific prompts or uploaded assets to steer the output toward your vision.
