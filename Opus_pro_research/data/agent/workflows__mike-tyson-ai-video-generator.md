### What are the best practices for writing prompts that generate high-quality Mike Tyson AI videos?

Crafting effective prompts for the Mike Tyson AI video generator requires understanding how Agent Opus interprets instructions and sources visual content. Start with clear structural guidance: specify video length, target platform, and primary purpose. A prompt like 'Create a 45-second Instagram Reel showcasing Mike Tyson training intensity with motivational voiceover' gives the system critical parameters for pacing and aspect ratio. Next, describe the visual narrative arc: 'Open with close-up heavy bag shots, transition to speed bag sequences, build to full-body conditioning work, close with intense face close-up.' This scene-by-scene guidance helps Agent Opus assemble footage in a logical, emotionally escalating sequence rather than random boxing clips. Be specific about mood and energy: words like 'aggressive,' 'relentless,' 'legendary,' or 'comeback story' guide both visual selection and motion graphics intensity. If you want a specific aesthetic, reference it: '1980s fight footage style with film grain' or 'modern high-contrast gym cinematography.' For voiceover content, either provide the exact script you want narrated or describe the message: 'Motivational narration about overcoming adversity, referencing Mike Tyson's comeback mentality.' Include audio preferences: 'Epic orchestral soundtrack' versus 'Hard-hitting hip-hop beat' versus 'Minimal ambient gym sounds.' Specify any required branding: 'Include \[YourBrand\] logo in bottom right corner throughout' or 'Feature \[Product\] in training sequences.' Avoid vague prompts like 'Make a Mike Tyson video'—the AI needs direction. Instead: 'Generate a 60-second YouTube Short featuring Mike Tyson training footage, emphasizing speed and power, with AI voiceover delivering three key lessons about discipline, set to an intense electronic soundtrack, ending with a call-to-action to subscribe.' The more specific your prompt, the closer the first output matches your vision, reducing iteration time and maximizing the Mike Tyson AI video generator's ability to deliver publish-ready content.
