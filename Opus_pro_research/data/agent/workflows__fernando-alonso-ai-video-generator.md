### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating Fernando Alonso racing videos?

For breaking news or same-day race reactions, you might need to supplement with your own photos or accept that the generator will use slightly older Alonso imagery. Second, while Agent Opus handles motion graphics and scene pacing automatically, it does not provide a manual editing interface. If you need frame-by-frame control or want to tweak individual transitions, this tool prioritizes speed and automation over granular adjustments. The trade-off is that you get publish-ready videos in minutes, but you sacrifice the ability to micromanage every visual decision. Third, the system works best when your prompts provide clear narrative structure. Vague requests like 'something about Alonso' produce generic results. The system's built-in motion graphics are professional and modern, but if your brand requires very specific After Effects-style effects, you may need to layer those separately.
