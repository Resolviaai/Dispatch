### How does an appliance repair video maker handle technical repair steps and part names?

Agent Opus is built to understand technical repair language. When you describe a repair process—whether it is replacing a washer transmission, swapping a dryer thermal fuse, or diagnosing a refrigerator compressor—the AI parses each step and generates corresponding visual scenes. You can input a simple prompt like 'show how to replace a dishwasher pump' or paste a detailed service manual section. The system identifies key components, tools, and sequences, then assembles scenes that illustrate disassembly, part removal, installation, and reassembly. It automatically sources images of the specific parts you mention—like capacitors, belts, motors, or control boards—from royalty-free libraries and web sources. If you upload your own product photos or diagrams, Agent Opus prioritizes those to keep videos accurate and on-brand. The AI also handles technical terminology naturally: you can say 'remove the agitator dogs' or 'test the bimetal thermostat,' and the video will reflect those exact steps. For best results, structure your prompt with clear action verbs and part names. For example, 'First, unplug the dryer. Second, remove the front panel. Third, disconnect the heating element wires. Fourth, install the new element and reconnect.' This gives the AI a logical flow to visualize. You can also include safety warnings, torque specs, or troubleshooting tips, and Agent Opus will integrate them as text overlays or voiceover callouts. The result is a professional repair tutorial that teaches viewers the exact process without requiring you to film, edit, or source stock footage manually.
