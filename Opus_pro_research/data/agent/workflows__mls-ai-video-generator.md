It identifies the most compelling selling points and arranges them in a logical narrative flow with matching visuals and voiceover pacing. If you prefer more creative control, you can write a custom script that emphasizes specific features, tells a neighborhood story, or addresses buyer objections directly. Agent Opus will follow your script's structure and tone, generating scenes that match each beat. You can also use a short prompt like "luxury waterfront condo in Miami, 3 bed 2 bath, rooftop pool, sunset views" and let the AI expand that into a full video narrative. For agents who work fast, the MLS description route is fastest. For those who want to differentiate their messaging or target niche buyers, custom scripts give you that edge. Either way, the MLS AI video generator delivers a finished video without requiring you to touch a timeline, trim clips, or adjust audio levels. The system handles scene assembly, pacing, transitions, and synchronization automatically, so your only job is to provide the content and approve the result.

### What are best practices for writing prompts in an MLS AI video generator?

Great prompts lead to great videos, and the MLS AI video generator rewards clarity and specificity. Start with the property type and location, then layer in the most visual and emotional details. Instead of "nice house for sale," try "modern farmhouse in Austin, open-concept kitchen with marble island, vaulted ceilings, backyard with fire pit and hill country views." The more sensory and concrete your language, the better Agent Opus can select matching visuals and craft compelling narration. Include the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, square footage, and standout features like smart home tech, recent renovations, or proximity to schools and parks. If the property has a unique story, mention it. For example, "historic brownstone in Brooklyn, original hardwood floors, renovated chef's kitchen, steps from Prospect Park" gives the AI rich material to work with. You can also specify the tone you want. Add phrases like "luxury feel," "family-friendly vibe," or "investor opportunity" to guide the voice and pacing. If you want the video to address a specific buyer persona, say so: "targeting young professionals who want walkability and nightlife." Agent Opus will adjust the narrative angle accordingly. Keep prompts between two sentences and two short paragraphs. Too short and the AI has to guess; too long and you might as well write a full script. If you're generating multiple videos for different listings, create a prompt template with placeholders for address, features, and price range, then swap in details for each property. This consistency helps you maintain brand voice across your video library while still customizing each piece.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of using an MLS AI video generator for real estate?

Agent Opus is incredibly powerful, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, the system generates videos from text and sourced visuals, so it works best when you provide clear, descriptive input. If your MLS listing is sparse or generic, the resulting video will lack the specificity that makes property videos compelling. Always enrich your prompts with sensory details, neighborhood context, and buyer benefits. Second, while Agent Opus can auto-source images from the web or stock libraries, the quality of those visuals depends on availability. If you're marketing a brand-new construction property with no online photos yet, you'll want to upload your own high-resolution images or renderings to ensure the video showcases the actual property accurately. The AI is excellent at matching generic real estate visuals to your script, but custom uploads always deliver the most authentic result. Third, the MLS AI video generator is designed for speed and automation, which means you won't have a traditional editing timeline to tweak individual frames or adjust scene timing manually. If you need frame-by-frame control, this tool prioritizes efficiency over granular editing. Most agents find the automated pacing and scene selection spot-on, but if you have very specific creative vision, you may need to refine your prompt or script to guide the output rather than editing after the fact. Fourth, voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples. If you upload a noisy or muffled recording, the cloned voice may not sound as polished. Record your sample in a quiet space with a decent microphone for best results. Finally, while Agent Opus outputs social-ready formats, you're still responsible for platform-specific best practices like adding text overlays for sound-off viewing or including strong hooks in the first three seconds. The video itself is publish-ready, but your distribution strategy and caption copy still matter for performance.
