### What are the best practices for writing prompts that generate effective AI spokesperson videos?

\<p>Effective AI spokesperson video prompts balance creative direction with specific details. Start by defining your core message and target audience. Instead of 'make a video about our product,' try 'create a 60-second AI spokesperson video explaining how our project management software helps remote teams stay coordinated, targeting startup founders who are overwhelmed by tool complexity.' This gives Agent Opus clear direction on tone, length, and messaging angle. Include key points you want covered, but don't over-script unless you have specific language requirements. The AI excels at taking conceptual direction and translating it into natural spokesperson delivery. A prompt like 'cover these three benefits: real-time collaboration, automated task assignment, and integration with existing tools' works better than writing out exact sentences, because the AI will structure those points for optimal video flow and pacing. Specify visual preferences when they matter to your brand. Mention if you want 'energetic social media style with fast cuts' versus 'professional corporate presentation with measured pacing.' Note any brand assets to include: 'feature our logo in the opening, show product screenshots when explaining features, and end with our website URL.' The more context you provide about use case, the better the output. 'AI spokesperson video for LinkedIn announcing our Series A funding' generates different creative choices than 'AI spokesperson video for TikTok teaching beginners how to use our app.' If you have specific calls-to-action, include them: 'end with an invitation to start a free trial' or 'close by directing viewers to our demo video.' For voice and avatar selection, you can specify preferences like 'professional female voice, corporate avatar' or 'casual male voice, tech startup avatar,' though the AI makes smart defaults if you don't. One powerful technique is providing example videos or references: 'create an AI spokesperson video similar in style to Apple product launches but for our accounting software.' The AI understands stylistic references and adapts them to your content. Avoid over-constraining creative decisions unless you have specific brand requirements. Prompts like 'use exactly these words in this order with these exact visuals' limit the AI's ability to optimize for video engagement. The best results come from clear strategic direction with creative flexibility for execution.\</p>
