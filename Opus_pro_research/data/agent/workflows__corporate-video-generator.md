You provide the content (brief, script, outline, or blog URL), and the corporate video generator determines pacing, scene length, and visual hierarchy. If you submit a 500-word training script, Agent Opus segments it into digestible scenes with voiceover, on-screen text, and supporting visuals. You can specify tone in your prompt (formal, conversational, motivational) and Agent Opus adjusts voiceover pacing and visual energy. ### What are best practices for prompts when using a corporate video generator?

Effective prompts for corporate video generation start with clear objectives and audience context. Begin by stating the video purpose: training new hires on compliance policies, announcing a product launch to clients, or explaining quarterly results to stakeholders. Agent Opus uses this context to set tone, pacing, and visual style. Next, provide structure. If you have a full script, paste it directly. If you are working from an outline, list key points in order. For example: 'Introduction to our new CRM platform. Section one: dashboard overview. Section two: contact management features. Section three: reporting tools. Closing: next steps for rollout.' The corporate video generator interprets this structure and creates corresponding scenes. Include brand details in your prompt or upload assets beforehand. Mention 'Use our blue and white color scheme' or 'Feature our logo in the opening and closing.' If you want specific imagery, describe it: 'Show office collaboration scenes' or 'Include product shots of our hardware.' Agent Opus sources matching visuals from stock libraries or uses uploaded assets. Specify voiceover preferences. If you want a professional female voice with a conversational tone, say so. If you have recorded your own voice for cloning, indicate that. For avatar videos, note whether you want an AI presenter or your own video avatar. Provide approximate length if it matters: 'Two-minute overview' or 'Ten-minute training module.' Agent Opus adjusts scene count and pacing accordingly. Avoid vague prompts like 'Make a corporate video.' Instead, write 'Create a three-minute onboarding video for new sales reps covering our product lineup, sales process, and CRM tools. Use our uploaded logo and a professional male voiceover. Tone should be welcoming and informative.' The more specific your prompt, the better the corporate video generator tailors output to your needs.

### What are the limitations of using a corporate video generator for complex business content?

Agent Opus excels at structured corporate content but has practical boundaries. For highly technical subjects requiring precise data visualization (detailed charts, graphs, financial models), the corporate video generator provides general visual support but may not render complex custom graphics. If your video needs a specific multi-axis chart or proprietary diagram, upload that image as a brand asset and reference it in your prompt. Agent Opus integrates uploaded visuals seamlessly but does not create bespoke data visualizations from raw numbers. For live-action requirements, Agent Opus uses stock footage, web-sourced imagery, and uploaded assets. If your corporate video must feature specific employees, office locations, or proprietary equipment, record that footage separately and upload it. The system incorporates your footage into the generated video alongside AI motion graphics and voiceover. It does not film new live-action content. For interactive or branching content (choose-your-own-path training, clickable decision trees), Agent Opus generates linear videos. If you need interactivity, export the video and integrate it into a learning management system with interactive overlays. The corporate video generator focuses on producing finished, publish-ready videos, not interactive modules. For extremely long-form content (hour-long webinars, full conference recordings), Agent Opus works best with segmented scripts. Break a 60-minute presentation into six 10-minute modules. Generate each separately, then combine them in post if needed. The system optimizes for corporate videos in the 1-15 minute range, where attention and pacing matter most. For multilingual content, Agent Opus supports multiple languages through AI voiceover, but you must provide translated scripts. It does not auto-translate your English script into Spanish or Mandarin. Prepare localized versions of your content, then generate separate videos for each language. Finally, for content requiring legal or compliance review, generate the video, review it internally, and request revisions by adjusting your prompt. Agent Opus produces drafts quickly, but final approval and compliance checks remain your responsibility. The corporate video generator accelerates production but does not replace human judgment on sensitive business communications.
