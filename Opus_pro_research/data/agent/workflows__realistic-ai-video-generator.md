### What are the best practices for prompts in a realistic AI video generator to get professional results?

Getting the most realistic output from Agent Opus starts with clear, detailed prompts that give the AI enough context to make smart creative decisions. Begin by defining your video's purpose and audience—are you educating, selling, entertaining, or building authority? A prompt like 'Create a video about productivity tips' is vague; 'Create a 60-second video for busy marketers explaining three time-saving automation tools, with energetic pacing and modern visuals' gives Agent Opus the direction it needs to assemble scenes, choose motion styles, and set the right tone. Include specifics about visual style if you have preferences—'clean and minimal,' 'bold and colorful,' 'professional and corporate'—but trust the realistic AI video generator to interpret these directions intelligently. If you're providing a script rather than a prompt, structure it with clear narrative beats: intro hook, main points with supporting details, and a strong call to action. Agent Opus uses these structural cues to determine scene length, transition timing, and visual emphasis. When uploading a blog article URL, ensure the content is well-organized with subheadings and clear sections, as the AI uses this structure to create a logical video flow. For voice and avatar choices, specify whether you want a formal or conversational tone, and whether an avatar should be present or if the video should be purely visual with voiceover. If you're cloning your own voice, provide a clean audio sample with minimal background noise and natural speech patterns—avoid overly scripted or monotone recordings. One advanced technique is to include scene-level guidance in longer scripts: 'Scene 1: Hook with fast cuts and bold text. Scene 2: Slow zoom on product image while explaining benefits. Scene 3: Customer testimonial with subtle background motion.' This level of detail helps the realistic AI video generator match your creative vision while still handling all the technical execution. Finally, iterate based on results—if your first video's pacing feels too fast or too slow, adjust your prompt's tone descriptors or script structure. Agent Opus learns from the context you provide, so the more specific and intentional your input, the more realistic and on-target your video output will be.
