### What are the best practices for writing prompts that produce high-quality NBA basketball videos?

Effective prompts for an NBA AI video generator balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by defining your video's purpose in one sentence, such as 'Create a 60-second highlight reel of Steph Curry's three-point shooting from the Finals' or 'Generate a tactical breakdown of the Lakers' defensive strategy in Game 7.' This focus helps the system prioritize the right visual elements and pacing. Next, include key details you want visualized: player names, specific plays, quarter timing, final scores, or standout statistics. The more context you provide about what makes a moment significant, the better the motion graphics and scene assembly will reflect that importance. For example, instead of 'show the game-winning shot,' write 'show the game-winning three-pointer with 2.3 seconds left, score tied 108-108, defender closeout from the left.' Agent Opus uses these details to source appropriate imagery and build tension in the visual sequence. If you want voiceover commentary, write it in a natural speaking style rather than bullet points. The voice cloning and AI voice features work best with conversational scripts that include pauses, emphasis, and personality. You can indicate tone by adding direction like 'excited energy' or 'analytical breakdown style' in your prompt. For branding consistency, mention any recurring visual elements you want, such as 'include team logo in the lower third' or 'use our standard intro card with channel name.' Agent Opus will integrate these assets throughout the video. Finally, specify your target platform and length upfront, like 'TikTok vertical format, 45 seconds' or 'YouTube Shorts, under 60 seconds.' This ensures the pacing, text size, and visual density match where your audience will watch.
