## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does a vertical video maker handle different input types like prompts versus full scripts?

Agent Opus adapts its vertical video generation workflow based on the input you provide. If you supply a short prompt or brief, the system expands it into a structured narrative, determines optimal scene count and pacing, then generates corresponding visuals, voiceover, and motion graphics. For example, a prompt like 'Explain the benefits of cold brew coffee for fitness enthusiasts' will produce a 30 to 60 second vertical video with three to five key points, each illustrated with relevant imagery and smooth transitions. When you provide a full script, Agent Opus respects your exact wording and timing cues. It parses the script into scenes, matches visuals to each line, and synchronizes voiceover or avatar delivery frame-by-frame. This is ideal when you have brand messaging guidelines or need precise language for compliance. If you paste an outline with bullet points, the vertical video maker treats each bullet as a scene anchor and fills in connective narration and visuals automatically. Blog URL input triggers a summarization pass where Agent Opus extracts key arguments, condenses them into a social-length narrative, and builds a vertical video that teases or explains the article's main thesis. Across all input types, the system prioritizes vertical 9:16 formatting, ensuring text overlays, avatars, and graphics fit mobile screens without clipping. You can also specify tone, target length, and whether you want an AI avatar or voice-only delivery, and the vertical video maker adjusts scene density and visual complexity accordingly.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce high-quality vertical videos?

Effective prompts for a vertical video maker balance clarity with creative freedom. Start by stating your core message or goal in one sentence, such as 'Teach beginners how to meal prep on a budget' or 'Announce our new product feature for remote teams.' This gives Agent Opus a clear narrative spine. Next, specify your target audience and tone. For instance, 'casual and encouraging for Gen Z fitness enthusiasts' or 'professional and data-driven for B2B decision-makers' helps the system choose appropriate visuals, pacing, and voiceover style. If you have a desired video length, include it. 'Create a 45-second vertical video' signals tighter scene counts and faster cuts, while 'Create a 90-second explainer' allows more detailed exposition. Mention any must-have visual elements like 'show our app interface,' 'include before-and-after comparisons,' or 'feature customer testimonial quotes as text overlays.' Agent Opus will prioritize sourcing or generating those assets. Avoid overly prescriptive shot-by-shot directions. The vertical video maker excels at creative assembly, so instead of 'Scene 1: wide shot of coffee beans, Scene 2: close-up of espresso machine,' write 'Illustrate the coffee-making process with dynamic product shots.' If you want a specific call-to-action, state it clearly: 'End with a prompt to download the app' or 'Close with our website URL on screen.' Finally, if you have brand assets like logos or product images, mention them in the prompt or upload them separately. The system will weave them into the vertical video naturally. Test short prompts first to see how Agent Opus interprets your style, then refine based on output.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using a vertical video maker for complex narratives?

While Agent Opus handles most vertical video use cases seamlessly, certain complex narratives require thoughtful prompt design. Long-form storytelling with multiple characters, subplots, or non-linear timelines can challenge the system's automatic scene assembly. For example, a 90-second vertical video that jumps between three different customer stories may produce disjointed transitions if the prompt does not clearly delineate each segment. To avoid this, structure your script or prompt with explicit scene breaks, such as 'Part 1: Customer A's problem, Part 2: Customer B's solution, Part 3: Results comparison.' Agent Opus will then treat each part as a distinct narrative block and apply appropriate visual separators. Highly technical or niche topics that require specialized imagery, such as medical procedures, engineering schematics, or legal diagrams, may not always find perfect stock matches. In these cases, upload your own reference images or product shots, and the vertical video maker will prioritize them over generic stock. If your vertical video needs precise timing for music sync, comedic beats, or dramatic pauses, provide a detailed script with timing cues like '\[pause 2 seconds\]' or '\[upbeat music starts here\].' Agent Opus respects these markers and adjusts pacing accordingly. Another edge case involves brand compliance for regulated industries like finance, healthcare, or legal services. If your vertical video must include specific disclaimers, disclosures, or approved language, supply the exact text in your script rather than relying on the system to paraphrase. Agent Opus will render it verbatim. Finally, if you need interactive elements like clickable links, polls, or swipe-up CTAs, note that the vertical video maker generates static MP4 files. You will need to add those interactive layers in your social platform's native editor after export. For most creators and marketers, these edge cases are rare, and Agent Opus's automatic vertical video generation covers the vast majority of social content needs without workarounds.
