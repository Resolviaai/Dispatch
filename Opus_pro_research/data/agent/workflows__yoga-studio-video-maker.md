### How does a yoga studio video maker handle different class formats and styles?

Agent Opus adapts to any yoga format you describe. For a power vinyasa promo, write a brief highlighting fast transitions, strength-building poses, and upbeat energy. Agent Opus generates dynamic motion graphics, quick scene cuts, and an energetic voiceover. For a restorative workshop, describe slow pacing, gentle holds, and calming cues. The system adjusts scene duration, selects softer visuals, and uses a soothing voice tone. You can input a detailed script with pose names and cues, or just a short prompt like "30-minute yin yoga for hip flexibility." Agent Opus interprets the intent and assembles scenes accordingly. If you run multiple class types, create a template prompt for each style. For example, your hot yoga promos might emphasize intensity and sweat, while prenatal videos focus on safety and modifications. The yoga studio video maker sources images that match your description, whether that is sunrise beach flows, candlelit studio shots, or close-ups of alignment. Upload your studio's signature props, wall colors, or instructor headshots, and Agent Opus weaves them into the visual flow. The result is a video that feels authentic to your teaching style and studio vibe, without you manually selecting every frame or transition.

### What are best practices for prompts in a yoga studio video maker?

Start with the class type and target audience. Instead of "yoga video," write "beginner-friendly hatha yoga for stress relief, 20 minutes, gentle pace." Agent Opus uses these details to set scene length, select calming visuals, and choose a warm, instructional voice. Include key poses or sequences if they define the class. For example, "sun salutation A focus with warrior variations" tells the system to emphasize those shapes visually. Mention the mood or outcome: "energizing morning flow" versus "evening wind-down practice." This guides music selection, color palette, and pacing. If you are promoting a workshop, describe the unique angle: "arm balance workshop for intermediate students, includes crow, side crow, and flying pigeon progressions." The yoga studio video maker will source images of those poses and structure the video to build excitement around the challenge. Upload your studio logo, instructor photo, and any props or decor that define your brand. Reference them in the prompt: "Feature studio logo at start, instructor headshot at end, use our signature purple bolsters." For voiceover, decide between your cloned voice or an AI voice. If you clone your voice, record a short sample with clear yoga cues so the system captures your teaching tone. For AI voices, test a few options to find one that matches your energy. Keep prompts concise but specific. A 50-word brief often generates better results than a 500-word essay. If the first video is close but not perfect, refine your prompt with one or two adjustments and regenerate. The more you use the tool, the faster you will learn which phrases produce your ideal style.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of a yoga studio video maker for complex sequences?

Agent Opus excels at generating promo and educational yoga videos from text, but it is not a replacement for live instruction or detailed pose breakdowns that require frame-by-frame precision. If you need to demonstrate micro-adjustments in alignment, such as the exact angle of a knee in warrior two or hand placement in chaturanga, a yoga studio video maker works best when paired with your own instructional footage or photos. Upload high-quality images of your instructors demonstrating key poses, and Agent Opus will integrate them into the video. The system sources additional stock and web images to fill gaps, but for highly specific anatomy cues, your own assets ensure accuracy. Another edge case is very long sequences. Agent Opus handles 30-second to 3-minute videos with ease, which covers most social promos and short tutorials. For a full 60-minute class recording, the tool is better suited to creating the promotional video or highlight reel rather than the entire class. If you want to promote a long workshop, write a script that teases the key segments, and Agent Opus generates a compelling preview. For niche styles like aerial yoga, acro yoga, or yoga with props like chairs or ropes, describe the unique elements clearly in your prompt. The more specific you are, the better the visual sourcing. If stock images for your niche are limited, upload your own photos or video stills. Agent Opus will prioritize your assets and supplement with the best available matches. Finally, if you are creating content in a language with limited AI voice options, test the available voices first. Most major languages are well supported, but regional accents or very niche dialects may require you to clone your own voice for the best result. The yoga studio video maker is designed for speed and automation, so it trades some granular control for efficiency. For most yoga studios, that trade-off is a win: you get professional videos in minutes instead of hours.
