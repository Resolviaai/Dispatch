### How does Agent Opus handle logo placement when I add logo to video from a prompt?

Agent Opus treats your logo as a persistent brand asset throughout the entire video generation process. When you upload your logo file (PNG with transparency works best), the system analyzes its dimensions, color palette, and visual weight. As Agent Opus assembles scenes based on your prompt or script, it automatically positions your logo in contextually appropriate locations: lower thirds during narration segments, corner watermarks during full-screen visuals, and prominent placements in intro and outro sequences. The AI motion graphics engine adapts animations to your logo's shape and style, so circular logos get different treatment than horizontal wordmarks. If your prompt describes a product demo, Agent Opus places the logo near product shots. If you're generating a testimonial video, the logo appears during speaker introductions and closing frames. This intelligent placement happens without any manual keyframing or timeline work on your part. You can also specify logo behavior in your prompt (for example, 'keep logo subtle in lower left' or 'feature logo prominently in opening'), and Agent Opus will adjust placement and opacity accordingly. The system ensures your logo never obscures critical visual information or competes with on-screen text, maintaining professional composition across every generated frame. Because Agent Opus generates the entire video from scratch rather than editing existing footage, logo integration is baked into the scene assembly process, not added as an afterthought.

Absolutely. Agent Opus stores your logo and brand assets in your account, so every video you generate automatically inherits consistent branding without re-uploading or reconfiguring settings. When you create a promotional video, an explainer, a product demo, or social content from different prompts, Agent Opus applies the same logo placement logic, color harmony, and visual style rules to maintain brand coherence. This format-aware placement ensures your logo is always legible and professionally positioned, regardless of where the video is published. You can also upload multiple logo variations (full color, monochrome, icon-only) and specify which version to use in different contexts via your prompt. Agent Opus will select the appropriate logo variant based on scene composition and background contrast, ensuring maximum visibility and brand impact across every video you generate.

### What are best practices for prompts when I want to add logo to video with specific branding goals?

Effective prompts for logo-integrated video generation combine content direction with branding cues. Start by describing the video's core message and structure, then add specific instructions about logo treatment. For example, 'Create a 60-second product launch video for our new app. Open with the logo animating in over a tech background, then show three key features with the logo watermarked in the lower right. End with a full-screen logo and call to action.' This gives Agent Opus clear guidance on logo prominence and timing. If you want subtle branding, specify 'keep logo discreet as a corner watermark throughout' or 'use logo only in intro and outro.' For high-impact branding, prompt 'feature logo prominently in every scene transition' or 'integrate logo into motion graphics as a recurring visual element.' You can also reference brand tone: 'professional and corporate' will yield conservative logo placement and animations, while 'energetic and bold' produces dynamic logo movements and larger scale. When generating videos from blog URLs or long scripts, Agent Opus automatically determines optimal logo placement based on content flow, but you can override this by adding branding notes to your prompt. For example, 'Generate video from this blog post, and emphasize the logo during key statistics and takeaways.' If you have multiple brand assets (logo, product images, team photos), describe how they should interact: 'Show product shots with logo in corner, then transition to team member speaking with logo in lower third.' Agent Opus interprets these layered instructions and assembles scenes that balance content clarity with brand visibility. The more specific your branding goals in the prompt, the more precisely Agent Opus tailors logo integration to your vision.

### How does Agent Opus source and combine visual elements when I add logo to video from a text brief?

Because Agent Opus builds videos from scratch rather than editing pre-existing footage, every visual decision considers logo integration from the outset, resulting in compositions that feel intentionally designed rather than retroactively branded.
