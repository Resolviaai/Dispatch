### What are the best practices for writing prompts that generate effective social media videos with this AI video generator?

Effective prompts for an AI video generator for social media managers balance clarity, context, and creative direction. Start with the core message or goal in one sentence, such as 'Announce our new app feature that saves users time on daily tasks.' This gives the AI a clear objective. Next, specify the target platform and audience, like 'for Instagram Reels targeting busy professionals aged 25 to 40.' Platform context helps the AI choose appropriate pacing, visual density, and tone. Then describe the desired emotional tone or style, such as 'energetic and modern' or 'calm and trustworthy.' This guides the motion graphics style and voiceover delivery. Include any must-have elements like 'show our logo in the first three seconds' or 'feature the product interface prominently.' If you have a script, paste it directly; if not, provide key talking points in bullet form. The AI will structure them into a coherent narrative. For visual direction, mention specific imagery if important, like 'use shots of people working on laptops' or 'include abstract tech graphics.' If you're generating from a blog URL, add a note about which sections to emphasize or whether to summarize or expand certain points. Avoid overly vague prompts like 'make a cool video about our product,' which leave too much open to interpretation. Conversely, don't over-specify every transition or timing detail; the AI handles pacing and scene flow better when you focus on message and mood rather than technical execution. For social media managers creating series or recurring content, develop a prompt template that includes your standard brand elements, platform, and tone, then customize the message for each video. This ensures consistency while speeding up the briefing process. Experiment with prompt length: sometimes a tight 50-word brief works best, other times a detailed 200-word outline gives the AI more to work with, especially for complex topics. Review your first few outputs to see how the AI interprets your style, then refine your prompts based on what works. Over time, you'll develop a feel for how much direction the system needs versus where it excels with creative autonomy.
