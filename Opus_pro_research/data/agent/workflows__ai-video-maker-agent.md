### How does an AI video maker like Agent Opus generate videos from a brief?

Agent Opus uses a multi-stage AI pipeline to convert text into finished video. When you submit a prompt or brief, the system first analyzes your intent and extracts key themes, messages, and visual concepts. It then generates a scene-by-scene structure, deciding what visuals, motion graphics, and pacing will best communicate your idea. Next, Agent Opus sources images and footage from royalty-free web sources or stock libraries, matching each scene to your content. It applies AI motion graphics like zooms, pans, transitions, and compositing effects to keep the video dynamic. For voiceover, you can clone your own voice by uploading a short sample, or choose from a library of AI voices. Agent Opus syncs the narration to the visuals, adjusting pacing so the video feels natural. If you want an avatar, you can upload your own footage or use an AI-generated presenter with lip-sync. Finally, the system exports the video in social-ready aspect ratios like 9:16 for TikTok and Reels or 16:9 for YouTube. The entire process happens automatically. You do not touch a timeline, trim clips, or manually place assets. The AI video maker handles scene assembly, visual sourcing, motion graphics, voiceover, and export in one workflow. This approach is ideal for creators and marketers who need professional video content at scale without the time and skill overhead of traditional editing. Whether you are making product demos, explainer videos, social ads, or educational content, Agent Opus delivers publish-ready output from a single text input.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts in an AI video maker?

Effective prompts for an AI video maker like Agent Opus balance clarity with creative flexibility. Start by defining the core message or goal. For example, instead of saying 'make a video about my product,' say 'create a 60-second product demo showing how our app helps freelancers track invoices and get paid faster.' This gives the AI video maker a clear objective and audience. Next, specify the tone and style. Do you want the video to be professional and corporate, or casual and energetic? Mention this in your prompt. For example, 'use a friendly, conversational tone with upbeat background music.' If you have a script or outline, paste it directly. Agent Opus can generate videos from full scripts, bullet-point outlines, or even blog post URLs. If you are starting from scratch, describe the key points you want to cover in order. For example, 'intro: problem statement. middle: show three features with screen recordings. outro: call to action with pricing.' This structure helps the AI video maker organize scenes logically. You can also specify visual preferences. For example, 'use modern, minimalist graphics' or 'include product screenshots and customer testimonials.' If you want a specific voiceover style, mention it. For example, 'use a professional female AI voice' or 'clone my voice from the sample I uploaded.' For avatar videos, specify whether you want an AI presenter or your own footage. Best practices also include being specific about length and format. For example, 'create a 30-second vertical video for Instagram Reels' or 'make a 2-minute horizontal explainer for YouTube.' Agent Opus will optimize pacing, aspect ratio, and visual density accordingly. Finally, iterate. Generate a first draft, review it, and refine your prompt based on what worked and what did not. The AI video maker learns from your input, so the more specific and structured your prompts, the better the output.

### How does an AI video maker handle voiceover and avatar synchronization?

Agent Opus uses advanced AI to generate and sync voiceover with visuals and optional avatars. For voiceover, you have two options: clone your own voice or use an AI voice from the library. Voice cloning works by uploading a short audio sample of your voice, typically 30 to 60 seconds. The AI video maker analyzes your tone, pitch, pacing, and accent, then generates narration that sounds like you. This is ideal for personal brands, founders, and educators who want authentic, recognizable voiceover without recording every script manually. If you prefer an AI voice, Agent Opus offers a range of professional voices with different tones, accents, and styles. You can preview them and choose the one that fits your brand. Once the voiceover is generated, Agent Opus syncs it to the visuals. The AI adjusts scene length, transitions, and pacing so the narration flows naturally with the video. For example, if a voiceover line is 5 seconds long, the corresponding scene will display for 5 seconds, with motion graphics timed to emphasize key words. If you add an avatar, Agent Opus takes synchronization further. You can upload your own video footage, and the AI will composite it into the generated video, or you can use an AI-generated avatar. AI avatars are digital presenters that lip-sync to the voiceover in real time. The AI video maker analyzes the audio waveform and generates mouth movements, facial expressions, and head gestures that match the speech. This creates a realistic, engaging presenter without filming yourself. Avatar synchronization is especially useful for explainer videos, tutorials, and social content where a human presence increases engagement. The AI handles all the technical work: audio analysis, lip-sync, compositing, and timing. You do not need to manually align audio tracks or adjust keyframes. The result is a polished, professional video where voiceover, visuals, and avatar work together seamlessly.
