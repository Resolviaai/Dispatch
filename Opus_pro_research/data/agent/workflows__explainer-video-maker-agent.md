### What are best practices for writing prompts in an explainer video maker?

Effective prompts for an explainer video maker like Agent Opus balance clarity with creative direction, giving the AI enough structure to generate a coherent narrative while leaving room for intelligent visual interpretation. Start with a clear objective: are you introducing a new product, explaining a process, or educating viewers about a concept? State this upfront in your brief, such as 'Create an explainer video introducing our project management app to small business owners.' Next, outline the core message in three to five key points. For example: 'Point one: teams waste time switching between tools. Point two: our app centralizes tasks, chat, and files. Point three: setup takes under five minutes.' This structure helps Agent Opus pace the explainer video appropriately, dedicating screen time to each idea. Include specific visual cues when you have strong preferences. Phrases like 'show a frustrated team juggling multiple apps' or 'animate a dashboard filling with completed tasks' guide the AI's visual generation without requiring you to storyboard every frame. If your brand has a distinct visual style, mention it: 'use bold, flat colors and geometric shapes' or 'keep visuals minimal and corporate.' For voiceover, specify tone and pacing: 'conversational and upbeat' or 'authoritative and measured.' If you're cloning your own voice, upload a sample that matches the energy you want in the final explainer video. When providing a script, write for the ear, not the eye. Use short sentences, active voice, and natural transitions. Avoid jargon unless your audience expects it, and define technical terms the first time they appear. Agent Opus will sync voiceover with on-screen text, so don't overload any single sentence with multiple complex ideas. If you're starting from a blog post or article, the AI will extract key points and condense them into a video-friendly narrative, but you'll get better results if you provide a summary or highlight the most important paragraphs. Finally, specify your target platform and audience. An explainer video for LinkedIn professionals will have a different tone and pacing than one for TikTok consumers. Agent Opus adjusts visual density, text size, and scene duration based on where your video will live, ensuring maximum impact on your chosen platform.
