### How does the FIFA AI video generator handle different types of football content like match highlights versus tactical analysis?

Agent Opus will vary pacing and visual treatment across segments, maintaining viewer engagement while delivering both entertainment and insight. This flexibility means one tool handles your entire FIFA content spectrum from transfer news snippets to full match retrospectives without requiring different workflows or manual editing to switch between styles.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-quality FIFA videos with accurate football context?

Effective prompts for the FIFA AI video generator balance specificity with creative freedom. Start with clear content framing such as match context, teams involved, and the video's purpose. A prompt like 'Create a tactical breakdown of Real Madrid's counter-attacking strategy in the 2024 Champions League final, focusing on Vinicius Jr's positioning and transition speed' gives Agent Opus precise direction for visual sourcing and narrative structure. Include desired video length, target platform, and tone. For example, '90-second Instagram Reel comparing Haaland and Mbappe's 2023-24 goal-scoring records with energetic pacing for Gen Z football fans' helps the system calibrate motion graphics intensity and information density. When working from match summaries or tactical notes, provide context that might not be obvious from raw stats. Instead of just listing 'Goals: Salah 23', Kane 18', Haaland 27', write 'Compare Premier League top scorers 2023-24, highlighting Haaland's efficiency versus Salah's creativity and Kane's all-around contribution.' This guides the AI toward meaningful visual comparisons rather than static number displays. For brand consistency, mention your channel's style in the prompt such as 'Use our signature blue and white color scheme with minimalist lower thirds' or 'Match the high-energy style of our previous Messi analysis videos.' Agent Opus will apply these preferences to motion graphics and visual assembly. Avoid overly vague prompts like 'Make a FIFA video about football.' The more specific your football context, desired narrative arc, and visual preferences, the more precisely the generator can assemble relevant imagery, appropriate pacing, and on-brand graphics that resonate with your audience and require zero post-generation editing.
