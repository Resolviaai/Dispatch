### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate effective Jimmy Fallon-style comedy videos?

The most effective prompts for the Jimmy Fallon AI video generator combine clear format direction with specific comedy content. Start by naming the format, like 'Tonight Show desk segment' or 'late-night monologue,' so Agent Opus applies the right visual template and pacing. Then describe your topic and angle: 'a desk bit roasting terrible startup ideas' or 'a monologue about awkward holiday traditions.' Include 3 to 5 specific jokes or talking points so the AI has concrete material to visualize. For example, 'joke 1: dating apps for pets, joke 2: subscription service for air, joke 3: NFTs of your own face.' This gives Agent Opus clear beats to structure around. If you want specific visuals, mention them: 'show fake app screenshots' or 'cut to stock footage of confused people.' Specify your desired voice and avatar: 'use my voice clone with a casual host avatar' or 'AI voice, no avatar, focus on graphics.' Mention your target platform and length: 'TikTok vertical, 45 seconds' or 'YouTube Shorts, 60 seconds.' The more specific your comedy beats and visual ideas, the better Agent Opus can execute your vision. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a funny video about technology.' Instead, write 'create a 50-second Tonight Show-style desk piece where I mock three absurd AI products, with motion graphics showing fake product demos.' That level of detail ensures the generated video matches your comedic intent and feels like a real late-night segment.
