### How does webinar to video generation work with different input formats?

If you're working from an outline or bullet points summarizing your webinar's main topics, the AI expands those into full scenes with appropriate pacing and visual support. You can also describe your webinar's content in a simple prompt—for example, 'Create a 60-second video highlighting our product demo from yesterday's webinar on email automation'—and Agent Opus will generate the entire video from that brief. The system analyzes your input to determine the best scene structure, visual pacing, and narrative flow. For transcript-based inputs, it automatically identifies quotable moments, key statistics, and logical break points for scene transitions. For outline or prompt-based inputs, it generates natural-sounding narration that matches your brand voice while maintaining the core message. Each approach produces a complete video with motion graphics, voiceover, and platform-optimized formatting. The webinar to video process is fully automated—you provide the content, Agent Opus handles scene assembly, visual sourcing, voiceover generation, and final rendering. No manual timeline work or editing required.

### What are best practices for prompts when converting webinar content to video?

Effective webinar to video prompts balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by identifying the core message or takeaway you want the video to communicate—this becomes your anchor. For example, 'Generate a 90-second video showcasing the three-step framework we taught in our webinar on content strategy' is more effective than 'Make a video about our webinar.' Include any critical details that must appear: specific statistics, product names, customer quotes, or visual elements like logos or product screenshots. If your webinar covered multiple topics, specify which segment to focus on: 'Create a video from the Q&A section where we discussed pricing objections' or 'Turn the case study portion into a 60-second testimonial video.' Mention your target platform and audience to help Agent Opus optimize pacing and tone: 'LinkedIn video for B2B marketers' will produce different results than 'TikTok video for Gen Z creators.' If you want a specific voice style, describe it: 'energetic and conversational' versus 'authoritative and data-driven.' You can also specify visual preferences: 'Use bold, high-contrast motion graphics' or 'Include product screenshots throughout.' For webinar to video conversion, longer prompts generally produce better results because they give the AI more context about your goals, audience, and brand voice. However, avoid overly prescriptive instructions about specific scenes or transitions—Agent Opus excels at determining optimal visual pacing and scene structure when given the creative freedom to do so. Test different prompt styles with the same webinar content to see which approach produces videos that best match your brand and resonate with your audience.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of webinar to video generation?

While Agent Opus handles most webinar to video scenarios effectively, understanding its limitations helps you set realistic expectations and optimize your workflow. The system works best with clear, structured webinar content—if your source material is highly technical, filled with jargon, or assumes deep domain expertise, you may need to provide additional context in your prompt to help the AI translate that content for a broader social media audience. Webinars with heavy visual components like live product demos, screen shares, or whiteboard sessions require special consideration: describe what was shown on screen in your prompt or provide screenshots, since Agent Opus generates new visuals rather than extracting them from video recordings. For webinars longer than 60-90 minutes, you'll get better results by breaking the content into focused segments rather than trying to condense everything into a single short video—identify the three or four key topics and generate separate videos for each. Audio quality from your original webinar matters if you're cloning your voice: clear, noise-free recordings produce better voice clones than audio with background noise, echo, or multiple speakers talking over each other. If your webinar included audience interaction, Q&A sessions, or live polls, you'll need to contextualize those elements in your prompt since the AI won't have access to the interactive components. Agent Opus excels at generating net-new videos from webinar content, but it doesn't extract or repurpose existing video footage—it creates fresh visual content based on your text input. This is actually an advantage for most use cases because it means you can generate social videos even if you don't have high-quality webinar recordings, but it's important to understand the distinction. Finally, while Agent Opus outputs are publish-ready, highly regulated industries may require legal or compliance review before posting, especially if your webinar discussed financial advice, medical information, or other sensitive topics.
