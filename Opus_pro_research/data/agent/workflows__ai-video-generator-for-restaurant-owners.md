### How does an AI video generator for restaurant owners handle different types of content like menu promos versus event announcements?

Agent Opus adapts to the content type you describe in your prompt. For a menu promo, you might write a brief like 'Highlight our new truffle mac and cheese, creamy close-up shots, upbeat music, mention it's available weekends only, show our logo and price $14.' The AI interprets that intent and assembles a video with food-focused visuals, animated price callouts, energetic pacing, and a clear call to action. For an event announcement—say, a live music night—you would describe the event details, desired mood (relaxed, jazzy), and any key info like date, time, and reservation link. Agent Opus then sources relevant imagery (live music, restaurant ambiance), applies slower pacing, adds text overlays for event details, and selects a background track that matches the vibe. The system recognizes context clues in your language: words like 'limited time,' 'new,' 'exclusive,' or 'celebrate' trigger different visual and audio treatments. You can also specify tone directly—'fun and casual' versus 'elegant and upscale'—and the AI adjusts motion graphics style, font choices, color grading, and music genre accordingly. If you are promoting a happy hour deal, the video might feature quick cuts, bold text animations, and a countdown timer graphic. If you are showcasing a chef's tasting menu, expect slower dissolves, refined typography, and a sophisticated soundtrack. The flexibility comes from natural language understanding: you do not need to learn preset templates or categories. Just describe what you want to communicate and how you want viewers to feel, and the AI video generator for restaurant owners builds the appropriate video structure, pacing, and aesthetic to match that goal.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce high-quality videos with an AI video generator for restaurant owners?

Effective prompts combine clear intent, specific details, and desired emotional tone. Start by stating the video's purpose: 'Promote our new brunch menu,' 'Announce our anniversary event,' or 'Highlight customer testimonials about our pizza.' Next, describe the key visual and audio elements you want: 'Show close-ups of our avocado toast and mimosas, upbeat morning music, include our logo and the tagline Brunch Done Right.' Mention any must-have information like prices, dates, or location details: 'Display $12 brunch special, available Saturdays and Sundays, mention our downtown location.' Specify tone and pacing: 'Keep it fun and energetic for a younger crowd' or 'Elegant and refined for a fine dining audience.' If you have uploaded brand assets, reference them: 'Use the hero shot of our ribeye steak, apply our burgundy and cream color scheme, show our steakhouse logo at the end.' The more context you provide, the better the AI video generator for restaurant owners can tailor scene selection, motion graphics style, voiceover tone, and music genre. Avoid vague prompts like 'Make a video about our restaurant.' Instead, be specific: 'Create a 30-second Reel promoting our Taco Tuesday deal—show our street tacos, vibrant colors, upbeat Latin music, mention $2 tacos and $5 margaritas, end with our logo and Come hungry, leave happy tagline.' If you are working from a script, paste the full text and indicate where you want visual emphasis: 'When the voiceover says handcrafted cocktails, show our bartender mixing a drink.' For blog-to-video conversions, provide the article URL and specify which sections to highlight: 'Turn this blog post about our farm-to-table sourcing into a 60-second video, focus on the local farm partnerships and seasonal ingredients.' Test different prompt styles and refine based on results. Over time, you will learn which phrases and structures produce the pacing, visuals, and tone that resonate with your audience.
