These story frameworks help the AI select appropriate stock footage, pacing rhythms, and visual metaphors. For example, 'Create a 45-second promo for a tactical flashlight with Jason Statham-level intensity, showing survival scenarios and close-up product shots' gives the generator clear anchors for both tone and content. Avoid overly technical language like 'crane shot' or 'Dutch angle' since Agent Opus handles composition automatically. Instead, describe the feeling: 'claustrophobic tension' or 'wide-open triumph.' Finally, state the platform and length upfront so the generator optimizes aspect ratio and pacing for TikTok, Reels, or YouTube Shorts. The best prompts blend emotional direction, narrative structure, and brand specifics, letting the Jason Statham AI video generator handle the cinematic execution.
