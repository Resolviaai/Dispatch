If you need specific clips from a match that happened hours ago, the AI pulls the closest available visuals, which might be stock Arsenal imagery or older match footage rather than that exact play. For time-sensitive content, provide a script or outline that describes the moment rather than assuming the AI has instant access to brand-new footage. The generator also cannot create original match footage or simulate plays that didn't happen. It assembles existing visuals, applies motion graphics, and structures scenes, but it's not rendering 3D player models or generating fake game action.
