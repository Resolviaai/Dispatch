### What are the limitations or edge cases of Seth Rogen AI video generator?

The Seth Rogen AI video generator is powerful, but understanding its limitations helps you get the best results. First, voice cloning quality depends on the reference audio. If you upload a low-quality sample with background noise or distortion, the cloned voice may sound less authentic. For best results, use clean audio with clear speech and minimal ambient sound. Agent Opus can work with 10-second clips, but longer samples give the AI more data to replicate tone and cadence accurately. Second, the system excels at generating videos from text, but it doesn't create original jokes or comedic content. If your script lacks humor, the Seth Rogen-style voice won't magically make it funny. Agent Opus delivers the tone and timing, but the comedy comes from your writing. Think of it as a performance tool, not a comedy writer. Third, while Agent Opus sources web and stock images automatically, it can't generate custom illustrations or animations beyond motion graphics. If you need a specific cartoon character or branded mascot, upload that asset yourself. The AI will integrate it into the video, but it won't create it from scratch. Fourth, the Seth Rogen AI video generator works best with scripts under 3 minutes. Longer videos are possible, but the AI may struggle with pacing and scene variety if the script is repetitive or lacks clear structure. Break long content into segments or provide detailed outlines to help Agent Opus maintain engagement. Fifth, voice cloning respects ethical guidelines. You can clone your own voice or use AI voices, but Agent Opus won't replicate a celebrity's voice without proper authorization. If you're creating Seth Rogen-style content, you're cloning the tone and delivery style, not Seth Rogen's actual voice. This keeps your content legally safe and avoids impersonation issues. Sixth, the system generates videos in one pass. If you want to tweak a specific scene or adjust a single line, you'll need to regenerate the entire video with a revised prompt. Agent Opus doesn't offer frame-by-frame editing, so plan your script carefully before generating. Seventh, while Agent Opus handles social aspect ratios automatically, it doesn't optimize for platform-specific algorithms. A video formatted for TikTok will fit the 9:16 frame, but you'll still need to add hashtags, captions, and posting strategies manually. Finally, the Seth Rogen AI video generator requires clear prompts. Vague instructions like 'Make it funny' or 'Add some visuals' won't produce great results. The more specific you are about tone, structure, and visuals, the better the output. If you hit an edge case, refine your prompt, upload higher-quality assets, or break your project into smaller videos. Agent Opus is designed for speed and automation, so understanding these boundaries helps you work within its strengths.
