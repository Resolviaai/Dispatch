### What are best practices for prompts when creating Patrick Mahomes AI video content?

Effective prompts for the Patrick Mahomes AI video generator balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by clearly stating your video's purpose and target platform. For example, 'Create a 60-second Instagram Reel celebrating Patrick Mahomes' 2023 MVP season' gives Agent Opus concrete parameters for length, format, and theme. Include key moments, statistics, or themes you want featured. 'Highlight his playoff performances, Super Bowl rings, and leadership qualities' directs the content focus without micromanaging scene selection. Specify your desired tone using reference language. 'Energetic and celebratory like NFL Films' or 'Inspirational and cinematic like a Nike ad' helps the AI match your vision. If you have brand requirements, state them upfront: 'Include our sports betting app logo in the lower third and use Kansas City Chiefs red as the primary accent color.' For voiceover style, describe the delivery you want: 'Authoritative sports commentator voice' or 'Conversational fan perspective.' If you're working from a script rather than a brief prompt, structure it with clear beats. Avoid overly prescriptive shot-by-shot instructions. Instead of 'Show Mahomes throwing, then cut to a receiver, then show the scoreboard,' write 'Showcase his precision passing and game-changing plays.' The AI excels at translating outcomes into visual sequences. Include a call-to-action if your video has a commercial purpose: 'End with a prompt to download our fantasy football app.' Finally, if you're creating a series, maintain consistency by referencing previous videos: 'Use the same graphic style as our Tom Brady tribute video.' This helps Agent Opus learn your brand's visual language across multiple projects.
