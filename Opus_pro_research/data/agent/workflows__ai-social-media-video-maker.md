### How does an AI social media video maker handle different input formats like prompts versus full scripts?

Agent Opus is designed to work with whatever level of detail you provide, making it flexible for both quick ideation and polished execution. If you give it a short prompt like 'Explain our new product feature in 30 seconds for TikTok,' the AI will expand that into a full narrative structure, select appropriate visuals, pace the scenes, and generate voiceover that matches the platform's tone. It infers the hook, body, and call-to-action based on best practices for social video. If you provide a complete script with scene-by-scene instructions, Agent Opus respects that structure and uses your exact wording for voiceover while still automating the visual assembly, motion graphics, and music sync. You can also submit an outline with bullet points, and the AI will flesh out the narrative while keeping your key messages intact. For blog or article URLs, Agent Opus extracts the core argument, condenses it into a social-friendly format, and builds a video that drives traffic back to the original post. The key advantage is that you control the level of creative input. Busy marketers can offload the entire creative process with a one-line brief. Content teams with detailed messaging can hand over a script and let Agent Opus handle the production. Either way, the output is a publish-ready video with no manual timeline work, no asset hunting, and no rendering guesswork. This flexibility makes the AI social media video maker ideal for high-volume workflows where some videos need tight brand control and others just need to ship fast.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts that get great results from an AI social media video maker?

The quality of your output starts with how you frame the input. For best results, include three elements in your prompt: the core message, the target platform, and the desired tone. For example, 'Create a 15-second Instagram Reel explaining our SaaS onboarding flow in a friendly, upbeat tone' gives Agent Opus clear guardrails. The AI knows to prioritize visual simplicity, fast pacing, and a conversational voiceover style. If you want to feature specific brand assets, mention them explicitly: 'Use our product dashboard screenshot and company logo in the intro.' Agent Opus will prioritize those visuals and build motion graphics around them. If you have a specific call-to-action, state it: 'End with a swipe-up to our free trial page.' The AI will structure the video to build toward that moment. Avoid vague prompts like 'Make a video about our company.' Instead, narrow the focus: 'Highlight three customer pain points we solve, using testimonial quotes and product shots, for LinkedIn.' The more context you provide about audience, platform, and goal, the better the AI can optimize pacing, visual style, and voiceover delivery. You can also reference existing content: 'Turn this blog post into a 60-second YouTube Short with a problem-solution-CTA structure.' Agent Opus will extract the key points, reformat them for vertical video, and generate a narrative arc that works for short-form. If you're experimenting with hooks, try multiple prompts with different angles and see which one performs. The AI social media video maker is fast enough that you can generate five variations in the time it used to take to edit one. Over time, you'll develop a prompt style that consistently delivers on-brand, high-converting videos with minimal iteration.

### What are the limitations or edge cases where an AI social media video maker might not be the right tool?

Agent Opus excels at generating net-new videos from text-based inputs, but it's not designed for every video workflow. If you need frame-by-frame control over animation timing, custom 3D rendering, or highly stylized motion design that requires manual keyframing, a traditional editing suite is still the better choice. Agent Opus automates the creative and production process, which means you trade granular control for speed and scale. For most social content, that trade-off is worth it. But if you're producing a brand film with specific directorial vision or a product demo that requires exact timing of UI interactions, you'll want more hands-on tools. Another edge case is live-action footage that you've already shot. Agent Opus generates videos from scratch using AI-sourced visuals, motion graphics, and optional avatars. If you have existing video files you want to edit, trim, or repurpose, Agent Opus isn't the right fit. It's built for creation, not post-production. Similarly, if your workflow depends on adding captions, subtitles, or manual transcript edits, those features aren't part of the Agent Opus system. The tool assumes you want a finished video from a text input, not a starting point for further editing. That said, the AI social media video maker is ideal for high-volume content teams, solo creators who need to post daily, and marketers who want to test multiple hooks without burning production budget. If your goal is to go from idea to published post as fast as possible, and you're willing to let AI handle the creative execution, Agent Opus is the fastest path. If you need creative control over every transition and visual choice, or if you're working with pre-shot footage, you'll want a different tool in your stack. The key is understanding what you're optimizing for: speed and scale, or pixel-perfect creative control.
