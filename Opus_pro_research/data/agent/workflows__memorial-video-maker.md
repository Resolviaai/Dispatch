### What are best practices for writing prompts or scripts for a memorial video maker to get the most heartfelt results?

The quality of your memorial video starts with the story you provide. For best results, focus on specific memories, character traits, and moments that capture the person's essence rather than generic statements. Instead of writing 'John was a great father,' describe a particular habit, phrase, or tradition that defined his role, such as 'Every Sunday morning, John made pancakes shaped like animals for his kids, a ritual that continued even when they were teenagers.' Specific details give the memorial video maker richer material to work with and result in a more personal, emotionally resonant tribute. Structure your input as a narrative with a beginning, middle, and end. You might start with early life or defining characteristics, move through key relationships and accomplishments, and close with legacy or the impact the person had on others. This arc helps the AI pace the video naturally. If you are working from a eulogy or prepared speech, paste the full text. The memorial video maker will identify natural section breaks and match visuals to each part of the story. If you are starting from scratch, aim for 300 to 800 words, enough to cover multiple facets of the person's life without overwhelming the video length. Include sensory or emotional language where appropriate. Phrases like 'her laugh filled every room' or 'he found peace in his garden' give the AI cues for selecting visuals and music that match the mood. Mention specific hobbies, places, or relationships to help the system source relevant stock imagery or position your personal photos effectively. Avoid overly formal or stiff language unless that matches the person's character. The memorial video maker responds well to conversational, heartfelt writing that sounds like you are speaking to family and friends. Finally, if you want certain photos to appear at specific moments, note that in your script, such as 'This is when she graduated medical school' next to a description of that milestone. The AI will align the image with that narrative beat, ensuring your personal media lands exactly where it matters most in the tribute.

If you prefer this route, the system automatically matches the AI voice to the sentiment and pacing of your script, ensuring the narration complements rather than competes with the memorial message. Agent Opus analyzes the vocal characteristics, tone, and cadence, then generates the full memorial video narration in that cloned voice based on your script. This approach adds an intimate, personal layer to the tribute, especially meaningful when the narrator has a close relationship with the person being honored. Voice cloning works well for eulogies delivered at services or for family keepsake videos where the narrator's presence is part of the tribute itself. The memorial video maker handles all audio mixing, pacing, and background music balance automatically, so you do not need to adjust levels or sync manually. Both AI and cloned voices are processed to ensure clarity and appropriate emotional tone. If you are unsure which to choose, consider your audience and the video's purpose. AI voices suit public or formal memorial settings where neutrality is preferred. Cloned voices suit intimate family tributes where personal connection is paramount. Either way, the memorial video maker delivers a polished, respectful voiceover that honors the story you are telling.
