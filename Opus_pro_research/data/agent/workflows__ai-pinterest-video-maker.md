### How does AI Pinterest Video Maker handle different Pin formats like product showcases versus tutorials?

AI Pinterest Video Maker adapts its visual assembly and pacing based on the type of content you describe in your prompt. For product showcases, the system emphasizes hero shots, close-up details, and lifestyle contexts while applying motion graphics that draw attention to features, pricing, or calls-to-action. When you input a tutorial or how-to concept, Agent Opus structures the video as a step-by-step sequence with numbered scenes, progress indicators, and visual transitions that guide viewers through each stage. You control the format by being specific in your prompt. For example, stating 'Create a product Pin for handmade candles highlighting scents and packaging' will generate a different visual flow than 'Make a tutorial Pin showing five ways to style a scarf.' The AI interprets intent from your description and selects appropriate stock imagery, pacing, and graphic overlays. If you provide a script or outline with clear sections, Agent Opus maps those to individual scenes automatically. For blog-to-video conversions, the system extracts key points and translates them into visual moments that work for Pinterest's discovery-focused audience. You can also specify aesthetic preferences like 'minimalist,' 'bold and colorful,' or 'lifestyle photography' to guide the visual sourcing. The result is a Pin that matches both the content type and the visual style Pinterest users expect, without requiring you to manually storyboard or select assets.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-performing Pinterest videos?

Effective prompts for AI Pinterest Video Maker combine three elements: a clear content goal, specific visual direction, and audience context. Start by defining what action you want viewers to take, such as 'Drive traffic to a blog post about sustainable fashion' or 'Showcase a new skincare product launch.' This helps Agent Opus prioritize messaging and pacing. Next, describe the visual style and key assets you want featured. For example, 'Use product shots of the serum bottle, before-and-after skin textures, and botanical ingredients' gives the AI concrete elements to incorporate alongside auto-sourced stock. If you have brand guidelines, mention them: 'Keep colors pastel pink and cream, include our logo in the lower third.' For audience context, specify who you're targeting and what problem you're solving: 'Target busy moms looking for quick breakfast ideas' or 'Appeal to DIY home decorators on a budget.' This shapes the tone, voiceover style, and visual metaphors. Pinterest rewards Pins that deliver immediate value, so prompts that emphasize outcomes perform well, like 'Show three ways to repurpose old jeans into home decor in under 60 seconds.' If you're converting a blog post, include the URL and add guidance: 'Focus on the top five tips and make each a distinct visual scene.' Avoid vague prompts like 'Make a video about my business.' Instead, try 'Create a 30-second Pin introducing my meal-prep service, highlighting time savings and fresh ingredients, with upbeat music and a friendly female voiceover.' The more specific your input, the more aligned the output will be with Pinterest's best practices for engagement and click-through.
