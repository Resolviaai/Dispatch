### What are best practices for writing prompts when using an AI video generator for cleaning service owners?

Effective prompts for cleaning service videos start with clarity about your goal and audience. Begin by stating the video type and target customer: "Create a 30-second Instagram Reel promoting our spring deep cleaning package for busy homeowners" or "Generate a testimonial video featuring a satisfied office cleaning client." Include specific service details that differentiate your business, like "eco-friendly products," "bonded and insured team," "same-day availability," or "100% satisfaction guarantee." Mention visual elements you want featured: "Show before-and-after bathroom transformations," "Highlight our team in branded uniforms," or "Include our logo and phone number in the final frame." If you have a script or talking points, paste them directly; Agent Opus will structure the video around your exact messaging. For voiceover, specify tone: "friendly and approachable for residential clients" or "professional and efficient for commercial accounts." Include a clear call to action: "Book online at \[website\]," "Call now for a free quote," or "DM us to schedule." If you're creating a series (weekly tips, monthly specials, seasonal campaigns), establish a consistent format in your first prompt, then reference it: "Use the same style as last week's video but focus on carpet cleaning instead of windows." The more context you provide about your brand voice, target market, and desired outcome, the more on-brand and effective your generated videos will be. Agent Opus learns from your asset library and prompt patterns, so over time you can use shorter prompts and still get consistent results.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using an AI video generator for cleaning service owners?

Agent Opus excels at creating polished marketing videos from text and images, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, video quality depends on the assets you provide: low-resolution photos, poorly lit before-and-after shots, or blurry team pictures will limit the final output's sharpness. Invest in decent smartphone photos of your work, and your videos will look professional. Second, Agent Opus generates videos based on your prompts and uploaded assets; it doesn't film new footage. If you need a specific action shot (like your team using a particular cleaning tool or working in a unique space), you'll need to capture that photo or short clip yourself and upload it. Third, highly complex narratives with multiple characters, locations, and plot points are harder to convey in short-form social videos; Agent Opus works best for focused messages like service promos, testimonials, tips, and explainers. Fourth, while Agent Opus auto-sources royalty-free stock footage when needed, generic stock images of cleaning can look impersonal; your own real photos of your team and results will always perform better. Fifth, voice cloning requires a clear audio sample; if your recording has background noise or poor quality, the cloned voice may sound less natural. Sixth, extremely niche or technical cleaning services (like biohazard remediation or industrial equipment cleaning) may require more detailed prompts to ensure the AI understands the context and emphasizes the right safety or compliance details. Finally, Agent Opus outputs finished videos optimized for social platforms; if you need a long-form documentary-style piece or a highly cinematic brand film, you may need additional production resources.
