### What prompts produce the best cinco de mayo video results?

Effective Cinco de Mayo video prompts combine three things: the topic, the mood, and the specific visual language you want featured. Lead with what the video is about (a party invite, a restaurant promo, a recipe, a family celebration), name the emotional tone (festive, warm, nostalgic, energetic), and reference concrete visual elements ("vibrant Mexican-flag colors," "warm string lights," "hand-lettered captions"). The more specific the visual references, the more precisely the first generation lands. If your content has clear sections — intro, main, outro, or chapter breaks — describe how visuals should shift between them so the agent paces transitions correctly. Avoid vague phrases like "make it festive"; instead anchor on three specifics: who it's for, what they should feel, and what the format looks like. Iterate by regenerating with refinements — usually two or three passes get you to a final cut.
