Case Study: Creative Agency

# Weeks to 15 Minutes:

How Bill Scaled

*16 YouTube Channels*

Bill and his partners at Joe2Joe run 16 YouTube channels and 400K+ subscribers on their flagship. Agent Opus replaced weeks of storyboarding, illustration, and editing with a 15-minute production cycle — and turned sample videos into a client acquisition engine.

## The Production Ceiling

Joe2Joe is a decentralized creative agency — four founding partners, 15 part-time contractors, and roughly 100 collaborators across a portfolio of 16 YouTube channels spanning near-death experiences, veteran stories, crypto, and more. Their flagship, The Other Side NDE, has over 400,000 subscribers with a three-month backlog of stories waiting to be told.

The bottleneck was always logistics. Every concept required manual storyboarding, hand-drawn illustration, After Effects animation, and a final pass through Premiere Pro. A single editor could spend weeks on one video. With a growing client roster and sponsor relationships, they needed a way to produce more without adding headcount.

"These processes were expensive and they were time-consuming, but they could put out some pretty good results. I needed a way to break through that production ceiling." — Bill

### Manual Storyboarding

Labor-intensive conceptualization that requires specialized artists and multiple feedback loops.

### After Effects Animation

High-technical-barrier software that demands hours of keyframing for even simple motion graphics.

### Premiere Pro Assembly

The final bottleneck where all assets are stitched together, often taking days for a single project.

### Weeks Long Production

A linear process that prevents scaling content output regardless of how many ideas are in the pipeline.

## The Agent Opus Solution

### 80–90% Automated Production

Bill showed us a podcast visualization generated from a single audio clip. His estimate for manual production: weeks to months. Agent Opus delivered it in 15 minutes.

### Voice Clone & Brand Consistency

With 16 channels spanning wildly different topics, brand consistency matters. Agent Opus's voice clone lets Bill maintain a recognizable presence across every channel.

### Sound Bite to Polished Video

The killer workflow: grab a sound bite from a client's channel, drop it into Story Mode, and get a fully produced concept video back in minutes. No storyboard. Just output.
