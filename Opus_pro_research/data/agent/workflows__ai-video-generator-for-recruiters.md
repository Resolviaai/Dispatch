### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce high-quality recruiting videos?

Effective prompts for an AI video generator for recruiters balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by defining the video's purpose and target audience in one sentence, such as 'Create a 45-second LinkedIn video to attract mid-level marketing managers to our SaaS startup.' This sets the tone, length, and platform context. Next, list three to five key messages or selling points you want to emphasize. For example, 'Highlight our fully remote culture, unlimited PTO policy, professional development budget, and fast-growing customer base.' The AI video generator for recruiters uses these points to structure scenes and prioritize information. If you have specific visual preferences, mention them: 'Show diverse team members collaborating on video calls, modern home office setups, and our product dashboard in action.' Agent Opus will search for stock footage and web images matching these descriptions and integrate them into the video. For voiceover, specify the desired tone: 'Use an enthusiastic, conversational AI voice that sounds like a recruiter speaking directly to the candidate.' If you want to include data or statistics, write them clearly: 'Mention that we have raised Series B funding and grown headcount by 200 percent in the past year.' The system will display these as animated text overlays synchronized with the voiceover. Avoid overly long prompts; 150 to 250 words is ideal. If you have a full script, paste it directly instead of summarizing it in a prompt. For brand consistency, upload your logo and specify brand colors once in your account settings so every AI video generator for recruiters output automatically includes them. Finally, if you are creating a series of videos such as multiple job ads or a candidate nurture sequence, use a consistent structure in your prompts so the videos feel cohesive. For instance, always start with a hook about company mission, follow with role-specific details, and end with a clear application CTA. This approach helps the AI video generator for recruiters maintain a recognizable style across your recruiting video library.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using an AI video generator for recruiters?

While Agent Opus handles most recruiting video needs automatically, understanding its limitations helps you set realistic expectations and plan workflows. First, the AI video generator for recruiters excels at structured, information-driven content like job ads, benefits overviews, and company culture summaries, but it is not designed for unscripted interviews or live event coverage. If you want to feature a candid employee testimonial or capture a spontaneous team moment, you will need to film that footage yourself and upload it as a custom asset, then reference it in a prompt so Agent Opus can incorporate it into a larger narrative video. Second, highly technical or niche roles may require domain-specific visuals that are not widely available in stock libraries. For example, if you are hiring for a specialized biotech position and need footage of a specific lab technique, the AI video generator for recruiters may default to generic lab imagery. In these cases, provide your own photos or video clips of the actual work environment to ensure accuracy. Third, while the platform supports voice cloning, the quality depends on the input audio. To clone your voice effectively, record at least two minutes of clear speech in a quiet environment with minimal background noise. Low-quality recordings will produce less natural-sounding clones. Fourth, personalization at scale works best when you provide structured data. If you want to generate 50 unique candidate outreach videos, prepare a spreadsheet with candidate names, target roles, and personalized talking points for each, then use batch generation features. Manually writing individual prompts for each video is inefficient. Fifth, the AI video generator for recruiters outputs videos in standard social media formats and aspect ratios, but if you need a custom resolution or format for a specific platform or event, you may need to adjust settings or use a secondary tool for final export. Sixth, while Agent Opus auto-sources royalty-free and stock images, it cannot access proprietary databases or licensed content you have purchased separately unless you upload those assets yourself. Finally, the platform generates complete videos end-to-end, so if you want granular control over individual scene timing, specific camera angles, or frame-by-frame edits, this tool prioritizes speed and automation over manual precision. For most recruiting use cases, these trade-offs are minor, but teams with highly specialized or creative video needs should plan to supplement AI-generated content with custom-shot footage and use Agent Opus for the bulk of their recruiting video production.
