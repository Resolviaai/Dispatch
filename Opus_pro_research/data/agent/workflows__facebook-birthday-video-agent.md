### What are the best practices for writing prompts that generate effective Facebook birthday videos?

Successful Facebook birthday video prompts balance specificity with creative freedom for the AI. Start by clearly stating the video's purpose and recipient context. Instead of 'make a birthday video,' write 'create a Facebook birthday video for John, a team member celebrating his work anniversary, featuring office photos and a warm, appreciative tone.' Include duration guidance, typically 15 to 45 seconds for optimal Facebook engagement. Specify visual style preferences like 'modern and clean,' 'fun and colorful,' or 'elegant and sophisticated' to guide the AI's aesthetic choices. Mention any brand elements that must appear, such as 'include our logo in the corner' or 'use our brand colors blue and gold throughout.' For voiceover, indicate the voice type you want like 'friendly female voice' or 'professional male narrator,' and provide the exact message text if you have specific wording. Reference any personal elements like 'show photos of Sarah with the team' or 'feature images of the customer's favorite products.' Describe desired motion graphics effects such as 'animated confetti,' 'text that pops in,' or 'smooth transitions between scenes.' The more context you provide about the relationship and occasion, the better the AI can tailor the emotional tone. Avoid overly prescriptive shot-by-shot directions; instead, focus on outcomes and feelings. Test with a simple prompt first, then refine based on results. The AI learns from natural language, so write as if briefing a video producer who understands Facebook best practices and birthday video conventions.
