The Manchester United AI video generator performs best when your prompt balances clarity, context, and creative direction. Agent Opus extracts headings, quotes, and key facts, then builds a video narrative around them. Mention aspect ratio and platform if it matters: 'vertical for TikTok' or 'landscape for YouTube' ensures the output is framed correctly from the start. Upload any club-specific assets like logos, custom graphics, or player photos before generating. The system prioritizes uploaded content over stock, so your branding stays front and center. ### What are the limitations or edge cases when using the Manchester United AI video generator for niche or complex football content?

The Manchester United AI video generator excels at standard fan content, match recaps, player highlights, and promotional videos, but certain niche or complex scenarios require extra prompt engineering or manual asset preparation. First, highly technical tactical analysis with on-screen diagrams, player movement arrows, or formation overlays is not auto-generated. If you need these, create the diagrams as static images or short clips, upload them as assets, and reference them in your script. For example, write 'Show tactical diagram of United's 4-2-3-1 formation' in your script, and Agent Opus will insert your uploaded diagram at that point. The system does not draw or animate tactical graphics from scratch. Second, licensed footage from official broadcasts is not included. Agent Opus sources royalty-free stock and web images, which means you won't get actual match footage from Sky Sports or NBC. If you have licensed clips or personal recordings, upload them as assets. The generator will weave them into the video alongside stock content. For fan channels without broadcast rights, this limitation is usually fine because stock action shots, player photos, and stadium imagery are sufficient for commentary and analysis formats. Third, real-time or live content is not supported. The generator works on-demand, not as a live streaming tool. If you want to create a 'live reaction' style video, record your reaction separately, upload it as an asset, and prompt Agent Opus to build the video around it with cutaways, graphics, and voiceover. Fourth, multi-language voiceover requires separate generations. If you need the same video in English and Spanish, generate the English version first, then create a second generation with a Spanish script and AI voice. The system does not auto-translate or dub. Fifth, very long-form content like 10-minute documentaries may require breaking the project into segments. Agent Opus handles videos up to several minutes efficiently, but for extended narratives, consider generating multiple shorter videos and stitching them together externally, or use a detailed script with clear chapter breaks so the system can pace the content appropriately. Sixth, obscure or non-English player names and football terminology may occasionally be mispronounced by AI voices. Voice cloning avoids this issue because you control pronunciation, but if using AI voices, listen to the preview and adjust the script spelling phonetically if needed. For example, spell 'Lisandro Martínez' as 'Liss-andro Mar-tee-nez' in the script to guide the AI. Finally, the generator does not fact-check stats or match details. If your prompt or script contains incorrect information, the video will reflect that. Always verify scorelines, player names, and dates before generating, especially for historical content or record-breaking moments. Despite these edge cases, the Manchester United AI video generator handles the vast majority of fan and marketing use cases out of the box, and most limitations can be worked around with thoughtful prompts and uploaded assets.
