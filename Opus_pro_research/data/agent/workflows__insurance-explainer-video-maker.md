### What are best practices for writing prompts or scripts for insurance explainer video makers?

Effective insurance explainer prompts focus on one clear outcome: what should the viewer understand or do after watching. Start by defining your audience and their knowledge level. A prompt like 'explain whole life insurance to first-time buyers' will generate different visuals and pacing than 'compare term vs whole life for financial advisors.' Be specific about the policy features you want to highlight, such as cash value growth, premium structures, or rider options. If you are pasting a script, structure it with short sentences and natural pauses so the AI voiceover sounds conversational rather than robotic. Break complex ideas into discrete steps. For example, instead of one long paragraph about the claims process, write separate lines for 'file a claim online,' 'adjuster reviews documentation,' and 'receive payout within 10 days.' This helps Agent Opus create distinct visual scenes for each step. Include any must-have visuals in your prompt, such as 'show a family at the dinner table when discussing life insurance beneficiaries' or 'display a side-by-side chart comparing HMO and PPO plans.' If you want to emphasize certain terms, use bold or ALL CAPS in your script so the motion graphics can highlight those words on screen. For voice and tone, specify whether you want a warm, reassuring style for consumer-facing content or a professional, data-driven approach for B2B audiences. Always preview the generated video and refine your prompt based on what worked. Over time, you will develop a library of high-performing prompts for different insurance products and client segments.
