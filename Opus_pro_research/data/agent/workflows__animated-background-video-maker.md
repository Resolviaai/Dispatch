### What are best practices for prompts when using an animated background video maker?

Effective prompts for an animated background video maker balance specificity with creative freedom, giving Agent Opus clear direction while allowing its AI to optimize visual execution. Start with the core visual concept: describe the primary elements you want animated ('floating geometric shapes,' 'abstract light streaks,' 'organic flowing forms'). Then specify motion characteristics using descriptive verbs and adverbs: 'slowly rotating,' 'pulsing rhythmically,' 'drifting diagonally,' 'accelerating smoothly.' These motion descriptors help the AI determine animation curves and timing. Color direction is crucial—provide either specific values ('deep navy blue #1a2332 with coral accents') or descriptive palettes ('warm sunset tones,' 'cool corporate blues and grays,' 'vibrant neon cyberpunk colors'). The animated background video maker interprets both technical and emotional color language. Include mood and energy level: 'high-energy,' 'meditative,' 'professional,' 'playful.' These terms guide pacing, contrast levels, and visual complexity. For brand-aligned backgrounds, reference your uploaded assets: 'incorporate my logo subtly in the corner with the brand color palette as the primary gradient.' Specify use case context when relevant: 'background for product demo video' triggers different compositional choices than 'Instagram story background'—the former might use more negative space for foreground content, while the latter maximizes visual interest edge-to-edge. Avoid over-constraining the AI with frame-by-frame instructions; instead, describe the desired outcome: 'I want viewers to feel calm and focused' works better than 'move shape A to position X at second 3.' Layer your prompt with primary, secondary, and accent elements: 'primary: soft gradient waves, secondary: subtle grid overlay, accent: occasional light flares.' This hierarchy helps the animated background video maker balance visual complexity. Test iteratively—your first prompt establishes the foundation, then refine based on output: 'make the movement 30 percent slower' or 'increase particle density' in follow-up prompts. Finally, reference existing styles when helpful: 'similar to Apple product launch backgrounds' or 'inspired by Spotify's animated branding' gives the AI concrete visual benchmarks while still generating original content.
