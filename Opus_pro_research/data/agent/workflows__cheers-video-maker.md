### What are best practices for prompts when using a cheers video maker?

Effective cheers video maker prompts balance clarity with creative freedom. Specify any visual assets you want featured, such as your logo, product shots, team photos, or specific brand colors. Mention the desired video length and platform, as this shapes pacing and aspect ratio. For example, a 15-second Instagram Reel requires punchier delivery than a 60-second LinkedIn post. If you have a script, paste it directly. The AI will time voiceover delivery, select matching visuals, and add motion graphics that emphasize key phrases. If you are working from a brief, describe the emotional arc: opening hook, celebration moment, call to action or thank you. Include tone guidance like energetic, heartfelt, humorous, or inspirational. The cheers video maker interprets these cues to select voice inflection, visual tempo, and graphic style. Avoid over-specifying visual details unless you have strong preferences. The AI excels at creative assembly when given outcome goals rather than shot-by-shot instructions. Do mention any must-have elements like a specific tagline, logo placement, or product feature. After your first generation, refine the prompt based on what worked. If the pacing felt rushed, ask for a longer version. If visuals felt generic, upload specific brand assets and reference them in your next prompt.
