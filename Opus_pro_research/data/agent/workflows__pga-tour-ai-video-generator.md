A simple 'Highlight Rory's best drives from round three' will show you how Agent Opus interprets golf content. Refine from there by adding detail, adjusting tone, or requesting specific graphics. The PGA Tour AI video generator learns from your input, so iterative prompts help you dial in the exact style and substance you need.
