### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating NFL videos with AI?

Agent Opus is powerful, but understanding its boundaries helps you get optimal results. First, the system generates videos from text inputs, so it cannot directly import or edit existing video files you have already recorded. If you have raw game footage you shot yourself, Agent Opus is not designed to trim or splice that. It excels at creating net-new NFL videos from descriptions, scripts, or articles, sourcing visuals automatically rather than manipulating user-uploaded video. Second, while the NFL AI video generator sources football imagery from web and stock libraries, it cannot guarantee access to official NFL broadcast footage due to licensing restrictions. It finds publicly available highlights, player photos, team logos, and football action shots, but if you need specific copyrighted game clips, you may need to provide those separately or adjust your content approach to work with available imagery. The system is transparent about what it sources, so you can review before publishing. Third, highly niche or very recent content may have limited visual assets. If you want a video about a rookie who just got drafted yesterday, the AI may find fewer images than for an established star. In these cases, the system adapts by using team imagery, position-specific visuals, or related football content to fill gaps. You can also supplement by uploading your own images if you have them. Fourth, Agent Opus generates complete videos in one pass. It does not offer a traditional editing timeline where you drag clips, trim frames, or manually adjust transitions. If you need that level of control, this is not the right tool. The trade-off is speed and simplicity. You get a finished NFL video instantly, but you sacrifice granular editing. For most creators, this is a massive win. For those who love hands-on editing, it may feel limiting. Fifth, voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples. If your source recording is noisy or inconsistent, the cloned voice may not sound as natural. Provide clean audio for best results, or use the AI voice options, which are consistently high-quality. Sixth, the system is optimized for social-length content (15 seconds to 5 minutes). If you want to generate a 30-minute NFL documentary, Agent Opus may not be the ideal fit. It shines for punchy, engaging videos designed for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and LinkedIn, where attention spans are short and production speed matters. Finally, while the AI is smart, it is not a football expert. It does not fact-check stats or verify play descriptions. If your script says Patrick Mahomes threw for 600 yards last week and that is wrong, the video will repeat that error. You are responsible for content accuracy. Agent Opus handles production, you handle editorial integrity. Within these boundaries, the NFL AI video generator is incredibly effective, but knowing the edges helps you plan projects that play to its strengths.
