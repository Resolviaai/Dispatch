### What are best practices for prompts when using an anniversary video maker?

Effective anniversary video prompts balance storytelling with specificity. Start with the core milestone: 'Celebrating 10 years of sustainable fashion' or 'Our app just hit 1 million users.' Agent Opus uses this anchor to set tone and pacing. Next, include 2-4 key moments or achievements you want highlighted. For example, 'Started in a garage, launched our first product in year two, expanded to 50 countries by year five, now leading the industry.' This gives Agent Opus a narrative arc to visualize. Mention any emotional beats: 'Thank our community for believing in us' or 'Excited for what's next.' Agent Opus will adjust music and pacing to match sentiment. If you have specific visuals in mind, note them: 'Show our original storefront photo, then our new headquarters.' Agent Opus will source or incorporate these. For brand consistency, specify voice style: 'Use our CEO's voice clone' or 'Professional female AI voice.' Indicate target platform: 'Vertical video for Instagram Reels' or 'Landscape for YouTube.' Agent Opus optimizes aspect ratio and duration accordingly. Avoid overly long prompts; 3-5 sentences usually suffice. If you need more control, switch to script format with scene-by-scene descriptions. Agent Opus handles both. The key is clarity about what matters most: the milestone itself, the emotional tone, and any must-have visual or audio elements. Agent Opus fills in the rest, sourcing images, creating motion graphics, and assembling a polished final video.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of using an anniversary video maker for complex stories?

Agent Opus handles most anniversary video scenarios automatically, but understanding its boundaries helps you get optimal results. For highly complex narratives with 10+ distinct story beats, consider breaking your anniversary into multiple shorter videos rather than one long piece. Agent Opus excels at 30-90 second videos with 3-5 key moments. Longer, more intricate stories may benefit from a script format where you explicitly define each scene transition. If your anniversary involves very niche or obscure visual references, Agent Opus may need help. For example, 'Show our prototype from 1987' requires you to upload that specific image, as Agent Opus won't find it in stock libraries. Similarly, highly technical product details or industry-specific jargon may need clarification in your prompt. Instead of 'Celebrating our ISO 27001 certification,' add context: 'Celebrating our ISO 27001 certification, a major security milestone that protects customer data.' Agent Opus then generates visuals around security and trust rather than abstract compliance. For anniversaries requiring exact timing, like syncing to a specific song or matching a live event schedule, provide a detailed script with timecodes. Agent Opus will follow your structure, but auto-generated pacing may vary slightly. Voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples of at least 30 seconds. If your source audio is noisy or heavily accented, AI voice options may deliver more consistent results. Finally, if your anniversary video needs legal disclaimers, specific copyright notices, or regulatory text, include that verbatim in your script. Agent Opus will display it as provided. For most standard anniversary videos, these edge cases won't apply, but knowing them helps you plan for unusual or high-stakes projects where precision matters.
