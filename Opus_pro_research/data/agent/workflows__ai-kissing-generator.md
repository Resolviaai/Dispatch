## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the AI kissing generator work step by step?

You upload two photos — one of each person, ideally face-forward with clear lighting and similar framing. Agent Opus analyzes both faces, builds a 3D understanding of pose, expression, and skin tone, and generates a short kissing animation that blends the two together. The agent handles the head turns, lean-in, lip contact, and pull-back as a continuous motion sequence — not a frame stitch. Output is a 3 to 8 second HD video, loopable on request, with cinematic lighting baked in. The system preserves the original faces as closely as possible so the people stay recognizable, while filling in the in-between motion the photos don't capture. Style presets adjust the overall look: cinematic adds warm grading and slow motion, candid keeps it natural and handheld, anime restyles both faces in anime form, and watercolor turns the entire clip into an illustrated painting. Most jobs render in 60 to 120 seconds.

### What photos work best for the AI kissing generator?

Two well-lit, face-forward photos with clear, unobstructed faces. Headshots and shoulder-up portraits work better than full-body shots because the agent has more pixels to work with on the faces. Avoid heavy sunglasses, masks, or anything covering the lips. Matching lighting between the two photos helps — if one person is shot in bright daylight and the other in a dim interior, the final composite can look stitched together. Resolution should be at least 768x768 for each input; higher is better. The two people don't need to be photographed together or even at the same time; the agent handles the spatial blend. For best results, the photos should show roughly similar expressions (both neutral or both smiling) since the agent interpolates from the source expressions into the kiss motion. If you want a specific final emotion — playful, tender, passionate — name it in the prompt.

### Is the AI kissing generator safe and respectful to use?

Only use photos of yourself or people who have given you explicit permission to generate this kind of content. Generating a kissing video of two people — especially without consent — can be deeply harmful and is not what this tool is for. Agent Opus is intended for creators making content with consenting adults: couples making anniversary videos, fan creators working with public-domain or licensed imagery, wedding-content professionals editing client photos, and storytellers using their own performers. Do not use the generator on photos of minors, on photos of public figures without their consent, or to create non-consensual intimate imagery of any kind — that use violates Agent Opus terms and most local laws. The agent blocks generations involving identifiable minors and applies additional review to flagged inputs. Always disclose AI-generated content when sharing publicly, especially when it depicts real people, so viewers know what they're seeing.

### What aspect ratios and platforms does the kissing video export to?

Each generation produces three aspect ratios in one job: 9:16 vertical for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts; 1:1 square for Instagram Feed and message-app sharing; and 16:9 landscape for YouTube and web embeds. The agent reframes each version intelligently — vertical centers both faces in the upper third where mobile viewers focus, square keeps the kiss centered, and landscape pulls back enough to show shoulders and background. Length variants are also produced automatically: a 3-second loop for Reels and Shorts, a 6-second standard cut, and an extended 8-second cinematic version. Files export as high-bitrate MP4 with H.264 encoding for universal playback and AAC silent audio (since the clip has no native audio — you can drop your own music in your editor of choice). For creators looking to add music or voiceover, Agent Opus can also generate a paired audio bed in a follow-up job.
