### How does an AI video generator for realtors handle property details and photos?

Agent Opus takes your input in multiple formats. You can paste an MLS description, write a short script highlighting key features, or provide a property listing URL. The AI parses the text to identify rooms, amenities, square footage, price, and location details. It then sources images in two ways: you can upload your own property photos and drone shots, or the system will automatically pull royalty-free stock images and web visuals that match the property type and neighborhood. For example, if you mention a modern kitchen with quartz countertops, the AI finds relevant kitchen imagery. If you reference a specific neighborhood or school district, it sources local landmarks or community photos. The system also integrates your branding assets. Upload your brokerage logo, headshot, and brand colors once, and every video includes them in lower thirds, end screens, or corner bugs. Motion graphics animate property stats like price, beds, baths, and square footage with smooth transitions. The AI decides scene pacing based on your script length, so a 30-second Reel gets tighter cuts while a 60-second YouTube Short allows more breathing room per feature. You never touch a timeline or manually place images. The entire visual assembly happens automatically, and you get a finished video that looks like a professional editor spent hours on it.

### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI video generator for realtors?

Start with clear structure. If you're working from an MLS description, paste the full text and add a one-sentence goal at the top, like 'Create a 45-second Instagram Reel highlighting the open-concept layout and backyard.' If you're writing a custom script, break it into logical beats: intro hook, property features, neighborhood perks, call to action. For example, 'Hook: Imagine hosting summer BBQs in this backyard. Feature 1: Chef's kitchen with island seating. Feature 2: Primary suite with spa bath. Feature 3: Walk to top-rated schools. CTA: Schedule your showing today.' The AI uses these beats to pace scenes and choose visuals. Be specific about what matters. Instead of 'nice kitchen,' say 'gourmet kitchen with stainless appliances and quartz counters.' Specificity helps the AI find better stock images and write more compelling on-screen text. If you want your cloned voice, mention tone: 'Use my voice, upbeat and welcoming.' If you prefer an AI narrator, specify gender and style: 'Professional female voice, warm and trustworthy.' For branding, note any special requests: 'Show my logo in the bottom right throughout' or 'End with my headshot and contact info.' If you're generating a market update or neighborhood tour instead of a single listing, structure it the same way: intro the topic, present 3 to 5 data points or highlights, close with your expertise positioning. The clearer your input, the tighter the output. Agent Opus handles the rest, assembling scenes, syncing voiceover, and rendering the final video without any manual tweaking.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of an AI video generator for realtors?

Agent Opus is designed for speed and automation, which means it works best when you provide clear, structured input. If your MLS description is vague or your script lacks detail, the AI will still generate a video, but it may choose generic stock images or produce less compelling motion graphics. The more specific you are about property features, neighborhood highlights, and desired tone, the better the output. One edge case: if you upload property photos with poor lighting or cluttered compositions, the AI will use them as provided. It does not retouch or enhance images. For best results, use high-quality listing photos or let the system source professional stock visuals. Another limitation: the AI cannot film new footage. If you need a custom drone flyover of a specific property or a walk-through shot of a unique architectural detail, you'll need to capture that separately and upload it. Agent Opus excels at assembling and animating existing assets, not creating original video footage. Voice cloning requires a clean audio sample. If your recording has background noise or inconsistent volume, the cloned voice may sound less natural. Record in a quiet space with a decent microphone for best results. If you prefer an AI voice, you're limited to the voices available in the library, though the selection covers a wide range of tones and styles. The system also does not handle live-action avatars in real time. If you want to appear on-screen, you can upload a video of yourself speaking, or use an AI avatar that lip-syncs to your script. The AI avatar option works well for talking-head intros or outros, but it won't replicate your exact mannerisms or gestures. Finally, Agent Opus generates one video per prompt. If you want multiple variations like a 15-second teaser, a 45-second Reel, and a 90-second YouTube version, you'll need to generate each separately with adjusted scripts. The upside: each generation is fast, so creating multiple cuts takes minutes, not hours.
