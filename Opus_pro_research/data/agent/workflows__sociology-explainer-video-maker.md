### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate effective sociology explainer videos?

Effective prompts for a sociology explainer video maker combine clear concept identification, audience context, and desired visual emphasis. Start by naming the specific sociological concept or phenomenon you want explained. Instead of 'make a video about culture,' write 'explain cultural relativism and its contrast with ethnocentrism for undergraduate students.' This specificity helps Agent Opus select appropriate terminology, examples, and visual metaphors. Include your target audience because sociology content for TikTok differs dramatically from graduate seminar supplements. A prompt like 'explain social stratification for high school students in 90 seconds' generates accessible language, relatable examples like school cliques or neighborhood differences, and simplified graphics. The same concept for academic audiences might include Marx, Weber, Bourdieu references, and complex diagrams of capital forms. Specify visual priorities when certain elements matter most. If you want data visualization prominent, write 'create a sociology explainer showing income inequality trends with emphasis on statistical charts and demographic breakdowns.' For theory-focused content, note 'visualize symbolic interactionism with emphasis on Mead's concepts of self and social interaction through animated scenarios.' Provide structure cues by outlining sections: 'Introduction to deviance, Merton's strain theory, labeling theory perspective, real-world applications, conclusion.' This guides the sociology explainer video maker's scene assembly and pacing. Include tone preferences like 'conversational and accessible' versus 'formal academic presentation.' When using scripts or articles as input, ensure they contain concrete examples alongside abstract theory. The system generates stronger visuals when it has both conceptual frameworks and real-world illustrations to work with. Avoid overly broad prompts that lack focus, and remember that more context in your input produces more targeted, effective sociology explainer videos.
