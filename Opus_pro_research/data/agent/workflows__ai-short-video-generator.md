## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does an AI short video generator handle different input types like prompts versus full scripts?

Agent Opus accepts four input formats: a short prompt or brief, a complete script, a structured outline, or a blog post URL. Each input type triggers a slightly different workflow, but the output is always a finished short video. When you provide a brief or prompt, the AI short video generator interprets your intent, expands the concept into scenes, and writes internal scene descriptions to guide visual selection and pacing. If you supply a full script, Agent Opus uses that text directly for voiceover and structures scenes around the narrative beats in your script. An outline input works similarly to a prompt but gives the system more structure to follow, so the generated video mirrors your section headings or bullet points. Finally, when you paste a blog or article URL, Agent Opus extracts the key points, condenses the content into a short-form narrative, and generates a video that summarizes or promotes the article. Regardless of input type, the AI short video generator applies the same motion graphics engine, visual sourcing logic, and voiceover synthesis. The main difference is how much creative interpretation the system performs. A prompt gives the AI maximum freedom to structure the video. A script gives you maximum control over the narrative. An outline or blog URL sits in the middle, balancing your structure with AI scene assembly. In all cases, you receive a publish-ready video with no manual editing required.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts that produce high-quality results with an AI short video generator?

Effective prompts for an AI short video generator balance clarity with creative freedom. Start by stating the core message or goal in one sentence. For example, instead of writing a vague prompt like 'make a video about productivity,' write 'explain three time-blocking techniques for remote workers in under 60 seconds.' Specificity helps the AI short video generator understand tone, pacing, and target audience. Next, include any must-have elements such as your brand name, a product feature, a call to action, or a specific visual style. If you want the video to open with a question, mention that. If you need the video to end with a URL or discount code, include it in the prompt. Agent Opus will incorporate these details into the generated scenes. Avoid overly long prompts with conflicting instructions. A prompt that says 'make it funny but also serious and educational but also entertaining' confuses the system. Pick one or two tones and stick with them. If you want humor, say so. If you want a professional explainer, specify that. The AI short video generator performs best when the creative direction is clear. Also, mention the platform or format if it matters. A TikTok video has different pacing than a LinkedIn post. If you write 'create a TikTok video about morning routines,' the system will optimize for vertical format, fast cuts, and trending visual styles. Finally, test and iterate. Generate a video, review the output, and refine your prompt based on what worked. The AI short video generator learns from specificity, so the more you clarify your intent, the closer the output will match your vision.

### What are the limitations or edge cases where an AI short video generator might not produce the exact result I want?

An AI short video generator like Agent Opus excels at creating short-form videos from text, but there are scenarios where the output may not match your expectations on the first try. First, highly niche or technical topics can be challenging. If your script references obscure industry jargon, rare products, or hyper-specific visuals that are not widely available in stock libraries or web images, the AI short video generator may substitute generic imagery. For example, if you write a script about a proprietary software interface, the system cannot generate a screenshot of that interface unless you provide it as an uploaded asset. In these cases, you should upload your own images or product shots to ensure accuracy. Second, complex narratives with multiple characters, locations, or plot twists are harder to convey in short-form video. The AI short video generator is optimized for concise, single-concept videos. If your script has three different speakers, five scene changes, and a twist ending, the system may struggle to pace the video correctly. Consider breaking complex stories into multiple shorter videos. Third, humor and cultural references are subjective. If your script relies on wordplay, sarcasm, or regional humor, the AI may not interpret the tone correctly. The voiceover might sound too serious, or the visuals might not match the joke. In these cases, review the output and adjust your script to make the humor more explicit. Fourth, the AI short video generator cannot generate custom illustrations, 3D animations, or branded motion graphics from scratch. It applies pre-built motion effects and sources existing images. If you need a custom animated logo reveal or a hand-drawn illustration, you will need to create that asset separately and upload it. Finally, the system works best with clear, structured input. If your prompt is vague or contradictory, the output will reflect that ambiguity. The more specific and focused your input, the better the result.
