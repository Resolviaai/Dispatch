### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating New Year videos from text prompts?

Understanding where a new year video maker excels and where it has constraints helps you set realistic expectations and optimize your workflow. Agent Opus generates complete videos from text, but highly specific, frame-by-frame creative visions may require iterative prompting. For example, if you want a particular celebrity likeness, copyrighted character, or exact real-world location (e.g., Times Square Ball Drop footage), the system sources royalty-free alternatives or AI-generated approximations rather than licensed content. If your New Year video concept depends on a specific viral trend or meme format that emerged days ago, the AI may not have trained on that exact reference; instead, describe the trend's mechanics ('split-screen before/after format with countdown timer') so the generator can recreate the structure with festive elements. For brand assets, upload high-resolution files; low-quality logos or product images may not integrate cleanly into motion graphics. The new year video maker handles standard aspect ratios (9:16 for Reels, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 for feed posts) automatically, but if you need a non-standard format, specify dimensions in your prompt. Voice cloning requires a clear audio sample; background noise or multiple speakers in the source file can affect quality, so record a clean 30-second clip for best results. If you're generating New Year videos in languages other than English, confirm language support and test prompts to ensure festive terminology translates correctly. The system excels at assembling scenes, applying motion graphics, and pacing content, but it doesn't perform manual edits like trimming specific frames or adjusting individual layer opacities; if you need that level of control, adjust your prompt to describe the desired outcome ('shorten intro to 3 seconds, emphasize product shot') rather than expecting a timeline editor. For testimonials or user-generated content, the new year video maker can incorporate text quotes or voiceover, but it won't fabricate fake testimonials; provide real quotes or customer stories. Finally, while the platform generates publish-ready videos, always preview output before posting to ensure the AI interpreted your prompt as intended, especially for high-stakes campaigns. Most edge cases resolve by refining prompts, uploading better source assets, or generating a second variation with adjusted instructions, and the speed of iteration means you can test multiple approaches in the time traditional editing would take for one video.
