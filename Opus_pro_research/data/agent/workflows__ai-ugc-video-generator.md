### What are the best practices for prompts when using an AI UGC video generator?

Effective prompts for AI UGC video generation focus on audience pain points, conversational tone, and clear outcomes. Start by defining who's speaking in the video. Is it a satisfied customer, a founder explaining the product, or a creator sharing a discovery? This sets the voice and perspective. Next, outline the core message in natural, spoken language. Instead of 'Our app increases productivity,' write 'I used to waste hours on manual data entry, but this app cut my workload in half.' The AI UGC video generator performs best with prompts that sound like real people talking, not marketing copy. Include specific benefits and relatable scenarios. Mention the problem first, then the solution your product provides. For example, 'Tired of expensive gym memberships you never use? I found this home workout app that actually keeps me motivated.' This structure mirrors how real UGC content builds credibility. Specify any visual elements you want featured, like 'show the app interface' or 'include product unboxing shots.' The AI will source or use your uploaded assets accordingly. If you want a particular tone, say so: 'excited and energetic' or 'calm and trustworthy.' The system adjusts pacing, music, and delivery to match. Keep prompts focused on one core message per video. UGC content performs best when it's simple and direct. Avoid cramming multiple features or CTAs into a single brief. Instead, generate multiple variations testing different hooks or angles. For voice, specify whether you want your cloned voice, an AI voice, or describe the voice style you need. The AI UGC video generator can match demographics and energy levels to your target audience. Finally, mention platform and length if relevant: 'Create a 15-second TikTok hook' or 'Generate a 30-second Instagram Reel.' This helps the AI optimize pacing and formatting for where the video will perform.

They're ideal for long-term brand ambassadorships, influencer campaigns, and content that requires deep authenticity or niche expertise. However, creator partnerships come with challenges: coordination overhead, variable quality, limited control over messaging, and high per-video costs that make testing expensive. Agent Opus as an AI UGC video generator solves the volume and speed problem. The trade-off is that AI-generated UGC, while authentic-looking, lacks the personal narrative and audience connection of a real creator's endorsement. The ideal strategy combines both: use AI UGC video generation for high-volume testing, evergreen content, and rapid iteration, then invest in real creator partnerships for flagship campaigns, community building, and content that benefits from genuine personal stories. This hybrid approach maximizes efficiency while preserving authenticity where it matters most.
