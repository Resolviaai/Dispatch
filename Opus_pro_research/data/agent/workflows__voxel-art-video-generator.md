### What prompts produce the best voxel art video results?

Effective voxel art video prompts combine three things: the subject, the mood, and the specific visual language you want featured. Lead with what the video is about, name the emotional tone (energetic, playful, contemplative), and reference concrete visual elements such as "3D cube-block construction" or "bold on-screen captions." The more specific the visual references, the more precisely the first generation lands. If your content has clear sections — intro, main, outro — describe how the visuals should shift between them so the agent paces transitions correctly. Avoid vague phrases like "make it cool"; the model treats those as low-signal and falls back to generic styling. Iterate by regenerating with small refinements — usually two or three passes get you to a final cut.
