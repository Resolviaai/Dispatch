### What types of prompts work best for generating high-engagement videos?

The most effective prompts for an engagement video maker give Agent Opus clear direction on message, tone, and audience while leaving room for creative visual execution. Start by defining your core message in one or two sentences, then specify your target audience and the emotional response you want to trigger. For example, instead of a vague prompt like 'make a video about productivity,' try 'create an energetic 30-second video for busy entrepreneurs showing how our app saves two hours per day, using bold motion graphics and an upbeat tone.' Include any must-have elements like product shots, brand colors, or specific talking points, but avoid over-scripting every visual detail. Agent Opus performs best when you describe the outcome and let the AI handle scene composition and pacing. If you are working from a blog post or article URL, the system extracts key points automatically, but you can add a brief like 'focus on the three main benefits and keep it under 60 seconds' to guide the structure. For voice and avatar choices, specify the style you want, such as 'professional female narrator' or 'casual founder explaining the concept.' The engagement video maker interprets natural language well, so write prompts the way you would brief a video producer. Test different approaches with your free generations to learn what resonates with your audience, then refine your prompt templates for consistent results across your content calendar.
