### What are best practices for prompts when creating hotel promotional videos?

Effective hotel video maker prompts balance specificity with creative freedom. Start with your core message: are you driving direct bookings, promoting a seasonal package, or building brand awareness? Then layer in concrete details. Instead of 'make a hotel video,' try 'create a 60-second Instagram Reel showcasing our beachfront suites, infinity pool, and farm-to-table restaurant, targeting couples planning romantic getaways.' Mention your unique selling points explicitly: 'highlight our pet-friendly policy and dog spa' or 'emphasize our historic 1920s architecture and original Art Deco details.' Include desired emotional tone: 'luxurious and serene' versus 'fun and family-friendly' produces dramatically different visual treatments. If you have a script or outline, paste it directly; Agent Opus will structure scenes around your narrative beats. For blog-to-video conversions, use URLs from destination guides, amenity deep-dives, or local attraction roundups. The system extracts key points and visualizes them automatically. Specify voice preferences: 'warm female voice' or 'authoritative male narrator' or 'use my voice clone for authentic host feel.' Mention any must-include assets: 'feature our new spa wing photos' or 'show our Michelin-starred chef.' Avoid vague requests; the more context you provide about audience, platform, and goals, the more precisely the hotel video maker delivers results that drive your specific business outcome.
