### How does an AI behind the scenes video maker handle different types of input?

Agent Opus accepts four input formats to generate behind-the-scenes videos, each optimized for different workflows. First, you can write a simple text prompt describing what you want to show. For example, type 'Show our design team brainstorming new product features, then prototyping on whiteboards, ending with the final product reveal.' Agent Opus interprets this brief, determines scene structure, and assembles a complete video. Second, you can paste a full script with specific narration and scene descriptions. The AI behind the scenes video maker follows your script exactly, matching visuals to each line and maintaining your intended pacing. Third, you can provide an outline with bullet points or section headers. Agent Opus expands each point into a scene, adds transitions, and creates a cohesive narrative flow. Fourth, you can submit a blog post or article URL. The system extracts key points, identifies visual moments, and transforms written content into video storytelling. Across all formats, Agent Opus integrates your uploaded brand assets like logos, product photos, and team images. It also auto-sources complementary royalty-free or web imagery to fill gaps. The AI determines optimal scene length, applies motion graphics to static images, and synchronizes voiceover timing. You get a publish-ready behind-the-scenes video regardless of whether you started with two sentences or a 2,000-word article. This flexibility means you can repurpose existing written content, quickly test video ideas with short prompts, or maintain precise control with detailed scripts.

### What are best practices for prompts when creating behind-the-scenes videos with AI?

Effective prompts for an AI behind the scenes video maker balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by defining the narrative arc. Behind-the-scenes content works best when it shows progression: problem, process, result. For example, 'Our team identified a gap in the market, spent three months prototyping solutions, and launched a product that solved X problem.' This structure gives Agent Opus clear beginning, middle, and end points. Next, specify visual anchors. Mention concrete elements you want featured: 'Show our warehouse, close-ups of packaging materials, and the final boxed product.' The AI behind the scenes video maker prioritizes these elements when sourcing or generating visuals. If you have specific brand assets, reference them: 'Include our new logo animation and product photos from the May shoot.' Agent Opus integrates uploaded files directly. For voiceover, indicate tone and pacing: 'Use an energetic, fast-paced narration' or 'Keep it calm and documentary-style.' If you want an avatar presenter, specify: 'Feature our CEO as on-screen narrator.' For script-based inputs, write conversationally. Behind-the-scenes videos feel authentic when narration sounds human, not corporate. Use contractions, ask rhetorical questions, and vary sentence length. Agent Opus preserves your voice while adding visual storytelling. Avoid over-prescribing visual details for every second. The AI behind the scenes video maker excels at interpreting intent and filling gaps creatively. Instead of 'Show a 3-second shot of a laptop, then a 2-second shot of coffee, then a 4-second shot of a notebook,' write 'Show our team working in the office.' The system determines optimal shot variety and pacing. Finally, test iteratively. Generate a video from a short prompt, review the output, then refine your brief with more specific direction where needed.
