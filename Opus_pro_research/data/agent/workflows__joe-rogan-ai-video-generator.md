### What are best practices for prompts when using the Joe Rogan AI video generator?

Effective prompts for Joe Rogan-style AI video generation balance specificity with creative freedom. Specify the tone, whether casual and humorous like Rogan's style, or more formal and educational. Include the target length in seconds or minutes so Agent Opus can pace the dialogue appropriately. Mention key topics or talking points you want covered, but avoid over-scripting unless you need exact phrasing. For example, a strong prompt might be: 'Two-person podcast interview about the future of AI in creative work, casual and curious tone, 120 seconds, cover automation benefits and ethical concerns.' Agent Opus will generate dialogue, assign voices, and build visuals around those parameters. If you want specific visual elements, mention them: 'include tech startup office B-roll' or 'show AI-generated art examples.' For voice and avatar preferences, specify gender, accent, or energy level if it matters to your brand. If you have a brand logo or product shots, note that in the prompt so Agent Opus integrates them naturally. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a video about AI' because the generator needs direction on tone, format, and focus. Test short prompts first to see how Agent Opus interprets your style, then refine with more detail. You can regenerate unlimited times during the free trial to dial in the perfect output. The best prompts give Agent Opus enough structure to stay on-message while leaving room for natural conversational flow and dynamic visual choices.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating Joe Rogan-style AI videos with Agent Opus?

Agent Opus excels at generating podcast-format videos from text, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, the generator works from text inputs only. You cannot upload existing video footage or audio files to remix or edit. If you have recorded podcast audio, you would need to transcribe it into a script first, then use that script to generate a new AI video. Second, while Agent Opus auto-sources web and stock images for B-roll, it cannot access private or proprietary media libraries unless you upload those assets directly. If your brand requires specific product shots or office footage, provide those files so Agent Opus can integrate them. Fourth, extremely long scripts over 10 minutes may require breaking into multiple video segments, as social platforms and rendering pipelines favor shorter formats. Fifth, highly technical or niche jargon may need clarification in your prompt so the AI voices pronounce terms correctly. You can phonetically spell complex words in the script to guide pronunciation. Sixth, the Joe Rogan AI video generator is optimized for conversational, interview-style content. If you need highly scripted, teleprompter-style delivery or news anchor formats, adjust your prompt to specify that tone, but the natural strength is casual dialogue. Seventh, while Agent Opus handles motion graphics and visual pacing automatically, it does not offer a manual timeline editor. If you need frame-by-frame control, Agent Opus may not fit that workflow. Finally, generated videos are social-ready and publish-quality, but they are AI-created content. Always review output for factual accuracy, tone alignment, and brand compliance before publishing, especially for sensitive or regulated industries.
