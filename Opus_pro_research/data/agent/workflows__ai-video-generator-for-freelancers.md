### What are best practices for writing prompts in an AI video generator for freelancers to get the best results?

Effective prompts for an AI video generator for freelancers balance clarity with creative direction. Start by defining the video's purpose: is it a product demo, explainer, testimonial recap, or social ad? State the goal upfront so Agent Opus can tailor pacing and visuals. Next, describe your target audience. Mention demographics, pain points, or interests so the AI selects appropriate tone and imagery. For example, a prompt for a SaaS explainer targeting startup founders will yield different visuals and pacing than a lifestyle brand targeting Gen Z consumers. Include key messages or talking points you want covered. Bullet out the must-have facts, benefits, or calls to action. The more specific you are about what to emphasize, the tighter the final video. Specify tone and style: professional, playful, urgent, inspirational. If you want a specific visual aesthetic, mention it. For instance, say modern minimalist graphics or vibrant, energetic motion if that matches your client's brand. If you have brand assets like logos or product images, note that you'll upload them. Agent Opus will integrate these into the generated scenes. Mention desired video length or platform. A 30-second TikTok ad requires different pacing than a 90-second LinkedIn explainer. Finally, if you want a specific voice style or avatar, call that out. The AI video generator for freelancers uses these cues to assemble scenes, select stock or web images, apply motion graphics, and pace the narrative. The more context you provide, the closer the first draft will be to your vision. You can always regenerate with refined prompts, but a well-crafted initial brief saves time and delivers client-ready results faster.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of an AI video generator for freelancers, and how do I work around them?

Agent Opus is designed to handle the majority of freelance video needs, but understanding its boundaries helps you set client expectations and plan projects effectively. First, highly niche or hyper-local visuals may require manual asset uploads. If a client needs footage of a specific building, event, or proprietary product that isn't available in stock libraries or web sources, you'll need to provide those images or clips. Agent Opus will integrate them seamlessly, but it can't generate visuals that don't exist in its training data or accessible libraries. Second, the system excels at narrative-driven, social-first video formats. If a client requests a feature-length documentary or a highly cinematic brand film with complex scene transitions and custom cinematography, Agent Opus may not be the right tool. It's optimized for explainer videos, social ads, product demos, and short-form content where speed and clarity matter more than Hollywood-level production value. Third, voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples. If you provide a noisy or low-fidelity recording, the cloned voice may lack polish. Record your voice sample in a quiet environment with a decent microphone for best results. Fourth, while Agent Opus generates publish-ready videos, you may occasionally want to tweak a specific scene or adjust pacing for a particular client preference. The system doesn't offer a manual editing interface, so if a client requests granular changes, you may need to refine your prompt and regenerate rather than making surgical edits. This trade-off is intentional: the AI video generator for freelancers prioritizes speed and automation over pixel-level control. Finally, if a project requires live-action footage of real people performing specific actions, you'll need to shoot that footage yourself and upload it as an asset. Agent Opus can incorporate it into the final video, but it won't generate photorealistic human performances from scratch. Understanding these boundaries lets you scope projects accurately, communicate capabilities to clients, and use Agent Opus where it shines: turning text into polished, social-ready videos fast.
