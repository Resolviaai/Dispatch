## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does a cloud video maker like Agent Opus generate videos from a brief?

Agent Opus uses a multi-stage AI pipeline to transform text into finished video. When you submit a prompt or brief, the system first analyzes your input to identify key topics, tone, and structure. It then generates a scene-by-scene plan that maps your message to visual sequences. For each scene, the cloud video maker selects or generates appropriate visuals, whether that means sourcing royalty-free stock images, pulling web imagery, or incorporating your uploaded brand assets like logos and product shots. Motion graphics are applied automatically based on scene context, adding kinetic text, transitions, and visual effects without manual keyframing. The voiceover layer is synthesized using either your cloned voice (if you've uploaded samples) or a selected AI narrator, with pacing and emphasis matched to the visual rhythm. Background music is added from a licensed library, and the entire composition is assembled in the cloud. The result is a publish-ready video file optimized for your chosen social platform, delivered without any timeline interaction or manual editing. This cloud-based approach means you can submit a brief from your phone during a commute and have a finished video waiting when you reach your desk. The system handles all technical decisions about pacing, visual hierarchy, and audio mixing, letting you focus on the message rather than the mechanics of video production.

### Can I use my own brand assets in a cloud video maker, or am I limited to stock libraries?

Agent Opus is designed to integrate your brand assets directly into the cloud video generation process. You can upload your logo, product images, team photos, screenshots, or any visual elements that represent your brand. These assets are stored in your cloud account and become available for automatic inclusion in every video you generate. When you submit a prompt or script, you can reference specific assets by name, or let the AI decide when to incorporate them based on context. For example, if your script mentions your product, the cloud video maker will pull the relevant product shot from your library and composite it into the appropriate scene with motion graphics and text overlays. This is fundamentally different from generic stock-only platforms. Your brand consistency is maintained across all videos because the system draws from your curated asset library rather than random stock footage. You can also upload voice samples to create a voice clone, ensuring every video sounds like you or your brand spokesperson. The cloud infrastructure means your asset library is accessible from any device, and updates propagate instantly. If you refresh your logo or add new product images, those changes are immediately available for future video generation. This combination of custom assets and AI automation gives you the brand control of traditional video production with the speed and scalability of a cloud video maker.

### What are the technical requirements for using a cloud video maker, and how does rendering work?

Agent Opus requires only a modern web browser and an internet connection. There are no software downloads, no minimum RAM or CPU specifications, and no local storage requirements. The entire video generation process happens on cloud servers, so your device is simply a control interface. This architecture has several practical advantages. First, you can start a video on your desktop, check progress on your phone, and download the final file from a tablet. Your projects and asset library are always in sync because they live in the cloud, not on a specific machine. Second, rendering speed is decoupled from your device performance. A five-year-old laptop generates videos just as fast as a new workstation because the heavy lifting happens on Agent Opus servers. The cloud video maker allocates processing resources dynamically, so complex videos with extensive motion graphics and long runtimes render in minutes rather than hours. Third, you avoid the storage burden of video files. Raw footage, project files, and intermediate renders never touch your hard drive. You simply download the final compressed video when it's ready, and even that can stream directly to social platforms if you prefer. The cloud infrastructure also enables collaborative workflows. Team members can access the same account, review in-progress videos, and generate content from different locations without file transfers or version conflicts. Updates to the platform happen server-side, so you always have access to the latest AI models and features without manual updates. This cloud-first design removes the traditional barriers to video creation: expensive hardware, complex software, and technical expertise.

### How does a cloud video maker handle different input formats like scripts versus short prompts?

Agent Opus adapts its generation process based on the input format you provide. For short prompts, the system acts as a creative partner, expanding your idea into a full video concept. If you submit something like 'explain our new pricing model,' the cloud video maker will generate a script structure, select appropriate visual metaphors, and create a complete narrative arc. This is ideal for quick content needs where you trust the AI to handle creative decisions. For detailed scripts, Agent Opus follows your written content closely, treating each paragraph or section as a distinct scene. The system analyzes your script for pacing cues, emphasis points, and structural breaks, then maps visuals and motion graphics to support your exact wording. This gives you precise control over messaging while still automating the visual production. Blog post URLs trigger a different workflow. The cloud video maker scrapes the article, extracts key points, and condenses the content into a video-appropriate narrative. It identifies the main argument, supporting evidence, and conclusion, then creates scenes that communicate those elements visually. This is particularly useful for repurposing written content into social video without manual summarization. Outlines fall between prompts and scripts. You provide the structure and key points, and Agent Opus fills in the connective tissue, generates supporting visuals, and creates a cohesive flow. This format works well when you know what you want to cover but don't want to write full narration. Regardless of input format, the cloud video maker maintains consistency in visual style, voiceover tone, and pacing. You can mix formats across different projects, and the system will adapt while keeping your brand elements consistent.
