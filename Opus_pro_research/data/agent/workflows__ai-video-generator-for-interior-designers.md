The system also accepts blog URLs. Paste a link to your portfolio post about a recent residential project, and the AI extracts the narrative, identifies design highlights, and converts the article into a video format. Regardless of input type, the AI video generator for interior designers prioritizes visual storytelling that aligns with design industry standards. You control the level of detail; Agent Opus handles the translation from text to motion.

### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI video generator for interior designers?

Effective prompts for interior design videos balance creative vision with specific details. Start by defining the project type and style. Instead of 'living room video,' write 'Mid-century modern living room with walnut credenza, teak coffee table, and burnt orange accent chairs.' The AI uses those specifics to source accurate imagery and craft a narrative that emphasizes period-appropriate elements. Include sensory and emotional language. Phrases like 'warm, inviting atmosphere' or 'clean lines and uncluttered surfaces' guide the AI's tone and visual selection. If you want the video to convey tranquility, mention 'soft neutral palette' and 'natural textures.' For energy and boldness, reference 'jewel-tone accents' and 'geometric patterns.' Specify the video's purpose and audience. A prompt for a client presentation might read 'Showcase the master suite renovation for a young professional couple, emphasizing the walk-in closet organization system and spa-like ensuite.' A social media prompt could be 'Create a 30-second Reel highlighting five budget-friendly ways to refresh a rental bathroom, targeting first-time renters.' Purpose shapes pacing, voiceover style, and call-to-action. Mention brand assets explicitly. If you want your logo in the intro, write 'Open with \[YourStudio\] logo, then transition to the entryway reveal.' If you have signature materials, list them: 'Feature our custom terrazzo samples and the linen drapery line we always recommend.' The AI video generator for interior designers integrates these assets into the final output. Provide context for before-after narratives. Instead of 'show transformation,' describe 'Start with the dated 1980s kitchen with laminate counters and fluorescent lighting, then reveal the updated space with marble backsplash and pendant fixtures.' The AI structures the video to emphasize contrast. Use industry terminology confidently. Terms like 'open floor plan,' 'coffered ceiling,' 'shiplap accent wall,' and 'herringbone tile' help the AI source precise visuals. Avoid vague requests; specificity yields better results. Finally, iterate. Generate a first draft, review the output, then refine your prompt. If the pacing feels rushed, add 'slow, contemplative pacing with lingering shots on texture details.' If the voiceover tone is too formal, request 'conversational, friendly narration as if explaining the design to a friend.' Agent Opus learns from your adjustments and improves alignment with your vision.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using an AI video generator for interior designers?

Agent Opus excels at generating polished videos from text, but understanding its boundaries helps you set realistic expectations. The AI sources imagery from royalty-free libraries and the web, which means highly specific or proprietary visuals may not always be available. If you need footage of a custom furniture piece you designed, upload that image as a brand asset rather than relying on auto-sourcing. The system integrates uploaded assets seamlessly, but it cannot generate original 3D renderings or CAD visualizations from scratch. For projects requiring precise architectural drawings or floor plans, prepare those visuals in your design software and include them in your asset library. The AI will incorporate them into the video narrative. Voiceover quality depends on input. If you clone your voice, record a clean sample in a quiet environment. Background noise or inconsistent audio in the source clip degrades the clone. AI voices are professional but may lack the nuanced inflection a human narrator brings to complex design concepts. Test both options and choose what aligns with your brand. The system does not perform manual editing tasks. You cannot trim individual frames, adjust caption timing, or rearrange scenes in a timeline interface. Agent Opus is a prompt-to-publish tool, not a traditional video editor. If you need granular control over every transition, this is not the right workflow. However, you can regenerate videos with revised prompts to achieve different pacing or structure. Aspect ratio outputs are social-ready, but the AI does not create interactive elements like clickable hotspots or embedded floor plan overlays. If your goal is an interactive virtual tour, you will need specialized software. Agent Opus focuses on linear video storytelling optimized for platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn. Language support is broad, but highly technical jargon or region-specific design terms may require clarification in your prompt. If you use niche terminology, add a brief explanation: 'Wabi-sabi aesthetic, which emphasizes imperfection and natural materials.' This ensures the AI selects appropriate imagery. The AI video generator for interior designers works best when you provide clear creative direction. Vague prompts like 'make a nice video' yield generic results. Specificity about style, audience, and purpose produces videos that align with your vision. Finally, the system does not replace human creativity. It accelerates production and handles technical execution, but your design expertise, storytelling instincts, and brand voice remain essential. Use Agent Opus as a tool that amplifies your output, not a substitute for your professional judgment.
