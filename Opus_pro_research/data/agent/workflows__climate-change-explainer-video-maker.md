### What are best practices for writing prompts or scripts for climate change explainer videos?

Effective prompts for a climate change explainer video maker balance clarity, structure, and visual cues. Start with a one-sentence thesis: 'Explain how Arctic ice loss accelerates global warming through albedo feedback' or 'Show the economic case for transitioning to renewable energy by 2040.' Agent Opus uses this framing to select visuals and pace the narrative. Next, break your content into logical sections. For a five-minute explainer, you might outline: intro hook, problem statement, mechanism or data, solutions or case studies, and call to action. Within each section, include specific data points or examples. Instead of 'temperatures are rising,' write 'global average temperature has increased 1.2 degrees Celsius since pre-industrial times, with the fastest warming in the Arctic.' Agent Opus will source Arctic temperature maps and animate the 1.2-degree rise. If you're working from a blog post or research article, paste the URL directly. The system extracts headings, key stats, and cited studies, then structures scenes around them. For maximum impact, flag visual opportunities in your script: 'show time-lapse of glacier retreat,' 'overlay CO2 concentration graph,' 'cut to wind farm footage.' Agent Opus interprets these cues and sources matching assets. Avoid jargon without context; if you use terms like 'radiative forcing' or 'tipping points,' add a brief plain-language explanation so the voiceover remains accessible. Specify tone: 'urgent but hopeful,' 'data-driven and neutral,' or 'activist and direct.' This guides voice selection and pacing. Finally, if you have brand guidelines, mention them: 'use our logo in the lower third,' 'feature our solar panel product shots in the solutions section.' The climate change explainer video maker integrates these assets automatically. By combining clear structure, specific data, visual cues, and tone direction, you give Agent Opus everything it needs to generate a compelling, accurate, and publish-ready climate video in one pass.
