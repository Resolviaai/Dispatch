### How do I write effective prompts for an AI video generator for lawyers to get the best results?

Effective prompts for legal video generation start with clarity about your video's purpose and audience. Begin by stating the video type and goal explicitly. For example, write 'Create a 60-second client FAQ video explaining how contingency fees work in personal injury cases' rather than just 'video about fees.' This specificity helps Agent Opus select appropriate pacing, visuals, and tone. Next, provide your actual script or detailed outline. The more complete your text input, the better the final video. If you are creating an explainer video, write out the full explanation in clear, conversational language as if you are speaking directly to a client. Break complex concepts into short paragraphs or bullet points, and Agent Opus will create distinct visual scenes for each section. Include any specific visual guidance in your prompt. If you want certain imagery like a courtroom, legal documents, or abstract justice concepts, mention those. For example, 'Show courtroom imagery when discussing trial process, then transition to document visuals for settlement discussion.' This guides the AI's visual selection. Specify your desired voice and avatar preferences. Write 'Use my cloned voice with professional male avatar' or 'Use warm female AI voice, no avatar, focus on motion graphics.' For brand consistency, note 'Include firm logo and use brand colors throughout.' If you are working from existing content like a blog post or article, you can simply provide the URL, and Agent Opus will extract the key points and generate a video. However, you will get more control by adapting that content into a script format first. Consider your platform and length. Write 'Create 30-second Instagram Reel' or 'Generate 3-minute YouTube explainer' so the system optimizes pacing and aspect ratio. Finally, iterate and refine. Generate a first version, review it, then adjust your prompt with more specific guidance about pacing, visual style, or content emphasis for the next generation.
