### What are the best practices for writing prompts in an AI video generator for course creators to get high-quality lesson videos?

Effective prompts for Agent Opus balance structure with creative freedom. Start by defining the lesson objective in one sentence: 'Teach beginners how to set up a WordPress site' or 'Explain the difference between gross and net profit.' This anchors the AI's narrative arc. Next, provide either a full script, an outline with key points, or a detailed brief that includes teaching beats. If you're using a script, break it into logical sections with headers like 'Introduction,' 'Step 1: Install WordPress,' 'Common Mistakes,' and 'Recap.' Agent Opus uses these markers to create scene boundaries and apply appropriate visuals. For outlines, list 3 to 7 main points and add a sentence or two under each. The AI will expand those into full scenes with voiceover and motion graphics. In your prompt, specify tone and pacing: 'conversational and slow for beginners' or 'fast-paced for advanced users.' Mention any must-have visuals: 'include the product dashboard screenshot in step 2' or 'show a pie chart when discussing revenue splits.' If you want a specific voiceover style, note it: 'use my cloned voice with a friendly tone' or 'AI narrator, professional and neutral.' For branding, reference uploaded assets: 'use the blue logo in the lower right' or 'apply the course intro animation.' Avoid vague prompts like 'make a video about marketing.' The AI video generator for course creators thrives on specificity. The more context you provide about audience level, lesson goals, and visual preferences, the closer the first output will be to your vision. You can iterate by regenerating with tweaked prompts, but a well-structured initial brief saves time. Finally, review the generated video and note what worked. Over time, you'll develop a prompt template that consistently delivers the lesson style your students expect, turning course production into a repeatable, scalable process.

### How does the AI video generator for course creators handle voiceover consistency across a multi-module course?

Agent Opus solves voiceover consistency through voice cloning and session memory. Upload a 30-second to 2-minute sample of your voice reading any text, and the AI builds a voice model that captures your tone, cadence, and pronunciation quirks. From that point forward, every lesson you generate uses that cloned voice, so students hear the same instructor across all modules. This is critical for course creators who want a personal connection but don't have time to record hours of narration. The cloned voice handles scripts of any length, maintains energy levels, and even adapts emphasis based on punctuation and context in your script. If you write 'This is important!' the AI voice will stress that phrase naturally. For creators who prefer not to use their own voice, Agent Opus offers a library of AI narrators with distinct personalities: professional, conversational, energetic, or calm. Pick one narrator for your entire course, and the system locks that voice profile to your project. This prevents the jarring experience of different voices across lessons. Beyond voice selection, Agent Opus ensures pacing consistency. If your first lesson has a moderate speaking speed, subsequent lessons match that tempo unless you specify otherwise in the prompt. The AI also normalizes audio levels, so volume doesn't fluctuate between modules. For courses with multiple instructors or guest experts, you can upload separate voice samples and tag lessons by speaker. Agent Opus will apply the correct voice model per module while keeping the overall production style cohesive. The AI video generator for course creators treats voiceover as a brand asset, not a one-off recording. This consistency builds trust with students and eliminates the technical overhead of managing audio files, noise reduction, and EQ across dozens of lessons.
