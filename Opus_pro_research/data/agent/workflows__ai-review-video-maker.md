### What are best practices for writing prompts and scripts for AI Review Video Maker?

Effective prompts for AI Review Video Maker balance structure with creative freedom. Start with a clear review framework: intro hook, product overview, feature breakdown, pros and cons, and final recommendation. In your prompt or script, specify which features deserve emphasis and how much time each section should occupy. For example, 'Spend 30 seconds on design and build quality, then 45 seconds on performance benchmarks with on-screen stats.' The more specific your visual cues, the better. Instead of 'show the product,' write 'zoom in on the USB-C ports, then pan across the aluminum chassis.' Agent Opus interprets these directions and applies appropriate motion graphics and camera movements. For voiceover, write conversational scripts that sound natural when spoken. Avoid overly formal language or dense jargon unless your audience expects it. If you're cloning your own voice, script the way you naturally speak, including pauses and emphasis. For AI voices, slightly shorter sentences and clear pronunciation cues help maintain energy. Include explicit callouts for on-screen text: 'Display price: $299' or 'Show comparison chart: Model A vs Model B.' These become dynamic overlays synced to your narration. When using product URLs, provide context in your prompt: 'This is a mid-range wireless earbud competing with AirPods; highlight battery life and noise cancellation.' The AI uses that context to prioritize relevant product details and source appropriate comparison visuals. Finally, specify your target platform and length upfront. A TikTok review needs a faster pace and hook-first structure compared to a YouTube deep dive. Agent Opus adjusts pacing, text size, and visual complexity based on these parameters, so front-load that information in your prompt for best results.
