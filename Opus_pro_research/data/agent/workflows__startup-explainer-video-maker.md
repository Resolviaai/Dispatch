### What are best practices for writing prompts or scripts for a startup explainer video maker?

To get the best results from Agent Opus as a startup explainer video maker, structure your input around clarity, storytelling, and visual cues. Start with a strong hook that states the problem your startup solves. For example, instead of saying 'Our app helps teams collaborate,' write 'Remote teams waste hours in email threads. Our app turns every conversation into an action item.' Agent Opus uses that problem-solution framing to generate opening scenes that grab attention. Next, outline your product's core features or benefits in simple, concrete terms. Avoid jargon and focus on outcomes. If your startup is a fintech tool, say 'Automate invoice reconciliation in seconds' rather than 'Leverage AI-powered financial workflows.' Agent Opus translates specific outcomes into visual metaphors and motion graphics. Include visual direction when it helps. Mention 'show a dashboard screenshot' or 'animate our logo with a tech feel' so Agent Opus knows which assets to prioritize. If you have a script with scene breaks, use clear markers like 'Scene 1: Problem intro' or 'Scene 2: Product demo.' This helps the AI pace the video and apply appropriate transitions. For voiceover tone, specify whether you want energetic, calm, authoritative, or conversational delivery. If you are cloning your own voice, record your sample in the tone you want the final video to match. Keep your total script or brief between 100 and 300 words for a 30 to 90 second explainer, which is the sweet spot for social platforms and pitch decks. Finally, mention your target audience and distribution channel. Saying 'This is for B2B SaaS buyers on LinkedIn' helps Agent Opus choose professional visuals and pacing, while 'This is for TikTok and Gen Z users' will result in faster cuts and trendier motion graphics. The more context you provide, the more tailored and effective your startup explainer video will be.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using a startup explainer video maker like Agent Opus?

Agent Opus is a powerful startup explainer video maker, but understanding its limitations helps you get the best results. First, Agent Opus generates videos from text input, so it cannot create content from vague or incomplete prompts. If you simply write 'Make a video about my startup,' the AI lacks the context to produce a compelling explainer. You need to provide a clear problem statement, product description, or script. The more detail you include, the better the output. Second, while Agent Opus auto-sources web and stock images, it cannot generate custom illustrations or 3D animations from scratch. If your startup requires highly specialized visuals like medical diagrams, architectural renderings, or animated characters, you should upload those assets yourself. Agent Opus will integrate them into the video, but it will not create them. Third, voice cloning requires a clean audio sample of at least 30 seconds. If your sample has background noise, multiple speakers, or poor recording quality, the cloned voice may not sound accurate. Use a quiet environment and a decent microphone for best results. Fourth, Agent Opus is optimized for explainer videos in the 30 to 90 second range. If you need a 10-minute product walkthrough or a feature-length documentary, the tool is not designed for that use case. It excels at short, punchy videos for social media, pitch decks, and landing pages. Fifth, while Agent Opus supports social-ready aspect ratios like 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1, it does not offer frame-by-frame editing or manual timeline control. If you need to adjust individual scene durations or rearrange clips, you would need to regenerate the video with updated prompt instructions rather than editing it directly. Finally, Agent Opus relies on AI to interpret your input, so results can vary based on how you phrase your prompts. If the first video is not quite right, refine your script or add more visual direction and regenerate. The tool learns from your input, so iterating on your prompts improves output quality. Despite these limitations, Agent Opus remains the fastest and most accessible startup explainer video maker for founders and marketers who need professional videos without hiring a production team.
