### What are the best practices for writing prompts and scripts for the Vin Diesel AI video generator?

Effective prompts for the Vin Diesel AI video generator follow the principles of his communication style: direct, powerful, and emotionally resonant. Use short, declarative sentences. The AI voice synthesis performs best with clear, punchy phrasing that allows for dramatic pauses and emphasis. Avoid run-on sentences or complex clauses. Words like breakthrough, legacy, family, loyalty, power, and destiny align with the Vin Diesel brand and trigger the AI to apply appropriate visual metaphors and vocal emphasis. The hero narrative always drives toward a decision point, so end with a direct invitation or challenge.
