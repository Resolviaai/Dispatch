### What are best practices for prompts in a meme video maker to get viral-ready content?

The most effective meme video maker prompts balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by naming the meme format if you're using a known template, like 'Create a Drake meme video where he rejects boring marketing and approves meme marketing.' If you're building an original concept, describe the setup, punchline, and visual style: 'Show a person confidently explaining their strategy, then cut to chaos with the caption THIS IS FINE.' Include timing cues like 'pause for effect' or 'quick cut' to control comedic rhythm. Specify text placement and emphasis: 'Big bold text saying NOBODY, then small text saying absolutely nobody, then the punchline.' Reference trending audio if relevant: 'Use the oh no song for the reveal.' For brand memes, describe how your product or logo fits the joke: 'Our app logo appears as the hero saving the day.' The meme video maker works best when you think like a meme creator, not a scriptwriter. Use internet language, reference viral moments, and focus on the punchline over production details. Agent Opus fills in motion graphics, timing, and visual sourcing automatically. If you're unsure, start with a simple concept and let the AI suggest improvements through iteration. The system learns from millions of viral videos, so it knows what makes memes shareable. Avoid over-explaining the joke or adding unnecessary context. Meme videos thrive on brevity and surprise, so trust the AI to handle pacing and visual emphasis while you focus on the core comedic idea.
