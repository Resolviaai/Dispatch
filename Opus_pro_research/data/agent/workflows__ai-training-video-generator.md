### What are best practices for writing prompts and scripts for AI training video generation?

Effective prompts and scripts for an AI training video generator follow instructional design principles adapted for AI interpretation. Start with clear learning objectives in your prompt, such as 'Create a 3-minute video teaching new hires how to process customer refunds in our CRM system' rather than vague requests like 'make a video about refunds.' Structure scripts with distinct sections: introduction stating what learners will accomplish, body content broken into logical steps or concepts, and conclusion reinforcing key takeaways. Use descriptive scene directions within your script to guide visual selection. Instead of writing 'The dashboard has many features,' specify 'Show the analytics dashboard with the revenue graph highlighted in the top-left corner.' This helps the AI training video generator source or create appropriate visuals. For procedural training, write scripts in second-person instructional voice using action verbs: 'Click the Refund button, then select the reason from the dropdown menu.' This creates clear, directive narration that matches step-by-step visuals. Include timing cues for complex concepts: 'Pause here to emphasize this compliance requirement' signals the system to extend scene duration. Specify when you want text overlays by formatting key terms in brackets or caps: 'The three pillars of our methodology are \[LISTEN\], \[ANALYZE\], and \[SOLVE\].' For technical accuracy, provide exact terminology, product names, and process steps rather than letting the AI paraphrase. If incorporating your brand assets, reference them explicitly: 'Show our company logo in the lower right' or 'Display the product screenshot from the Q4 launch.' When using voice cloning, write scripts in your natural speaking style including conversational phrases and pacing you'd actually use when training someone in person. For global training, write scripts in simple, clear language that translates well and avoid idioms or cultural references that may not localize effectively. The more specific and structured your input, the more precisely the AI training video generator delivers instructional content that matches your training standards and learning outcomes.
