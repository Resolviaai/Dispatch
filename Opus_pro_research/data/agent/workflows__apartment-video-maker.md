### How does an apartment video maker handle different property types and listing details?

Agent Opus processes apartment descriptions with contextual intelligence, adapting video structure to match property type and target audience. When you input a studio apartment listing, the apartment video maker emphasizes space efficiency, natural light, and urban location benefits through tight scene pacing and close-up visual emphasis. For luxury multi-bedroom units, it automatically extends runtime, highlights premium finishes with slower pans, and incorporates amenity footage. You can provide input as a short prompt like 'modern 2BR with city views and gym access' or paste a full MLS description with square footage, appliance details, and neighborhood information. The system parses key selling points and translates them into visual sequences. If you mention 'stainless steel appliances,' the apartment video maker sources relevant stock imagery or uses your uploaded kitchen photos, then adds motion graphics highlighting those features with text overlays or animated callouts. For properties with unique features like rooftop access, pet-friendly policies, or smart home integration, include those details in your prompt and the AI will dedicate scene time proportional to their marketing value. The apartment video maker also adjusts tone based on context clues: luxury language triggers elegant transitions and sophisticated color grading, while 'affordable' or 'student housing' prompts yield energetic pacing and practical feature focus. You maintain full control by refining prompts, but the default intelligence handles 90 percent of structural decisions, ensuring every apartment video aligns with real estate marketing best practices without requiring you to specify shot lists or storyboard sequences.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce high-converting apartment videos?

Effective apartment video maker prompts balance specificity with creative freedom, giving Agent Opus enough detail to generate accurate visuals while letting AI handle cinematic decisions. Start with property fundamentals: location type (urban high-rise, suburban garden complex, historic building conversion), unit configuration (studio, 1BR, 3BR townhome), and standout features (floor-to-ceiling windows, renovated kitchen, in-unit laundry). Then add emotional hooks that connect with your target renter or buyer: 'perfect for young professionals,' 'family-friendly with nearby parks,' 'pet lovers welcome.' These phrases guide tone and pacing. Include 3 to 5 specific visual anchors, like 'granite countertops,' 'walk-in closet,' 'private balcony with sunset views,' or 'modern open floor plan.' The apartment video maker uses these to structure scenes and source complementary imagery. If you have property photos, mention them explicitly: 'Use uploaded kitchen and living room shots, then show neighborhood walkability with stock footage.' For voiceover style, specify 'warm and inviting,' 'professional and informative,' or 'energetic and modern' to shape AI narration tone. Avoid over-prescribing shot sequences; instead of 'start with exterior, then hallway, then kitchen,' say 'highlight the renovated kitchen and spacious living area' and let the apartment video maker determine optimal flow. If targeting specific platforms, note 'optimized for Instagram Reels' or 'YouTube Shorts format' to influence aspect ratio and pacing. For competitive markets, include differentiators: 'no broker fee,' 'move-in specials,' 'flexible lease terms.' The apartment video maker will emphasize these in text overlays or voiceover. Finally, if you're generating multiple videos for similar units, create a base prompt template and swap unit-specific details, ensuring brand consistency across your portfolio while maintaining unique selling points for each apartment video.
