### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI presenter video maker?

Effective prompts for AI presenter video generation start with clarity about your goal. Specify the video type upfront: product demo, explainer, tutorial, announcement, or testimonial. This guides Agent Opus in selecting appropriate avatar energy, visual style, and pacing. Include your target audience in the prompt, such as 'for B2B decision-makers' or 'for beginner creators,' so the AI presenter adjusts tone and complexity. Describe key visual elements you want featured: 'show our app interface,' 'highlight pricing tiers,' or 'include customer logos.' Agent Opus will source or generate those visuals and integrate them naturally into the presenter video. If you have brand assets like logos or product images, mention them explicitly. For voice and avatar, specify preferences: 'use my cloned voice with a professional female avatar' or 'energetic male AI voice with casual avatar.' When writing the script portion of your prompt, use conversational language as if speaking directly to the viewer. Avoid dense paragraphs; short sentences and clear transitions help the AI presenter deliver with natural rhythm. Include stage directions in brackets if you want specific emphasis: 'pause here for effect' or 'gesture toward graphic.' For longer videos, structure your prompt with section headers so Agent Opus can create visual chapters. Mention desired video length as a guideline, though the AI prioritizes content completeness over strict duration. Finally, specify platform and aspect ratio: 'vertical for TikTok' or 'widescreen for YouTube.' The more context you provide about purpose, audience, and desired outcome, the more precisely the AI presenter video maker tailors the final output to your needs.

### What are the limitations of an AI presenter video maker for complex or niche topics?

Agent Opus handles most content types effectively, but understanding its boundaries helps you optimize results. For highly technical subjects with specialized jargon, the AI presenter may mispronounce uncommon terms or acronyms. Mitigate this by including phonetic spellings in parentheses within your script, such as 'Kubernetes (koo-ber-net-eez).' The system will adapt pronunciation accordingly. When discussing niche industries or emerging technologies, provide brief context in your prompt so Agent Opus can source relevant visuals. For example, 'video about quantum computing for enterprise IT leaders' helps the AI select appropriate imagery rather than generic tech stock footage. The AI presenter video maker excels at structured content but may struggle with highly abstract or philosophical topics that lack concrete visual anchors. If your subject is conceptual, include explicit visual direction in your prompt: 'use metaphorical imagery like gears for process' or 'show data visualizations for growth metrics.' For content requiring real-world footage of specific locations, events, or proprietary processes, Agent Opus relies on web-sourced and stock imagery. If you need footage of your actual office, factory, or event, upload those assets beforehand or accept that the AI will use representative visuals. The system does not generate photorealistic custom scenes beyond what's available in stock libraries. Regarding avatar limitations, AI presenters currently handle standard gestures and expressions but may not convey highly nuanced emotions like sarcasm or subtle irony as effectively as a human actor. For content where emotional subtext is critical, consider using your own avatar with voice cloning for more authentic delivery. Finally, while Agent Opus generates social-ready videos, extremely long-form content like 60-minute webinars may be better suited to segmented generation: create multiple shorter presenter videos covering chapters, then combine them in post if needed. The AI presenter video maker is optimized for videos under 15 minutes, where pacing and viewer engagement are easier to maintain. Understanding these edges lets you play to the system's strengths while working around constraints for specialized use cases.
