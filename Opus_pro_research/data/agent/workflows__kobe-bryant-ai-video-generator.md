### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-quality Kobe Bryant tribute videos?

Effective prompts for the Kobe Bryant AI video generator balance specificity with creative direction, giving Agent Opus enough context to make smart visual and narrative choices while leaving room for AI-driven assembly. Avoid overly vague prompts like 'Make a Kobe video' and instead provide narrative structure: 'Start with early career struggles, transition to championship success, end with legacy impact.' If you want the video to include your brand or product, mention it explicitly: 'Connect Kobe's relentless improvement mindset to our productivity app's core values.' The best prompts give Agent Opus a clear creative direction while trusting the AI to handle scene assembly, motion graphics, voiceover pacing, and visual sourcing.
