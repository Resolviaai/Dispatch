### How does AI recap video maker handle different types of events and milestones?

AI recap video maker is built to handle any recap scenario—product launches, conferences, webinars, campaign results, quarterly updates, team milestones, or seasonal highlights. You provide the context and key moments in your prompt or script, and Agent Opus interprets the narrative structure. For a product launch recap, you might describe the announcement, demo highlights, customer reactions, and results. For a conference, you could list keynote themes, speaker quotes, attendee engagement, and takeaways. Agent Opus then assembles scenes that match the emotional arc and pacing of your story. It sources visuals that align with each moment—whether that's your uploaded event photos, product screenshots, or auto-sourced stock imagery that fits the theme. AI motion graphics emphasize key data points, quotes, or milestones with animated text overlays and transitions. The voiceover (your cloned voice or an AI narrator) ties the scenes together with a cohesive narrative. The result is a recap that feels intentionally designed for your specific event type, not a generic template. You can also adjust tone and pacing by tweaking your prompt—use energetic language for a hype recap or measured phrasing for a professional quarterly update. This flexibility makes AI recap video maker ideal for creators who cover diverse events, marketers who need brand-consistent recaps across campaigns, and founders who want to celebrate team wins without hiring a video team.

### What are best practices for writing prompts in AI recap video maker?

The best prompts for AI recap video maker combine structure, specificity, and storytelling. Start with a clear opening: what event or milestone are you recapping, and why does it matter? Then list the key moments or highlights in chronological or thematic order. For example, a product launch recap might include the announcement hook, feature demo, customer testimonial, and results or next steps. A conference recap could cover the opening keynote theme, breakout session highlights, networking moments, and closing takeaway. Be specific about visuals you want—mention uploaded assets like logos, event photos, or product shots, and describe the mood or style (energetic, professional, celebratory). If you have data points (attendance numbers, engagement metrics, revenue milestones), include them so Agent Opus can feature them with motion graphics. Specify voiceover tone: upbeat and fast-paced for social hype, or calm and authoritative for investor updates. Mention your target platform if pacing matters—TikTok recaps are snappier than LinkedIn recaps. You can also provide a script or outline instead of a freeform prompt for tighter control over scene order and narration. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a recap video'—Agent Opus works best when you give it narrative structure and context. Think of your prompt as a creative brief: the more detail you provide about what happened and how you want it told, the more polished and on-brand your recap will be. Test a few variations to find the prompt style that matches your workflow, whether that's bullet points, a short paragraph, or a full script.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of AI recap video maker?

AI recap video maker excels at generating complete recap videos from text, but there are scenarios where you'll need to adjust your approach. First, Agent Opus works best when you provide clear narrative structure. If your prompt is too vague or lacks key moments, the output may feel generic or miss important highlights. Solution: write a detailed brief, script, or outline that lists the story beats you want to cover. Second, while Agent Opus auto-sources stock and web imagery, it can't read your mind about hyper-specific visuals. If you need exact event photos, product shots, or branded graphics, upload them as assets rather than relying solely on auto-sourcing. Third, voice cloning requires a clean audio sample—background noise or poor recording quality will affect the cloned voice. Record your sample in a quiet space with a decent microphone for best results. Fourth, AI recap video maker generates a finished video in one pass; it's not designed for iterative scene-by-scene editing. If you need to tweak individual scenes, adjust your prompt and regenerate rather than expecting a manual editing UI. Fifth, extremely long recaps (10+ minutes) may require breaking your story into multiple shorter videos, as Agent Opus is optimized for social-length content (30 seconds to 3 minutes). Sixth, if your recap involves live-action footage or complex animations that require custom motion design beyond AI motion graphics, you may need to supplement Agent Opus output with additional tools. Finally, while Agent Opus supports multiple languages for voiceover, nuanced cultural or regional storytelling may require extra prompt detail to ensure the tone lands correctly. Understanding these boundaries helps you use AI recap video maker effectively: it's a powerful text-to-video engine for narrative-driven recaps, not a replacement for custom animation studios or live-action production. For 95% of event, campaign, and milestone recaps, it delivers exactly what you need with zero manual editing.
