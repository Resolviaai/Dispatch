### What are the best prompt strategies for generating effective chiropractic education videos with an AI video generator for chiropractors?

Effective prompts for chiropractic video generation balance clinical detail with patient-friendly language and include clear visual cues to guide scene selection. Start with a one-sentence outcome statement, such as 'Explain how poor posture causes neck pain and how chiropractic adjustments restore alignment,' then expand with 3-5 bullet points covering key concepts: causes, symptoms, treatment approach, and expected outcomes. Agent Opus performs best when your input separates ideas into distinct sections, because the system maps each section to a video scene with corresponding visuals and motion graphics. For example, a prompt structured as 'Introduction: Define forward head posture. Problem: Show how it strains cervical muscles. Solution: Demonstrate spinal adjustment technique. Outcome: Highlight pain relief and improved mobility' will generate a four-scene video with logical flow and varied visuals. Include specific visual requests like 'show cervical spine X-ray,' 'animate vertebral realignment,' or 'use stock footage of desk workers with poor posture' to steer the AI toward relevant imagery. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a video about back pain,' which produce generic results; instead, specify the condition, audience, and goal: 'Create a 60-second Instagram Reel explaining how lumbar adjustments reduce sciatica pain for office workers, using simple language and spine diagrams.' If you are working from a blog post or article, paste the full text and add a one-line instruction at the top, such as 'Turn this blog into a 90-second YouTube video for patients considering chiropractic care.' Voice and tone matter: if you want a warm, reassuring delivery, note 'friendly, empathetic tone' in your prompt; for professional referral content, specify 'clinical, authoritative tone.' You can also request specific calls to action, like 'End with: Schedule your free consultation at \[practice name\],' and Agent Opus will include that in the final narration and on-screen text. Regenerate and refine: if the first output is too technical or too simplistic, adjust your prompt's language level and try again. The system learns from specificity, so the more detail you provide about audience, condition, and desired visuals, the better the final video will align with your practice's messaging and patient education goals.
