### What are best practices for prompts when creating timelapse videos with AI?

Effective timelapse prompts for Agent Opus should clearly define the transformation you want to show, the timeframe or stages involved, and the emotional or practical outcome. Start by stating the subject and scope, such as transforming a blank canvas into a finished mural or documenting a startup's first year from idea to launch. Then specify the key stages or milestones you want represented, even if you phrase them conceptually. For example, a product development timelapse might include stages like initial sketches, prototype testing, final design, and market release. The AI uses these markers to structure visual progression and pacing. Include sensory or stylistic guidance to shape the motion graphics and visual tone. Phrases like fast-paced and energetic, smooth and contemplative, or dramatic with bold transitions help the AI choose appropriate zoom speeds, crossfade styles, and visual rhythm. If your timelapse involves real-world assets like logos, product images, or specific locations, mention them explicitly so Agent Opus integrates your brand visuals seamlessly. For voiceover-driven timelapses, provide a script or outline that narrates the transformation, and the AI will synchronize visual changes with your spoken milestones. Avoid vague prompts like make a timelapse video; instead, be specific about what changes and why it matters. If you're working from a blog post or article, ensure it describes a clear before-and-after or multi-stage process, as the AI extracts progression cues from your content structure. You can also specify the intended platform and audience, such as a 30-second Instagram Reel for product launches or a 60-second LinkedIn video for case study storytelling, and Agent Opus will adjust pacing and aspect ratio accordingly. The more context you provide about the transformation's meaning and the audience's expectations, the more intentional and engaging your AI-generated timelapse will be.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating timelapse videos with AI?

Agent Opus excels at creating timelapse videos from text-based inputs, but certain scenarios require thoughtful prompt design to achieve optimal results. The AI generates timelapses by assembling visuals and applying motion graphics, so it works best when your transformation story can be represented through imagery and narration rather than requiring live-action footage of a specific real-world event. For example, if you want a timelapse of your exact office renovation with your actual construction crew, Agent Opus will create a stylized representation using sourced construction imagery and your brand assets, not a camera-captured recording of your specific site. This makes it ideal for conceptual, illustrative, or representative timelapses, but not a replacement for traditional timelapse photography of unique, one-time events. Another consideration is visual specificity. If your timelapse depends on highly niche or proprietary visuals that aren't available in stock libraries or web sources, you'll need to upload those assets directly. For instance, a timelapse showing the evolution of a custom software interface would benefit from screenshots or design mockups you provide, as the AI may not find exact matches through automated sourcing. The motion graphics engine is optimized for smooth, professional transitions, but extremely fast-paced timelapses with dozens of micro-stages may require simplification in your prompt to ensure visual clarity. If you're compressing a year-long process into 30 seconds, focus on 5 to 7 key milestones rather than trying to represent every incremental change, as too many transitions can overwhelm viewers. Voiceover synchronization works best when your script or narration aligns naturally with visual stages. If your narration is highly detailed or technical, consider structuring it to match clear visual beats, so the AI can time transitions effectively. For avatar-led timelapses, the AI avatar performs best in presenter or explainer roles rather than as a character within the timelapse action itself. Finally, while Agent Opus outputs social-ready videos, extremely long-form timelapses beyond 90 seconds may require breaking the story into multiple shorter videos or focusing on a condensed narrative arc. Understanding these boundaries helps you craft prompts that leverage the AI's strengths, delivering polished, engaging timelapse videos that achieve your storytelling and marketing goals.
