### What are best practices for writing prompts in a science explainer video maker?

Effective prompts for a science explainer video maker start with a clear learning objective and a defined audience. Begin by stating the concept you want to explain and the level of detail required. For instance, 'Create a 60-second video explaining photosynthesis for high school students' gives Agent Opus a scope, a time constraint, and an audience context. If you are working from a script, structure it with an introduction that poses a question or problem, a body that walks through the mechanism or process step by step, and a conclusion that reinforces the key takeaway. Use concrete language and avoid jargon unless you define it within the script. For example, instead of saying 'eukaryotic transcription factors bind to promoter regions,' you might say 'special proteins latch onto DNA to start copying a gene.' This clarity helps the AI select visuals that match your narrative. When you want specific visuals, call them out: 'Show a diagram of the water cycle with evaporation, condensation, and precipitation labeled' or 'Animate a neuron firing an action potential.' Agent Opus will source or generate those elements. If you are converting a blog post or article, choose content that already has a logical flow and clear section headers. The system uses those structural cues to divide the video into scenes. For voice and tone, specify whether you want a formal academic narration or a conversational style. You can also indicate pacing: 'Pause after each step to let the concept sink in' or 'Keep it fast-paced for social media.' Finally, mention any branding or visual identity requirements, such as 'Include our lab logo in the corner' or 'Use blue and white color scheme.' These details ensure your science explainer video aligns with your channel or course aesthetic while maintaining educational rigor.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of a science explainer video maker for highly specialized topics?

Agent Opus excels at generating science explainer videos for a wide range of topics, but there are edge cases where additional input or iteration may be required. Highly specialized or cutting-edge research topics, such as novel quantum computing algorithms or newly published medical breakthroughs, may require you to supply detailed scripts or custom visuals because the AI relies on existing knowledge and stock resources. If your topic involves proprietary data, unpublished research, or niche terminology not widely documented, provide a comprehensive script with definitions and visual references. For example, if you are explaining a new cancer therapy mechanism, include diagrams of the molecular pathway or upload images of your lab results. Agent Opus will integrate these into the video, but it cannot invent visuals for concepts it has never encountered. Another edge case is when you need precise mathematical notation, chemical formulas, or code snippets displayed on screen. While Agent Opus can generate motion graphics and text overlays, complex equations may require you to specify exact formatting in your prompt or provide a reference image. For instance, 'Display the Schrödinger equation in LaTeX format' or 'Show the chemical structure of benzene with bond angles labeled.' The system will do its best to render these accurately, but reviewing the output and iterating on your prompt ensures precision. Additionally, if your science explainer video needs to cite specific studies, show data tables, or include peer-reviewed references, you should include those details in your script. Agent Opus will incorporate them as text overlays or voiceover mentions, but it does not auto-generate citations. Finally, while Agent Opus handles most aspect ratios and social platforms, extremely long-form content like 20-minute lectures may be better broken into multiple shorter videos, each focused on a single concept. This approach also improves viewer retention and makes your content more shareable. By understanding these limitations and providing clear, detailed input, you can use Agent Opus to produce high-quality science explainer videos even for advanced or specialized topics.
