These three men didn't just play the market, they wrote the rules.

So who are the greatest investors of all time?

First up, Benjamin Graham, the "father of value investing."

His masterpiece, "The Intelligent Investor," is still considered the bible for investors.

His edge was simple but revolutionary: buy stocks for less than their intrinsic value, creating a margin of safety.

This discipline of buying assets worth a dollar for fifty cents made him a legend

Next, the notorious Jesse Livermore, the "Great Bear of Wall Street."

A pioneer of day trading, Livermore's signature achievement was making approximately one hundred million dollars by shorting the market during the 1929 crash.

He mastered the art of speculation, reading market psychology and price patterns when others couldn't.

While his career had incredible highs and lows, his legendary trades are still studied today.

Finally, the Oracle of Omaha himself, Warren Buffett.

As the chairman and CEO of Berkshire Hathaway, he built one of the world's largest companies.

Buffett took Graham's value investing and evolved it, focusing on buying wonderful companies at a fair price and holding them for the long term.

His unparalleled track record and folksy wisdom have made him arguably the most successful investor in history.
