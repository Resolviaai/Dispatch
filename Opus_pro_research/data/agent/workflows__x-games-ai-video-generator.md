### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate compelling X Games-style videos?

Effective prompts for the X Games AI video generator balance creative vision with specific direction. Start with the core action. Instead of 'make a skateboarding video,' write 'a 60-second skateboarding video showcasing street tricks, focusing on kickflips and rail grinds in urban settings.' The specificity helps Agent Opus select appropriate footage and pacing. Include the emotional tone you want. Phrases like 'high-energy competition atmosphere' or 'laid-back lifestyle vibe' guide the music selection, editing rhythm, and voiceover style. For competition content, mention the event context: 'X Games Big Air finals with crowd reactions and athlete celebrations' produces different results than 'solo practice session in an empty skate park.' Describe the narrative arc if you want more than a highlight reel. 'Start with an athlete preparing gear, build to increasingly difficult tricks, climax with a never-before-landed move' gives Agent Opus a story structure to follow. The generator sequences clips to match that progression and times the voiceover to emphasize key moments. Specify visual elements that matter to your brand. 'Include close-ups of protective gear and equipment' or 'show athlete faces and reactions between tricks' ensures those elements appear. For product launches, write 'feature the new snowboard model in action shots, with logo visible' and Agent Opus prioritizes those visuals. Length matters. A 15-second Instagram Reel needs a different approach than a 90-second YouTube Short. Shorter videos work best with a single trick or moment. Longer formats allow for setup, multiple attempts, and payoff. Tell Agent Opus the platform in your prompt for optimized pacing. Voice and avatar choices affect tone significantly. 'Energetic male voiceover with hype commentary' creates a different feel than 'calm female narration explaining technique.' If you want an on-camera host, specify 'AI avatar introducing each segment' or 'athlete avatar providing first-person perspective.' Finally, reference specific X Games aesthetics when relevant. 'Use slow-motion for aerial tricks' or 'add speed ramps during approach and landing' tells the motion graphics system exactly what effects to apply and when.
