### How does a university video maker handle different content types like admissions videos, campus tours, and event recaps?

Agent Opus adapts its video generation to the specific content type you provide. For admissions videos, you might input a brief describing your program's unique value, target student demographics, and key differentiators. Agent Opus interprets that brief and assembles scenes that highlight academic rigor, campus facilities, student success stories, and career outcomes, sourcing relevant imagery from stock libraries or your uploaded campus photos. For campus tours, you can paste a script that walks through residence halls, libraries, athletic facilities, and student centers. Agent Opus sequences those locations visually, adds motion graphics to emphasize key features like new lab equipment or renovated dorms, and applies a voiceover that guides prospective students through the experience. Event recaps work similarly: describe the event (homecoming, commencement, guest lecture series), and Agent Opus generates a highlight reel with dynamic pacing, upbeat music, and text overlays that capture the energy and community spirit. The system recognizes context clues in your input (words like 'tour,' 'admissions,' 'event,' 'program overview') and adjusts scene selection, pacing, and visual style accordingly. You can also specify tone in your prompt (inspirational, informative, celebratory) to fine-tune the output. Because Agent Opus supports prompts, scripts, outlines, and even blog URLs, you have flexibility in how you feed content. A blog post about a new engineering program becomes a promotional video; a bullet-point outline of campus amenities becomes a virtual tour. The university video maker handles all content types within a single workflow, so your team doesn't need separate tools or processes for different video projects.

### What are best practices for prompts when using a university video maker for admissions and marketing content?

Effective prompts for a university video maker balance clarity, context, and creative direction. Start by defining the video's purpose and audience. For example, 'Create a 60-second admissions video for prospective undergraduate students highlighting our business school's experiential learning, global partnerships, and career placement rate.' That single sentence tells Agent Opus the length, audience, key themes, and tone. Next, include specific details that make your university unique. Instead of 'show campus,' say 'feature our new LEED-certified science building, student-run coffee shop, and rooftop garden.' Agent Opus will prioritize those elements in scene selection and sourcing. If you have brand guidelines, mention them: 'Use our primary blue and gold color palette, include our logo in the lower third, and maintain an optimistic, forward-looking tone.' For voiceover, specify whether you want a formal, authoritative voice (ideal for program overviews) or a warm, conversational voice (better for student testimonials). If you're cloning a real voice, note that: 'Use Dr. Smith's voice clone for the introduction.' When describing pacing, use terms like 'fast-paced for social media,' 'slow and cinematic for website hero video,' or 'energetic for event recap.' If you're working from a script, paste the full text and add a brief prompt at the top: 'Turn this campus tour script into a 90-second video with upbeat music and quick cuts between locations.' For blog-to-video projects, provide the URL and specify the angle: 'Convert this blog post about our study abroad programs into a 45-second Instagram Reel emphasizing student experiences and global destinations.' Avoid vague prompts like 'make a good video about our university.' The more specific you are about audience, purpose, tone, visuals, and length, the more precisely Agent Opus can generate a university video that aligns with your goals. Experiment with different prompt styles (brief vs detailed, script vs outline) to find what works best for your team's workflow.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using a university video maker for complex storytelling or highly specific campus footage?

While Agent Opus excels at generating polished university videos from text, certain scenarios require workarounds or additional planning. First, highly specific campus footage. If your prompt requires a particular angle of your new library at sunset or a specific shot of your mascot at a recent game, Agent Opus sources imagery from stock libraries and the web, which may not include that exact moment. The solution: upload your own photos and video clips. Agent Opus integrates user-provided assets, so if you have a library of campus footage, drone shots, or event photography, include those files when generating your video. Agent Opus will prioritize your uploads over stock imagery, ensuring the final video reflects your actual campus. Second, complex narrative structures. Agent Opus handles straightforward storytelling (introduce problem, present solution, call to action) very well, but if your video requires a non-linear narrative, flashbacks, or intricate scene transitions, you may need to break the project into multiple prompts or provide a highly detailed script with explicit scene-by-scene instructions. For example, instead of 'create a video about our history,' write a script with timestamps: 'Scene 1 (0-10 seconds): founding in 1885, sepia-toned archival photos. Scene 2 (10-25 seconds): growth through the 20th century, color footage of campus expansions. Scene 3 (25-40 seconds): present day, vibrant student life and modern facilities.' The more granular your input, the more control you have over pacing and structure. Third, real-time or live footage. Agent Opus generates videos from text and static assets, so it cannot incorporate live-streamed events or real-time footage. If you need to include live commencement speeches or real-time sports highlights, record that footage separately and provide it as an uploaded asset. Fourth, highly technical or niche academic content. If your video explains advanced research (quantum computing, biomedical engineering breakthroughs), ensure your script or prompt includes clear, accessible language. Agent Opus generates visuals based on the text you provide, so overly technical jargon without context may result in generic imagery. Simplify complex concepts or include visual metaphors in your prompt: 'Use animated DNA strands to illustrate gene editing research.' Fifth, multilingual videos with specific cultural nuances. Agent Opus supports multiple languages and accents, but if your video targets international students and requires culturally specific imagery (traditional attire, regional landmarks, local festivals), specify those details in your prompt and upload relevant photos. Finally, legal and compliance considerations. If your university has strict guidelines about student privacy, photo releases, or accessibility (closed captions, audio descriptions), ensure your uploaded assets comply with those policies. Agent Opus generates videos based on the content you provide, so your team remains responsible for legal and ethical compliance. For most university video projects (admissions, campus tours, event recaps, program promotions), Agent Opus delivers professional results instantly. For edge cases requiring highly specific footage, complex narratives, or real-time content, plan your inputs carefully and leverage the ability to upload custom assets.
