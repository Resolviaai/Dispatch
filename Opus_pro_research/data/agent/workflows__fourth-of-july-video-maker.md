### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate effective Fourth of July videos?

Effective Fourth of July video prompts balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by stating your core message clearly: 'Announce our Independence Day weekend hours' or 'Promote July 4th catering packages.' Then add contextual details that guide the AI's visual choices. Mention your audience like 'families planning backyard BBQs' or 'small business owners celebrating with their teams' so Agent Opus selects appropriate imagery and tone. Include desired emotional beats such as 'nostalgic and warm' or 'exciting and high-energy' to influence motion graphics pacing and music selection. Specify any must-have visual elements: 'show our new patio furniture line' or 'feature our restaurant's outdoor seating' ensures your products appear in patriotic contexts. If you have brand guidelines, mention color preferences beyond the standard red-white-blue, like 'incorporate our teal brand accent' so the AI balances patriotic themes with your identity. Describe the customer journey you want to portray: 'family arriving at our store, browsing grills, making a purchase' gives Agent Opus a narrative arc to visualize. For promotional content, state your offer explicitly: '40% off all American-made products, valid July 1-7' so the AI emphasizes those details visually and verbally. Avoid overly prescriptive shot-by-shot directions; instead, trust the AI to handle scene composition while you focus on messaging and outcomes. Good prompts run 50 to 200 words and answer: What's the goal? Who's the audience? What's the offer or message? What mood fits Independence Day for your brand? The system excels when you provide strategic direction and let it execute the tactical video production details.

Agent Opus handles most Fourth of July video scenarios effectively, but understanding its boundaries helps you plan better content.
