### What are best practices for prompts when using an internal communication video maker for training or policy videos?

Effective prompts for an internal communication video maker balance clarity, structure, and context. Start with the video's purpose and audience: 'Create a 90-second onboarding video for new customer support hires explaining our ticketing system' gives Agent Opus clear intent. For training videos, outline key learning points or steps. Instead of a vague prompt like 'Make a video about data security,' write 'Generate a training video covering three topics: password best practices, recognizing phishing emails, and reporting security incidents. Use motion graphics to illustrate each threat type.' This structure helps the internal communication video maker organize scenes logically and pace content for comprehension. If you have a full script, paste it directly. Agent Opus will parse the script, identify natural scene breaks, and generate visuals that match each section. For policy updates or announcements, include the 'why' and 'what's next': 'Announce our new hybrid work policy. Explain the three-day in-office requirement, highlight flexibility benefits, and direct employees to the HR portal for details.' This gives the AI enough context to create persuasive, informative content rather than just reading bullet points. Specify tone and style: 'friendly and conversational' for team updates, 'formal and authoritative' for compliance training. Mention any required branding: 'Include our logo and use our office background.' If you want a specific visual approach, describe it: 'Use animated icons to represent each department' or 'Show real employee photos in the intro.' For longer content, break your prompt into sections or provide an outline. Agent Opus handles multi-chapter videos by treating each section as a distinct scene cluster. Finally, if you're generating a series of related videos like a training curriculum, maintain consistent language and structure across prompts so the internal communication video maker produces a cohesive series. The more context and structure you provide, the more polished and targeted your output will be.

### How does this internal communication video maker handle sensitive or confidential information in corporate videos?

Agent Opus processes your input to generate video content, but you control what information you include in prompts and scripts. For sensitive internal communications, follow these best practices. First, avoid including confidential data like employee personal details, financial specifics, or proprietary processes directly in your prompt unless necessary for the video's purpose. Instead, use placeholders or general descriptions: 'Explain the new performance review process' rather than pasting actual employee review data. The internal communication video maker generates visuals and narration based on your input, so if you provide only high-level policy descriptions, the output will reflect that appropriate level of detail. Second, if your video requires specific data points like metrics or names, include them in your script, but review the generated video before sharing. Agent Opus does not store or reuse your prompts for other users, so your content remains isolated to your account. Third, for highly confidential topics, generate the video using general language, then add sensitive details manually in a follow-up step outside the platform if your workflow requires it, though Agent Opus delivers publish-ready output that typically needs no editing. Fourth, use the internal communication video maker's brand asset feature to control visual content. Upload only approved, non-confidential images and logos. Agent Opus will supplement with auto-sourced stock and web images that match your topic, but these are generic and publicly available, not proprietary. Fifth, if your organization has compliance requirements around video content, treat Agent Opus output like any other internal communication: route it through your standard review and approval process before distribution. The tool accelerates production, but you retain full editorial control. Finally, for voice cloning, use only voices you have permission to clone, and ensure any avatars or presenter images are authorized for internal use. Agent Opus gives you the flexibility to create compliant, professional internal communication videos while respecting your organization's data security and privacy policies.
