### What are best practices for prompts when using an instagram ad video maker?

Effective prompts for instagram ad video maker workflows balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by defining your target audience and their pain point in one sentence: 'busy parents who need quick meal solutions' or 'freelancers struggling with invoicing.' This helps the AI choose relevant visuals and voiceover tone. Next, state your product and its core benefit: 'our meal kit delivers pre-prepped ingredients in 20 minutes' or 'our invoicing app automates payment reminders.' Include your desired call-to-action: 'shop now,' 'download free,' or 'claim 20% off.' If you have a specific hook or opening line, include it: 'start with a question: Are you tired of spending hours on dinner?' Agent Opus will use that as the first scene. Mention any visual assets you want featured: 'use our product shot of the red blender' or 'include our logo in the top-right corner.' If you have a preferred voiceover style, note it: 'upbeat female voice' or 'calm, authoritative male voice.' Avoid overly long prompts—150 to 250 words is the sweet spot. The AI interprets natural language, so write as if briefing a video producer. For instagram ad video maker projects, always specify the platform and format: 'vertical video for Instagram Stories' or 'square 1:1 for Instagram feed.' This ensures the AI optimizes text size, scene pacing, and visual hierarchy for mobile viewing. If you want to test multiple angles, create separate prompts with different hooks: one focusing on speed, another on cost savings, a third on social proof. Agent Opus will generate distinct videos from each, letting you A/B test creative without rewriting scripts or re-editing footage.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of using an instagram ad video maker like Agent Opus?

Agent Opus excels at generating polished, publish-ready Instagram ads from text, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, the AI cannot create original 3D product renders or custom illustrations from scratch—if your ad concept requires a photorealistic 3D animation of a product that does not exist yet, you will need to provide reference images or use stock footage as a starting point. Agent Opus can composite and animate existing images with motion graphics, but it does not replace a 3D modeling pipeline. Second, highly niche or technical products may require more detailed prompts. If you are selling enterprise SaaS with complex workflows, a two-sentence prompt may not capture enough context. In these cases, use the script or outline input to specify exactly which features to highlight and in what order. Third, the AI optimizes for Instagram's mobile-first viewing environment, so if you need a landscape video for YouTube pre-roll or TV spots, you will want to specify that in your prompt or regenerate with different aspect ratio settings. Fourth, voiceover timing is automated based on script length and scene pacing—if you need a specific pause or emphasis on a word, include that direction in your script: 'pause for two seconds after the question' or 'emphasize the word free.' Fifth, while Agent Opus auto-sources royalty-free stock footage and web images, it cannot access proprietary databases or licensed content you have not uploaded. If your brand has exclusive product photography or customer testimonial videos, upload those to your workspace before generating. Sixth, the instagram ad video maker workflow is optimized for ads under 60 seconds—longer formats like explainer videos or tutorials are supported, but Instagram ad performance peaks at 15 to 30 seconds, so the AI defaults to concise, high-impact storytelling. Finally, Agent Opus does not include manual editing tools—if you want to tweak individual frames, adjust scene timing, or add custom transitions, you will need to regenerate with a refined prompt rather than drag-and-drop edits. This trade-off is intentional: the system prioritizes speed and volume over granular control, making it ideal for marketers who need to test many creative concepts quickly rather than perfect a single video over days.
