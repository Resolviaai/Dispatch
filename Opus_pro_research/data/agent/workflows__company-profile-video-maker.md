### How does a company profile video maker like Agent Opus handle different input formats?

Agent Opus accepts four primary input types for company profile video generation: a short text prompt describing your company and key messages, a full script with scene-by-scene narration, a bullet-point outline of your brand story, or a direct URL to your About page or blog post. When you provide a prompt—such as 'Create a two-minute company profile video highlighting our mission to democratize AI, our team of 50 engineers, and our three flagship products'—Agent Opus interprets the intent, structures a narrative arc, and generates scenes that introduce your brand, showcase milestones, and close with a call to action. If you paste a script, the AI respects your exact wording and paces scenes to match your narration beats. For outlines, Agent Opus expands each bullet into a full scene with supporting visuals and motion graphics. When you input a URL, the system parses the page content, extracts key facts and messaging, and assembles a video that mirrors your written brand story. This flexibility means you can start from wherever you are in your content workflow—whether you have polished copy or just a rough idea—and still get a professional company profile video. The AI adapts scene length, visual density, and pacing based on input length and complexity, ensuring that a 30-second elevator pitch and a five-minute deep-dive both feel cohesive and engaging.

### What are best practices for prompts when using a company profile video maker?

To get the best results from a company profile video maker like Agent Opus, structure your prompt around three pillars: who you are, what you do, and why it matters. Start with a one-sentence company description—'We're a B2B SaaS platform that automates invoice processing for mid-market finance teams.' Then list two to four key messages or proof points: number of customers, years in business, unique technology, awards, or team credentials. Include any specific visuals you want featured—'Show our dashboard UI, our office in Austin, and our CEO headshot.' Specify tone and audience: 'Professional tone for enterprise buyers' or 'Energetic and founder-focused for Series A investors.' If you have a desired video length, mention it: 'Create a 90-second company profile video.' The more concrete your prompt, the more precisely Agent Opus can tailor scene composition, pacing, and visual emphasis. Avoid vague requests like 'Make a cool video about our company'; instead, say 'Highlight our patented AI engine, our 10,000-user milestone, and our commitment to data privacy, ending with a demo CTA.' If you're creating multiple versions—one for recruitment, one for sales, one for investors—write separate prompts that emphasize different value propositions. Agent Opus will adjust narrative focus, visual style, and voiceover tone accordingly. You can also reference competitor videos or style benchmarks: 'Similar pacing to a Slack brand video, but with more data visualizations.' This gives the AI a creative anchor while keeping your unique brand story front and center.

### How does a company profile video maker handle voiceover and avatar options?

Agent Opus offers two voiceover paths for company profile videos: AI-generated voices or user voice cloning. AI voices span multiple languages, accents, and tones—professional, warm, authoritative, conversational—so you can match the voice to your brand personality and target audience. If you're creating a corporate overview for enterprise buyers, choose a calm, confident narrator. For a startup pitch video, pick an energetic, founder-style voice. This is invaluable for company profile videos where authenticity and personal connection matter—investors and recruits respond more strongly to a founder's real voice than a generic narrator. On the avatar front, you can add an AI-generated presenter who delivers your script on-camera, or upload video footage of a real team member and Agent Opus syncs their lip movements to the narration. Both options integrate seamlessly with motion graphics, product shots, and B-roll, so your company profile video feels cohesive whether it's avatar-led or purely visual. You can also mix formats: open with an AI avatar introduction, transition to product demos and motion graphics, and close with a real team member CTA.
