### How does AI Google Ads Video Maker handle different input types like prompts versus product URLs?

AI Google Ads Video Maker accepts four input formats, each optimized for different workflows. If you provide a short prompt (e.g., 'Create a 15-second ad for eco-friendly water bottles highlighting durability and sustainability'), Agent Opus interprets your intent, generates a script, selects relevant visuals, and assembles scenes with motion graphics that emphasize your key selling points. For longer scripts, the system parses your exact wording, matches voiceover pacing to your narrative structure, and choreographs visuals to reinforce each claim or benefit. When you paste an outline (bullet points or numbered steps), Agent Opus expands each point into a full scene, ensuring logical flow and visual variety across the ad. The most powerful option is a product URL or blog post link: Agent Opus scrapes your landing page, extracts product features, benefits, pricing, and imagery, then auto-generates a script that mirrors your brand voice and converts page content into a video narrative. Regardless of input type, the output is always a complete, publish-ready Google Ads video with synchronized voiceover, motion graphics, and branded visuals. Best practice: for maximum control, start with a detailed script; for speed, use a URL and let AI handle scripting and scene selection. You can always regenerate with tweaked prompts to refine tone, pacing, or visual emphasis without manual editing.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate high-converting Google Ads videos?

Effective prompts for AI Google Ads Video Maker balance clarity, specificity, and strategic intent. Start by defining your core offer and unique value proposition in one sentence (e.g., 'Promote our AI-powered project management tool that cuts meeting time by 50% for remote teams'). Next, specify your target audience and their pain point ('targeting startup founders overwhelmed by Slack chaos'). Include your desired call-to-action ('end with a 7-day free trial offer and signup link'). For visual direction, mention key brand elements ('use our teal and white color scheme, include our logo in the lower third') and any must-have product shots ('show the dashboard interface and mobile app'). Tone matters: specify 'energetic and aspirational' versus 'calm and authoritative' to guide voiceover style and pacing. If you have a specific Google Ads format in mind (e.g., 15-second bumper ad, 30-second skippable), state the duration upfront so Agent Opus optimizes scene count and pacing. Advanced tip: reference competitor angles you want to counter ('emphasize ease of use, unlike complex tools like Asana') or seasonal hooks ('highlight back-to-school productivity for September launch'). Avoid vague prompts like 'make a cool ad'—the more context you provide about audience, offer, objections, and desired emotion, the more precisely AI Google Ads Video Maker tailors scene selection, motion graphics intensity, and voiceover cadence to your campaign goals. After your first generation, refine by adding constraints ('shorten intro to 3 seconds' or 'add customer testimonial quote in text overlay') and regenerate instantly.

### How does AI Google Ads Video Maker optimize videos for Google's technical specs and best practices?

AI Google Ads Video Maker is purpose-built for Google Ads compliance and performance. Every generated video automatically adheres to Google's technical requirements: correct aspect ratios (16:9 for in-stream and discovery ads, 1:1 for feed placements, 9:16 for Shorts and vertical video campaigns), file size limits (under 1GB), and codec standards (H.264 video, AAC audio). But technical compliance is just the baseline. Agent Opus also applies Google's creative best practices to maximize ad performance. For skippable in-stream ads, the system front-loads your value proposition in the first five seconds—the critical window before viewers can skip—using bold text overlays, dynamic motion graphics, and immediate product visibility. For bumper ads (six seconds non-skippable), Agent Opus compresses your message into a single, punchy concept with rapid scene transitions and a clear brand recall moment. The AI analyzes Google's own guidance on attention metrics: it places logos in the lower third or corner to avoid obscuring key visuals, uses high-contrast text for mobile readability, and times voiceover pacing to match the 150-180 words-per-minute sweet spot that balances comprehension and urgency. Motion graphics are calibrated to Google's recommendation of 'movement in every frame'—static slides kill performance, so Agent Opus ensures continuous visual interest through animated text, product rotations, and scene transitions. For Performance Max campaigns, where Google's algorithm auto-optimizes creative combinations, Agent Opus generates multiple asset variations (different headlines, CTAs, visual hooks) that give the algorithm more to work with. Export is seamless: videos arrive in MP4 format with embedded metadata (title, description fields pre-filled) ready for direct upload to Google Ads Manager. No re-encoding. No format errors. No rejected uploads due to spec violations. Advanced users can specify campaign types in their prompts ('create a 15-second bumper ad' or 'generate a 30-second skippable in-stream ad') and Agent Opus tailors pacing, CTA placement, and scene density accordingly. The result: videos that not only meet Google's rules but are engineered to win auctions and drive conversions from the first impression.
