## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the reaction video generator work end to end?

You describe what you want — a topic, a mood, a script outline, or just a vibe — and Agent Opus assembles a full reaction video from it. The agent handles every part of production: scene composition, B-roll selection, motion graphics, captions, voiceover, beat-synced transitions, and platform-specific resizing. The signature look — split-screen layouts, reaction face inserts, on-screen text annotations, and pacing tuned to viewer surprise — is baked into the generation by default, so the output reads as on-aesthetic from the first frame without you having to art-direct every shot. You can override or customize any element in the prompt: change the color palette, adjust pacing, swap the music style, or specify on-screen text. The system runs frequency analysis on any uploaded audio to time visual intensity to the music, and pacing analysis on uploaded scripts to match scene cuts to natural breaks. Most projects go from idea to finished export in under five minutes, with three aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) rendered simultaneously.

### What prompts produce the best reaction video results?

Effective reaction video prompts combine three things: the topic, the mood, and the specific visual language you want featured. Lead with what the video is about (a recipe, a tour, a tutorial, a tribute), name the emotional tone (energetic, contemplative, intimate, playful), and reference concrete visual elements ("split-screen layouts," "soft natural light," "hand-lettered captions"). The more specific the visual references, the more precisely the first generation lands. If your content has clear sections — intro, main, outro, or chapter breaks — describe how visuals should shift between them so the agent paces transitions correctly. Avoid vague phrases like "make it cool" or "something interesting"; the model treats those as low-signal and falls back to generic styling. You can also reference real creators, films, or moodboards as anchors and the agent interprets the aesthetic without copying directly. Iterate by regenerating with refinements — usually two or three passes get you to a final cut.

The system also accepts your own reference video clips, photos, or product shots and weaves them into the generated sequences so the final cut blends real and AI-generated content seamlessly. For brands, the agent handles standard creator-economy needs (subscribe overlays, end-card CTAs, lower thirds) and platform-specific formatting (Reels stickers, YouTube Shorts captions, LinkedIn thumbnail framing) automatically.

Short-form vertical formats on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts are the strongest performers — that's where commentary creators, reaction communities, and meme-account makers spend their time and where algorithmic discovery is highest. Agent Opus optimizes for these platforms by default with hook-forward 15-to-60-second cuts, captions burned in, and pacing tuned to mobile attention spans.
