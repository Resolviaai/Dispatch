### What are best practices for writing prompts for an investor update video maker?

The best prompts for an investor update video maker are structured, specific, and narrative-driven. Start by outlining the key sections of your update: opening summary, metrics and milestones, challenges or pivots, next steps, and closing call to action. For each section, write one to three sentences that describe what you want to communicate. For example, 'Open with our mission statement and a quick recap of Q4 goals. Then highlight three key metrics: revenue growth, customer retention, and product launches. Follow with a brief discussion of supply chain challenges and how we addressed them. Close with our Q1 roadmap and a thank-you to investors.' This structure gives Agent Opus a clear narrative arc to follow. Be explicit about tone and pacing. If you want a confident, optimistic tone, say so. If you need a slower pace for complex data, mention that. Include specific numbers, dates, and names so the platform can emphasize them visually. For example, 'Emphasize that we hit 10,000 active users in December' will trigger motion graphics that highlight that milestone. If you have branded assets like logos, product screenshots, or team photos, reference them in your prompt or upload them directly. Agent Opus will integrate those visuals into the video. Avoid vague language like 'make it engaging' or 'add some charts.' Instead, say 'use animated bar charts to show revenue growth' or 'include a product demo screenshot when discussing the new feature launch.' The more detail you provide, the more tailored the output. Finally, specify your audience and distribution channel. If this video is for a private investor email, you might want a longer, more detailed format. If it's for LinkedIn, you might want a punchy 60-second summary. Agent Opus adapts scene length, pacing, and aspect ratio based on these cues, so including them in your prompt ensures the final video matches your distribution strategy.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of using an investor update video maker?

While Agent Opus is a powerful investor update video maker, there are some scenarios where you'll need to provide extra guidance or adjust your expectations. First, highly technical or niche financial concepts may require more detailed prompts. If your update includes complex terms like 'diluted EPS' or 'convertible note tranches,' the platform will do its best to source relevant visuals, but you may need to specify exactly what kind of chart or diagram you want. For example, instead of saying 'explain our cap table,' you might say 'show a pie chart breaking down equity ownership by investor group.' This level of detail ensures the AI generates the right visual metaphor. Second, Agent Opus auto-sources imagery from stock libraries and the web, which means it may occasionally select visuals that are generic or not perfectly aligned with your brand aesthetic. If you have specific product shots, team photos, or proprietary charts you want to include, upload them as part of your brief. The platform will prioritize your assets over auto-sourced content. Third, while the AI motion graphics are sophisticated, they are not custom animation. If you need highly stylized 3D renders, hand-drawn illustrations, or bespoke infographics, you may need to create those externally and upload them for Agent Opus to incorporate. The platform excels at assembling and animating existing assets, but it doesn't replace a dedicated motion design team for one-of-a-kind creative work. Fourth, voice cloning requires a clean audio sample. If your recording has background noise, echo, or inconsistent volume, the cloned voice may not sound as natural. Record your sample in a quiet space with a decent microphone for best results. Fifth, Agent Opus generates videos based on the information you provide, so if your script is vague or lacks structure, the output may feel disjointed. The platform can't infer details you haven't shared. If you want a specific milestone highlighted, mention it explicitly. Finally, while Agent Opus outputs social-ready videos, it doesn't handle distribution or analytics. You'll need to upload the final video to your investor portal, email platform, or social channels manually. The platform focuses on generation, not publishing. Understanding these limitations helps you get the most out of an investor update video maker.
