## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does blog to video handle long articles versus short blog posts?

Blog to video intelligently adapts to article length and structure. For long-form content like 2,000-word guides or tutorials, Agent Opus identifies key sections and creates a condensed video that captures the main points, typically running 60 to 90 seconds for social platforms. You can guide this process in your prompt by specifying which sections to emphasize or requesting a specific video length. For shorter blog posts under 500 words, the system generates tighter videos around 30 to 45 seconds, perfect for quick tips or announcements. The AI analyzes your article's heading structure, paragraph breaks, and content hierarchy to determine natural scene divisions. If your blog has clear H2 or H3 subheadings, Agent Opus uses those as scene markers. For articles without strong structure, it identifies topic shifts and key points automatically. You can also paste just an excerpt or specific paragraphs if you want to focus the video on one section of a longer piece. The blog to video process preserves your article's core message while adapting pacing and visual density for video consumption. Longer articles get more dynamic scene transitions and varied visuals to maintain engagement, while shorter posts receive focused, punchy treatment that drives a single call to action.

### What are best practices for prompts when converting blog posts to video?

Effective blog to video prompts combine article context with visual direction and audience intent. Start by specifying your target platform and audience, such as 'Create a 60-second TikTok video for small business owners' or 'Make a LinkedIn video for B2B marketers.' This helps Agent Opus choose appropriate pacing, tone, and visual style. Next, highlight which parts of your blog matter most. If your article covers five tips but you want to focus on three, say 'Focus on tips 2, 4, and 5 about email automation.' If your blog includes data or statistics, mention 'Emphasize the 73 percent conversion stat with on-screen text.' For visual direction, describe the mood and style you want. Phrases like 'modern tech aesthetic with blue and white colors' or 'warm, friendly vibe with lifestyle imagery' guide the AI's visual sourcing and motion graphics choices. Specify voiceover preferences clearly. 'Use my cloned voice with an energetic delivery' or 'Choose a calm, authoritative AI voice' ensures the audio matches your brand. If your blog promotes a product or service, include a clear call to action in your prompt like 'End with a signup CTA' or 'Drive viewers to the product page.' For blogs with complex topics, request simplified explanations such as 'Break down the technical concepts for beginners' or 'Use analogies to explain blockchain.' The blog to video system works best when you treat your prompt as a creative brief that bridges your written content and your video goals.

### How does blog to video source and select visuals for different article topics?

The blog to video visual sourcing engine combines semantic understanding with contextual relevance to match imagery with your article content. When you input a blog URL, Agent Opus analyzes the full text to identify key concepts, entities, and themes. For a blog about 'sustainable fashion,' it recognizes related concepts like eco-friendly materials, ethical manufacturing, and clothing recycling, then searches both web sources and stock libraries for relevant imagery. The system prioritizes visuals that match the specific context of each scene. If your article section discusses cotton farming, Agent Opus sources cotton field imagery rather than generic fashion photos. For abstract concepts like 'brand trust' or 'customer loyalty,' the AI selects metaphorical visuals such as handshakes, smiling customers, or quality badges that communicate the idea visually. You can guide visual selection through your prompt. Specify preferences like 'Use product photos over stock imagery' or 'Prioritize charts and data visualizations for the statistics section.' If your blog already contains images, you can reference them with 'Use the infographic from section three' or 'Include the before-and-after photos from the case study.' The blog to video system also adapts visual density to content type. Tutorial blogs get step-by-step visual sequences, listicle blogs receive distinct imagery for each point, and narrative blogs flow with cinematic visuals that support the story arc. For technical or niche topics where relevant stock footage is limited, Agent Opus generates AI motion graphics, animated text, and icon-based visuals to illustrate concepts clearly. The result is a video where every visual element reinforces your article's message and maintains viewer engagement from first frame to last.
