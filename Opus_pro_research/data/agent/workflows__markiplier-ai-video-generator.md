### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate Markiplier-style gaming videos?

Effective prompts for a Markiplier AI video generator balance structure with creative direction. Start with the gaming context: specify the game title, genre, and the specific moment or concept you're covering. Instead of "make a gaming video," write "Five Nights at Freddy's jumpscare reaction compilation focusing on nights 3-5 with escalating tension." Include commentary style cues: "enthusiastic and scared," "sarcastic and funny," or "informative with occasional jokes." The more specific your tone direction, the better the AI calibrates voiceover energy and visual pacing. Describe key beats or story structure: "Start with confident gameplay, build tension as animatronics approach, climax with jumpscare reaction, end with frustrated but determined commentary." This guides scene assembly and pacing. Mention specific visual elements you want: "include facecam reactions in bottom right corner," "add meme overlays when I die," "zoom in on scary moments." For script-based generation, format your commentary with natural pauses and emphasis. Write how you'd actually speak: "Okay okay okay... we're fine. We're TOTALLY fine. \[pause\] WE'RE NOT FINE!" The AI interprets these cues for timing and inflection. Include bracketed stage directions for visual cues: "\[zooms in on door\] \[sudden jumpscare\] \[cuts to shocked reaction\]." Specify any brand elements: "use my channel intro, include my logo in bottom left, match my usual red and black color scheme." For compilation-style videos, list the moments you want: "moment 1: first encounter with enemy, moment 2: failed speedrun attempt, moment 3: unexpected plot twist reaction." The generator assembles these into a cohesive narrative. Avoid vague prompts; instead of "funny gaming video," specify "Minecraft hardcore mode death compilation with increasingly frustrated commentary and dramatic slow-motion on final death."
