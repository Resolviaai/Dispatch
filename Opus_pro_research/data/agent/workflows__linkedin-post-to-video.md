## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does LinkedIn post to video generation handle different post lengths and formats?

Agent Opus adapts to the structure and length of your LinkedIn content automatically. For short text posts under 200 words, the system creates punchy 15 to 30 second videos with fast cuts, bold text overlays, and energetic pacing perfect for feed stops and Stories. Longer thought leadership posts or article links get broken into logical scenes with smooth transitions, B-roll visuals, and voiceover pacing that matches reading speed. If your post includes lists, quotes, or key stats, Agent Opus highlights them with motion graphics and visual emphasis. The AI analyzes your content structure and decides how many scenes to create, which points deserve visual focus, and how to pace the narrative so viewers stay engaged from start to finish. You can guide this with prompts like 'fast-paced for Reels' or 'thoughtful and detailed for LinkedIn feed' and the system adjusts scene count, transition speed, and visual density accordingly. The result is a video that feels native to the platform and format you are targeting, not a one-size-fits-all template stretched across every use case.

### What are best practices for prompts when converting LinkedIn posts to video?

Start with the core message and desired tone in your prompt. For example, 'Turn this post into an inspiring 30-second video for LinkedIn feed with upbeat music and bold text' gives Agent Opus clear direction on pacing, mood, and platform. Specify visual style if you have preferences like 'use product shots and minimal stock footage' or 'modern motion graphics with dark background and neon accents.' If your LinkedIn post has a call to action, mention it explicitly in the prompt so the final scene reinforces it visually. Include voice preferences upfront, such as 'use my cloned voice with confident delivery' or 'choose a warm, conversational AI voice.' For longer posts or articles, guide the narrative structure with prompts like 'break into three key points with transitions between each' or 'start with a hook question, then explain the solution.' If you are repurposing the same post across multiple platforms, create separate prompts for each format. LinkedIn feed videos can be slower and more detailed, while TikTok and Reels versions should be faster with bigger text and quicker cuts. The more specific your prompt, the less iteration you need, but Agent Opus is smart enough to make good default choices if you keep it simple. Test a few variations to learn what works for your content style and audience, then refine your prompt templates for consistency across future videos.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of LinkedIn post to video generation?

Agent Opus handles most LinkedIn content types smoothly, but a few edge cases require workarounds. Very short posts under 50 words may produce videos that feel repetitive or stretched, so consider adding context in your prompt like 'expand on the core idea with examples' to give the AI more material to visualize. Posts with heavy technical jargon, niche acronyms, or industry-specific terms may need clarification in the prompt so the system sources appropriate visuals and explains concepts clearly. If your LinkedIn post links to external content behind paywalls or login walls, Agent Opus cannot access the full text, so paste the article body directly or provide a summary in your prompt. Highly visual posts that rely on embedded images, charts, or infographics work best when you upload those assets separately so the system can incorporate them into scenes rather than trying to recreate them from text descriptions alone. Voice cloning requires a clean audio sample without background noise, so record in a quiet space for best results. If your post includes multiple speakers, quotes, or dialogue, specify in the prompt how you want voices assigned, such as 'use my voice for main points and a different AI voice for the quoted expert.' Finally, LinkedIn native video specs favor square or vertical formats, so if you are generating for LinkedIn feed specifically, mention that in your prompt to avoid horizontal 16:9 defaults. These are minor adjustments, and most users find the system intuitive after generating their first few videos and learning how prompts shape output.
