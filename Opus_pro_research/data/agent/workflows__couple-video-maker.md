### What are best practices for prompts when creating couple videos with AI generation?

Effective prompts for couple video maker workflows balance specific detail with creative flexibility. Start by defining your core message or story arc: 'Our anniversary trip to Paris focusing on unexpected moments' gives Agent Opus clear direction while allowing creative interpretation. Include emotional tone explicitly since relationship content relies heavily on feeling: specify 'heartwarming,' 'humorous,' 'inspirational,' or 'vulnerable' to guide visual and pacing choices. For date recaps or experience videos, structure your prompt chronologically but highlight key moments: 'Morning coffee shop conversation, surprise picnic setup, sunset beach walk, evening reflection' creates natural scene breaks. When generating relationship advice content, frame as teaching points: 'Three communication mistakes couples make, with relatable examples and actionable solutions' produces clear, educational structure. Specify voice and presence preferences: 'narrated in my voice with warm, conversational tone' or 'dual narrator format alternating between partners' perspectives.' For authenticity, include specific details that make your relationship unique rather than generic romance tropes. Mention any visual elements you want incorporated: 'include our logo in corner, use our brand colors for text overlays, show our product in lifestyle context if relevant to relationship content creators.' If you have existing scripts, paste them directly; the couple video maker handles full narration. For outline-based generation, bullet your key points and let AI expand them into full narrative. Test different prompt lengths: sometimes a concise concept ('how we handle disagreements, honest and practical') produces better results than over-specified instructions. The system learns from natural language, so write as if explaining your video idea to a creative partner who understands relationship content.

This means highly personal, one-of-a-kind experiences (your actual wedding ceremony, specific family interactions) require different approaches. For couples wanting their actual faces and specific locations, avatar technology provides representation but may not capture every nuance of your unique appearance or setting. The couple video maker handles English-language content most effectively; other languages may have varying quality levels. Video length is optimized for social platforms (typically under 3 minutes); longer-form content may require multiple generations or different approaches. Rights and imagery sourcing are handled automatically, but if you need specific branded products, locations, or copyrighted elements featured, you'll need to provide those assets or describe alternatives.
