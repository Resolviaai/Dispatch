## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does an AI motion graphics generator handle complex visual sequences?

Agent Opus breaks your input text into logical scenes, then assembles each scene with appropriate motion graphics elements, transitions, and timing. When you provide a prompt or script, the system analyzes the narrative structure and identifies key moments that need visual emphasis. For example, if your script mentions a product feature, the generator will source relevant imagery, apply motion graphics treatments like zooms, pans, or animated overlays, and time everything to match your voiceover pacing. The AI handles layer compositing automatically, meaning multiple visual elements can appear simultaneously with proper depth and movement. You can include brand-specific assets like logos or product photos in your input, and the generator will incorporate them into the motion graphics sequences at contextually appropriate moments. The system also manages visual consistency across scenes, ensuring color schemes, animation styles, and graphic treatments feel cohesive throughout the video. This approach eliminates the manual keyframing and timeline work that traditional motion graphics require, while still producing professional animated sequences with dynamic movement and visual interest.

### What types of prompts work best for motion graphics generation?

The most effective prompts for an AI motion graphics generator combine clear messaging with visual direction. Start with your core message or script, then add notes about visual style, pacing, and emphasis. For instance, instead of just 'Explain our new app feature,' try 'Explain our new app feature with fast-paced visuals showing phone screens, highlight the one-tap checkout process, use our brand blue, upbeat tone.' The generator responds well to structural cues like 'Start with a problem statement, then show three benefits, end with a call to action.' You can specify visual treatments: 'Use bold text overlays for key stats' or 'Show product shots with zoom-in effects.' If you have a blog post or article, the AI motion graphics generator will extract the main points and create visual sequences automatically, but you can guide it by highlighting which sections deserve emphasis. Prompts that mention pacing help too: 'Keep it punchy for social media' versus 'Allow time for detailed explanation.' The system handles both short-form content (15-60 seconds for social) and longer explainers (2-3 minutes). Including your brand assets in the prompt ensures they appear in the motion graphics. The key is balancing creative freedom with enough direction that the output matches your vision and brand standards.

### Can I use my own brand assets and maintain visual consistency?

Yes, Agent Opus is designed to work with your existing brand materials. You can upload logos, product images, brand colors, and even reference visual styles, and the AI motion graphics generator will incorporate them throughout the video. When you provide brand assets, the system treats them as primary visual elements rather than generic stock imagery. Your logo might appear as a watermark, an intro/outro element, or integrated into scene transitions. Product shots get featured prominently with motion graphics treatments that highlight key details through zooms, rotations, or animated callouts. The generator maintains visual consistency by analyzing your brand materials and applying similar color palettes, graphic styles, and animation speeds across all scenes. If you upload multiple videos over time, the system can learn your preferred visual language and replicate it in new motion graphics. This is especially valuable for teams producing regular content series where brand consistency matters. You can also specify visual guidelines in your prompts: 'Use minimalist animations' or 'Apply bold, high-contrast graphics.' The AI motion graphics generator balances your brand requirements with platform best practices, ensuring videos look on-brand while still performing well on social feeds. This eliminates the usual tension between maintaining brand standards and adapting to platform-specific content styles.

### How does voice and avatar integration work in motion graphics videos?

Agent Opus offers flexible voice and avatar options that integrate seamlessly with motion graphics. For voiceover, you can clone your own voice by uploading a sample, or choose from AI voices with different tones, accents, and speaking styles. The AI motion graphics generator automatically syncs the voiceover timing with visual elements, so text overlays, product reveals, and scene transitions happen at the right moments in the narration. This sync is critical for motion graphics because visual emphasis needs to match verbal emphasis. If your script says 'three key benefits,' the generator will time the appearance of each benefit graphic to align with when you say it. For avatar integration, you can appear on-screen as a presenter with motion graphics elements surrounding you, or use an AI avatar if you prefer not to be on camera. The avatar option works well for explainer videos and tutorials where a human presence adds credibility, while pure motion graphics without avatars suit product demos and data-driven content. The system handles the compositing automatically, placing the avatar in a position that doesn't compete with motion graphics elements. You can specify avatar placement in your prompt: 'Show me in the bottom right with graphics filling the main frame.' Voice cloning is particularly powerful for teams creating content at scale, because one voice sample enables unlimited motion graphics videos with consistent narration, no recording sessions required.
