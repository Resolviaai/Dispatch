The recruitment video maker scales with your hiring needs, whether you're filling one niche role or launching a campus recruiting blitz across dozens of positions.

### What are best practices for writing prompts in a recruitment video maker?

Effective prompts for recruitment video generation start with clarity about your audience, message, and desired outcome. Begin by identifying the candidate persona: Are you targeting early-career talent, mid-level professionals, or senior leaders? Next, outline the core value proposition. What makes this role and your company compelling? Include specifics like growth trajectory, team culture, impact on product or mission, and unique perks. Agent Opus performs best when you provide concrete details rather than generic language. Instead of 'We're looking for a great engineer,' write 'We're hiring a senior backend engineer to scale our API infrastructure, mentor junior developers, and shape our technical roadmap. Our team values ownership, async collaboration, and work-life balance.' If you have a job description, paste the full text or key sections. Agent Opus extracts the most visually and emotionally resonant points and structures them into a narrative arc. For tone, specify whether you want the video to feel energetic and fast-paced, warm and conversational, or polished and corporate. Mention any brand guidelines, such as color schemes, logo placement, or visual style. If you're using your own voice clone, note the speaking style you prefer, such as friendly, authoritative, or enthusiastic. For multi-role campaigns, create separate prompts for each position to ensure messaging stays relevant. Finally, indicate the platform and format: a 15-second Instagram Reel requires tighter pacing than a 60-second LinkedIn video. Agent Opus uses these cues to optimize scene length, text density, and visual rhythm for maximum engagement.
