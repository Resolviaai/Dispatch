Agent Opus adapts to any resume format or length you provide. You can paste a traditional chronological resume, a skills-based resume, a brief career summary, or even just bullet points of your key achievements. The resume to video generator analyzes your input and determines the best way to structure the video narrative. For longer resumes with multiple roles and detailed accomplishments, the AI intelligently prioritizes the most impactful information and creates a compelling story arc that keeps viewers engaged. You can guide the focus by adding notes in your prompt, like emphasizing recent roles, highlighting specific skills, or showcasing portfolio work. The system automatically breaks your content into logical scenes, each with appropriate pacing and visual emphasis. If you have a short elevator pitch or LinkedIn summary, the generator expands it with professional visuals and motion graphics to create a complete video. For detailed multi-page resumes, it condenses the narrative while preserving your unique value proposition. The AI also adapts tone and style based on your industry. A creative professional's resume video will feature dynamic transitions and bold graphics, while a corporate executive's video will use clean, professional layouts and measured pacing. You maintain full control by refining your prompt, specifying which experiences to emphasize, and indicating your target audience. The resume to video generator handles all the technical work of scene assembly, timing, and visual hierarchy so your career story comes across clearly and memorably regardless of your original resume format.

### What are best practices for writing prompts for a resume to video generator?

The most effective prompts for a resume to video generator combine your core career narrative with specific guidance on tone, audience, and key messages. Start by identifying your goal: are you applying for a specific role, building your personal brand, or showcasing expertise to potential clients? Include this context in your prompt. For example, instead of just pasting your resume, write something like: 'Create a 60-second resume video for a senior product manager role, emphasizing my experience leading cross-functional teams and launching SaaS products. Target audience is tech startup founders and VPs of Product.' This gives the AI clear direction on what to prioritize and how to frame your story. Next, specify the tone you want. Professional and polished? Energetic and creative? Warm and approachable? The resume to video generator will match visuals, pacing, and even music to your desired tone. Include specific achievements you want highlighted, especially quantifiable results like revenue growth, user acquisition, or successful projects. The AI will create visual emphasis around these key points with motion graphics and dynamic text. If you have industry-specific terminology or certifications that matter to your audience, mention them explicitly. The generator will ensure they're prominently featured. You can also specify visual preferences: 'Use clean, minimal graphics' or 'Include dynamic transitions and bold colors.' For voiceover, indicate whether you want a formal, conversational, or enthusiastic delivery. If you're using voice cloning, the AI will match the script to your natural speaking style. Finally, mention your target platform and length. A 30-second LinkedIn video has different requirements than a 90-second portfolio piece. The resume to video generator optimizes pacing, information density, and visual complexity accordingly. The more specific your prompt, the more tailored and effective your final video will be.
