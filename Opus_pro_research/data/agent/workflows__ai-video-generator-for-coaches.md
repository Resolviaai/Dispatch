### How does an AI video generator for coaches handle different content types like frameworks, testimonials, and teaching moments?

Agent Opus adapts to whatever coaching content you need to create by analyzing the structure and intent of your input. When you paste a coaching framework with steps or principles, the system breaks each component into its own scene with supporting visuals and pacing that gives viewers time to absorb each concept. For client success stories or testimonials, Agent Opus emphasizes emotional beats and results-focused language, using visuals that highlight transformation. Teaching moments and quick tips get faster pacing with punchy graphics that work for short-form platforms. The key is how you frame your prompt or script. If you write 'Explain my 5-step confidence framework for new entrepreneurs,' Agent Opus structures five distinct scenes with transitions and visual metaphors for each step. If you write 'Share how my client Sarah went from burnout to breakthrough in 90 days,' the system creates a narrative arc with before-and-after visuals and testimonial-style pacing. You can also input raw outlines like bullet points from a coaching session, and Agent Opus expands them into full narration with appropriate visuals. The motion graphics engine automatically matches visual complexity to content density, so a detailed framework gets cleaner, more focused graphics while a motivational message gets bolder, more energetic animations. This flexibility means you can generate everything from 15-second Instagram tips to 3-minute YouTube explainers from the same tool, just by adjusting what you feed it and how you describe the desired outcome.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that generate effective coaching videos?

The most effective prompts for coaching video generation combine three elements: your core teaching point, your target audience, and the emotional outcome you want viewers to experience. Start with a clear statement of what you are teaching, like 'Explain why most coaches underprice their services and how to fix it' rather than vague requests like 'Make a video about pricing.' Agent Opus uses your specificity to select relevant visuals and structure the narrative flow. Next, define who you are talking to. Adding 'for new life coaches' or 'for established business consultants' helps the system choose examples, language complexity, and visual metaphors that resonate with that audience. Finally, hint at the emotional journey: 'so they feel empowered to raise rates' or 'so they recognize the mistake before it costs them clients.' This guides tone, pacing, and the balance between education and motivation. For script-based inputs, write in your natural coaching voice rather than formal or academic language. Agent Opus preserves your personality, so if you normally say 'Here is the thing most coaches miss' instead of 'A common oversight in the coaching industry,' use your natural phrasing. The voice cloning and avatar features amplify authenticity when your script sounds like you. When using outlines or bullet points, add brief context notes. Instead of just 'Step 1: Identify your niche,' write 'Step 1: Identify your niche (explain why generalists struggle to get clients).' Those parenthetical notes help Agent Opus generate richer narration and more targeted visuals. Avoid over-specifying visual details unless you have strong preferences; the AI motion graphics engine excels at matching visuals to concepts when given semantic freedom. If you do want specific imagery, mention it clearly: 'Use mountain climbing visuals as a metaphor for client progress.' Finally, test short prompts first to learn how the system interprets your style, then scale up to longer, more complex coaching content once you see what works.
