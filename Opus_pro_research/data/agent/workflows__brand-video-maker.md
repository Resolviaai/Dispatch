### How does a brand video maker like Agent Opus handle different input types?

Agent Opus accepts four main input formats, each designed to match how brand teams actually work. First, you can write a short prompt or creative brief describing your brand message, target audience, and key points. The AI interprets your intent and builds a complete video structure around it. Second, if you already have a full script with specific language or calls to action, paste it in and Agent Opus will pace scenes, select visuals, and sync voiceover to match your exact wording. Third, you can provide an outline with bullet points or section headers, and the system expands each point into a cohesive narrative with supporting visuals. Fourth, paste a blog post or article URL, and Agent Opus extracts the core message, reformats it for video storytelling, and generates scenes that communicate your written content visually. Across all four methods, the brand video maker automatically integrates your uploaded logos, product images, and brand colors. It sources additional royalty-free or web images to fill gaps, applies AI motion graphics for professional polish, and outputs a publish-ready video. The key advantage is flexibility: whether you're working from a napkin sketch or a fully written script, Agent Opus adapts to your workflow and delivers a finished brand video without requiring you to learn video editing software or hire a production team.

### What are best practices for writing prompts in a brand video maker?

Effective prompts for Agent Opus balance clarity with creative freedom. Start by defining your core message in one sentence: what do you want viewers to remember? Then specify your audience, whether that's potential customers, investors, or internal teams, because the tone and pacing will shift accordingly. Include any must-have elements like product features, brand values, or a specific call to action. If you have visual preferences, mention them: for example, 'show our app interface in action' or 'use warm, energetic colors.' The brand video maker works best when you provide context rather than micromanaging every frame. Instead of saying 'show a person typing on a laptop for three seconds,' say 'demonstrate how our tool saves time in a typical workday.' Agent Opus will interpret that intent and select appropriate visuals, pacing, and motion graphics. If you're cloning your voice, write the script in your natural speaking style, including pauses or emphasis where you'd naturally use them. For avatar videos, describe the presenter's role: 'friendly founder explaining our mission' or 'expert consultant walking through a case study.' Upload your logo and any hero product shots before generating, so the system can weave them into the narrative. Finally, keep initial prompts concise but rich in intent. You can always regenerate with adjustments, and Agent Opus learns from your feedback to refine future outputs. The goal is to give the AI enough direction to align with your brand while letting it handle the technical execution of scene assembly, timing, and visual polish.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of using a brand video maker like Agent Opus?

Agent Opus is designed for text-to-video generation, which means it excels when you can describe or script what you want to communicate. However, there are scenarios where it's not the right fit. First, if your brand video requires highly specific live-action footage, like a scripted commercial with actors performing precise actions, Agent Opus won't replace a film crew. It sources royalty-free images, web visuals, and can integrate your uploaded product shots, but it doesn't shoot original live footage. Second, if you need frame-by-frame control over animation timing or want to manually adjust every transition, Agent Opus isn't built for that level of granular editing. It's a prompt-to-publish system, not a timeline editor. You describe the outcome, and the AI handles execution. Third, extremely niche or technical visuals may require custom assets. For example, if you're explaining a proprietary manufacturing process with no existing reference images, you'll need to upload those visuals yourself. Agent Opus can incorporate them, but it won't generate them from scratch. Fourth, if your brand video relies on real-time data visualization or interactive elements, those fall outside the scope of a static video generator. Fifth, very long-form content, like 20-minute webinars or detailed tutorials, may be better suited to traditional editing workflows where you can manually structure complex narratives. Agent Opus shines for social videos, explainers, promos, and brand stories typically under five minutes. Finally, while voice cloning is highly accurate, it works best with clear audio samples. If your recording has background noise or inconsistent tone, the clone may not match your expectations. Understanding these boundaries helps you use Agent Opus effectively: it's a brand video maker for teams who want fast, polished, AI-generated content without manual production, not a replacement for every video use case.
