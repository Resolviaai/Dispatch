### What are the best practices for writing prompts for an AI art video generator?

Crafting effective prompts for an AI art video generator requires understanding how to communicate your vision clearly while leaving room for creative interpretation. Start with a clear statement of your video's core purpose and intended audience. Specify whether you want an abstract art piece, a product showcase, an educational explainer, or a brand story. Include emotional tone descriptors like energetic, calming, inspiring, or mysterious to guide the visual mood. Describe key visual elements you envision, such as color schemes, imagery types, or specific objects that should appear. If you have brand assets like logos or product photos, mention where and how they should integrate into the composition. Provide context about the platform and audience, as a TikTok art video requires different pacing and visual density than a LinkedIn piece. For script-based prompts, write conversational narration that flows naturally, avoiding overly technical language unless your topic demands it. Break complex ideas into clear segments, as this helps the AI structure scenes logically. Use vivid, concrete language when describing abstract concepts, giving the generator tangible visual hooks to work with. Specify duration preferences if you have platform constraints, though the AI will optimize pacing automatically. Mention any must-have moments or key messages that cannot be omitted, ensuring critical content makes it into the final video. Avoid over-prescribing every detail, as this can limit the AI's ability to create cohesive visual flow and surprising creative choices. Instead, establish guardrails and goals, then trust the system to handle execution. If you want specific motion graphics styles, reference them by name or description, such as kinetic typography, parallax scrolling, or morphing transitions. For voice and audio, indicate whether you prefer energetic or measured delivery, and mention any accent or gender preferences for AI voices. Include any legal or compliance requirements, such as avoiding certain imagery types or including specific disclaimers. Test different prompt lengths and styles to discover what works best for your needs. Some creators prefer detailed outlines, while others get better results from concise, evocative descriptions. Review generated videos to understand how the AI interprets your language, then refine your prompting approach based on results. The goal is clear communication that inspires creative execution rather than rigid instructions that constrain possibility.
