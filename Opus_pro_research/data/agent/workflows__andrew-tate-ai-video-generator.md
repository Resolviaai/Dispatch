### What are the limitations or edge cases when using the Andrew Tate AI video generator for motivational content?

To avoid this, break complex ideas into separate videos or provide a structured outline that guides the narrative. If your motivational content requires very specific imagery, such as a particular location, product, or person, you can upload custom images to ensure the visuals align with your message. If you need longer videos, such as five-minute YouTube videos, you may need to generate multiple segments and combine them, or provide a more detailed script that the system can parse into extended content. Background noise, poor recording quality, or inconsistent tone in your sample can affect the output. Record your voice sample in a quiet environment with clear enunciation for optimal cloning.
