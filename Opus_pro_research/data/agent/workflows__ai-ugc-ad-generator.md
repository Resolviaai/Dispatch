### What prompts produce the highest-converting AI UGC ad creative?

Effective AI UGC ad prompts combine three things: the hook, the product or offer, and the specific creative angle. Lead with what the ad is trying to communicate — a problem-solution flow, a transformation reveal, a fast-paced demo, a testimonial — name the emotional tone (urgent, confident, friendly, aspirational), and reference the visual language you want featured ("casual handheld camera framing," "product hero shot," "on-screen caption call-out"). The more specific the references, the more precisely the first generation lands. For variation testing, hold the structure constant and vary only the hook line or color palette — the agent regenerates fast enough to test 10+ creative permutations in an afternoon. Avoid vague phrases like "make a good ad"; anchor on three specifics: who it's for, what they should do next, and what the format looks like. Most ad creatives reach a final cut in two or three iteration passes.
