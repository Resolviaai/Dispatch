### How do I optimize prompts for the Adam Sandler AI video generator to get the funniest results?

Effective prompts for the Adam Sandler AI video generator balance specificity with creative freedom, focusing on character motivation, escalating absurdity, and clear comedic structure. Start with a strong character concept that includes a defining quirk or flaw, like a guy who takes everything literally or a woman who solves problems with outdated 90s technology. Describe the scenario setup in one sentence, then outline how the situation escalates in two or three beats, ending with a punchline or twist. For example, a man tries to impress a date by cooking dinner but uses a blowtorch instead of a stove, burns the kitchen, and ends up ordering pizza while pretending he planned it all along captures setup, escalation, and resolution. Include sensory and tonal details that evoke Sandler's aesthetic, like casual clothing, everyday locations, self-deprecating dialogue, or nostalgic props. Specify the emotional arc if relevant, since his best comedy blends ridiculous situations with genuine heart, like a character learning a small life lesson through absurd failure. For dialogue-heavy sketches, write out key lines or catchphrases you want the character to deliver, and the AI will build around those anchor points. Reference specific comedy techniques like callback jokes, visual gags, or fourth-wall breaks if you want those elements included. Avoid overly complex prompts with too many characters or locations, since the AI performs best with focused scenarios featuring one or two main characters and a single primary setting. If you are creating parody content, explicitly name what you are parodying and describe the comedic angle, like a parody of a motivational speaker who gives terrible advice. For brand comedy, integrate your product naturally into the character's world rather than forcing it, like a character who uses your app to solve a ridiculous problem. Test different prompt lengths to find your sweet spot; some creators get better results with concise three-sentence prompts, while others prefer detailed paragraph-long briefs that specify visual style, pacing, and tone. Review your first few generated videos to see which prompt elements the AI interprets most effectively, then refine your approach based on what consistently produces the funniest output. The system rewards clarity and structure, so think of your prompt as a comedy blueprint rather than a vague concept.
