### What are best practices for prompts when using an affiliate video maker to maximize conversions?

Effective prompts for affiliate video creation focus on clarity, audience targeting, and conversion goals. Start by defining your audience and their pain point. Instead of a generic prompt like 'make a video about this blender,' write 'create a 60-second video for busy parents showing how this blender makes healthy smoothies in under two minutes.' The more specific your audience and outcome, the better Agent Opus tailors scenes, pacing, and messaging. Include the product's top three benefits or unique selling points in your prompt. If you are promoting a project management tool, specify 'highlight team collaboration, deadline tracking, and mobile access.' This ensures the video emphasizes what drives conversions for that product. Specify the desired call-to-action and urgency. Prompts like 'end with a limited-time discount code and link in bio' or 'close with a free trial offer' guide the final scene and voiceover script. If your affiliate link includes a bonus or exclusive deal, mention it in the prompt so the video reinforces that value. Indicate the platform and format. A prompt for TikTok might say 'create a fast-paced 15-second hook-driven video,' while a YouTube prompt might request 'a 90-second in-depth review with feature breakdown.' Agent Opus adjusts pacing, text density, and visual complexity accordingly. Provide context on tone and style. Prompts like 'casual and relatable for Gen Z' or 'authoritative and data-driven for B2B buyers' shape the voiceover delivery and visual aesthetic. If you have brand guidelines or a personal review style, describe it. For example, 'use my cloned voice with an enthusiastic but honest tone' or 'keep it minimal and modern with bold text overlays.' Include any must-have visuals or proof elements. If the product has a standout feature, testimonial, or before-and-after result, call it out in the prompt. Agent Opus will prioritize those elements in the scene assembly. Finally, iterate and refine. Generate a first version, review it, then adjust your prompt with more specific direction. Over time, you will develop a prompt formula that consistently produces high-converting affiliate videos for your niche and audience.
