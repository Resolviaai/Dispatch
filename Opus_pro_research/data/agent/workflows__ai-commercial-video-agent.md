### How does AI commercial video generation handle different input formats?

Agent Opus accepts four input types for AI commercial video creation: short prompts, full scripts, structured outlines, and blog or product-page URLs. For prompts, describe your product, target audience, and key message in a few sentences—Agent Opus expands this into a complete narrative, sources visuals, and assembles scenes. If you provide a script, the system parses dialogue and action cues, then matches each line to appropriate visuals, motion graphics, and pacing. Outlines work similarly: list your talking points or scene ideas, and Agent Opus fills in the creative gaps, selecting imagery and transitions that support your structure. When you paste a blog or article URL, the AI extracts core arguments, product benefits, and calls to action, then translates written content into a visual story with voiceover narration. Regardless of input format, the output is always a finished, publish-ready commercial video with synchronized voiceover, branded assets, motion graphics, and social-optimized aspect ratios. This flexibility means you can start from wherever you are in the creative process—whether you have a polished script or just a product idea—and still receive a professional AI commercial video without manual editing or post-production work.

### What are best practices for writing prompts that produce high-converting AI commercial video?

Effective prompts for AI commercial video generation balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by naming your product or service and its primary benefit in the first sentence—this anchors the narrative and ensures the AI prioritizes your core message. Next, define your target audience and the problem your product solves; for example, 'busy parents who need meal-prep solutions' gives Agent Opus context to select relatable visuals and tone. Include one or two emotional hooks or unique selling points, such as 'saves three hours per week' or 'uses only organic ingredients,' so the AI can emphasize differentiation. Mention any required brand elements—logo placement, product shots, color schemes—so the system integrates them automatically. Avoid overly prescriptive scene-by-scene instructions; instead, describe the desired outcome or feeling, like 'upbeat, testimonial-style ad that builds trust' or 'fast-paced demo showing the app in action.' If you have a call to action, state it clearly: 'End with a limited-time discount code and website URL.' Agent Opus interprets these cues to assemble scenes, pace transitions, select motion graphics, and sync voiceover, producing an AI commercial video that aligns with your strategic goals while leveraging AI creativity for visual storytelling. The sweet spot is typically 50 to 150 words—enough detail to guide the narrative without constraining the AI's ability to optimize pacing, imagery, and engagement.

### What are the technical limitations or edge cases when generating AI commercial video?

Agent Opus handles most commercial video use cases automatically, but understanding its boundaries helps you set realistic expectations and optimize your workflow. First, highly niche or hyper-local visuals—such as specific street addresses, proprietary machinery, or custom product prototypes—require you to upload those images as brand assets; the AI cannot generate or source imagery that does not exist in public stock libraries or web sources. If your product is brand-new or visually unique, provide high-quality photos during setup. Second, complex multi-speaker dialogues or intricate narrative arcs may benefit from a structured script rather than a short prompt; while Agent Opus excels at expanding brief ideas, extremely detailed storytelling with precise timing is easier to control when you provide a full script with scene notes. Third, voiceover quality depends on input: voice cloning works best with clean, 30-second audio samples recorded in a quiet environment, and AI voices, while professional, may lack the emotional nuance of a trained human actor for highly dramatic or comedic reads. Fourth, motion graphics are optimized for modern, clean aesthetics—if your brand requires vintage, hand-drawn, or highly stylized animation, you may need to supply custom graphic elements as overlays. Fifth, video length is optimized for social platforms, typically 15 to 90 seconds; longer-form commercials (two minutes or more) may require breaking your content into multiple prompts or scenes. Finally, real-time data or live integrations—such as pulling current stock prices or user-generated content feeds—are not supported; Agent Opus generates static videos based on the information provided at the time of creation. Within these guardrails, AI commercial video generation is remarkably flexible, handling everything from product demos to testimonial-style ads to explainer videos with minimal input and zero manual editing.
