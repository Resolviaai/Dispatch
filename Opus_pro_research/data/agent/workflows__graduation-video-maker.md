### What are best practices for prompts when creating graduation videos with this tool?

Effective prompts for a graduation video maker focus on emotional tone, key milestones, and the audience you are celebrating. Start by identifying the graduation level and context, such as high school Class of 2025, college engineering department, or kindergarten moving up ceremony. This helps Agent Opus select appropriate visual style, pacing, and music. Next, specify 3 to 5 key moments or themes you want highlighted: overcoming challenges during remote learning, championship sports victories, academic honors, community service projects, or memorable school traditions. The more specific your milestones, the better the AI can structure scenes and source relevant imagery. Describe the emotional journey you want viewers to experience. A prompt like 'Start with freshman year excitement, build through challenges and growth, peak with senior achievements, end with hopeful future outlook' gives Agent Opus clear narrative arc guidance. Mention any specific individuals, teams, or groups that should be featured if you are creating a personalized tribute rather than a general class video. Include brand elements you need: school colors, mascot, logo placement, or motto integration. For voiceover tone, specify whether you want inspirational and uplifting, nostalgic and reflective, celebratory and energetic, or formal and ceremonial. If you have a particular song or music genre in mind, mention it, though Agent Opus will auto-select appropriate graduation music if you do not. Avoid overly long prompts; 4 to 8 sentences typically work best. A strong graduation video maker prompt example: 'Create an uplifting 90-second video celebrating Lincoln High Class of 2025. Highlight our journey from virtual freshman year through return to campus, emphasizing resilience and community. Feature robotics team nationals win, drama club spring musical, and senior prom. Use school colors navy and gold with logo. Inspirational voiceover ending with forward-looking message about future success. Upbeat contemporary music.' This gives Agent Opus everything needed to generate a compelling, on-brand graduation tribute.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using a graduation video maker for complex ceremonies?

While Agent Opus as a graduation video maker handles most graduation video scenarios automatically, understanding its limitations helps you plan content for optimal results. The system works best with 10 to 50 individual photos or a script of 300 to 1200 words. If you have 200 senior portraits and want each student featured individually, consider creating multiple videos by class section or department rather than one massive compilation. The AI can handle large batches, but shorter, focused videos often have more impact and are easier to share on social platforms with time limits. For ceremonies with multiple speakers, complex program structures, or hour-long events, Agent Opus excels at creating highlight reels and tribute videos rather than full-length ceremony documentation. If you need a complete ceremony recording, use traditional video capture; then use the graduation video maker to create shareable social clips, alumni outreach videos, or donor recognition pieces from that footage. The tool automatically sources web and stock imagery to complement your uploaded photos, which works beautifully for general graduation themes like caps, diplomas, and campus shots. However, if your ceremony has highly specific visual requirements like a unique venue, custom stage design, or particular decorations, upload photos of those elements rather than relying solely on AI sourcing. Voice cloning requires a clean audio sample of 30 seconds to 2 minutes. If you want to clone a principal's voice for narration but only have a noisy gymnasium recording, the quality may not meet expectations. In that case, choose from Agent Opus's AI voice library, which offers professional narration quality. The graduation video maker outputs social-ready aspect ratios including 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, and 1:1 for Instagram feed. If you need a specific custom resolution for a venue display screen, you may need to adapt the output. Music selection is automatic and royalty-free, perfect for online sharing, but if you have rights to a specific licensed song that must be used, you will need to handle that audio separately. Finally, while Agent Opus generates publish-ready videos, it does not provide a manual editing interface. If you receive a video that is 95 percent perfect but you want to swap one photo or adjust one scene, the best approach is to refine your prompt and regenerate rather than expecting frame-by-frame editing control. Think of the graduation video maker as a creative partner that delivers complete videos from clear instructions rather than a traditional editing suite.
