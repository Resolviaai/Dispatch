### What are best practices for prompts when creating pitch deck videos with AI?

Effective pitch deck video maker prompts balance structure with creative direction. Start with your core message and audience: 'Create a 90-second investor pitch for a B2B SaaS platform targeting enterprise HR teams' gives Agent Opus essential context for tone, pacing, and visual style. Then provide your narrative arc. Even a simple structure helps: 'Open with the problem statement, show our solution demo, highlight three customer logos, present traction metrics, close with funding ask.' The AI uses this framework to sequence scenes and allocate time appropriately. Be specific about brand elements. Instead of 'use my logo,' say 'place logo in top-right corner throughout, use brand colors blue and orange for motion graphics, integrate product dashboard screenshot at 30-second mark.' Specificity ensures visual consistency without requiring manual editing. For voiceover, indicate style and energy: 'confident, conversational tone' or 'authoritative executive voice' or 'enthusiastic founder energy.' If cloning your voice, record a sample that matches your desired pitch delivery. The pitch deck video maker will match that cadence and emotion. Call out data visualization needs explicitly: 'animate growth chart showing 300% revenue increase' or 'display customer testimonial quotes as text overlays' or 'show competitor comparison table with checkmarks.' The AI handles the motion design, but clear direction ensures it emphasizes the right metrics. Avoid vague requests like 'make it engaging' or 'add some graphics.' Instead: 'use kinetic typography for key stats, add subtle parallax on product images, keep transitions smooth and professional.' Finally, specify output format and platform: 'vertical 9:16 for Instagram Reels' or 'landscape 16:9 for investor email and LinkedIn' or 'square 1:1 for Twitter.' Agent Opus optimizes composition and text sizing for each format. The best prompts combine narrative structure, brand specifics, visual direction, voiceover style, and output format into one clear brief that lets the AI handle all technical execution.
