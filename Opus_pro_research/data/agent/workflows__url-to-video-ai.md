## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the URL to video AI convert a link into a finished video?

Paste any public URL and Agent Opus does the rest end to end. The agent reads the content behind the link — the narrative, key points, data, and structure — and turns it into a scene-by-scene video plan. From there it generates everything a finished video needs: AI motion graphics matched to the content, a natural-sounding voiceover, burned-in captions, music, and beat-synced transitions. You can add a one-line prompt alongside the URL to steer tone, style, or target platform ("make it punchy for TikTok," "keep it analytical for LinkedIn"), and the agent adjusts pacing and visual density accordingly. There is no timeline to manage and no editing skills required — the conversion runs automatically and most links become publish-ready videos in under ten minutes. If you want changes, refine with a follow-up prompt and the agent regenerates just that element instead of starting over. Every conversion renders multiple aspect ratios from the same job, so one link produces videos for every platform you publish to.

### What kinds of links can I convert to video?

Agent Opus handles the full range of content URLs. Blog posts and articles are the strongest performers — the agent extracts the argument, pulls the key takeaways, and builds a tight script around them. News stories convert well for commentary and explainer formats, with the agent summarizing developments into a hook-forward short. Product and landing pages become promo videos: features, benefits, and calls to action are lifted from the page and presented with motion graphics. Listicles and how-to guides map naturally to step-by-step scenes. You can also start from a video link — drop in a YouTube URL and the agent works with the underlying content to produce new social-ready cuts. The common requirement is that the link is publicly accessible and has substantive content to work with; paywalled or login-gated pages can't be read. For best results, convert links with clear structure — headers, numbered steps, or a strong central argument give the agent the cleanest scene plan.

### How is an AI link to video converter different from editing the video myself?

Manually turning a link into a video means reading the content, writing a script, storyboarding scenes, hunting stock footage, recording voiceover, syncing captions, editing a timeline, and exporting separate versions for each platform — typically hours of work per video, multiplied across every piece of content you want to repurpose. Agent Opus collapses that into a single step: the agent performs the reading, scripting, visual selection, narration, captioning, and formatting automatically, and delivers 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 cuts from one conversion with intelligent reframing rather than naive cropping. The practical difference shows up in volume: instead of choosing which one article deserves a video this week, you can convert your whole content library and keep every channel fed. Quality stays consistent because the agent applies your brand preset and platform conventions on every run. And unlike template-based converters, the output is generated around your specific content — scenes, graphics, and pacing are built from what the link actually says, not dropped into a generic layout.
