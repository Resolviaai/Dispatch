### What are best practices for prompts when using a christmas video maker for seasonal campaigns?

Effective prompts for a christmas video maker start with clear goals and audience context. Begin by stating the video's purpose: 'Create a 30-second Christmas sale announcement for Instagram Reels targeting millennial shoppers' or 'Generate a heartfelt holiday thank-you video for email subscribers.' This frames the AI's decisions on pacing, tone, and visual style. Next, describe key visual elements you want. Instead of 'make it festive,' say 'include falling snow, red and green color scheme, product shots of our winter collection, and animated gift boxes.' Specificity helps Agent Opus source the right imagery and apply appropriate motion graphics. If you have brand assets like logos, product photos, or specific color codes, mention them and upload files. The christmas video maker integrates these seamlessly into generated scenes. For voiceover, specify delivery style: 'warm and conversational,' 'energetic and promotional,' or 'sincere and emotional.' If you're cloning your voice, record a sample that matches the tone you want in the final video. Include a rough script or key talking points rather than leaving voiceover entirely to AI generation. Even bullet points like 'Highlight 40% off sale, mention free shipping, end with holiday wishes' give the AI structure to build around. Mention your target platform and duration upfront. 'Generate a 15-second TikTok Christmas video' produces different pacing than 'Create a 60-second YouTube holiday explainer.' Agent Opus adjusts scene count, text density, and visual complexity based on platform norms. Finally, iterate. Your first prompt might yield 80% of what you want. Refine by adding details like 'more emphasis on product features' or 'slower pacing in the opening.' The christmas video maker learns from your adjustments and improves output alignment with each generation.
