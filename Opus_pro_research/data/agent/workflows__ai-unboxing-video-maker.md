### How should I structure prompts for AI Unboxing Video Maker to get the most engaging reveal sequences?

Effective prompts for AI Unboxing Video Maker balance product information with narrative guidance. Start by describing the packaging itself, as this sets viewer expectations and establishes quality perception. Mention box size, materials, printing quality, and any special touches like magnetic closures or embossed logos. This context makes the eventual product reveal more impactful. Next, outline the reveal sequence you envision. Do you want to show the box opening first, or start with a closed box and build anticipation? Should accessories be revealed before or after the main product? For tech products, consider revealing cables and documentation first to build toward the hero product. For gift items, you might reverse this to lead with the wow moment. Specify which product features deserve emphasis and when they should be highlighted. If your product has a standout design element, unique texture, or innovative mechanism, call this out in your prompt so the AI knows to create motion graphics that showcase it. Include the emotional tone you want to convey. Words like premium, exciting, practical, innovative, or luxurious guide the pacing and visual treatment. A premium watch unboxing should feel different from a budget phone accessory unboxing, and your prompt language signals this distinction. For voiceover, describe the key talking points in the order you want them addressed. You do not need a full script, just the main benefits and features in logical sequence. The AI will generate natural narration that flows with the visual reveals. If you have specific brand phrases or value propositions that must be included, list them explicitly. Finally, mention your target platform and audience. An unboxing for YouTube can be longer and more detailed, while TikTok versions need faster pacing and immediate hooks. The AI Unboxing Video Maker adjusts reveal timing, graphic intensity, and information density based on these platform specifications.
