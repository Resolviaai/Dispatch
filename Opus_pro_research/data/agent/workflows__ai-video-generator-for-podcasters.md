If you are working from an outline or bullet points, Agent Opus expands those into a structured narrative, generates supporting visuals, and builds a cohesive video. For podcasters who publish show notes or blog summaries, you can drop in a URL and the AI extracts key points, structures them into video scenes, and sources relevant imagery. The system understands podcast-specific content like episode intros, guest introductions, key takeaways, and calls to action. It automatically adjusts pacing for different content types so interview highlights get faster cuts while educational segments get more breathing room. Voice cloning works across all input types, so whether you provide a detailed script or a rough outline, the final video sounds like you. The AI also recognizes common podcast structures like cold opens, ad breaks, and outros, and can generate separate video segments for each. This flexibility means you can generate videos at any stage of your podcast workflow, from pre-production planning with outlines to post-production promotion with full transcripts.

### What are the best practices for writing prompts when using an AI video generator for podcasters?

Effective prompts for podcast video generation focus on three elements: content structure, visual style, and audience intent. Start by clearly defining your episode segment. Instead of a vague prompt like 'make a video about my podcast,' specify 'generate a 60-second teaser for episode 47 about remote work productivity, highlighting three actionable tips.' This gives the AI clear boundaries and content goals. For visual style, reference your podcast brand and the platform you are targeting. A prompt like 'use energetic motion graphics with my brand colors, optimized for Instagram Reels' tells the AI to prioritize fast pacing, vertical format, and vibrant visuals. If you want a more subdued, professional look for LinkedIn, specify 'clean, minimal motion graphics with data visualizations, landscape format for LinkedIn video.' Include any specific assets you want featured, such as 'include my podcast logo in the intro, guest headshot at 15 seconds, and product demo screenshot at 40 seconds.' For voice and avatar, be explicit: 'use my cloned voice with an AI avatar in business casual' or 'voiceover only, no avatar, focus on text and motion graphics.' The AI performs best when you provide context about your audience and goal. 'Create a video to convert YouTube viewers into podcast subscribers' will generate different calls to action and pacing than 'create a video to establish thought leadership on LinkedIn.' You can also reference specific moments: 'turn the segment from 12:30 to 15:00 where I explain the framework into a standalone explainer video.' The more specific your prompt, the less iteration you will need, but Agent Opus also lets you refine by submitting follow-up prompts that build on the initial generation.
