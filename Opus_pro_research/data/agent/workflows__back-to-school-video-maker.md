### What are best practices for writing prompts for back to school videos?

Effective prompts for the Back to School Video Maker balance specificity with creative freedom. Start by clearly stating your video's purpose and target audience: 'Create a back to school supply list video for elementary parents' or 'Generate a high school orientation video for incoming freshmen.' Include your desired video length—30 seconds for social media teasers, 60-90 seconds for comprehensive announcements, or 2-3 minutes for detailed tours or policy explanations. Specify key information that must appear: dates, locations, specific supplies, important policies, or contact details. The more concrete facts you provide, the more accurate your video will be. However, you don't need to script every word. Phrases like 'emphasize our new science lab' or 'highlight our flexible payment plans' give the AI direction while allowing it to craft engaging narration. Mention tone preferences: 'friendly and welcoming,' 'professional and informative,' or 'exciting and energetic.' If you have brand assets, note them: 'Include our school logo and use our blue and gold colors.' For voice, specify whether you want a male or female voice, approximate age range, and delivery style. The Back to School Video Maker works beautifully with both minimal prompts and detailed scripts. A simple 'Create a 45-second video announcing our August 15th open house with campus tours and meet-the-teacher sessions' produces excellent results, as does a full paragraph describing your event, target audience, key benefits, and call to action. Avoid overly technical video production terms—focus on what you want to communicate, not how to film it. The AI handles all visual storytelling, scene composition, and motion graphics automatically based on your content goals.
