### What are best practices for prompts when using an AI video generator for marketing consultants?

The most effective prompts for marketing consultant videos combine strategic clarity with visual direction. Start by defining the video's purpose and audience: is this a pitch to a prospective client, an internal campaign walkthrough for a client team, a case study showcasing results, or a thought leadership piece for LinkedIn? Specify the core message or strategic framework you want to communicate, such as a three-pillar brand positioning strategy, a customer journey map, or a campaign funnel breakdown. Include any key data points, metrics, or outcomes that should be visualized, like conversion rate improvements, audience growth figures, or ROI projections. If you have specific brand assets, mention them: client logos, product screenshots, campaign creative samples, or brand color palettes. Agent Opus will source and integrate these elements automatically. For voiceover, indicate whether you want a professional consulting tone, an energetic pitch style, or a calm explanatory narration, and specify if you want your cloned voice or an AI voice. Describe any visual metaphors or motion graphic styles that fit the strategy, such as animated funnels for conversion strategies, network diagrams for multi-channel campaigns, or timeline animations for phased rollouts. If the video needs to follow a specific structure, outline it: problem statement, strategic solution, implementation steps, expected results. The more context you provide about the consulting engagement, the client's industry, and the strategic challenge being addressed, the more tailored and impactful the video will be. Avoid vague prompts like 'make a marketing video.' Instead, try: 'Create a three-minute video explaining our content marketing strategy for a B2B SaaS client, covering audience segmentation, content pillar framework, distribution channels, and projected lead generation metrics, using the client's logo and product dashboard screenshots.' Specificity yields videos that feel custom-built for each engagement, reinforcing your consulting brand and making every deliverable feel premium.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when using an AI video generator for marketing consultants?

While Agent Opus handles the vast majority of marketing consultant video needs, understanding its boundaries helps you set realistic expectations and plan workflows accordingly. First, highly specialized or niche industry visuals may require more detailed prompts or uploaded reference images. If a client operates in a very technical field with proprietary product imagery or unique visual metaphors, you will get better results by uploading those assets or providing detailed descriptions rather than relying solely on auto-sourced stock. Agent Opus excels at general marketing concepts, campaign frameworks, and business strategy visuals, but hyper-specific industry jargon or obscure visual references may need clarification in your prompt. Second, the system generates complete videos from a single prompt, which means if you need to make granular adjustments to a specific scene's timing or visual composition, you would refine the prompt and regenerate rather than editing a timeline manually. This is by design, it keeps the workflow fast and eliminates editing complexity, but it does mean you should invest time in crafting a detailed initial prompt rather than expecting to tweak individual frames afterward. Third, while Agent Opus supports voice cloning and AI avatars, the quality of voice cloning depends on the clarity and length of your voice sample. For best results, record a clean, two-to-three-minute sample in a quiet environment, speaking naturally in the tone you use with clients. If you skip this step and use AI voices, the narration will still be professional, but it won't carry your personal consulting brand. Fourth, video length and complexity have practical limits. Agent Opus is optimized for videos in the one-to-five-minute range, which covers most consulting deliverables like pitch videos, campaign explainers, and case studies. If you need a 30-minute webinar-style video, you would break it into multiple shorter videos or use Agent Opus for key segments and combine them in a presentation tool. Finally, while the system auto-sources images and footage, it cannot access proprietary client data, internal analytics dashboards, or confidential campaign assets unless you upload them. For videos that showcase specific client results or internal tools, plan to provide those assets as part of your input. None of these limitations are dealbreakers; they simply mean you should approach Agent Opus as a powerful co-pilot that handles 95% of video production automatically, while you provide strategic direction, brand assets, and quality control to ensure every video meets your consulting standards.
