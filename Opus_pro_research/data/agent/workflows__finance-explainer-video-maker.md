### What are best practices for prompts when using a finance explainer video maker?

Effective prompts for finance explainer videos balance clarity, structure, and context. Start by defining your audience and objective in the brief: are you explaining a concept to beginners (like budgeting basics) or professionals (like options trading strategies)? Agent Opus uses this context to adjust pacing, visual complexity, and voiceover tone. For example, a prompt like 'Explain compound interest to college students in under 60 seconds' will generate a fast-paced, visually simple video with relatable examples, while 'Walk financial advisors through estate planning tax strategies' will produce a longer, data-rich video with charts and technical visuals. Structure your prompt or script with clear sections: introduction, key points, and conclusion. Agent Opus interprets these breaks as scene transitions, so a well-organized script translates directly into a well-paced video. If you're working from a blog post or article URL, ensure the content has clear headings and subheadings—the system uses these to segment scenes and match visuals. Include specific visual cues in your prompt when needed. For instance, 'Show a rising line graph when discussing stock market growth' or 'Use a pie chart to illustrate portfolio diversification' helps Agent Opus select the right imagery. If you have branded assets like your firm's logo, product screenshots, or custom infographics, upload them before generating the video and reference them in the prompt: 'Include our retirement calculator screenshot in the second scene.' For voiceover, specify tone and pacing: 'Use a calm, authoritative voice for a risk management explainer' or 'Keep the tone conversational for a beginner's guide to investing.' If you've cloned your own voice, Agent Opus will apply it automatically, maintaining consistency across all your finance content. Finally, test and iterate. Generate a short video first, review the output, and refine your prompt based on what worked. Over time, you'll develop a prompt style that consistently produces finance explainer videos that match your brand and audience needs.

### What are the limitations or edge cases when generating finance explainer videos from text?

Agent Opus is designed to handle most finance explainer video use cases, but understanding its boundaries helps you get the best results. First, highly specialized or niche financial topics may require more detailed prompts. For example, if you're explaining a complex derivative strategy or a region-specific tax regulation, provide extra context in your script or brief. The more detail you include—definitions, examples, and visual cues—the more accurately Agent Opus can match visuals and pacing to your content. Generic prompts like 'Explain investing' will produce generic videos; specific prompts like 'Explain dollar-cost averaging for beginner investors using real-world examples' yield much better results. Second, Agent Opus auto-sources visuals from stock libraries and the web, which means it may not always find the exact chart or graph you envision. For critical data visualizations—like your firm's proprietary research or client-specific portfolio breakdowns—upload those assets directly and reference them in your prompt. This ensures accuracy and avoids reliance on generic stock imagery. Third, while Agent Opus handles voiceover sync and scene pacing automatically, extremely long scripts (over 10 minutes) may require breaking into multiple videos or sections. The system is optimized for social-ready content—typically 30 seconds to 3 minutes—so if you're creating a comprehensive financial course or webinar, consider generating a series of shorter explainer videos instead of one long piece. Fourth, real-time data and live market updates are not supported. Agent Opus generates videos based on the script or brief you provide, so if you're explaining current stock prices or breaking financial news, you'll need to update your script manually before each generation. It's not a live data feed; it's a text-to-video engine. Fifth, compliance and regulatory language in finance often requires legal review. Agent Opus generates videos based on your input, but it doesn't validate compliance with SEC regulations, FINRA rules, or other financial advertising standards. Always review generated videos for accuracy and compliance before publishing, especially if you're a registered financial advisor or working in a regulated industry. Finally, while Agent Opus supports brand assets and voice cloning, it doesn't replace strategic creative direction. The system executes your vision efficiently, but you still need to define your messaging, audience, and goals. Think of it as a production accelerator, not a strategy replacement. Within these boundaries, Agent Opus is exceptionally powerful for creating finance explainer videos at scale, but the best results come from clear prompts, uploaded assets, and thoughtful review.
