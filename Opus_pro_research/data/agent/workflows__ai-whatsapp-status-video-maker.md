### What are best practices for prompts when using the AI WhatsApp Status Video Maker?

Effective prompts for WhatsApp Status videos focus on a single clear message and include context about tone, audience, and desired action. Start with the core idea: 'Promote our new course launch with urgency' or 'Share a customer testimonial about faster results.' Then add details like 'upbeat tone, target small business owners, end with a swipe-up CTA.' The AI WhatsApp Status Video Maker uses these cues to select visuals, pace scenes, and choose voiceover style. For product-focused Status videos, mention the product name and key benefit: 'Highlight our project management tool's new collaboration feature, show teams working together, professional but friendly vibe.' If you want your brand assets included, note that in the prompt: 'Use our logo in the opening scene, feature our brand colors throughout.' Agent Opus will source relevant stock or web images and integrate your uploaded logos or product shots. Because WhatsApp Status videos are short and vertical, avoid multi-topic prompts. Instead of 'Talk about our three new features and our company story,' focus on one feature or one story per video. This keeps the visual narrative tight and the voiceover punchy. If you're using voice cloning, specify the tone you want: 'Conversational and warm' or 'Energetic and motivational.' The AI adjusts pacing and music to match. For event announcements, include date, time, and urgency cues: 'Announce our webinar on Friday at 3 PM, create FOMO, show countdown graphics.' The more specific your prompt, the more tailored the output, but even a single sentence like 'Celebrate hitting 10,000 customers with confetti and upbeat music' will generate a complete, engaging WhatsApp Status video ready to post.

### What are the limitations or edge cases of the AI WhatsApp Status Video Maker?

Agent Opus excels at generating WhatsApp Status videos from text, but there are scenarios where you'll get the best results by adjusting your approach. First, the system is optimized for 30-second vertical videos, which is WhatsApp's Status limit. If your prompt implies a longer narrative or multiple topics, the AI will condense the content to fit the format, which may mean some details are simplified or omitted. For complex multi-step tutorials or detailed product demos, consider breaking the content into a series of Status updates rather than cramming everything into one video. Second, while Agent Opus auto-sources images from the web and stock libraries, highly niche or brand-specific visuals may require you to upload your own assets. For example, if you're announcing a new physical product that isn't publicly available yet, upload product photos so the AI can feature them prominently. The system will blend your uploads with motion graphics and stock imagery, but it can't generate visuals for unreleased or proprietary items. Third, voice cloning works best with clear, high-quality audio samples. If your voice clone sounds off, re-record your sample in a quiet environment and try again. Fourth, WhatsApp Status videos are designed for mobile vertical viewing, so the AI prioritizes bold text, close-up visuals, and high-contrast graphics. If your brand aesthetic is subtle or text-heavy, you may need to adjust your prompts to emphasize visual simplicity and punchy messaging. Fifth, the AI WhatsApp Status Video Maker generates finished videos, meaning there's no timeline or editing UI to tweak individual scenes after generation. If you want a different visual or pacing, regenerate the video with a revised prompt rather than expecting manual editing controls. Finally, while Agent Opus supports multiple languages for voiceover, the quality and naturalness of AI voices vary by language. Test a few options to find the best fit for your audience. These limitations are minor compared to the speed and ease of generating WhatsApp Status videos, but knowing them helps you set realistic expectations and optimize your workflow for daily posting.
