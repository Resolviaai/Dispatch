## OpusClip mobile app FAQ

### Is this the official OpusClip mobile app?

Yes, this is the official OpusClip mobile app. Download it from the Apple App Store to get the latest version, updates, and support.

### Can I edit videos in the OpusClip mobile app?

Yes. OpusClip mobile includes a built-in editor so you can polish your clips without leaving your phone. After you generate clips, you can jump into editing to refine the output before you publish.

### Does OpusClip mobile have a subtitle editor?

Yes. The app includes a subtitle editor so you can quickly clean up transcripts, fix wording, correct mistakes, and make sure everything reads naturally before posting.

### Can I style captions (fonts/colors/caption styles) on mobile?

Yes. You can edit and style captions directly in the built-in editor, adjust caption text and apply caption styles (like fonts and colors) to match your brand or personal look.

### What can I do in the mobile app?

Upload a long video, generate multiple short clips with AI, preview results, edit and style captions, apply templates, and save to your camera roll to share on social media.

### What devices are supported?

The iOS app works on iPhone running iOS 16 or newer. (Your App Store listing has the full compatibility details.)
