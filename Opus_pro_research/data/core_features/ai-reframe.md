### **Can I adjust the aspect ratio of my content?**

Absolutely! You can change your aspect ratio in Editor > Clip Layout Settings. You can adjust your aspect ratio to either 9:16 (Portrait), 1:1 (Square), or 16:9 (Landscape).

### **Can I customize my frame?**

Yes! You can use prompts to target specific elements like faces, actions, or objects, giving you control to create personalized and professional results. Meanwhile, you can also manually adjust your frame. [Learn more](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-to-manually-reframe-content)

### **Does AI Reframe handle moving objects or dynamic scenes?**

Absolutely. AI Reframe tracks moving objects, like a ball in sports footage or a speaker walking on stage, ensuring the focus stays on what matters most, even in dynamic environments.
