## Got questions?

### Do I need to adjust the settings manually, or does Speech Enhancement work automatically?

When you turn on Speech Enhancement, OpusClip automatically applies the default levels for Noise Reduction and Voice Volume Enhancement. If you want more control, you can adjust each setting independently to fine-tune the result.

### What does Speech Enhancement improve?

Speech Enhancement reduces background noise while improving voice clarity and volume, helping low-quality recordings sound clearer, louder, and more professional.

### What languages are supported?

All languages are supported. It improves overall audio quality by reducing background noise and enhancing voice clarity and volume, regardless of the language being spoken.

### Does it work on long recordings and podcasts?

Yes, Speech Enhancement supports both long recordings and podcasts. If you'd like to enhance multiple clips, simply select and enhance each clip individually.

### Is Enhance Speech included in the free plan?

Currently, Speech Enhancement is not available on the Free plan. Users on the Pro plan and above can use Speech Enhancement for free up to 10 times per day.
