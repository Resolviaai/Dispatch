## Got questions?

### Does voice cloning happen automatically?

Yes. OpusClip clones the voice from your source video during dubbing. There is no setup and no voice training.

### What if my video has several speakers?

Multi-speaker detection works on all plans. Every speaker is dubbed using the cloned voice of the primary speaker.

### Can I edit the translation after dubbing?

Yes. The translated transcript is editable inline and your changes appear in the subtitles straight away. The dubbed audio track is not regenerated.

### How many languages can I dub into?

25, listed above.

### What does dubbing cost?

10 credits per minute on Pro and Business plans.

### What if my audio is too noisy to clone?

Dubbing needs at least 30 seconds of reasonably clean speech. We show an error instead of shipping a poor clone, and your credits are not spent.

### Can I dub a YouTube video?

Yes. Paste the link, upload a file, or connect Google Drive.
