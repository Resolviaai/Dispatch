### **Why are labor costs and hourly rates so high?**

An **hourly rate** represents the amount a video editor charges per hour of work, typically ranging from $50-$150 per hour for professional services. A standard 10-hour edit project costs between $500-$1,500 in labor alone. This creates significant barriers for solo creators and small businesses who need consistent content output but lack budget for skilled professionals.

### **What causes multi-platform formatting bottlenecks?**

A **formatting bottleneck** occurs when creators must manually resize, re-caption, and retarget a single video for multiple platforms. Each platform demands specific requirements:

• **YouTube Shorts**: 9:16 aspect ratio, 60-second maximum

• **Instagram Feed**: 1:1 square format, optimized captions

• **TikTok**: 9:16 vertical, trending audio integration

• **LinkedIn**: 16:9 horizontal, professional tone

This process averages 3-4 hours per video, inflating total production costs by 40-60% beyond initial creation time.

### **What hidden expenses increase video production costs?**

Software licensing fees range from $30-$100 monthly per tool, with most creators requiring 3-5 different applications. A **revision cycle** represents the number of times a video undergoes re-editing after stakeholder feedback, averaging 2-3 rounds per project. Cloud storage costs approximately $0.02 per GB monthly, accumulating rapidly with high-resolution footage. [According to industry research](https://www.surveymonkey.com/mp/ai-marketing-statistics/), 90% of marketers plan to adopt AI tools to curb these hidden expenses.
