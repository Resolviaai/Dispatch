## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even with advanced AI video tools, certain pitfalls consistently trip up creators. Avoid these common errors:

- **Vague prompts:** "Make a cool video" gives AI nothing to work with. Specific, detailed briefs produce better results regardless of generation speed.
- **Ignoring subject consistency:** If your video features recurring characters or products, verify they maintain consistent appearance across scenes.
- **Skipping the review step:** Faster generation can encourage rushing. Always review before publishing.
- **Over-relying on single models:** Multi-model platforms exist because different models excel at different tasks. Let the platform leverage this diversity.
- **Forgetting audio:** Visual generation improvements don't help if your video lacks proper voiceover, music, or sound design.
- **Neglecting aspect ratios:** A video optimized for YouTube looks wrong on TikTok. Plan your format requirements from the start.

## Pro Tips for Better AI Video Results

Experienced creators have learned techniques that consistently improve AI video output. Apply these strategies to your projects:

- **Use reference descriptions:** Instead of abstract concepts, describe scenes in terms of specific visual references. "Bright, airy office like an Apple commercial" communicates more than "modern workspace."
- **Break complex scenes into components:** If a scene involves multiple elements, describe each clearly rather than hoping the AI interprets a complex prompt correctly.
- **Plan for transitions:** Consider how scenes will connect. Consistent lighting, color palette, and visual style across scenes makes assembly smoother.
- **Leverage voiceover for pacing:** AI-generated voiceover helps establish rhythm and timing that visual generation can match.
- **Test with shorter versions first:** Before committing to a full-length video, generate a condensed version to validate your approach.
