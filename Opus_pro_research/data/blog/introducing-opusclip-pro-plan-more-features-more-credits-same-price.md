## **What’s changed?**

###### In short, we launched two new plans - **Pro Plan** and **Starter Plan**.

###### OpusClip Pro Plan comes with more AI features and 50% more credits than before, and is designed for professional creators, marketers, and teams; while Starter Plan comes with basic features and is designed for individual creators.

## What’s included in the Pro Plan?

OpusClip Pro Plan is our latest offering with all of the most advanced AI features. Designed for professional creators, marketers, and teams, it aims to help you create more visually-engaging shorts with less time and more efficiency.

Key *new* features Include:

### AI B-Roll

Our AI adds contextually relevant b-rolls to your clips to enhance storytelling and increase visual appeal.

### Team Workspace

You can create your own team workspace, and invite your members to create engaging short videos seamlessly.

### Export to Adobe Premiere Pro

Directly export the short videos to Adobe Premiere Pro as XML files for further editing.

### Social Media Scheduler

Autopost and schedule your shorts to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

### Various Aspect Ratio

Easily switch among 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 aspect ratios so that you can always occupy the most screen space wherever you post.

### Multiple Brand Templates

Create up to 5 shared brand templates with custom fonts, logos, and more to help teams stay consistent and on brand.

For the full list of Pro Plan features and its pricing, please click [here](https://www.opus.pro/pricing).

- Analytics
