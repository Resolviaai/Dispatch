# 7 Best Video Marketing Software for Campaigns in 2026

For a video marketing campaign, OpusClip is our first pick: it turns one flagship video into a run of on-brand clips with captions and brand templates, then schedules them to connected social accounts from one workspace. The other six tools each cover a different stage of a campaign: Descript for editing the hero video, Canva for campaign visuals, HeyGen for translated versions, Buffer and Hootsuite for planning and measuring social posts, and Wistia for hosting and measuring video on your own site. After OpusClip, we ordered the list by where each tool sits in a campaign, then compared price, free-tier limits and how clearly each vendor documents what it does. For a smaller team with a single channel, start with our guide to [video marketing software for small businesses](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-video-marketing-software-small-business).

- **Best for turning one hero video into a multi-channel clip run: OpusClip.** Clipping, captions, brand templates and a scheduler sit in one place.
- **Best for editing the flagship video: Descript.** Editing works like editing a document, and the pricing page lists a Brand Studio on Business.

## How to choose video marketing software for campaigns

A campaign needs more than one video, so match tools to the stages you actually run rather than looking for one app that does everything.

- **Plan the calendar and the channels.** Look for a visual content calendar, tags to group posts by campaign, and a per-channel price you can estimate: Buffer, Hootsuite.
- **Create the hero asset.** Look for transcript editing or template-based design: Descript, Canva.
- **Repurpose it into many posts.** Look for clip selection, captions, reframing and brand templates in one flow: OpusClip. See also [best video repurposing tools](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-video-repurposing-tools).
- **Localize it.** Look for translation and dubbing with a language count you can check: HeyGen, OpusClip (dubbing on Pro and Business), Descript (dubbing on Business).
- **Schedule it.** Look for the networks you post to and whether scheduling is automatic or a reminder: OpusClip, Buffer, Hootsuite, Canva. For a closer look, see [best tools to edit and schedule short-form video](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-tools-edit-schedule-short-form-video).
- **Host and measure.** Look for engagement data per video and links to your marketing platform: Wistia, plus the analytics inside Buffer and Hootsuite.
- **Count seats and channels.** Hootsuite and Descript price per person, Buffer per channel, Wistia per plan with extra users on top.

## 2. Descript: Best for editing the flagship campaign video

*Underlord in Descript. Source: Descript website*

**Considerations:** Pricing is per person and media hours cap usage. The pricing page describes clip creation and drafting social posts as AI features, and we did not find a social scheduler listed there, so it needs a scheduling tool beside it.

- **Choose it if** one hero video needs careful editing by transcript before anything else happens.
- **Skip it if** the main job is scheduling or posting to many channels.

## Red flags to watch for

### Free plans that watermark or cap output

OpusClip Free adds a watermark and stops clips being exportable after 3 days, HeyGen Free watermarks and caps videos at 1 minute, and Wistia Free carries Wistia branding. **Trial test:** export one real campaign asset on the free plan and check what ends up on it.

### Per-channel and per-user pricing that compounds

Buffer charges per channel, Hootsuite and Descript charge per person. **Trial test:** count the channels, seats and brands in a real campaign before comparing plans.

### Scheduling that is not automatic for every network

Buffer lists reminder notifications for some channels, and OpusClip's bulk scheduling is YouTube only. **Trial test:** schedule one post to each network you use and confirm it publishes without a reminder.

### Analytics that stop at the post

A scheduler can show impressions without showing what viewers watched, and Buffer's table does not list advanced analytics for TikTok business profiles or YouTube (Shorts). **Trial test:** find the metric your campaign report needs, such as watch behavior or conversions, before you pay.

### Integrations on the top tier

Wistia's marketing platform connections start at the $329 plan, and HeyGen lists HubSpot and Zapier on Business. **Trial test:** check which plan includes the integration the team needs.

### Prices you cannot confirm

Canva's plan prices do not appear in the page text we read, and Hootsuite's plan cards load with a script. **Trial test:** confirm the price at checkout before comparing plans.

## Frequently asked questions

### What's the best software for video marketing campaigns?

For a campaign built from one hero video, OpusClip fits best: it clips the video, captions and brands each clip, and schedules posts on Pro. Descript, Canva and HeyGen cover creating and localizing assets, Buffer and Hootsuite cover scheduling and reporting, and Wistia covers hosting and engagement data. See [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing).

### What's the best platform for video marketing?

No single platform covers every stage. A common setup pairs a clipping and scheduling tool such as OpusClip with a host such as Wistia for landing pages. For a smaller team, our guide to [video marketing software for small businesses](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-video-marketing-software-small-business) lists lower-cost options.

### How do I turn one video into a whole campaign?

Start with a long video, let a clipping tool pick the strongest moments, caption and brand each clip, then schedule the clips across the weeks of the campaign. OpusClip's [long video to short video tool](https://www.opus.pro/tools/long-video-to-short-video-ai) handles the clipping step.

### Which tool schedules video posts to several networks?

OpusClip (Pro), Buffer, Hootsuite and Canva's Social Content Planner all list scheduling. OpusClip's help center lists YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels and more on Pro, and bulk scheduling is YouTube only. Check each vendor's channel list for the networks you use.

### How do I measure a video marketing campaign?

Combine two views: post performance from a scheduler such as Buffer or Hootsuite, and viewing behavior from a host such as Wistia, which lists heatmaps, engagement graphs and A/B testing on Business. Tag every post by campaign so the numbers can be grouped later.

### Can one tool localize a campaign video?

HeyGen's translator page lists 175+ languages. OpusClip dubs into 25 languages with a clone of the speaker's voice on Pro and Business, and Descript lists translate and dub in 30+ languages on Business. See [OpusClip's AI dubbing](https://www.opus.pro/ai-dubbing).

### What is the best free video marketing software?

OpusClip, Descript, HeyGen, Buffer and Wistia each list a free plan, and Canva lists a Pro free trial. Watermarks, caps and branding differ, so export a real asset first.

### Do these tools replace an agency or a video editor?

Not for complex productions. They automate the repetitive work of clipping, captioning, resizing and scheduling. OpusClip is not a replacement for a full non-linear editor, though Pro can export projects to Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve for further editing.
