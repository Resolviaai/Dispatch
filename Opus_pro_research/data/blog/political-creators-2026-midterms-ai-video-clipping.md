## Common Mistakes Political Creators Make with Video Clipping

Even with powerful AI tools, creators can undermine their own efforts. Avoid these pitfalls:

- **Over-relying on AI selections:** AI identifies engaging moments but cannot assess political nuance. Always review clips for context and accuracy.
- **Ignoring platform differences:** A clip that works on TikTok may flop on LinkedIn. Customize your approach for each audience.
- **Neglecting captions:** Political content often contains names, policy terms, and statistics that auto-captions may miss. Review and correct caption errors.
- **Posting without hooks:** The first three seconds determine whether viewers stay. Ensure each clip opens with a compelling statement or question.
- **Forgetting calls to action:** Every clip should guide viewers toward deeper engagement, whether that means following your account, watching the full video, or visiting your website.
- **Inconsistent posting schedules:** Algorithms reward consistency. Use AI clipping to maintain steady output even during busy news periods.
