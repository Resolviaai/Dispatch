# 12 Meta Muse Prompts for Creators and Video Marketers

**The best Meta Muse prompts for creators give Muse a clear job, a source video, and a rule for when to ask you.** Below are 12 copy-paste prompts that turn Muse into a video assistant: clipping long videos into Reels with OpusClip, writing captions, planning a posting calendar, checking results, and handling the admin around your content. Swap the details in brackets and paste them into Muse.

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, launched on September 8, 2026, and available in the US on iOS, Android, the web at muse.ai, and in WhatsApp. It works in its own cloud browser and keeps going after you close the app. For video editing, these Muse prompts for video have Muse run OpusClip in that browser, because OpusClip does the clipping, captions, vertical framing, and scheduling.

## How to write good Meta Muse prompts

- **Name the tool:** say "in OpusClip" so Muse knows where to do the edit.
- **Give a source:** a link, a folder, or "my latest YouTube video."
- **Define done:** how many clips, how long, which platforms.
- **Set an approval rule:** "Show me before scheduling" protects your accounts.
- **Make it repeat:** "Every Monday" turns a prompt into a routine, since Muse keeps working in the background.

## 12 Meta Muse prompts for creators and video marketers

### Clipping and editing

#### 1. Turn one video into Reels

Open OpusClip and sign in with my saved login. Clip this video: \[link\]. Pick the 4 best vertical clips between 20 and 60 seconds with captions. Show them to me before doing anything else.

#### 2. Clip every new upload

Every Monday, check \[my YouTube channel\] for videos posted last week. Clip each one in OpusClip, keep the 3 strongest moments, and send me the shortlist for approval.

#### 3. Clean up a clip

In OpusClip, remove filler words and long pauses from clips 1 and 3, start clip 2 at the question, and move captions higher so they clear the Instagram buttons.

#### 4. Mine your back catalog

Find my 10 most-viewed YouTube videos from last year. A few at a time, clip each in OpusClip and save the best 2 clips from each to a collection called Evergreen. Do not post anything.

### Captions and copy

These Muse AI prompts for Instagram focus on the words around the video.

#### 5. Write Reels captions in your voice

Write an Instagram caption for each approved clip. Match the tone of my last 10 posts, keep each under 150 characters, and add 3 relevant hashtags.

#### 6. Adapt one clip for every platform

For this clip, write an Instagram Reels caption, a TikTok caption, a YouTube Shorts title, and a LinkedIn post. Use OpusClip's generated copy as a starting point.

#### 7. Rewrite weak hooks

Look at the first line of each clip's transcript. If it does not grab attention in the first 3 seconds, suggest a stronger starting point in the same clip.

### Planning and publishing

#### 8. Build next week's calendar

Take my approved clips and propose a posting schedule for next week: one Reel a day at 11am, with the strongest clip on Tuesday. Wait for my OK, then schedule them in OpusClip.

#### 9. Fill empty days

Every Sunday, check my scheduled posts in OpusClip. If any day next week has nothing, suggest an unused clip from my Evergreen collection and ask me before scheduling it.

#### 10. Cross-post everywhere

Schedule the approved clips to Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts in OpusClip, one platform per day, starting Monday. Ask me before confirming.

### Results and admin

#### 11. Weekly performance check

Every Friday, look at how this week's Reels did. Tell me the top 2 by views and saves, what they had in common, and what kind of clip to look for next week.

#### 12. Handle brand collaboration emails

Check my inbox for brand collaboration requests this week. Summarize each one with the brand, the ask, and the deadline, and draft a polite reply asking for their budget. Do not send anything without my approval.
