## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even with improved AI capabilities, certain approaches still lead to suboptimal results. Avoid these pitfalls:

- **Vague prompts:** Better AI understanding does not mean you can be lazy with descriptions. Specific, detailed prompts still produce better results.
- **Ignoring model strengths:** Different models excel at different things. Let Agent Opus auto-select, or learn which models handle which content types best.
- **Skipping iteration:** Faster generation times mean you can and should iterate. Do not settle for the first output when refinement takes minutes.
- **Overcomplicating single scenes:** Break complex ideas into multiple scenes rather than cramming everything into one generation.
- **Forgetting audio:** Visual improvements are exciting, but videos need sound. Use Agent Opus voiceover and soundtrack features to complete your content.
