## Common Mistakes Independent Podcasters Make With Video Clips

Even with AI assistance, certain errors can undermine your clipping strategy. Avoid these pitfalls:

- **Posting clips without context:** Each clip should make sense to someone who has never heard your show. Add brief text overlays or captions that establish who's speaking and why it matters.
- **Ignoring caption accuracy:** Auto-generated captions sometimes miss technical terms, names, or industry jargon. Always review and correct before posting.
- **Using the same clip everywhere:** Customize your approach for each platform. What works on TikTok may need adjustment for LinkedIn.
- **Forgetting the call to action:** Every clip should tell viewers where to find the full episode. Include your podcast name and a simple instruction like "Full episode in bio."
- **Posting inconsistently:** Algorithms reward regular posting. Three clips per week consistently outperforms ten clips one week and none the next.
