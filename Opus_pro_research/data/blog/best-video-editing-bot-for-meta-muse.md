# Best Video Editing Bot for Meta Muse (2026)

**OpusClip is the best video editing bot for Meta Muse.** Muse is great at running errands across the web, and OpusClip does the part Muse cannot do well by itself: it finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into vertical clips with captions, writes the post copy, and schedules them to Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and more. Muse can open OpusClip in its built-in browser and run the whole job for you.

Meta launched Muse on September 8, 2026. It is a personal AI agent that works inside its own secure virtual machine in Meta's cloud, with a built-in browser you can watch. Meta says Muse can open a browser, fill out forms, send email, and book and pay for things, and it keeps working after you close the app. Muse is available in the US on iOS, Android, the web at muse.ai, and inside WhatsApp, with a free tier and paid plans at $20 and $100 a month.

## Can Meta Muse edit videos?

Not on its own, at least not well. Muse is a general-purpose agent: it browses, fills in forms, manages email, and shops. Meta has not announced a built-in video editor for Muse. What Muse can do is operate the tools you give it access to. So the real question is which video tool Muse should use, and that choice decides whether you get polished Reels or a rough cut.

Meta Muse video editing works best as a handoff. Muse decides what needs to happen and keeps track of it. A dedicated video editing bot does the frame-level work: picking moments, tracking the speaker for vertical framing, and timing captions to the word.

## What a video editing agent for Muse needs to do

- **Work in a browser:** Muse does most of its work in a web browser, so the tool needs a full web app that an agent can use.
- **Find the best moments by itself:** Muse should not have to watch an hour of footage. The tool should rank the moments for it.
- **Make Reels-ready output:** vertical 9:16 framing that follows the speaker, captions for muted viewing, and the right length for the feed.
- **Take edits in plain language:** "remove filler words" or "move captions higher" instead of a timeline.
- **Publish to Instagram and beyond:** the job is not done until the clip is scheduled.
- **Keep your sign-in safe:** Muse supports 1Password, so it can use logins you already have without you pasting passwords into a chat.

Instagram's own editor is free and already in your pocket. It is built for hands-on editing on your phone: you pick the footage, cut it, and add text yourself. ### 3. Desktop editors on your Mac

Meta says Muse can operate apps on your Mac, so in principle it could click through professional software such as Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. These editors give total control, but they are designed for a human at a timeline. An agent clicking through them is slow and easy to break, and you still have to tell it exactly where every cut goes.

**Best for:** flagship videos you want to finish by hand after Muse prepares the files.

### 4. Other browser-based editors

Any web editor is technically reachable through Muse's browser. Simple jobs such as trimming one clip can work. Tools that expect you to drag clips on a timeline are much harder for an agent, and most do not rank the best moments in a long recording.

**Best for:** one-off edits in a tool you already pay for.

### 5. A human editor, managed by Muse

The manual baseline still works. Muse can email a freelancer your footage and brief, follow up, and collect the finished files. Quality is high, but it takes days and costs money on every video.

**Best for:** brand campaigns with a real budget.
