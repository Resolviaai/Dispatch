# 10 Best AI Tools to Edit Raw Footage Into Ready-to-Post Videos in 2026

For raw footage of one person talking to camera, OpusClip is our first pick: its AI Producer takes an unedited recording and, through a chat in Claude or ChatGPT, returns a finished video with cuts, captions, b-roll, music and sound effects, and OpusClip's clipping and editor handle the rest of the path to a post. Descript is the pick for editing by transcript, Gling for stripping bad takes and silences, Kapwing and VEED for browser edits that include screen recordings, and CapCut for free manual editing. We ranked 10 tools by how far each one gets raw, unedited footage toward a video that is ready to post, then by price, free-tier limits and how clearly each vendor documents what it does.

## Key takeaways

- **Best for raw talking-to-camera footage into a finished video: OpusClip.** AI Producer edits in chat; the clipping tool and editor reframe, caption and post.
- **Best for editing raw recordings by editing the transcript: Descript.** It also lists a screen recorder.
- **Best for removing bad takes and silences from a raw recording: Gling.** Desktop only.
- **Best for caption-led short videos: Submagic and Captions.** Both are built around short vertical posts.
- **Best for edits that start with a screen or webcam recording: Kapwing and VEED.** Both record inside the browser editor.
- **Best free starting points:** CapCut Free, Vizard Free, Kapwing Free (watermark) and OpusClip Free (watermark). Check watermarks, export length and credits before committing.

## How to choose

Start with what the raw footage is, then match it to the tools built for that input.

- **If you mainly need to turn one raw talking-to-camera video into a finished post**, look for an editor you can direct in plain language, plus captions and reformatting: OpusClip, Captions.
- **If you mainly need to edit spoken footage by reading and deleting text**, look for transcript editing: Descript, Riverside, Kapwing.
- **If you mainly need to cut out retakes, silences and filler words**, look for bad-take and silence detection you can review before it cuts: Gling, OpusClip, Descript, Submagic.
- **If you mainly need to edit screen recordings**, look for a built-in screen recorder and a timeline: Descript, Kapwing, VEED. OpusClip's AI Producer does not support screen recordings yet.
- **If you mainly need to turn a long raw recording into several short clips**, look for highlight detection, reframing and captions together: OpusClip, Vizard.
- **If you mainly need to edit on a phone or for free**, look for a mobile app and a usable free tier: CapCut, Captions, OpusClip.
- **If you mainly need to record and edit in one place**, look for recording and editing under one plan: Riverside, Kapwing, VEED.

For the narrower talking-head job, see [our talking-head editing tools roundup](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-ai-tools-edit-talking-head-videos); for the wider category, see [our AI video editors roundup](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-ai-video-editors).

## Red flags to watch for

### No way to review automatic cuts

A tool that removes retakes and silences without showing what it cut leaves no way to rescue a good line. **Trial test:** run a recording with a deliberate retake and confirm you can see and undo each cut.

### Watermarks on free exports

OpusClip, Gling and Kapwing list watermarks on their free plans. **Trial test:** export a finished video before deciding the free plan is enough.

### Output length caps

Kapwing Free caps exports at 4 minutes, Vizard Free at 10 minutes and Submagic Starter at 2 minutes per video. **Trial test:** run your normal-length recording, not a short demo clip.

### Credits that expire or meter heavily

VEED credits do not carry over, OpusClip Pro credits roll over for 2 months, and Captions credits roll over for up to three times the monthly allowance. **Trial test:** estimate a normal month of use against the credit allowance, not a single project.

### Input limits that are easy to miss

OpusClip's AI Producer does not support screen recordings or multi-camera edits yet, and Gling is desktop only. **Trial test:** upload your actual file type and camera setup before committing to a plan.

### Prices you cannot confirm

Captions' page shows iOS prices only, CapCut's page says displayed pricing is for reference only, and some pricing pages load their figures with JavaScript. **Trial test:** check the price at checkout before comparing plans.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI tool to edit raw footage?

For raw footage of one person talking to camera, OpusClip fits best: AI Producer edits it in chat and returns a finished video, and the clipping tool and editor handle captions, reframing and posting. Descript, Gling, Kapwing and VEED suit other kinds of raw footage better. See [OpusClip's AI video editor](https://www.opus.pro/ai-video-editor).

### Can AI edit raw footage into a ready-to-post video?

Yes, within limits. Per its own page, OpusClip's AI Producer can cut filler words and pauses and add captions, motion graphics, b-roll, music and sound effects to a talking-to-camera video, and outputs one MP4 at up to 1080p. Review the result before posting, since automatic cuts can miss context.

### Which AI tool removes bad takes and silences automatically?

Gling, OpusClip, Submagic, Descript, Kapwing (Smart Cut for silences) and Riverside all list some form of bad-take, filler or silence removal on their own pages. OpusClip's help center describes bad takes as highlighted inline so they can be reviewed before cutting.

### Can AI edit screen recordings?

Descript, Kapwing and VEED list screen recording alongside editing. OpusClip's AI Producer does not support screen recordings yet, per its page. If a screen recording is the main input, start with one of those three.

### What is the best free AI tool to edit raw footage?

CapCut Free, Vizard Free (60 credits, 720p), Kapwing Free (watermark) and OpusClip Free (60 credits, watermark) are the free starting points, and AI Producer can be started for free. See [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing) for paid plans from $15 a month.

### Can ChatGPT or Claude edit my raw footage?

Through OpusClip's AI Producer, yes: it hands your footage to the Claude connector or the ChatGPT plugin, where you ask for the edit in chat. The AI that plans the edit runs on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan, and a paid ChatGPT plan is needed for the plugin.

### What is the best AI tool to edit talking-head footage for TikTok and Reels?

OpusClip, Submagic and Captions are built around short vertical posts with captions. OpusClip also reframes to 9:16 and posts to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts from the Starter plan. See [editing your real footage with AI](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-video-editing-without-avatars).

### Do AI editors replace a professional editor?

Not for complex timelines. These tools automate the repetitive cuts, captions and formatting on raw footage; OpusClip is not a replacement for a full non-linear editor, though Pro can export projects to Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve for further editing.
