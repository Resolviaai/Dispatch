## **How Do You Prepare Videos for AI Clipping?**

**Properly prepared source material enhances AI accuracy and clip quality by up to 40%.** Well-organized content with clear audio and distinct segments produces better automated results. The AI performs best when it can clearly identify speaker changes, topic transitions, and emotional peaks in your content.

### **How Do You Identify Key Moments?**

**Create a content map** before uploading by marking timestamps for different content types:

**Hooks (0-3 seconds)**: Attention-grabbing openings that stop scroll behavior and draw viewers in immediately.

**Value-adds (middle segments)**: Educational content, insights, or entertainment that provides clear viewer benefit.

**Calls-to-action**: Subscriber requests, product mentions, or engagement prompts that drive specific actions.

Use a simple spreadsheet or video player's bookmark feature to note these moments. Consider using this template structure:

- **Timestamp** | **Content Type** | **Description** | **Platform Suitability**

This systematic approach ensures important content isn't overlooked during AI processing and helps you understand which segments perform best across different platforms.

### **What Platform Specifications Should You Consider?**

**Aspect ratio and duration limits vary significantly** across platforms, and proper preparation prevents reformatting work later:

**TikTok Requirements**:

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Maximum duration: several minutes (TikTok has raised the limit well past 60 seconds)
- Optimal hook time: First 3 seconds

**Instagram Reels**:

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Maximum duration: 3 minutes to be recommended to non-followers
- Best performance: 15-30 seconds

**YouTube Shorts**:

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Maximum duration: 3 minutes (since October 2024)
- Algorithm preference: 45-60 seconds

**Instagram Feed Posts**:

- Aspect ratio: 1:1 (square) or 4:5
- Maximum duration: videos now post as reels (3 minutes for recommendations)
- Engagement sweet spot: 15-30 seconds

**Twitter/X Video**:

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape) or 1:1 (square)
- Maximum duration: 140 seconds
- Optimal length: 30-60 seconds

Understanding these constraints allows [AI reframing tools](https://www.opus.pro/ai-reframe) to automatically optimize your content for each platform without manual adjustments.
