## Common Mistakes Creator-Entrepreneurs Make

Even with powerful tools, strategy matters. Avoid these pitfalls:

- **Treating all platforms identically:** What works on TikTok does not automatically work on LinkedIn. Customize your clip selection for each audience.
- **Ignoring captions:** Most social video is watched without sound. Always include captions, and OpusClip handles this automatically.
- **Over-automating without review:** AI suggestions are excellent starting points, but human judgment should guide final selections. Review clips before publishing.
- **Neglecting brand consistency:** When managing multiple ventures, it is easy to let visual standards slip. Use brand kits to maintain professionalism.
- **Forgetting the call to action:** Every clip should have a purpose. Make sure viewers know what to do next, whether that is visiting a product page or watching the full video.

## Pro Tips for Maximum Impact

- **Repurpose your best performers:** When a clip does well on one platform, adapt it for others. Success often translates across platforms with minor adjustments.
- **Create content series:** Numbered or themed clip series build anticipation and encourage followers to watch multiple pieces.
- **Use hooks from your long-form content:** The most engaging moments in your podcasts or videos often make the best standalone clips. Trust the AI to find them.
- **Test different caption styles:** Some audiences prefer minimal captions while others engage more with animated text. Experiment to find what works.
- **Batch your review sessions:** Instead of reviewing clips one at a time, batch your approval process to maintain efficiency.
