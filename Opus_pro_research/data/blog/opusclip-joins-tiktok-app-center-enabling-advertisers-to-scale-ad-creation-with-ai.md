OpusClip Joins TikTok App Center, Enabling Advertisers to Scale Ad Creation with AI

August 23, 2024

OpusClip brings its AI video editing tool to TikTok App Center, helping advertisers of all sizes create high-performing ad creatives at scale from long video assets, with a single click.

TikTok App Center is available from July 30, 2024. OpusClip is among the first to join TikTok's App Center, an easy-to-access hub for advertisers to seamlessly find various apps to solve their marketing needs. The apps in the App Center complement TikTok Ads Manager by offering specialized, budget-friendly solutions with free trials, to help brands thrive effortlessly on TikTok's platform.


For any brands that want to create high-performing ad creatives at scale, OpusClip can edit your product review footage or testimonials into more than 10 polished ads, ranked by virality score, which assesses video quality and potential reach. Advertisers are seeing significant ROI with OpusClip:

Produce 3x more short video ads

Save 90% editing cost, with zero learning curve

10x faster turnaround time

Increase click rate by 10+% with ad hook score

Using OpusClip to create ads videos is very easy. All you need to do is dropping your videos to OpusClip, and you will get dozens of clips in 10 minutes.

Advertisers can use OpusClip in different ways to boost their ads creation:

Create UGC testimonial ads at scale: Record videos of influencers reviewing products, then use OpusClip to generate highlight reels with animated captions. This helps you create powerful organic-looking testimonial ads, which is a top-performing creative style on TikTok.

Generate ad variations for A/B testing: Use OpusClip to cut raw video assets into multiple hooks and highlight clips, accelerating ad creation for experimentation.

Re-formating: Instantly resize 16:9 TV commercials to 9:16 or 1:1 aspect ratio 60-second short ads.

Expand global reach: With support for over 20 languages and the ability to translate captions, OpusClip enables advertisers to distribute and gain exposure globally.

Trusted by worldwide ads strategists

Let OpusClip help your brand get started on TikTok. Now available in TikTok's App Center, in the "Creative" category. Click here to use OpusClip now.
