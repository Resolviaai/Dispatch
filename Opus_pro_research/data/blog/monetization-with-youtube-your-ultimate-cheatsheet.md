## **What Are the Complete YouTube Partner Program Requirements?**

More eyeballs on your videos equal more money in your Adsense account. Simple.

Here's how to join the [YouTube Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) fast and maximize monetization:

- Follow YouTube's monetization policies
- Reside in a region where the program is available
- Get 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers with 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days
- Link your channel to an AdSense account
- Have no active Community Guidelines strikes

Start with the classic—AdSense. It pays ad revenue on your YouTube videos based on view count. One of the conditions for joining the YouTube Partner Program is having an Adsense account.

Here's how to set up your Google AdSense account:

- Make sure your YouTube content ticks all the boxes for AdSense eligibility
- Go to the AdSense website and click "Sign Up Now"
- Enter your YouTube channel and payment details
- Link your AdSense account to your YouTube channel in YouTube Studio

## **Frequently Asked Questions About YouTube Monetization**

### **How much money can you make from YouTube monetization?**

YouTube earnings vary significantly based on factors like niche, audience size, engagement rates, and monetization methods used. Creators typically earn $1-5 per 1,000 views from ad revenue alone, but successful creators often make more from alternative revenue streams like sponsorships, merchandise, and memberships. Top creators can earn millions annually, while smaller channels might make anywhere from $100-10,000 per month depending on their strategy and audience engagement.

### **Can you monetize YouTube Shorts in 2025?**

Yes, YouTube Shorts can be monetized through the YouTube Partner Program. Creators earn from the Shorts Fund and ad revenue sharing. To qualify, you need either 1,000 subscribers with 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days, or meet the traditional requirements of 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 watch hours. Shorts monetization has become increasingly important as short-form content continues to dominate social media consumption.

### **What happens if your YouTube channel gets demonetized?**

Demonetization means you lose the ability to earn ad revenue from your videos, but you can still use other monetization methods like channel memberships, Super Chat, and merchandise. Common causes include community guideline violations, copyright strikes, or inappropriate content. You can appeal demonetization decisions through YouTube Studio, and once issues are resolved, monetization can typically be restored. Prevention is key - always follow YouTube's community guidelines and monetization policies.

### **How long does it take to get approved for YouTube monetization?**

YouTube monetization approval typically takes 1-4 weeks after you meet the eligibility requirements and submit your application. The review process includes checking your channel for compliance with community guidelines, copyright policies, and advertiser-friendly content standards. During peak periods, reviews may take longer. You can check your application status in YouTube Studio under the "Monetization" tab.

### **Do you need a certain number of views to start making money on YouTube?**

There's no specific view count requirement to start making money on YouTube, but you do need to meet subscriber and watch time thresholds. For the YouTube Partner Program, you need 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 watch hours in the past 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in the past 90 days. However, you can start earning through other methods like affiliate marketing, sponsorships, or selling products regardless of your subscriber count, as long as you have an engaged audience.
