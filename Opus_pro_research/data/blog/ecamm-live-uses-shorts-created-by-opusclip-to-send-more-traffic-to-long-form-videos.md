Ecamm Live uses shorts created by OpusClip to send more traffic to long-form videos

August 31, 2023

Introduction

Paul Duncan is an amazing volunteer moderator for Ecamm, a powerful live streaming app. He helps the team on YouTube videos behind the scenes, including building a searchable database for Ecamm, updating video transcripts, adding video chapter markers, and repurposing content.

Use shorts to attract more traffic back to long-form videos

Paul would find interesting snippets or a concept that people may not have thought about into quality shorts, which can lead viewers into the long form content Ecamm has within their YouTube channel and elsewhere.



What drew you in

Jaw-dropping speed in creating high-quality clips

Paul came to know OpusClip through Mr. Camera Junkie, an amazing content creator and Ecamm advocate several months ago shortly after OpusClip was launched. When he loaded the video and got the clips, he was amazed at how little time it took OpusClip to do what it can do, versus how much more time for a person to go through the video trying to pick out all the different pieces, stitch them together and then edit them.


Result

Paul gets more time back thanks to OpusClip

To Paul, the biggest value OpusClip has brought to him is the time saving aspect. For every video repurposing task, OpusClip can save him at least one hour. As he said "When you're doing six or eight videos a week, having to go through and grab content from livestreams, it's a tremendous amount of time."

Paul also really enjoys how OpusClip ranks clips. It's not 100% correct, but those clips that Opus gets are like spot on.
