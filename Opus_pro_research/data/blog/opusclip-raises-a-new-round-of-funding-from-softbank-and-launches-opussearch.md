# OpusClip raises a new round of funding from SoftBank Vision Fund and launches OpusSearch

We're excited to announce that OpusClip has secured new funding from SoftBank Vision Fund 2, marking another important milestone in our journey to empower creators and businesses through AI video editing.

This investment comes at an exciting moment for OpusClip, as we continue to see remarkable growth. Over [10 million creators and brands](https://www.opus.pro/yearinreview) worldwide, including renowned organizations such as Univision, Billboard, iHeartMedia, Visa, LinkedIn, Jubilee, Impact Theory, and Grant Cardone, have leveraged our platform to create more than 172 million video clips in the last year alone, accumulating over 57 billion views. This growth underscores our mission to amplify authentic video storytelling with AI and enable creators to unlock greater potential from their content.

"OpusClip's AI tool is changing everything. OpusClip isn't just for media companies - it's for any business owner who needs to maintain a social presence without sacrificing hours for video editing." said [Tom Bilyeu](https://www.youtube.com/c/TomBilyeu), CEO of Impact Theory.


## Introducing OpusSearch: Monetize your video catalog with ease

Building on our momentum, we're thrilled to introduce our latest product—[OpusSearch](https://www.opus.pro/opussearch), designed specifically for professional creators and businesses.

After working with 10M+ creators over the past 1.5 years, we’ve noticed three major things:

▶︎ Most professional creators and brands invest heavily in producing valuable content, but much of it remains underutilized. As a result, they are losing millions of dollars on the table every year

▶︎ Social algorithms increasingly prioritize audience-content fit

▶︎ Quickly hopping onto trends with relevant clips is essential for staying visible and engaging audiences effectively

OpusSearch addresses these challenges by serving as your custom AI brain that knows your video better than you do. With OpusSearch, professional creators can:

▶︎ Instantly search and reuse anything in their video catalogs

▶︎ Easily create a network of topic-specific channels for distinct audiences

▶︎ Respond to trends in minutes instead of hours

▶︎ Turn video archives into new revenue stream

Top creators like [Jubilee](https://www.youtube.com/@jubilee), [Nate Bargatze](https://www.youtube.com/@nateland), and [Zack Justice](https://www.instagram.com/zachjustice/?hl=en) have already started using OpusSearch and are seeing impressive results, with creators typically [achieving positive ROI within just two months](https://www.opus.pro/from-zero-to-monetization-for-a-new-youtube-channel-in-40-days).

*"Our team at Jubilee Media is stoked to be among the first to explore how OpusClip and OpusSearch can streamline our short-form post-production process for the hundreds of videos we produce each year."*

*Zack Warren, Creative Director at Jubilee Media*

Our mission with OpusSearch is simple: empower professional creators to reclaim control over their content and maximize their revenue opportunities. We’re gradually [opening up access](https://www.opus.pro/contact-videosearch) to ensure a high-quality experience for all users.
