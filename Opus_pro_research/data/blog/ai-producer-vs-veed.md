# AI Producer vs VEED (2026): Which AI Video Editor Should You Use?

**Short answer:** pick [AI Producer by OpusClip](https://producer.opus.pro/plugin) if you want a talking-head video edited for you inside ChatGPT: upload your footage, describe the result, and get a finished cut with captions, B-roll, motion graphics and music, with most of that editing at no OpusClip cost. Pick VEED if you want a browser editor where you run each AI tool yourself on a timeline, with extras like eye contact correction and AI avatars. Both edit real footage. The difference is who does the editing.

Below is a side-by-side look at how each one works, what each costs, where each is stronger, and a brief you can run in both. Facts about VEED come from VEED's own pricing, product and help pages, checked on October 7, 2026.

## AI Producer vs VEED at a glance

- **How you edit.** AI Producer: describe the edit in a ChatGPT chat and review a finished cut. VEED: a timeline editor in the browser, with one-click AI tools you apply clip by clip.
- **Where it runs.** AI Producer: a plugin in ChatGPT's Plugin Directory, running in Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app. VEED: web app, plus iOS and Android apps.
- **Price.** AI Producer: most editing in the plugin uses no OpusClip credits, and the work counts against your ChatGPT plan's Codex usage. VEED: Free, then Creator at $25, Pro at $45 and Studio at $79 per user per month on monthly billing, according to VEED's pricing page.
- **Free tier limits.** VEED's Free plan exports are watermarked, capped at 720p and 10 minutes, with 10 minutes of auto subtitles a month, and most AI tools are "Try once". AI Producer exports at up to 1080p.
- **Filler words and pauses.** AI Producer: part of the automatic rough cut. VEED: Magic Cut, Remove Silences and Remove Filler Words tools.
- **B-roll and motion graphics.** AI Producer: B-roll matched to your words and motion graphics drawn to match them, placed for you. VEED: AI B-Roll and a stock library you add in the editor.
- **Extras.** VEED adds eye contact correction, background removal, AI avatars, dubbing and 4K export on higher plans. AI Producer adds scene restyle, camera movement and auto layout.
- **Inside ChatGPT.** AI Producer edits your uploaded footage in the chat. VEED's ChatGPT plugin generates AI avatar videos, and its custom GPT creates a VEED project and links you to the VEED editor.

## How editing works in AI Producer

You install AI Producer from the Plugins section of ChatGPT, open a Codex chat in the ChatGPT desktop app, and attach a video of one person talking to camera. Then you write a brief the way you'd brief an editor: the platform, the mood, and anything you don't want. The plugin reads the footage and transcript, removes filler words and pauses, and adds captions, B-roll, motion graphics, music and sound effects. When a choice matters, such as caption style, it shows options with previews right in the chat.

You review the cut in the AI Producer preview and ask for changes in plain words: bigger captions, no music, a tighter opening. Each change updates the same project, and export renders an MP4 at up to 1080p in 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1. Our guide to [editing videos in ChatGPT](/blog/can-chatgpt-edit-videos) walks through the setup.

## How editing works in VEED

VEED is a timeline editor first. Its help center calls the timeline "the most used place to edit." You upload footage in the browser and apply AI tools such as Magic Cut, Eye Contact, Clean Audio and AI B-Roll, then review what each one changed on the timeline. Edit by Script lets you cut footage by deleting words from the transcript.

VEED also offers guided flows. Auto-Edits takes raw footage and applies the edits you toggle, and Clips turns long videos into short ones. For natural-language editing, VEED's open-source OpenEdit, which VEED labels a beta, runs inside coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex and turns a described edit into an MP4, though its README says "There is no GUI and no timeline."

## Pricing compared

- **AI Producer in ChatGPT.** Transcription, captions, B-roll search, voice enhancement, music and sound effects use no OpusClip credits in the plugin, so you can stay on a free OpusClip plan. Scene restyle, which redraws your background with AI, uses credits. The editing counts against your ChatGPT plan's Codex usage, which is limited on Free and Go plans and larger on paid plans.
- **VEED Free.** $0, with watermarked 720p exports up to 10 minutes, 10 minutes of auto subtitles a month, and one try of most AI tools.
- **VEED Creator.** $25 per user per month, or $147 per user per year, with watermark-free 1080p exports and 500 AI credits a month.
- **VEED Pro.** $45 per user per month, or $265 per user per year, with 4K export and 2,500 AI credits a month.
- **VEED Studio.** $79 per user per month, or $465 per user per year. Enterprise pricing is custom.

VEED prices are for US visitors as shown on VEED's pricing page on October 7, 2026, and VEED notes its plans are charged per seat.

## Where VEED is stronger

- **Hands-on control.** If you like adjusting every cut on a timeline, VEED is built for that.
- **More tools in one place.** Eye contact correction, background removal, AI avatars and dubbing are all in the same editor.
- **Works on more devices.** VEED runs in any browser and has iOS and Android apps. AI Producer's plugin needs the ChatGPT desktop app for now.
- **Higher resolution.** VEED's Pro plan and up export in 4K.

## Where AI Producer is stronger

- **A finished cut from one brief.** You describe the result once and review an edit, instead of running tools one at a time.
- **Editing inside ChatGPT.** If you already plan and write in ChatGPT, the edit happens in the same place, with no new app to learn.
- **Most editing at no OpusClip cost.** Captions, B-roll, cleanup and music in the plugin use no OpusClip credits.
- **Graphics built from your words.** Motion graphics and B-roll are chosen from what you say and placed on the right words.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose AI Producer if** you film yourself talking to camera, want a polished first cut without editing it yourself, and already use ChatGPT on desktop.

**Choose VEED if** you want full manual control in a browser timeline, need tools like eye contact correction or AI avatars, or edit mostly on your phone.

Plenty of creators will use both: AI Producer to get from raw footage to a strong first cut fast, and a timeline editor for the occasional project that needs frame-by-frame work.

## Try the same edit in both

The fairest test is one brief on one clip. Film a 60-second talking-head video and give each tool this:

Cut the filler words and long pauses. Add bold captions in the lower third, a few B-roll shots on the key points, and quiet background music. Make it 9:16 for Reels.

In AI Producer, paste it into your Codex chat with the clip attached. In VEED, apply the matching tools one by one. Then compare the result and how long it took you to get there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is VEED free?

VEED has a Free plan. According to VEED's pricing page, Free exports are watermarked and capped at 720p and 10 minutes, auto subtitles are limited to 10 minutes a month, and most AI tools can be tried once.

### Is AI Producer free?

Most editing in the AI Producer plugin uses no OpusClip credits, including captions, B-roll, voice cleanup, music and sound effects. AI scene restyle uses credits, and the editing counts against your ChatGPT plan's Codex usage.

### Can VEED edit videos inside ChatGPT?

Not the way AI Producer does. VEED's ChatGPT plugin generates AI avatar videos, and its custom GPT creates a VEED project and links you to the VEED editor to finish the work. AI Producer edits the footage you upload, inside the chat.

### Which is better for talking-head videos?

For a fast, polished first cut with captions, B-roll and graphics, AI Producer, because it builds the whole edit from one brief. For hands-on control and extras like eye contact correction, VEED.

### Does AI Producer have eye contact correction?

No. Eye contact correction isn't one of AI Producer's editing skills, so if it's essential to your videos, VEED offers it.

### What is a good free VEED alternative?

If you already use ChatGPT, AI Producer by OpusClip is a strong option: most of its editing uses no OpusClip credits, and it works inside ChatGPT instead of a separate editor.
