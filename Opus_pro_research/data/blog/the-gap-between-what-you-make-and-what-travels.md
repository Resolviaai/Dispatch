Big media brands publish long-form video every day, and their audiences already live on platforms built for short clips. Yet four large channels we looked at posted no Shorts in 60 days, and one show whose last ten Shorts averaged almost half a million views has not posted one in over two years. This piece looks at what these teams make, what travels, and why the clips stop.

It is not a talent problem. It is a capacity problem

The team behind a 21 million subscriber show knows how to cut a clip. The clips it did publish average close to half a million views each. It stopped anyway.

Short-form loses when it competes with the episode for the same editors and the same hours, and it always competes, because it is treated as optional. Every day the gap stays open, another multi-hour episode joins a catalog nobody is clipping.
