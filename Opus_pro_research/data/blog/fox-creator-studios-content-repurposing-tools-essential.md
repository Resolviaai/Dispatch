Track which clips perform best on each platform. Use these insights to refine what you look for when selecting future clips. Over time, you will develop an intuition for what works, and your AI tools will help you find more of it.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even with the right tools, creators sometimes undermine their repurposing efforts. Watch out for these pitfalls:

- **Posting identical content everywhere:** Each platform has unique culture and expectations. Adapt your clips accordingly.
- **Ignoring captions:** A significant portion of social media viewing happens with sound off. Captions are not optional.
- **Inconsistent branding:** Random fonts, colors, and styles make your content look amateur. Use brand kits religiously.
- **Neglecting hooks:** The first 2-3 seconds determine whether viewers keep watching. Ensure every clip opens strong.
- **Over-repurposing single videos:** Audiences notice when you extract too many clips from one source. Balance depth with variety.
- **Skipping quality review:** AI tools are powerful but not perfect. Always review clips before publishing.
