AI video editors excel at automating repetitive tasks and providing intelligent suggestions, but they cannot fully replace human creativity and strategic thinking in 2025. Most modern AI video editing platforms are cloud-based, requiring only a stable internet connection and a modern web browser. However, for optimal performance, we recommend at least 8GB RAM, a recent processor (Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 equivalent), and a dedicated graphics card for local processing features. Some platforms like Runway ML may require more powerful hardware for advanced AI features, while others like OpusClip work well on standard business computers.

### **How accurate are AI-generated subtitles and transcriptions?**

AI-generated subtitles and transcriptions have achieved 95%+ accuracy rates in 2025 for clear audio in common languages like English, Spanish, and French. Tools like Descript and OpusClip use advanced speech recognition models that handle multiple speakers, accents, and background noise effectively. However, accuracy can decrease with poor audio quality, heavy accents, or technical terminology. Most platforms allow easy manual corrections and learn from user edits to improve future accuracy.
