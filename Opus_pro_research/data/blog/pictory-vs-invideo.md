# Pictory vs. InVideo: Which Video Tool Is Right for You?

With so many video editing tools on the market, finding the one that fits your content workflow can be a challenge. Two popular options, Pictory and InVideo, both promise to simplify video creation, but they serve different types of creators and use cases.

Pictory focuses on turning long-form text or video content into short, repurposed clips using AI. InVideo, on the other hand, offers a massive library of customizable templates for fast, branded content creation.

In this post, we'll compare the two across features, pricing, ease of use, and best use cases, so you can confidently choose the tool that fits your style and goals.

## **Main Takeaways:**

- Pictory is ideal for fast, automated video creation from text or long-form content, making it perfect for content repurposing
- InVideo offers enhanced creative control with customizable templates, suited for marketers wanting polished, branded videos
- Pictory's AI handles most of the heavy lifting, while InVideo gives you full editing freedom via a timeline-based interface
- If you value speed and simplicity, Pictory wins; if customization and visual detail matter more, InVideo is the better fit
- Both tools offer beginner-friendly experiences, but Pictory has a gentler learning curve thanks to its automation-first design

## **What Is Pictory?**

Pictory is an AI-powered video creation tool that transforms text and long videos into short, engaging content without requiring editing skills. You simply upload your script or recording, and the platform automatically generates a video with matching visuals, captions, and music.

The platform shines when you need to [repurpose existing content](https://www.opus.pro/blog/repurposing-video-content-with-ai-for-maximum-roi) like blog posts, podcast episodes, or webinar recordings. Many content creators struggle with the time it takes to create videos from scratch, and Pictory solves this by automating the most tedious parts.

### **Pictory Features & Benefits**

- **Automated video creation:** Turn blog posts, scripts, or long videos into short clips with AI assistance
- **Content repurposing:** Extract the best moments from webinars, Zoom calls, and interviews
- **Auto-captioning:** Generate accurate subtitles that boost engagement in sound-off environments
- **Voice synthesis:** Convert text to natural-sounding voiceovers in multiple languages

Pictory works best for [content marketers](https://www.opus.pro/blog/the-benefits-of-using-ai-video-tools-for-your-marketing-campaigns), coaches, and businesses looking to increase their video output without hiring editors.

### **Pictory Pricing**

The Pictory free plan lets you create up to 3 videos per month with a watermark, perfect for testing the platform. For serious creators, Pictory pricing plans start at $19/month for the Starter tier, which includes 200 video minutes, basic AI tools, and removes watermarks.

Higher tiers offer more credits and advanced features like voice cloning. All Pictory pricing plans include access to stock media and the core AI features that make video creation quick and painless.

**Time-Saving Tip:** Pictory's text-to-video feature can turn a 1,500-word blog post into a 2-minute video in under 10 minutes, complete with visuals and captions.

## **What Is InVideo?**

InVideo is a template-based video editor focused on creative customization and professional-looking results. Unlike Pictory's automation-first approach, InVideo gives you hands-on control over every element in your video.

The platform offers thousands of templates for different video types, from social media posts to advertisements. You can drag and drop elements, add text animations, and adjust timing to create videos that match your exact vision.

### **InVideo Features & Benefits**

- **Template library:** Access 5,000+ ready-made video templates for every platform and purpose
- **Creative control:** Customize layers, animations, and transitions with precise timeline editing
- **Text effects:** Add eye-catching animated text that captures attention in crowded feeds
- **Stock assets:** Choose from millions of royalty-free videos, images, and music tracks

InVideo appeals to social media managers, small businesses, and creators who want polished videos with a distinctive look.

### **InVideo Pricing**

InVideo offers a free plan with watermarked exports and basic features. Paid plans start at $28/month, giving you unlimited exports without watermarks and access to premium templates.

The Plus plan ($28/month) adds team collaboration features and more premium stock footage options. All plans include the core editing tools that make InVideo popular with video creators.

## **Pictory vs. InVideo: Detailed Comparison for Creators**

When comparing InVideo vs Pictory, the choice depends on your specific workflow and content goals. Let's break down the key differences to help you decide.

### **User Interface & Ease of Use**

Pictory features a streamlined interface focused on automation. You'll find wizard-like workflows that guide you through each step of video creation. The learning curve is minimal, making it perfect if you're new to video editing.

InVideo offers a more traditional editor with layers, timelines, and creative controls. While not difficult to learn, it requires more hands-on work to achieve your desired result. The trade-off is greater creative freedom.

- **Pictory's strength:** Get results quickly with minimal learning
- **InVideo's strength:** More control for those who want to fine-tune every detail

### **Text-to-Video & Script-Based Editing**

When evaluating InVideo AI vs Pictory, the text-to-video capabilities show clear differences in approach.

Pictory excels at [turning written content into videos](https://www.opus.pro/blog/how-to-use-ai-to-create-video). You paste your script, and the AI analyzes your text to select relevant visuals, create scene breaks, and generate a complete video draft. This automation saves hours of [manual work](https://www.opus.pro/blog/how-to-automate-video-editing-with-ai-video-editing-software).

InVideo allows you to add text to videos, but doesn't automatically generate scenes from your script. You'll need to manually select footage and build your video scene by scene.

### **Editing Tools & Workflow**

The InVideo vs. Pictory comparison shows different philosophies about the editing process:

- **Pictory workflow:**
  1. Upload content (text or video)
  2. Let AI generate a draft
  3. Make light edits to refine
  4. Export and share
- **InVideo workflow:**
  1. Choose a template
  2. Add your media and text
  3. Customize every element
  4. Preview, adjust, and export

Pictory saves time through automation, while InVideo gives you more creative control at the cost of additional work.

### **Stock Library & Assets**

Both platforms provide access to stock media, but with different approaches.

Pictory's AI automatically selects stock footage based on your script's content. This hands-off approach saves time but sometimes requires manual adjustments to get the perfect visual match.

InVideo offers a larger library of stock assets that you can browse and select yourself. This gives you more control over the look and feel of your video, but requires more time spent searching and selecting.

### **Captions, Subtitles & Accessibility**

In a mobile-first world, captions are essential for video engagement.

Pictory automatically generates captions from your script or video's audio. These captions are styled to match your video's look and can be edited if needed. The process is almost entirely automated.

InVideo lets you add caption text manually, with options to animate and style it. While this gives you more creative control, it takes more time to implement, especially for longer videos.



### **Pricing & Plans** 

Both Pictory and InVideo offer free trials, but the value of their paid plans depends on your content needs.

Pictory's free version includes watermarking and limited exports, while paid tiers (starting at $19/month) unlock longer video processing and additional branding features, ideal for solo marketers or educators.

InVideo's free plan also includes watermarked exports, but its paid options (from $28/month) offer unlimited exports, making it a better fit for high-volume social creators or small teams producing content regularly.

Both tools support individual users, with InVideo offering more team-friendly collaboration features on higher plans.

### **Target Audience & Use Cases**

The InVideo AI vs. Pictory comparison shows they serve different creative needs:

**Pictory works best for:**

- Content marketers repurposing blog posts into videos
- Coaches turning webinars into social clips
- Businesses creating product explainers from scripts
- Anyone who needs videos but lacks editing skills

**InVideo excels for:**

- Social media managers creating platform-specific content
- Marketers making ads with precise branding requirements
- Small businesses creating promotional videos
- Anyone who enjoys the creative editing process

### **Customer Support & Community**

Both platforms offer help centers and tutorial libraries to get you started.

Pictory provides comprehensive onboarding resources designed for non-editors. Their support focuses on helping you understand the AI features and get results quickly.

InVideo has built a larger community of users who share templates, tips, and creative ideas. Their support extends beyond technical help to include creative inspiration and best practices.
