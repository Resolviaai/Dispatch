## Clip, edit, posting, search, automate. All in OpusClip

Over the past few months, we have rolled out a lot of exciting upgrades, all focused on helping you create videos and grow audiences faster, with less effort. From smarter AI models, automated workflow, to a faster editor, OpusClip now brings your whole video creation workflow into one seamless platform.

#### The smartest AI clipping & reframe models got smarter

We recently upgraded ClipAnything and ReframeAnything, our proprietary AI models, to make them even sharper and more versatile.

The new ClipAnything picks 32.5% more relevant clips and now supports more video types, like gaming, vlogs, documentaries, and music.

ReframeAnything lets you resize any video for any platform and keeps moving subjects centered with AI object tracking. And if you want more control, manual tracking lets you instruct AI exactly what to follow — just like Tesla’s FSD, you get the ease of autopilot with the option to take control anytime.

#### Auto import, edit, and schedule

We’re making your workflow more automated than ever. OpusClip now automatically imports videos from your YouTube channel and generates clips without you lifting a finger. And with auto scheduling coming soon, posting will become even more hands-free. It’s all about helping you stay consistent while doing less manual work.

#### A growing suite of AI editing tools

OpusClip is no longer just an AI clipping tool. It's evolving into a powerful, all-in-one AI video editor, built not only to speed up your workflow, but to help you grow faster.

Over the past few months, we've added a growing suite of AI editing tools designed to handle more of your creative needs. From [AI Captioning](https://clip.opus.pro/dashboard/caption?) and [AI B-Roll](https://clip.opus.pro/dashboard/broll?), to [Audio Enhancement](https://clip.opus.pro/dashboard/enhance_speech) and [AI Thumbnail Generator](https://clip.opus.pro/youtube-thumbnail-maker), you now have more flexibility to make quick tweaks to your videos.

#### The new editor

We’ve redesigned the editing experience with a new interface and more intuitive AI tools. The new OpusClip AI editor is 50% faster and 60% smoother, and gives you just the right level of control without slowing you down.

#### OpusSearch

We recently launched [OpusSearch](https://www.opus.pro/opussearch), the first AI growth agent that can automatically search, reuse, and monetize any video from your catalog. Designed for professional creators and brands with large video libraries, OpusSearch turns their old videos into new audience and revenue, and helps them build a modern media empire in today’s attention-driven world.
