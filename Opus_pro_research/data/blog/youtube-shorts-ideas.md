## A primer: What the YouTube Shorts algorithm likes (and doesn’t like)

With the rise of short form video apps, it is easy to think they are all the same and therefore, use the same techniques across all platforms. But knowing how to create your content specifically for YouTube Shorts can make a huge impact on your results. A big [difference between YouTube Shorts and TikTok](/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-tiktok), for example is time limits. On TikTok, you have a bit more freedom when it comes to time, as your videos can be up to 3 minutes long. Whereas on Shorts, you are limited to 60 seconds. Don’t let this fool you though, and don’t think the time limit is a setback, the algorithm prefers short videos. Why, you may ask? The shorter your video is and the longer it can capture someone’s attention (think the opposite of a storytime that doesn’t begin until several minutes in, so you just scroll away) the higher the changes your video gets watched all the way through, and gets bumped up the algorithm

**To break things down, here are specific factors that the YouTube Shorts algorithm measures:**

1. **Engagement:** What do we mean by engagement? Engagement indicates that your video catches people’s attention one way or another and encourages them to like, share and leave comments on your Short. The YouTube algorithm pays close attention to the sum of these interactions, then pushes your video out to more people if users seem to enjoy and interact with your content. YouTube pays attention to patterns when it comes to Shorts with high engagement and will bump up Shorts that adhere to these in the algorithm. For this, focus on larger trends within your target audience, for example, if you notice story times get engagement and you have a funny story to tell, take a stab at it!
2. **Adherence to trends:** Let’s say the YouTube Shorts algorithm notices videos with a certain sound are currently doing well and keeping users on the app for longer. Your likelihood of going viral if you use that sound will skyrocket! Think about it, we couldn’t escape the “corn song” or most recently the “capybara song” as much as we wanted to, and I think I watched just about every video under those sounds. If you can identify trends in popular videos and use this to your advantage, that puts you on top.
3. **Originality:** While adhering to trends is important, it is also essential you do your own thing. Users get easily bored if they encounter 1,000,000 videos that follow the same script. While Shorts focuses on trends to push the algorithm, it also places value on original Shorts with high early engagement to stay ahead of the curve and catch the newer trends.****
4. **Similarity to other Shorts users have engaged with:** Thanks to its size as the leading video platform, YouTube understands the watch history and interests of its users, and matches your Shorts with them. It uses this knowledge to show people Shorts they might be interested in based on their personal preferences. In this way, the YouTube Shorts algorithm operates more intelligently and personalized than by just showing trending Shorts to random users.

## The top 10 themes you can follow to come up with great YouTube Shorts ideas

It isn’t always easy coming up with YouTube Shorts ideas completely fresh. Here are the top 10 themes we’ve noticed are most successful on YouTube Shorts.

### 1. Talking Head Style

Catchy clips taken from interviews, podcasts, films, and even monologues make for some of the most engaging YouTube Shorts when selected thoughtfully. This style can be leveraged no matter what niche you are in, and you can even use AI software like OpusClip to help generate them for you.

### 2. Funny Videos

Whether bloopers or a funny dog video, people love to laugh and any lighthearted content that makes users crack a smile is likely to do well. Imagine you are a sports creator; you can add funny commentary over a sports clip.

### 3. Motivational Videos

We all love a good motivational video, and the most important part is motivational videos can take on so many forms, from a video of a student studying hard to a motivational speech, this is your chance to get people off their seats and ready to put in some work!

### 4. Educational/Informative

This can be anything from fun facts and trivia to long videos educating your users on a certain topic. Let’s say you’re a science creator, a video focusing on animal fun facts is likely to be engaging and interesting to your niche.

### 5. How to/Step-by-Step Tutorial

While this category could fall under educational, what we mean by tutorials is instructions helping users within your niche achieve a particular goal. Let’s say you are a college student; you can make a video explaining the step-by-step process of college applications. A completely different niche can use this format to teach users how to make a certain pasta dish or even train their dog.

### 6. Lifestyle

We love being nosey and learning more about people within our niche. Lifestyle videoscan range from travel videos to “Get ready with me” videos. Any way you can engage with your audience and show your human side and what your day-to-day life looks likecan help popularize your page. When trying to come up with Lifestyle-related YouTube Shorts ideas, just think to yourself: “What kind of lifestyle content would **I** want to watch?”.

### 7. Before and After

These videos are great for shock value. What I mean by this is here is where you can show off your amazing decoration videos or how simple ingredients became a fancydessert. These can be applied to any niche, from physical fitness to make-up and even construction.

### 8. Product Reviews/Unboxings/First Impressions

In the era of amazon prime, this is your opportunity to share your knowledge and your opinion on certain products. If you are a cooking creator for example, here is where you review that fancy knife set or new appliance.

### 9. Storytimes

Thinking of stories to tell is one of the easiest ways to come up with YouTube Shorts ideas. Here, you can share anything and everything about yourself. Story times help make creators more personable and make audiences see you as relatable. Take advantage of this opportunity and tell your audience your stories!

### 10. Creative short film

This is your time to be creative and film that aesthetically pleasing video we all love.Anything from painting to even cleaning can become aesthetic with the right color schemes and added effects. Visually appealing videos are likely to do well within the algorithm.
