## What is AI content creation? Defining the benefits

Let's start by clarifying what we mean by AI generated content. [AI content creation](https://www.copy.ai/blog/ai-content-creation) refers to the process of using AI tools to create, improve, and repurpose content--whether written, video, or imagery.

**How does it work?** AI tools use machine learning (ML) algorithms to learn and [understand](https://www.copy.ai/blog/ai-content-creation) human language patterns to then reproduce and mimic them at a large-scale. This is the context where you'll hear the term "generative AI," or, AI that is able to create text, images, or video in response to prompts. One of the most commonly known examples of an AI content generator is ChatGPT, for example.

**How can content marketers benefit?** Using an [AI content genrator](https://www.copy.ai/blog/ai-content-creation) can help content marketers meet the demand to create a constant flow of fresh, relevant, captivating content that their business uses to drive leads and convert traffic. Manually producing content at scale requires large teams and more than a drop of creativity--it's labor and resource-intensive to come up with content ideas and then execute them, making it hard for smaller businesses to compete with corporate competitors. AI tools like AI content generators can help you create more content, produce content at a faster rate, create various versions of content to personalize campaings, and efficiently repurpose content for multiple channels, formats, geographies, audience personas, and more.

## Ethical and privacy concerns to consider when using AI

When using AI writers or other content creation tools that produce AI generated content, you need to be conscientious of authenticity and transparency. Here are some [best practices](https://medium.com/@thelaunchboxus/8-best-practices-for-ethically-using-ai-to-accelerate-content-creation-e4563434229e#:~:text=from%20Diverse%20Datasets-,One%20of%20the%20best%20practices%20for%20ethically%20using%20AI%20to,of%20all%20individuals%20and%20communities.) to keep in mind with your AI content generation:

1. Find a balance between AI generated text and human written content. This means having an editor check everything created with AI to at least verify the truthfulness and accuracy. Think of AI as a new member of your team who reports to you, rather than a set-it-and-forget-it solution.
2. Prioritize fact-checking to make sure that you aren't creating misinformation or misrepresenting the truth.
3. Disclose AI generated content to your audience to enhance transparency, especially with social media content.
4. Train your team to use platforms responsibly and intelligently. Always be looking for ways to get smarter about your approach to generative AI.

## The implementation phase: How to choose the right AI tools

When looking to take on AI powered content creation tools, there are a lot of choices in a crowded market with nearly infinite types of solutions out there. To be sure that the tools actually help your team and don't overcomplicate your methods, you'll need to be strategic about how you approach implementation. But, if you do things right, you'll start feeling the benefits of efficiency and scalability fairly quickly. Here are some [tips](https://martech.org/how-to-do-an-ai-implementation-for-your-marketing-team/) we recommend you keep in mind when you start your strategy for AI content creation:

1. **Identify use cases:** There are always going to be endless areas that you could replace with AI solutions, but not every one is necessary for your particular team. Figure out where you are struggling the most, what areas are lagging, and what takes up the most time and budget. Do your copywriters feel weighed down by email subject lines? Are product descriptions taking up a disproportionate amount of bandwidth? Pick one small task that you want to solve for and start there.
2. **Try out different vendors**: Different tools offer different features, with various pricing and pacakages. Don't always settle on the first generative AI solution you find. Speak to vendors, try out free versions of their AI powered tools, and if you're at an enterprise level, connect with their customer success team to learn what integrations and customizations may be possible.
3. **Integrate into existing workflows**: AI solutions will be the most immediately helpful when you insert them into your existing processes, rather than restructuring your whole team around this new tool. If you typically have one content strategist who edits all written content, have them be the person who checks r the AI copy. Or, if your copywriter typically hands off the ad copy through a project management sheet, keep doing that. You don't have to overcomplicate things by reinventing the wheel.
4. **Keep in mind room for growth**: Even though it's important to start with one aspect of your content strategy when choosing a solution, do keep in mind the scalabiity of the tools you test out. Ultimately, it will be easier if you keep most of your written content on one generative AI platform, most of your video content edited within one platform, and so on. Adding a tool for grammar, plus a different tool for captions, a third tool for ads, and a fourth tool for SEO will just keep the labor-intensive feel that you already have without AI.
5. **Eduate the team**: Just becuase AI seems to do much of the heavy-lifting, doesn't mean your team intuitively knows how to use the tools to their best effectiveness. Make sure you put time and resources into educating your team so that everyone knows how to best use the tools responsibly, to help you avoid content marketing mishaps down the road.
6. **Scale from there**: Once you start to get into a rhythm, you can begin to add new tools to your mix and further develop your processes from there. Keep optimizing for efficiency and build a full content marketing methodology that works for you.
