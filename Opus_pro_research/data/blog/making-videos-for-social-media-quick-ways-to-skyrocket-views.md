## Understanding Your Audience: Who Are You Creating Videos For?

Know your audience before you start making video content. Otherwise, your videos might miss the mark on social media and fail to achieve your video marketing goals.

Would you sell lawn movers to people living on a boat?

I didn’t think so.

Start by identifying your target audience. Check out their demographics—age, location, and activity patterns.

Find out their preferences and habits by looking at the analytics on social media platforms:

- Is your audience glued to TikTok and enjoys snappy clips?
- Or is it professionals on LinkedIn seeking insightful content?
- Are they night owls on Instagram or early risers on Facebook?

Knowing these details helps you connect better with your audience on social media. Then you can tailor your video marketing strategy accordingly.

You already know that specific social media platforms cater to different crowds.

TikTok attracts younger users with quirky and fast-paced videos. Meanwhile, LinkedIn is perfect for reaching professionals who want polished and insightful content.

Instagram focuses on visuals and quick hits.

Instagram Stories are great for [short videos](https://www.opus.pro/blog/how-to-make-short-videos-from-youtube-long-videos) that grab attention fast.

Facebook is ideal for longer videos. Here, social media users tend to engage with detailed content.

Finally, YouTube is the go-to for deeper dives, like vlogs, reviews, or educational pieces. It’s perfect for content that demands time and holds your audience’s attention.

Keep that in mind to make the most engaging videos ever. Or you might end up like a karaoke DJ at a library—totally off-key.

## Tips and Best Practices For Creating Engaging Video Content

Video content should be incredibly engaging for viewers. Make them feel a thrill similar to staying up for "just one more" episode.

Here’s how you do it:

### 1. Storytelling

People love a good story—always have, always will. A well-told story doesn’t just capture attention; it grabs the audience by the collar and doesn’t let go.

Your video should have a clear narrative that draws viewers in emotionally. Doesn’t matter if you’re making them laugh or cry. Your story needs to resonate with your audience.

### 2. Subtitles and Captions

Use captions to keep people hooked. That’s especially for those sneaky scrollers who watch videos on mute.

Did you know that nearly [74% of Facebook videos](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-video-statistics/) are watched without sound? Captions make sure your message is still loud and clear, even in silence. Plus, they’re great for accessibility—making your content inclusive for everyone.

### 3. Call to Action (CTA)

Lastly, seal the deal by telling your viewers what to do next.

Want them to visit your website? Subscribe to your channel? Buy your product?

Make your message clear.

Be bold! Think of your CTA like a dating app bio. You’ve got to say exactly what you want. Or you’re just swiping left on opportunities.

A strong video CTA can boost click rates by as [much as 380%](https://www.sixthcitymarketing.com/2023/03/24/statistics-to-know-about-ctas-in-2023/).

Short-form video content is perfect for creating engaging content that grabs attention quickly.

## Optimizing Video for Each Social Media Platform

Got your video ready? Great! But where to post it?

Not all social media platforms are created equal.

You won’t serve a fancy cheesecake on a paper plate, right? Then don’t mismatch your videos and platforms.

Here’s how you can optimize video content for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or Snapchat:

### 1. Resolution and Aspect Ratios

On Instagram, square videos (1:1) and vertical videos (4:5) rule the feed. But Instagram Stories and Reels thrive on 9:16.

TikTok loves vertical too. Keep it 9:16, or your video might look out of place.

YouTube? Stick with 16:9 for those widescreen vibes.

And for Snapchat, 9:16 is best.

For Facebook, a mix of formats can help reach different segments of your social media audience.

### 2. Upload Strategy

Posting engaging video content is more than just hitting “post.”

Focus on metadata, thumbnails, subtitles, and captions. Use relevant keywords in your metadata. Create eye-catching thumbnails. Write snappy descriptions with a clear call to action.

All this increases your video searchability. That’s crucial for effective video marketing. Videos with captions are [watched 12% longer](https://subtitlebee.com/blog/exploring-tiktok-subtitles-rise-video-captions-viral-transcriptions/) than those without. That’s on TikTok, Facebook, and other platforms.

## Analyzing Video Performance

So you’ve dazzled your viewers with killer video marketing. But how do you know if it’s working?

By studying the numbers.

In the world of video marketing and social media, metrics are everything.

1. Watch time is great for longer videos. High watch time means your content is binge-worthy and relevant to your social media audience.
2. Conversion rates: Are people taking action after watching? Clicking links, subscribing, or buying? If not, it’s time to rethink your approach, maybe through better editing or sharper content.
3. Engagement: Likes, shares, and comments tell you how people find your content.

If your viewers are interacting, your social media content is doing something right.

Analytics tools like Google Analytics, YouTube Insights, and Facebook Analytics break down the nitty-gritty. They tell you who’s watching, for how long, and what they do next. Use this data to tweak your social media strategy.

You may realize that a certain platform needs more love. Or your content needs a little extra spice to grab attention on social media.
