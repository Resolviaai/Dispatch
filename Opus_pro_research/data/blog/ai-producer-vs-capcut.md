# AI Producer vs CapCut (2026): Which AI Video Editor Should You Use?

**Short answer:** pick [AI Producer by OpusClip](https://producer.opus.pro/plugin) if you want a talking-head video edited for you inside ChatGPT: upload your footage, describe the result, and get a finished cut with captions, B-roll, motion graphics and music, with most of that editing at no OpusClip cost. Pick CapCut if you want to edit by hand on a timeline, with a big library of templates and effects, especially on your phone. Both edit real footage. The difference is who does the editing.

Below we compare how each one works, what each costs, where each is stronger, and a brief you can run in both. Facts about CapCut come from CapCut's own website, help center and US app store listings, checked on October 7, 2026.

## AI Producer vs CapCut at a glance

- **How you edit.** AI Producer: describe the edit in a ChatGPT chat and review a finished cut. CapCut: a multi-track timeline where you arrange clips and apply AI tools yourself.
- **Where it runs.** AI Producer: a plugin in ChatGPT's Plugin Directory, running in Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app. CapCut: desktop apps for Windows and Mac, a web editor, and iOS and Android apps.
- **Price.** AI Producer: most editing in the plugin uses no OpusClip credits, and the work counts against your ChatGPT plan's Codex usage. CapCut: free to start; on the US App Store, CapCut lists Standard at $9.99 a month and Pro at $19.99 a month.
- **Filler words and pauses.** AI Producer: part of the automatic rough cut. CapCut: a Remove filler words option in its captions tools, plus transcript-based editing.
- **B-roll and graphics.** AI Producer: B-roll matched to your words and motion graphics drawn to match them, placed for you. CapCut: a built-in stock media library, effects and templates you add on the timeline.
- **Extras.** CapCut adds a huge template library, AI eye contact on mobile, and script-to-video generation. AI Producer adds scene restyle, camera movement and auto layout.
- **Inside ChatGPT in the US.** AI Producer works in ChatGPT today. CapCut's page for its CapCut × Codex plugin says it is "Now available outside the U.S.; U.S. launch coming soon."

## How editing works in AI Producer

You install AI Producer from the Plugins section of ChatGPT, open a Codex chat in the ChatGPT desktop app, and attach a video of one person talking to camera. Then you brief it like an editor: the platform, the mood, and anything you don't want. The plugin reads the footage and transcript, removes filler words and pauses, and adds captions, B-roll, motion graphics, music and sound effects. When a choice matters, such as caption style, it shows options with previews in the chat.

You review the cut in the AI Producer preview and ask for changes in plain words. Each change updates the same project, and export renders an MP4 at up to 1080p in 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1. Our guide to [editing videos in ChatGPT](/blog/can-chatgpt-edit-videos) covers the setup step by step.

## How editing works in CapCut

CapCut is a manual editor at heart: you arrange clips on a multi-track timeline and apply tools to them. Its AI features live inside that editor, including Auto captions, Remove filler words, transcript-based editing, AI movement for camera-style zooms, Enhance voice and Reduce noise. CapCut notes that some features are only available in certain regions.

CapCut's chat-style tools focus on making new video. Its newsroom describes Video Studio as focused on "pure generation," and its AI video maker turns a script into a video with avatars and voiceover. Auto Cut trims and syncs clips to beats, speech pauses or text prompts on mobile and desktop. For editing your own footage by chat inside ChatGPT, CapCut points to CapCut × Codex, which isn't available in the US yet.

## Pricing compared

- **AI Producer in ChatGPT.** Transcription, captions, B-roll search, voice enhancement, music and sound effects use no OpusClip credits in the plugin, so you can stay on a free OpusClip plan. Scene restyle, which redraws your background with AI, uses credits. The editing counts against your ChatGPT plan's Codex usage, which is limited on Free and Go plans and larger on paid plans.
- **CapCut Free.** CapCut is free to download and use. Its help center says videos "can usually be exported without a watermark," and that a watermark comes from a default ending clip or a built-in template element.
- **CapCut Standard and Pro.** CapCut doesn't publish a price list; its help center says prices vary by region, device and promotion. The US App Store lists Standard at $9.99 a month and Pro at $19.99 a month as in-app purchases.

CapCut's own pages differ on which AI tools are free. One help article says features such as auto-editing and AI background removal "are only available to active members," while its AI video editor page says most AI-powered editing tools are free. Check the app before you rely on a specific tool.

## Where CapCut is stronger

- **Hands-on control.** If you like building every cut yourself, CapCut's timeline gives you full control.
- **Templates and effects.** CapCut's template and effects library is one of the largest in any editor.
- **Every device.** CapCut runs on Windows, Mac, the web, iOS and Android. AI Producer's plugin needs the ChatGPT desktop app for now.
- **Making video from scratch.** CapCut's script-to-video and generation tools create new footage. AI Producer edits footage you filmed.

## Where AI Producer is stronger

- **A finished cut from one brief.** You describe the result once and review an edit, instead of building it clip by clip.
- **Editing inside ChatGPT, in the US, today.** If you plan and write in ChatGPT, the edit happens in the same place.
- **Most editing at no OpusClip cost.** Captions, B-roll, cleanup and music in the plugin use no OpusClip credits.
- **Graphics built from your words.** B-roll and motion graphics are chosen from what you say and placed on the right words.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose AI Producer if** you film yourself talking to camera, want a polished first cut without editing it yourself, and already use ChatGPT on desktop.

**Choose CapCut if** you enjoy editing by hand, want templates and trending effects, or edit mostly on your phone.

Many creators will use both: AI Producer to get raw footage to a strong first cut fast, and a timeline editor when a project needs frame-by-frame work. ## Try the same edit in both

The fairest test is one brief on one clip. Film a 60-second talking-head video and give each tool this:

Cut the filler words and long pauses. Add bold captions in the lower third, a few B-roll shots on the key points, and quiet background music. Make it 9:16 for TikTok.

In AI Producer, paste it into your Codex chat with the clip attached. In CapCut, build the same edit on the timeline with its captions, filler-word and stock tools. Then compare the result and how long each took.

## Frequently asked questions

CapCut is free to download and use, with paid Standard and Pro plans. CapCut's own pages differ on which AI tools need a membership, so some features may require a paid plan.

Not in the US yet. CapCut's page for its CapCut × Codex plugin says it is available outside the US, with a US launch "coming soon." AI Producer by OpusClip edits your footage inside ChatGPT today.

### What is a free CapCut alternative that edits for you?

AI Producer by OpusClip. Instead of editing on a timeline, you describe the edit in ChatGPT and get a finished cut, and most of that editing uses no OpusClip credits.

### Which is better for talking-head videos?

For a fast, polished first cut with captions, B-roll and graphics, AI Producer, because it builds the whole edit from one brief. For hands-on control and templates, CapCut.

### Does AI Producer work on my phone?

Not yet. The plugin runs in Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app, and a mobile version is in development. CapCut has iOS and Android apps.

### Can I use AI Producer and CapCut together?

Yes. AI Producer exports a standard MP4, so you can take its cut into any editor for final touches.
