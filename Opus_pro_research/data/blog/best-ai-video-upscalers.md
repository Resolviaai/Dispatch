## Red flags to watch for

### Resolution claims with no source caveat

A page that promises 8K from any input is overselling. VEED's own video enhancer page, for example, states that footage not filmed at 4K cannot be turned into true 4K, and that its enhancer adjusts settings such as brightness and sharpness. **Trial test:** upscale one deliberately soft clip and compare it with the original at full screen before trusting the headline resolution.

### Watermarks on free exports

OpusClip's free plan lists a watermark (and no Upscale). **Trial test:** export one short clip on the free plan and check it for a watermark before building a workflow around it.

### Credits that expire or scale with length

Topaz's monthly cloud credits do not roll over, and OpusClip's 4K step adds 11 credits per 10-second block, so a long clip costs more than a short one. **Trial test:** note the credit cost shown for one real clip, then multiply by a normal month of clips.

### Short input caps and download windows

OpusClip Upscale and Media.io both state a 5-minute maximum, and OpusClip keeps upscaled videos in History for 7 days. **Trial test:** run the longest file you actually work with, and download the result the same day.

### No manual override

One-click tools hide model and scale choices; if a result looks plastic or over-smoothed, there may be nothing to tune. **Trial test:** view a face at 100% zoom after upscaling and look for a setting that changes the result.

### Confusing export with upscaling

OpusClip's help center states that its 4K export is not upscaling and only applies when the source is genuinely 4K. **Trial test:** check the source file's resolution in its properties before choosing a 4K export.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI video upscaler?

It depends on the job. For sharpening a short clip inside the workflow where it is also clipped, captioned and posted, we rank OpusClip first (we make it). For dedicated, high-control upscaling on Mac and Windows, Topaz Video is the specialist in this list. DaVinci Resolve Studio suits upscaling inside a professional timeline, and Runway suits teams that also generate footage. We did not test output quality across tools, so test two or three on the same clip.

### How do I unblur a video?

Run it through an AI upscaler or enhancer. In OpusClip, open Upscale from the Quick Start menu, add the video by YouTube link, upload or Google Drive, click Upscale, then download the result from the History tab. See the [Unblur Video page](https://www.opus.pro/tools/unblur-video) for the steps. AI can make soft or compressed footage noticeably sharper, but it cannot fully recover a shot that was badly out of focus.

### Is there a free AI video enhancer for 4K?

There are free routes, with limits. CapCut Desktop is a free download whose upscaler offers HD, UHD and 4K targets, Video2X is free open-source software under AGPLv3, and Media.io lists a free tier with daily check-in credits. OpusClip's Upscale is not on the free or Starter plans. Check each vendor's current terms for watermarks, credits and regional availability.

### How do I upscale a video to 4K online?

Browser-based options include Media.io, which lists MP4 and MOV input up to 5 minutes and output up to 4K, and OpusClip, which runs in the browser and offers a 4K option on Pro, Max and Business for videos up to 5 minutes. In OpusClip the 4K step adds 11 credits per 10-second block (rounded up) on top of the base cost shown on the Upscale button.

### Can AI upscaling add detail that was never in the footage?

Not reliably. AI upscalers infer plausible detail from existing frames. OpusClip's tool page says Upscale cannot invent detail that was never captured, and CapCut's page says the visible improvement depends heavily on the condition of the source. Previewing before exporting is the safest habit.

### Is OpusClip's 4K export the same as upscaling?

No. OpusClip's help center says 4K export only activates when the uploaded source is genuinely 4K (3840 by 2160) and is not upscaling. Upscale is a separate feature in the Quick Start menu.

### Which OpusClip plans include Upscale?

The Upscale help article lists Pro, Max and Business, and not Free or Starter. The public pricing page lists Free, Starter, Pro and Business, so see the pricing page and the help article for the current plan names.
