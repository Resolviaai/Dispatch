# Muse for Small Business: How to Automate Your Video Marketing

**Muse for Small Business can automate most of your video marketing when you pair it with OpusClip.** Muse knows your business, connects to your Instagram professional account and Facebook Page, and handles the busywork. OpusClip turns the videos you already have, such as webinars, product demos, and customer interviews, into short captioned clips ready for Reels and every other feed. Together they give a small team a steady posting schedule without a video editor on staff.

Meta announced the small business version of Muse on September 29, 2026. Here is what it does and a practical plan to automate video marketing with it.

## What Muse for Small Business is

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, launched on September 8, 2026. It runs in its own secure virtual machine with a built-in browser and keeps working after you close the app. According to TechCrunch, Muse for Small Business is free with usage limits, with a paid subscription for more usage. It connects to Meta tools including Instagram professional analytics, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts, and to business software such as Shopify, Dropbox, Slack, Zoom, Klaviyo, Notion, and QuickBooks. Meta says it learns what a business sells, how the brand sounds, and what customers ask about most.

## Why video is where Meta Muse for business pays off

Most small businesses already have video. They record webinars, product walkthroughs, customer calls, and founder updates. What they lack is time to turn that footage into the short, captioned clips that perform on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. That repurposing work is repetitive and predictable, which makes it a perfect job for an AI agent for small business marketing, as long as the editing is handled by a tool built for it.

Muse brings the business context and the follow-through. OpusClip brings the editing: it finds the best moments in a long video, reframes them to vertical with the speaker centered, adds captions, and schedules posts.

## What each tool does

- **Muse:** knows your products and brand voice, gathers footage from Zoom or Dropbox, reads your Instagram analytics, writes the brief, and keeps the calendar on track.
- **OpusClip:** turns long videos into ranked short clips, adds captions and vertical framing, generates post copy, and schedules to Instagram Reels, Facebook Pages, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and more.
- **You:** approve the clips and captions before they go live.

## A small business video marketing automation plan

### 1. Collect the footage you already have

Ask Muse to find your recent recordings:

Find all webinar and customer call recordings from the last 30 days in Zoom and our Dropbox marketing folder. List them with length and topic.

### 2. Clip them in OpusClip

OpusClip accepts Zoom, Dropbox, Google Drive, and YouTube links directly, so there is no need to download and re-upload. Muse can open OpusClip in its browser and paste each link.

Open OpusClip and sign in with our saved login. Submit each recording on that list. For each, keep the 3 best clips under 60 seconds that explain a product benefit or answer a common customer question.

### 3. Write copy in your brand voice

Muse already knows how your brand sounds. Ask it to use OpusClip's generated captions as a starting point and rewrite them in your voice, with one clear call to action per post.

### 4. Approve and schedule

Muse shows you the clips and captions. After you approve, it schedules them in OpusClip to your Instagram professional account and Facebook Page, plus any other channels you use. A reasonable starting pace is three to five posts a week.

### 5. Check what worked

Because Muse connects to Instagram professional analytics, you can close the loop:

Every Friday, look at our Instagram analytics for the Reels we posted this week. Tell me which topics got the most views and saves, and use that to choose next week's clips.

## Five video jobs that suit a small business

- **Webinar highlights:** one webinar can become a week of Reels, each answering a single question.
- **Product demo snippets:** short clips that show one feature or product at a time, for Instagram, Facebook, and your Shopify store's social channels.
- **Customer stories:** the best 30 seconds of a testimonial call, with captions, make strong social proof.
- **Founder updates:** a weekly video from the owner, clipped into a few short posts.
- **FAQ videos:** record answers to the questions Muse says customers ask most, then clip one answer per post.

## Guardrails for a business account

- **Require approval for posting:** Muse separates read access from write access and asks before sensitive actions. Tell it to always ask before scheduling or posting on your business accounts.
- **Use a brand template:** set your fonts and colors once in OpusClip so every clip matches.
- **Check claims:** review any clip that mentions prices, offers, or results before it goes live.
- **Share access carefully:** save the OpusClip login in 1Password, which Muse supports, rather than pasting passwords into a chat.
